# Executive Summary

Research conducted Feb 18, 2026 into physician AI feature requests beyond documentation and prior authorization. Based on 8 Firecrawl queries across physician pain points, competitor analysis (Heidi, Abridge, Nabla), and successful healthcare AI implementations.

<aside>
💡 Key Insight: The market is moving from documentation → comprehensive physician productivity platforms. MedScrub's PHI proxy + CDR foundation provides unique competitive advantage to build these features safely.

</aside>

# 🏥 Tier 1 Features: Immediate Impact & Differentiation

## 1. Intelligent Form Automation (Heidi's #1 Priority)

- Auto-populate insurance claims from visit conversations
- Patient intake assessments completed during check-in chat
- Referral packages with clinical context extracted automatically
- Quality measure reporting (HEDIS, MIPS) from documentation

**ROI: 5-10 minutes saved per patient visit**

## 2. AI Phone Agent (Huge Market Gap)

- Medication adherence check-ins between visits
- Appointment reminder calls with rescheduling capability
- Post-visit care instructions delivered via phone
- Chronic disease monitoring calls (diabetes, hypertension)

**ROI: Reduces no-shows 15-25%, improves outcomes, frees staff**

## 3. Real-time Clinical Decision Support (Abridge's Latest)

- UpToDate integration during documentation workflow
- Drug interaction alerts based on conversation context
- Care gap identification (overdue screenings, follow-ups)
- Billing optimization suggestions during documentation

**ROI: Improves quality scores, reduces liability, increases revenue**

# 💡 Strategic Insights

## What Physicians REALLY Want

1. Administrative burden elimination (forms, calls, coordination)
2. Clinical decision support at point of care (not separate apps)
3. Proactive patient management (risk identification, outreach)
4. Revenue optimization without workflow disruption

## 🏆 Competitive Gaps

- **No one combines PHI proxy + CDR + intelligent automation**
- Heidi lacks clinical decision support integration
- Abridge lacks patient communication automation
- Everyone lacks comprehensive chronic care management

## 📈 Revenue Opportunities

- **CCM billing: $148K additional annual revenue per physician**
- Quality bonuses: MIPS/value-based care improvements
- Efficiency gains: 30-60 minutes saved per physician day
- No-show reduction: 15-25% improvement in appointment utilization
