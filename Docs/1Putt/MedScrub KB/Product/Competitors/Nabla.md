## Company Overview

Nabla is a French AI company building ambient AI documentation and clinical workflow tools. Pivoted from healthcare AI research to ambient scribe with rapid growth. Now expanding into 'agentic AI' — autonomous clinical workflow agents. 150+ health organizations deployed.

### Key Facts

- Founded: ~2018 | HQ: Paris, France (US operations)
- Total Funding: $120M | Series C: $70M (June 2025, led by HV Capital)
- 150+ health organizations deployed
- NEJM AI trial published — validated efficiency gains (rare for this space)

## Product & Features

- Ambient AI scribe — real-time conversation to clinical notes
- Dictation mode — structured voice-to-note
- Clinical nudges — AI suggestions during documentation review
- Agentic AI expansion (Series C focus) — autonomous clinical workflow agents
- API available ([docs.nabla.com](http://docs.nabla.com)) — embeddable AI for other platforms

## EHR Integrations

- Epic integration
- athenahealth integration
- Claims to support 'All EHRs'

## Pricing

- Free tier available
- Paid: ~$119/month per clinician
- Enterprise: Custom
- Market context: Abridge ~$208/mo, Nuance DAX ~$600/mo, Ambience ~$233-267/mo

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:** NEJM validation (rare!), API-first (embeddable), freemium, agentic AI direction, good EHR integrations, competitive pricing.

**Weaknesses:** French company building US brand, no CDR/data platform, no prior auth, 'agentic AI' vague — yet to prove autonomous workflows, no PHI proxy.
