## Company Overview

Abridge is the market leader in AI-powered clinical documentation. Their platform listens to clinician-patient conversations and generates structured, billable clinical notes. Founded in Pittsburgh by cardiologist Shiv Rao, MD. Most well-funded pure-play ambient AI scribe company.

### Key Facts

- Founded: 2018 | HQ: Pittsburgh, PA | CEO: Shiv Rao, MD
- Total Funding: ~$800M+ | Valuation: $5.3B (June 2025)
- Series E: $300M (a16z, June 2025) | Series D: $250M (Feb 2025) | Series C: $150M (Feb 2024)
- Investors: a16z, Khosla Ventures, NVIDIA, Spark Capital, CVS Health Ventures, Bessemer
- Revenue model: Enterprise subscription ~$2,500/clinician/year
- Best in KLAS 2025 AND 2026 for Ambient AI

## Product & Features

- Ambient AI scribe — listens to clinician-patient conversations, generates structured clinical notes
- Revenue Cycle module — AI-powered coding suggestions, charge capture from conversations
- Nursing documentation module (expanded 2025)
- Contextual Reasoning Engine — proprietary AI for clinical context understanding
- EHR integrations: Epic (primary), expanding to others
- Enterprise-only — no SMB/solo physician play

## Major Customers

- Kaiser Permanente (40 hospitals, 8 states — 'largest GenAI project in healthcare')
- Mayo Clinic (2,000+ physicians enterprise-wide)
- Johns Hopkins, Duke Health, Yale New Haven, UPMC, Mount Sinai, Sutter Health, Emory, Corewell

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:** Massive funding ($800M+), enterprise trust (KLAS winner 2yr running), deepest hospital system penetration, own AI engine, revenue cycle expansion, NEJM-published.

**Weaknesses:** Enterprise-only (no SMB/solo docs), expensive ($2,500/yr per clinician), no PHI proxy layer, no CDR/data platform, no prior auth functionality. Pure documentation play — not a clinical workspace.

## MedScrub Differentiation

Abridge sells to large health systems. MedScrub targets independent/solo physicians. Abridge has no PHI safety proxy, no CDR (Medplum), and no prior auth prep. MedScrub is a full clinical workspace; Abridge is a documentation point solution. Different market, different architecture.

🔑 Key gap: Abridge ignores the 200K+ independent physicians who can't afford $2,500/year enterprise contracts.
