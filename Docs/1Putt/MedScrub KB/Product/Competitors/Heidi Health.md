## Company Overview

Heidi Health is an Australian healthtech company building an AI scribe platform for individual clinicians. They are the closest competitor to MedScrub in terms of target market — individual doctors with a freemium model. Founded in Melbourne, rapidly expanding into the US.

### Key Facts

- Founded: ~2021 | HQ: Melbourne, Australia
- Total Funding: ~$96.6M | Valuation: $465M (Series B, Oct 2025)
- Series B: $65M (Oct 2025, led by Point72 / Steve Cohen)
- Other investors: Blackbird VC, Headline, Latitude, Possible Ventures, Archangel

## Product & Features

- AI ambient scribe — listens to consultations, generates clinical notes
- Freemium model: Free tier + Pro at $70-99/mo per clinician ($66.60/mo annual)
- Custom note templates and personalization (Pro feature)
- Patient summaries, referral letters, follow-up generation
- Multi-specialty (GP, specialists, veterinary)
- US expansion: Beth Israel Lahey Health, MaineGeneral, Urology America, GI Alliance

## Pricing

- Free tier: Basic AI scribe
- Pro: ~$70-99/month per clinician
- Enterprise: Custom pricing

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:** Freemium drives adoption, targets individual clinicians (same as MedScrub ICP), multi-specialty, global reach, strong growth, vet market expansion.

**Weaknesses:** No PHI safety proxy, no CDR/data platform, no prior auth, no coding/billing, no deep EHR integration (no FHIR), Australian company still learning US healthcare.

## MedScrub Differentiation

Heidi is our most direct competitor by market segment. Same ICP. But Heidi is JUST a scribe — MedScrub is a full clinical workspace with PHI proxy (trust), CDR (data gravity), and prior auth prep (revenue protection). Heidi has no data platform underneath.

Heidi's freemium model is a challenge — we need a compelling reason for docs to pay more. Our moat: PHI proxy means their data is actually safe, plus prior auth saves real money.

🎯 This is the competitor to study most closely. Track US expansion, pricing, feature additions.
