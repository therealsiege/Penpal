## Company Overview

TORTUS is a London-based generative AI startup building a clinical documentation assistant for the UK NHS. First UK company to meet NHS DTAC compliance for AI clinical documentation. Strong trial results from Great Ormond Street Hospital.

### Key Facts

- Founded: ~2023 | HQ: London, UK
- Funding: $4.2M Seed (Feb 2024, led by Khosla Ventures)
- First UK company to meet NHS DTAC standard for AI
- NHS partners: Great Ormond Street Hospital (GOSH), multiple London trusts
- GOSH-led trial called 'transformative' — significant workload reduction

## Product & Features

- AI ambient scribe — automates clinical documentation from conversations
- 'The Shell' — proprietary hallucination removal system (safety differentiator)
- Surgery Intellect — GP surgery management module
- E-Learning course for clinician training
- NHS-focused compliance (DTAC, ISO certified)

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:** NHS first-mover, Khosla backing, 'Shell' hallucination removal compelling safety story, strong GOSH trial, HSJ report modelled £1B+ NHS savings.

**Weaknesses:** Very early ($4.2M seed only), UK/NHS-only, no US presence, no EHR integrations outside NHS, no revenue cycle or prior auth.

## MedScrub Differentiation

Not a US competitor today. NHS-only with NHS-specific compliance. Worth monitoring: if they raise Series A and enter US, their safety narrative ('The Shell') could compete with MedScrub's PHI proxy trust story.

🔑 No threat today, but watch for US expansion signals. Khosla invested in both TORTUS and Abridge.
