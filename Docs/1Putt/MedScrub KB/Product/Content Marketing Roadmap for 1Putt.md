# Content Marketing Roadmap for 1Putt Health

**Objective:** Establish 1Putt as the go-to advisor for healthcare startup founders on compliance + technical strategy

**Timeline:** 6-month roadmap (March-August 2026)

**Expected ROI:** 2-3x increase in inbound leads, rank for 50+ long-tail keywords, establish brand authority

## CONTENT STRATEGY

### Positioning & Messaging

**Core Positioning:**
"1Putt is the trusted technical advisor for healthcare startup founders — the only firm that combines compliance expertise (HIPAA/HITRUST), technical depth (FHIR/Epic), and founder empathy in one team."

**Key Messages:**
1. **Compliance is not optional** — It's a competitive advantage
2. **HIPAA now saves Series A delays later** — Early architecture decisions prevent costly rewrites
3. **Founders need a technical advisor who understands regulations** — Not an auditor, not a pure architect
4. **1Putt = co-founder mindset** — Small team, hands-on engagement, founder-first

**Differentiation vs. Competitors:**
- vs. Osplabs: Compliance-first approach (not just FHIR implementation)
- vs. HealthTech Strategies: Technical depth (not just GTM)
- vs. Fractional CTO services: Healthcare specialization (not generic tech)
- vs. Itirra: Explicit compliance + strategy focus (not just "bespoke integration")
