## 3. athenaAmbient Objection Handler

### Objection: "athenahealth is giving us free ambient scribing with athenaAmbient. Why would we pay for MedScrub?"

**Response Framework (Acknowledge → Differentiate → Quantify → Bridge)**:

**Acknowledge**: "That's a great point. athenaAmbient is solid for basic note-taking, and since it's free with athenaOne, it's a no-brainer for your SOAP notes."

**Differentiate**: "What athenaAmbient doesn't do -- and can't do -- is find the revenue hidden in your patient panel. It writes notes. We find money. Specifically:

- **CCM Revenue**: We identify every patient with 2+ chronic conditions and automate the monthly care management workflow -- that's $800/patient/year athenaAmbient leaves on the table.
- **APCM Revenue**: New CMS program launched 2025 that most practices don't even know about. $624-$1,032/patient/year.
- **MIPS Penalty Avoidance**: athenaAmbient won't stop you from losing 9% of your Medicare reimbursement. Our MIPS dashboard will.
- **Payer Intelligence**: We analyze your payer contracts and catch underbilled codes."

**Quantify**: "For a practice your size, we typically find $100,000-$400,000 in untapped annual revenue. That's not a documentation savings -- that's cash you're not collecting today."

**Bridge**: "Use athenaAmbient for your notes. Use MedScrub for your revenue. They're complementary, not competitive."

---

### Objection: "We're waiting to see what athena does with Sage AI. Seems like they'll add more features."

**Response**: "Sage is a chart QA chatbot -- think of it as asking questions about your notes. It's useful, but it doesn't touch revenue workflows. athenahealth's CEO literally said 'ambient is a feature, not a business.' They're not building a revenue engine. That's what we do."

---

### Objection: "We already have Abridge / Suki / Heidi."

**Response**: "Perfect -- you've already solved the documentation problem. Now solve the revenue problem. [Abridge/Suki/Heidi] writes notes. We turn those notes into billing opportunities. Our CCM workflows, APCM eligibility tracking, and MIPS dashboards work alongside any ambient scribe. Think of us as the revenue layer on top of your clinical AI."
