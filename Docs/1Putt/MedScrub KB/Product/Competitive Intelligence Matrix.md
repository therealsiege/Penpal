## MedScrub's Competitive Advantages

### Uncontested Capabilities (No Competitor Offers These)

1. **CMS Revenue Program Automation** (CCM, RPM, APCM, MIPS) -- No ambient scribe competitor automates these billing workflows
2. **FHIR-Native Clinical Data Repository** -- Infrastructure-level differentiation
3. **Multi-EHR Revenue Intelligence** -- Cross-system payer and billing insights
4. **Small Practice Revenue Engine** -- Competitors target enterprise; MedScrub targets 1-5 provider practices

### athenaAmbient Overlap (Where We Lose)

- SOAP note generation -- athenaAmbient does this for free
- Basic ICD-10/CPT suggestions -- athenaAmbient does this for free
- Same EHR base -- 99% of MedScrub leads are athenahealth practices

### Strategic Response

The ambient scribing feature becomes a **lead generation hook**, not the product. The sale is:
- "athenaAmbient writes your notes. MedScrub finds the $800/patient/year you're leaving on the table."
