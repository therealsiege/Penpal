## 1. Term

This Agreement shall commence on February 1, 2026 and continue for a period of three (3) months, ending on May 1, 2026, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Section 5.

## 2. Services

Contractor agrees to provide engineering support services to Company, including:

a) Enhancing customer software deliverables

b) Fixing bugs and resolving technical issues

c) Contributing to incubator projects as opportunities arise

## 3. Compensation

Company shall pay Contractor a monthly retainer of Five Thousand Dollars ($5,000.00), payable within fifteen (15) days following the end of each month of service.

## 4. Independent Contractor; Non-Exclusive Engagement

Contractor is an independent contractor and not an employee of Company. Contractor is responsible for all taxes and benefits. This is a part-time, non-exclusive engagement. Contractor is free to maintain other employment or client relationships, provided such work does not conflict with Contractor's obligations under this Agreement.

## 5. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with fourteen (14) days written notice. Upon termination, Contractor shall be paid for services rendered through the termination date.

## 6. Confidentiality

Contractor agrees to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary information disclosed by Company during the course of this engagement.

## 7. Non-Solicitation and Non-Circumvention

a) **Non-Solicitation of Clients.** During the term of this Agreement and for a period of twenty-four (24) months following its termination, Contractor shall not directly or indirectly solicit, contact, or accept work from any client or customer of Company for whom Contractor performed services or about whom Contractor received confidential information during this engagement.

b) **Non-Circumvention.** If any client or customer of Company contacts Contractor directly to request services, Contractor shall immediately notify Company and shall not engage with such client except through Company. Contractor agrees not to circumvent, avoid, or bypass Company in any business relationship with Company's clients.

c) **Referral Obligation.** Any business opportunity arising from work performed under this Agreement or from introductions made by Company shall be referred to Company. Contractor shall not accept such opportunities independently.

d) **Liquidated Damages.** Contractor acknowledges that a breach of this Section would cause irreparable harm difficult to quantify. In the event of breach, Contractor agrees to pay Company liquidated damages equal to the greater of (i) any fees received from the client, or (ii) twelve (12) months of the monthly retainer.

## 8. Intellectual Property

All work product, code, documentation, and deliverables created by Contractor under this Agreement shall be considered work-for-hire and shall be the sole property of Company or Company's customer, as applicable. Contractor assigns all rights, title, and interest in such work product to Company or the applicable customer and agrees to execute any documents necessary to effectuate this assignment.
