# Combined Packing Slip and Shipping Label

Status: On Development
Requirement: After a customer places an order (personalizes a card and completes checkout), one of the assets that must be generated is a combined packing slip and shipping label on a single 8.5 x 11 inch sheet printed at 300 DPI in black and white. 

The Packing Slip will be the top half and will include a preview of the card, order number, order date, shipping details, and order details. The bottom half of the sheet will be the Shipping Label, generated in Ship Station, rotated and centered.

To generate the Combined Packing Slip & Shipping Label, the application shall use the following PDF as the first layer.  [Insert second attachment] 
Then, add the following elements on layers on top, adhering the following size, placement and attributes.

1. Previews of the Customized Card

For cards that are landscape orientation, the preview images shall meet the following width, height and (x,y) placement. The BACK preview of the card will be w=900, h=645, (x,y)=(385,770). The FRONT preview of the card will be w=900, h=645, (x,y)=(230,370), and it will partially overlap the back preview.

For cards that are portrait orientation, the preview images shall meet the following width, height and (x,y) placement. The BACK preview of the card will be w=645, h=900, (x,y)=(640,520). The FRONT preview of the card will be w=645, h=900, (x,y)=(230,370), and it will partially overlap the back preview.

2. Order Details Textbox
Order details will be generated by the application and printed within a textbox that has no border that is w=980, h=710, placed at (x,y) = (1370,640). Font for all text shall be “EB Garamond” 10pt, which at 300 DPI should be 30 pixels tall, and align=left. The text shall be:

”Order #: [OrderID]
Order Date: [Month], [Date], [Year]
[empty line]
Ship to:
[Customer Name]
[Address Line 1]
[Address Line 2], if applicable
[City], [State] [ZIP]
[Phone #]
[empty line]
{left align} Holiday Photo Cards {/left align} {right align} [Cost] (formatted as $ with two decimals) {/right align}
{left align} Shipping {/left align} {right align} [Shipping Cost] (formatted as $ with two decimals) or the text “Included” if no charge {/right align}
{left align} Sales Tax {/left align} {right align} [Sales Tax Cost] formatted as $ with two decimals {/right align}
[Horizontal Rule, 1 pixel, 100% width of the 980px textbox]
{left align} Total {/left align} {right align} [Order Total] formatted as $ with two decimals {/right align}”

3. Card Count Ordered textbox
Below the Order Details Textbox shall be one more textbox that is w=980, h=70, located at (x,y)=(1370,1370), that prints:
”[Number of cards ordered] cards”.  The font shall be EB Garamond ExtraBold 18pt (or 54 pixels tall), and align=center.

4. Shipping Label
The Shipping Label comes from the ShipStation integration, which will always use UPS. The Shipping Label is 4”x6” (or 1200x1800 pixels at 300 DPI), rotated left 90 degrees, and placed at (x,y) = (375,1875).
