## History

Founded 2020 in Iowa by Dr. Jon Lensing (CEO) and Christian Williams (COO). Rural Iowa origins — Lensing's father was the only local OBGYN. Originally sent providers to underserved rural areas; pivoted to exclusive telehealth during COVID-19.

## Business Model

B2B2C white-label telehealth infrastructure — "clients bring the brand, OpenLoop brings the rest." Serving companies that want to offer telehealth under their own brand.

**Notable client:** Grindr/Woodwork — launched telehealth for LGBTQ+ audience on OpenLoop infrastructure.

## Engineering Organization

**Practices:**
- Two-week sprint cadence (aspirational, not rigidly enforced)
- Daily 70-80 person cross-functional leadership sync
- Cloud Center of Excellence committee (weekly)
- Project-based daily syncs for high-priority initiatives

**Known Challenges:**
- Team-by-team variance in definition of done
- Scope creep and MVP definition struggles
- High urgency culture treating everything as "burning fire"
