### Cancellation Flow & Retention

- Chargebee retention module (Brightback) offers significantly more pre-built UI and retention features compared to current Stripe approach
- Tool includes advanced features like requiring users to talk to clinician before accessing cancel button
- Integration will require reintegration work but provides better retention capabilities
- XMD now has option to run cancellation flow without offering coupon

### Microdosing Monthly Communications

- Current journey sends duplicate optional check-in questionnaire that mirrors refill intake form, causing confusion
- Decision made to align with other multi-month programs (ED, TRT) and not send monthly check-ins
- Patients can book appointments anytime without monthly prompts
- Solution will follow HRT model with portal updates for patient visibility
- After visit summary should clearly state when next check-in is due
- Marissa will audit all microdosing patients and coordinate shutting off redundant communications
- Additional complexity with Fridays client who may be moving from monthly to quarterly cadence

### Booking Link Issues

- 50 ED appointments were incorrectly scheduled as medical weight loss appointments for MedVie yesterday
- Issue stems from newer agents using wrong booking links via chat and phone
- Mix of agent training issues and automation problems contributing to the problem
- Some cases involve providers submitting patient support forms that trigger incorrect automations
- Broader question raised about whether separate initial and refill booking links are necessary
- Woodwork currently has eight different booking links across programs, creating poor customer experience
- Long-term solution requires routing based on Stripe subscription data
- New ticketing system through Jira will provide concrete data on booking issues to inform solutions

### Titan Lab Processing Concerns

- Titan claims lab processing takes 4-6 business days, but initial data review shows faster turnaround
- Only 30 patients in system with limited completed test kits, making it difficult to assess patterns
- Most examples show 1-2 day processing times after kit received at lab
- Titan may be looking at different date ranges (ordered to resulted vs. lab processing time)
- Team needs specific patient examples from Titan to investigate properly
- Titan considering moving to different lab provider (By Ash)
- Junction currently provides limited status visibility, requiring manual support intervention
- Need better visibility into patient-driven issues like missing data collection or insufficient quantity
- Junction may have detailed event data in API that isn't being mapped to internal systems
- Sample error statuses exist but sub-statuses explaining why samples failed are not being received

### Healthy/Titan Integration Priority

- Clinical team needs to review integration scope and priorities
- Balancing against other priorities: summary view go-live, replacing Zoho flows, order management system, and speed metrics
- Compliance concerns exist but team needs to prioritize with clinical input
- Providers continue reporting AI accuracy issues in AIEO channel

### Resource Capacity & Tracking

- Discussion about how to track team capacity and determine when additional headcount is needed
- PMO building reporting based on task assignments rather than hours
- Sean working on identifying incremental resource needs across projects
- Leaders should prepare formal headcount plans when requesting additional resources
- Company wants to avoid requiring individual hour tracking for all employees

### Junction Labs Data & Events
