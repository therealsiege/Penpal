# Product Infirmary Health Note

Hi Dr. Clarkson and Infirmary Team,

We are very excited with the utility of the eSpiral “eSpiral” tool. Since it’s inception the tool has grown into a “must have” utility for Dr. Clarkson and meeting his quality goals for the residency program. In short, Dr Clarkson uses the tool to more effectively curate H&L support for every patient his residents see. Quite frankly, without the tool an army of Dr Clarkson’s would be needed to keep up!

**Main Features**

- H&L deep dive workspace
- H&L practice advisory reminders
- Interactive sign off (transitions of care)
- FHIR CDS Service (customizable cards on patient_view)

With these features combined Dr Clarkson can use eSpiral to guide his residents in a new and innovative workflow that goes something like this:

1. Dr Clarkson launches the eSpiral tool from the EHR button
2. He then interacts with the data to curate a list of problems deemed most important
3. Once satisfied with the curated data, Dr. Clarkson creates a practice advisory from it
4. From the tool he uses the tool to record advice for the resident and clicks save
5. When the resident opens the chart, the practice advisory is available help guide the patient interaction
