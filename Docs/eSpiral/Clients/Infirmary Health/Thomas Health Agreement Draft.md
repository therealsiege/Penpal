# Thomas Health Agreement Draft

## **eSpiral Subscription and Services Agreement**

**This Agreement** is entered into by and between:

- **eSpiral LLC**, a Delaware limited liability company with principal offices at [Address] (“eSpiral” or “Vendor”),
- and **Infirmary Health System, Inc.** d/b/a **Thomas Health**, with offices at [Address] (“Customer” or “Thomas Health”),

Effective as of **[Effective Date]**, the parties agree as follows:

---

### **1. Purpose and Scope**

This Agreement governs Thomas Health’s access to and use of eSpiral, a hosted clinical chart review tool integrated with Epic via SMART on FHIR and CDS Hooks, designed to support resident education, clinical oversight, and quality improvement.

---

### **2. Services Provided**

**eSpiral** shall provide the following services:

- Access to the eSpiral web-based application embedded in Epic
- Custom configuration of practice advisories and feedback modules
- Ongoing maintenance, updates, and support
- Hosting in a HIPAA-compliant cloud environment
- Integration support via Epic Vendor Services (staging + production)

---

### **3. Fees and Payment (Waved for year 1)**

| **Description** | **Amount** |
| --- | --- |
| Monthly Subscription | $40 per active physician per month |
| Minimum Monthly Fee | $2,000/month (covers up to 50 physicians) |
| Setup Fee (One-Time) | $5,000 (waived for pilot/early adoption) |
| Custom Add-Ons (optional) | Billed separately upon scope agreement |
- Invoices will be issued on a **monthly basis** in arrears.
- Payment terms: **Net 30 days** from receipt of invoice.

---

### **4. Term and Termination**

- **Initial Term:** One (1) year from Effective Date.
- **Auto-Renewal:** Unless either party provides notice 60 days prior to renewal.
- **Termination for Cause:** Either party may terminate with 30 days’ notice in the event of a material breach not cured during that time.
- **Data Return:** Upon termination, Vendor will return all PHI and customer-specific data in a mutually agreed format within 30 days.

---

### **5. Compliance and Security**

- eSpiral shall maintain compliance with **HIPAA** and **SOC 2 Type 2** standards.
- A **Business Associate Agreement (BAA)** is attached as **Exhibit A**.
- All data is stored in encrypted format (AES-256) and transmitted via TLS 1.2+.

---

### **6. Service Levels & Support**

**Standard Support Includes:**

- Epic Vendor Services-based staging updates
- Slack or email-based support response within 1 business day
- Bug fix and maintenance releases deployed weekly or as needed

**Optional Advanced Support:**

Available upon request for extended hours, on-prem monitoring, or EMR-specific custom workflows.

---

### **7. Intellectual Property**

All intellectual property in and to the eSpiral platform remains the sole property of Vendor. Thomas Health is granted a **limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable license** to access and use the platform during the Term.

---

### **8. Confidentiality**

Both parties agree to protect Confidential Information with the same degree of care used to protect their own. Neither party shall disclose Confidential Information to third parties except as required by law or as necessary to perform under this Agreement.

### **9. Insurance**

Vendor maintains:

- General Liability
- Cyber Liability / Tech E&O

### **10. Governing Law**

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of **Delaware**, without regard to its conflict of law principles.

---

### **11. Entire Agreement**

This Agreement, including Exhibit A (BAA) and any Statements of Work (SOWs), constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes any prior agreements or understandings.
