4. **Project abstract (1,000 characters)**: Summarize your submission briefly, including information about the problem you’re trying to solve, how you have solved/are solving it and how your solution works in practice.
5. **Project rationale, impact and innovation (3,500 characters)**: Describe the problem/issue your submission is trying to address. Who does the problem affect? What is the impact (in terms of reduction of morbidity/mortality, number of people/patients affected, etc.) of solving the problem, both in the long as well as in the short term? In what ways is your project innovative?
6. **Project design and implementation (7,000 characters)**: How did your design address the problem? How did you implement your solution and what requirements must be met to be able to do so? What challenges did you have to overcome?
7. **Project evaluation and sustainability (3,500 characters)**: What kind of qualitative and quantitative data did you gather to evaluate your project, and what conclusions do you draw from them?
