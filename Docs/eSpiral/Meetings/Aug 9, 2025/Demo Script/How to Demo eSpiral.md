# How to Demo eSpiral

### Use your Epic vendor services account to launch a demo of eSpiral

![Screenshot 2025-08-09 at 1.25.33 PM.png](../../../References/Demo%20Script/How%20to%20Demo%20eSpiral/Screenshot_2025-08-09_at_1.25.33_PM.png)

1. Login to https://vendorservices.epic.com/HyperspaceSimulator
2. Create a Simulation using the parameters below to define your launch
- [https://staging.espiral.healthcare/fhir/launch](https://espiral.ngrok.dev/fhir/auth/launch)
- patientId=%EPTID%
1. Generate a url and copy that to your favorite browser
