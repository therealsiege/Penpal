Summary

### Technical Bugs Identified

The user demonstrated several glitches in the patient timeline visualization tool during a live demo with Epic:   

- **Year default issue**: The timeline currently defaults to the earliest year (Q3/Q4) instead of 2026, requiring multiple clicks to advance to the current year
- **Date migration problem**: Medical conditions from late 2024 and 2025 are incorrectly appearing in 2024 instead of their proper year    This affects multiple conditions on the timeline and may be related to the year toggle functionality
- **Single vs. multiple conditions**: Single conditions appear to display correctly, but multiple conditions from the same time period experience a "weird jog" and placement issues
- **Q3/Q4 labels**: These remain unfixed while Q1/Q2 were already corrected
- **Double-click behavior**: When double-clicking conditions from 2018, they unexpectedly jump positions on the timeline

@Clint Johnson indicated confidence in being able to replicate and fix these issues    

### Feature Requests

**Color palette system**  :

- Request for 3 pastel background colors to group related medical problems
- Would allow tracking multiple problem types (e.g., cardiac, GI) in a single view without creating separate canvases
- Colors would act as visual grouping/lassoing mechanism independent of the medical symbol icons
- Pastels chosen specifically so they won't obscure the existing symbols
- @Clint Johnson assessed this as low difficulty to implement

**Medical image integration**  :

- Request for blank canvases to accept copy-pasted medical images (CT scans, X-rays)
- Images would be miniaturized and draggable to appropriate positions on the timeline
- Date information would come from the image itself or be manually entered
- Intended for pivotal diagnostic findings to enhance visualization
- @Clint Johnson believes this can be implemented quickly
