# Aug 15, 2025

Summary

### Project Status

The team is waiting for Epic's approval of their API calls list. Epic representatives (Glenn and Terry) have expressed concerns about security and potentially expensive API calls. The team has addressed the security concerns but is still awaiting final approval to proceed. The consensus is to request access to Epic's test environment (TST or REL) to work through configuration issues, particularly with practice advisories, before deploying to production. This approach would reduce perceived risk for Epic while allowing necessary technical configuration.

### Product Development

The team has made significant updates to both the marketing website and product UI:

- The website now targets residency program directors as the primary persona
- Marketing language focuses on "high-quality teaching at scale" without limiting to Epic specifically
- Product has been reskinned with modern UI/UX using D3 visualization library
- Interactive demo capabilities allow potential users to experience the product without installation
- Color scheme uses healthcare green and trust blue for psychological impact

### Feature Discussion

The core functionality centers around residency education and clinical guidance:

- Practice advisories that can be triggered when residents open patient charts
- Visual representation of patient problems in a spiral interface
- Recording capability for program directors to leave guidance for specific conditions
- Potential for scaling educational feedback (currently Dr. Clarkson manually sends individual feedback to residents)
- Configuration options for how advisories appear (button-triggered or automatic)

### Future Enhancements

Several potential enhancements were discussed:

- Adding a future timeline visualization showing upcoming appointments, tests, or treatment milestones
- Particularly valuable for clinical trials where missing timeline events has serious consequences
- Possible AI integration ("Clarkson bot") that could identify relationships between different conditions
- Potential expansion beyond residency programs once the product proves successful
