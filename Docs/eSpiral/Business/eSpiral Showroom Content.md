# eSpiral Showroom Content

App Name: eSpiral

Summary: eSpiral supports hospital staff in promoting wellness and managing burnout. It enhances situational awareness by displaying a chronological, spiral-shaped overview of a patient's problem list, along with important CT scans, lab results, and symptoms. It allows users to search the records, connecting symptoms, labs, and images in a 3D timeline. Helping create visual reminders for medical discussions, improving the efficiency of patient care discussions during rounds, tumor conferences, and care transitions.
