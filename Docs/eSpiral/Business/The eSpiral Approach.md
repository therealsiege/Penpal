# The eSpiral Approach

Dr Clarkson uses the eSpiral’s eSpiral tool to more effectively curate H&L support for every patient his residents see. In short, without the tool an army of Dr Clarkson’s would be needed to keep up!

The tool provides a way for increased quality, patient problem detection, and communication across the program. Here’s how it works

**Main Features**

- H&L deep dive workspace
- H&L practice advisory reminders
- Interactive sign off (transitions of care)
- FHIR CDS Service (customizable cards on patient_view)

These features facilitate guidance of resident interaction at the point of care with a simple and efficient workflow:
