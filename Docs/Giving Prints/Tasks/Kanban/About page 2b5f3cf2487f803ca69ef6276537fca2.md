Q: Is GivingPrints a nonprofit organization? 
A: While GivingPrints is a for-profit company, we exist to raise money for non-profits. We donate everything to the charities our customers select after we cover printing costs, technology & platform fees, marketing, shipping & packaging, general overhead, a small profit for the investors who helped get off the ground, and income taxes (Yes, the IRS takes cuts into the donations we can make).

Q: Can I select any 501c3 charity?
A: Yes! The organization must be in good standing with the IRS. Occasionally, other extenuating circumstances may make an organization ineligible to participate. 

Q: How much goes to charity?
A: Starting out, we’re committed to at least 10% of revenue. But at scale, we estimate 30-35% of revenue  will go to charity. 

Q: Can I claim a tax deduction?
A: No. GivingPrints claims the tax deduction.

Q: When do the charities receive their donations?
A: Within 45 days after the close of our fiscal quarters. Like most businesses, we close our books each quarter. It also takes us time to track down how to disburse funds for small or first-time organizations.

Q: How can I help?
A: 1) Order cards!  2) Tell your friends!  and 3) If you’re active at a non-profit organization, send us a note. We’re happy to feature charities on the Charity Selection page who help share the word about GivingPrints.
