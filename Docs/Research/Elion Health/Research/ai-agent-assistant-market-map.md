## Exec Summary

Contact center staffing shortages and surging call volumes are reshaping how health systems handle patient communication. **AI agent assistants**—real-time copilots for human agents—are emerging as a key solution.

These systems enhance speed, accuracy, and consistency by transcribing calls, surfacing knowledge, guiding agents during live interactions, and automating after-call documentation. In healthcare, they fill the gap between fully automated voice systems and human expertise, enabling agents to focus on the complex, empathy-driven conversations that still require a human touch.

The market now spans **CCaaS-integrated platforms, AI-first specialists, and healthcare-native solutions**, each with distinct strengths in scalability, sophistication, and workflow integration.

# **What Do Contact Center AI Agent Assistants Do?**

AI agent assistants aren’t meant to replace human agents but to _augment_ them, transforming patient phone calls into a positive and efficient interaction. The goal is to enhance the speed, accuracy, and consistency of human staff, enabling them to effectively handle the complex calls where they’re needed most. Core capabilities of these platforms include:

- **Real-Time Call Transcription and Summarization:** Instantly converts speech to text for live analysis and creation of concise summaries to minimize after-call work.

- **Contextual Knowledge Lookup:** Automatically surfaces relevant information based on the caller's specific query in real time.

- **Live Agent Prompting and Guidance:** Provides on-screen recommendations, next-best actions, compliance reminders, or script adherence checklists to guide agents during conversations.

- **Real-Time Agent Coaching and Quality Assurance (QA):** Analyzes conversations for sentiment, tone, and protocol adherence, and offers feedback or flags calls for supervisor review.

- **After-Call Work (ACW) Automation:** Frees up agent time by automatically composing call notes, updating PM, CRM or EHR fields, and creating follow-up task tickets.

It’s helpful to think of a call center agent co-pilot as a navigational system for patient interactions. While the human agent is driving the car, the co-pilot is mapping the road, providing turn-by-turn directions, alerting the driver to hazards, and auto-logging the trip details after the journey. This allows the driver to focus on the passenger (the patient).

# **AI Agent Assistant Market Landscape**

The agent assistant landscape is organized into three segments: comprehensive, industry-agnostic platforms; focused, standalone AI specialists, and purpose-built, healthcare-native solutions.

## **Comprehensive CCaaS Platforms (Industry-Agnostic)**

Large, industry-agnostic vendors offering AI agent assist as an integrated _feature_ within a broader Contact-Center-as-a-Service (CCaaS) suite dominate this segment. Their value proposition is a single platform for all contact center operations, from telephony and routing to AI-powered agent support. _Examples:_ [Bright Pattern](https://elion.health/products/bright-pattern), [Dialpad](https://elion.health/products/dialpad), [Five9](https://elion.health/products/five9), [Genesys](https://elion.health/products/genesys), [NICE](https://elion.health/products/nice), [RozieAI](https://elion.health/products/rozieai), [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk), [UJET](https://elion.health/products/ujet), [XSELL](https://elion.health/products/xsell-technologies), and others.

## **Standalone AI Agent Assistance Specialists (Industry-Agnostic)**

This segment consists of focused, AI-first startup vendors offering agent assistant, intelligence, or coaching tools as standalone products. Their value proposition is advanced AI that can integrate with a client's existing telephony and CRM systems. _Examples:_ [Abstrakt](https://elion.health/products/abstrakt), [Balto](https://elion.health/products/balto), [Convin](https://elion.health/products/convin), [Justcall](https://elion.health/products/justcall), and [Level AI](https://elion.health/products/level-ai).

## **Healthcare-Native Contact Center Solutions**

This segment includes vendors whose solutions are purpose-built for specific healthcare workflows, with domain expertise, HIPAA compliance, and pre-built EHR integrations. Their value proposition is domain specificity, a stronger understanding of patient interactions, and typically deeper workflow integration into provider operations as opposed to just call center operations. _Examples_: [Cedar](https://elion.health/products/cedar-support), [Comet](https://elion.health/products/comet) by Innovaccer, [Laguna Health](https://elion.health/products/laguna-health), [Switchboard MD](https://elion.health/products/switchboard-md-call-center), and [Verbal](https://elion.health/products/verbal).

# **Looking Ahead: The Future of AI Agent Assistants**

Already, 70% of inbound patient calls at some health systems are [handled](https://www.healthcareitnews.com/news/nebraska-medicine-ai-means-40-fewer-calls-need-human-intervention) by AI, with seamless handoffs to human agents with full conversational context and patient history. That percentage is only expected to [grow](https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-ai-in-healthcare/) as AI adoption continues to accelerate.

While current call center workflows are usually human _or_ AI, some vendors are piloting voice-cloning capabilities, allowing AI to take over mid-call using a soundalike voice when a human agent determines the interaction has shifted to routine information delivery.

As the foundational models continue to improve, the underlying voice and transcription technology will become more and more commoditized over time. As a result, the true differentiators for products will be depth of integration and data feedback loops:

- The best solutions will be those that build the best scaffolding around the core tech, including seamless connections and data sharing with EHR systems, contact center platforms, and clinical workflows.

- Additionally, data feedback loops to continually improve agent assistant performance, as well as develop a highly detailed training set for full automation, will ultimately provide a stronger moat than any core technology.

## AI Agent Assistant Frequently Asked Questions

## 1\. What is an AI agent assistant in healthcare?

An **AI agent assistant** is a real-time copilot that supports human contact center agents during patient interactions. It provides transcription, knowledge lookup, and live guidance to improve speed, accuracy, and consistency.

## 2\. How are AI agent assistants different from AI phone bots?

Phone bots automate entire calls, while **agent assistants work behind the scenes** to augment humans. They handle real-time summarization, compliance reminders, and after-call documentation, allowing agents to focus on complex or sensitive calls.

## 3\. What benefits do AI agent assistants offer to healthcare contact centers?

They reduce average handle time, automate after-call work, enhance compliance, and increase first-call resolution, helping overworked call centers manage higher volumes with the same staff.

## 4\. Which types of vendors offer AI agent assistant capabilities?

The market includes:

- **Comprehensive CCaaS platforms** (e.g., Five9, Genesys, NICE)

- **AI-first specialists** (e.g., Balto, Convin, Level AI)

- **Healthcare-native solutions** (e.g., Cedar, Comet by Innovaccer, Laguna Health)

## 5\. How is the technology evolving?

As voice models improve, **core transcription and summarization will commoditize**, shifting differentiation to EHR integration, feedback loops, and healthcare workflow alignment. Vendors are also testing **voice cloning** for partial AI call automation.
