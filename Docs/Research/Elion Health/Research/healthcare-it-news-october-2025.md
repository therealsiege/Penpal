# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: October 2025 Edition

**Date:** November 3, 2025

---

Colin DuRant

In October 2025, we tracked 105 health tech vendor product, partnership, and funding announcements. This month’s news cycle marks the highest volume we’ve seen in over 18 months of tracking these stats, driven largely by the volume of announcements that accompanied HLTH this year. The key trends we’re watching this month center on: RCM functions moving closer to the point of care as well as continuing to consolidate into full-stack agentic platforms, the growing adoption of AI tools for time-critical vascular emergencies, funding for AI contact center solutions, and the never-ending operationalization of interoperability plumbing.

# 1\. Revenue Cycle Goes Point of Care

A quiet but profound shift is underway: RCM tendrils are creeping closer to the point of care, breaking down whatever “church and state” barrier might have previously existed between care delivery and RCM. Several October announcements point to a world in which billing, coverage verification, and coding no longer occur after the encounter but rather _as_ the encounter unfolds:

- [Optum](https://elion.health/products/optum-coverage-insights)’s Optum Real [pilot at Allina Health](https://www.optum.com/en/about-us/news/page.hub5.eliminate-waste.html) demonstrates real-time claims adjudication and coverage visibility within the clinical workflow.

- [Cohere Health](https://elion.health/products/cohere-utilization-management)’s [integration with](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cohere-health-improves-provider-experience-with-ai-powered-ambient-prior-authorization-at-the-point-of-care-302585634.html) [Microsoft’s Dragon Copilot](https://elion.health/products/microsoft-dragon-copilot) uses the ambient data stream from live visits to trigger prior-authorization logic on the fly.

- [Ambience Healthcare](https://elion.health/products/ambience-cdi)’s [inpatient ICD-10 CDI module](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250930600029/en/Ambience-Healthcare-Becomes-First-Ambient-AI-Platform-to-Launch-Inpatient-CDI-at-the-Point-of-Care-Built-on-OpenAI) and [Suki](https://elion.health/products/suki)’s [expansion into CPT and E/M code generation](https://www.mobihealthnews.com/news/exclusive-suki-enhances-ambient-ai-advance-clinical-coding) illustrate how clinical documentation tools are continuing to evolve from scribing aids into revenue cycle tools.

As AI tools capture and structure encounter data in real time, the financial and administrative processes dependent on that data (coding, CDI, prior auth, care coordination, discharge, etc.) can be triggered automatically during the patient visit. The real-time data exchange unlocks a world of new efficiencies, but also raises questions on how distracting revenue-focused activities might be in the clinical setting.

# 2\. RCM Consolidation Continues Toward Full-Stack, Agentic Automation Platforms

As ambient tooling converges with RCM workflows, traditional RCM vendors continue to consolidate claims management functions with AI-driven point solutions. [R1 RCM](https://elion.health/products/r1-rcm) [acquired](https://elion.health/products/phare-health) [Phare Health](https://elion.health/products/phare-health) for its autonomous coding and CDI capabilities and [partnered with Sierra](https://www.r1rcm.com/news-and-press/r1-announces-strategic-partnership-with-sierra-to-revolutionize-ai-driven-experiences-in-healthcare/), an enterprise agentic AI customer experience vendor, to automate revenue cycle conversations via both voice and chat. At the very end of September, RCM automation platform [Smarter Technologies](https://elion.health/products/smarterdx-smarterdenials) [announced its acquisition](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6838ba8411db86bb9b76b6c5/68db1ec6c31d6a70093b1197_Notes.pdf) of [Pieces](https://elion.health/products/pieces), a clinical summaries tool. Even infrastructure players like [Infinitus](https://elion.health/products/infinitus), [which launched](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/infinitus-launches-model-context-protocol-mcp-server-to-bring-standardized-ai-interoperability-to-healthcare-302566243.html) a Model Context Protocol server to standardize interoperability between AI agents, are racing to define the connective tissue that will let these systems coordinate independently.

In this platform race, the winning vendors will own the logic and context that tie administrative actions to financial outcomes. Health systems will need to decide which layers they want to control, and which they can safely outsource to vendor agents operating on shared infrastructure. The consolidation underway today will determine who controls the “operating system” for healthcare revenue over the next decade.

# 3\. AI for Time Critical Vascular Emergencies Is Leading Clinical Adoption

The clearest signal that clinical AI is crossing from pilots to operations is in stroke. At the end of the month, Baptist Health (Arkansas) [said](https://www.rapidai.com/press-release/baptist-health-strengthens-statewide-stroke-care-network-with-rapidais-deep-clinical-ai) its statewide stroke network now runs on [RapidAI’](https://elion.health/products/rapidai) s “deep clinical AI,” explicitly to connect teams and accelerate diagnosis across multiple hospitals. This is network-level adoption aimed at shaving minutes off treatment times and standardizing decisions across sites.

At the same time, [Aidoc](https://elion.health/products/aidoc) is pushing the edge of where those minutes are saved. Their recent announcements [emphasize](https://www.aidoc.com/learn/blog/stradins-hospital-building-on-stroke-care-excellence-with-ai/) mobile-first coordination and instant image review, enabling on-call neuro and interventional teams to evaluate scans from anywhere, which is exactly what mobile stroke units and distributed coverage models need to work.

And the pattern is spilling into cardiology. [Viz.ai](https://elion.health/products/viz-ai) [launched](https://www.viz.ai/news/viz-ai-launches-viz-acs) Viz ACS to unify ED and cardiology around suspected heart attacks, sharing real-time ECG and case data to reduce false activations and coordinate the team before hitting the STEMI pager.

Health system leaders should treat cardiovascular and stroke AI as a blueprint for broader clinical AI rollout: Start with time-sensitive, high-volume pathways where AI-driven coordination can tangibly shorten time-to-treatment and where governance frameworks can later be replicated across other specialties.

# 4\. Capital Is Flowing Toward the Contact Center

Investor activity in October underscored that the contact center has become a hot frontier for healthcare AI. Companies developing voice-driven patient access and triage tools ( [Assort Health](https://elion.health/products/assort-health) ( [$76M Series B](https://fortune.com/2025/09/30/exclusive-assort-health-raises-76-million-series-b-to-build-on-voice-ai-healthcare-platform/)), [Hyro](https://elion.health/products/hyro) ( [$45M strategic growth funding](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/hyro-raises-45m-strategic-growth-round-to-accelerate-ai-agent-adoption-in-healthcare-302589268.html)), [Attuned Intelligence](https://elion.health/products/attuned-intelligence) ( [$13M seed round](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/attuned-intelligence-raises-13m-to-transform-hospital-call-centers-with-supervised-ai-302576027.html)), and [OutcomesAI](https://elion.health/products/outcomesai) ( [$10M seed round](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20251014914018/en/OutcomesAI-Secures-%2410M-Seed-Financing-Led-by-Sant-Ventures-to-Scale-AI-Enabled-Nursing-Care))) collectively raised more than $140 million in new capital. The funding wave reflects confidence that conversational agents can automate high-volume, rules-based interactions like scheduling, intake, and eligibility checks to ultimately reduce costs and headcount as hospital margins grow ever tighter.
