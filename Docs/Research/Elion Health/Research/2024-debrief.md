# Elion’s 2024 Debrief

**Date:** December 16, 2024  
**Author:** Bobby Guelich, CEO, Elion

**Categories:** AI Ambient Scribes, Electronic Health Record (EHR)

---

Each week, we here at Elion track the latest healthcare IT news, from product announcements to M&A activity and implementations ranging from modestly sized pilots to enterprise-wide rollouts across the nation’s largest health systems.

We also take deeper dives into areas of rapid innovation where AI is shifting the vendor landscape. Here we’re rounding up the biggest headlines and insights we covered this year—along with a few of our own product rollouts—all broken down by the numbers.

## 1\. 2024 was officially the year of the AI ambient scribe.

This category has ballooned to a whopping [62 vendors](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) as of press time, including new rollouts from niche EHRs, practice management solutions, and specialty-specific vendors within even the last month.

[AI ambient scribes](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) were also the single highest-viewed category within [our vendor database](https://elion.health/). The top-viewed AI scribe this year was [Ambience Healthcare](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os), followed by [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla-copilot), [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge), and [Nuance DAX](https://elion.health/products/nuance-dax) in that order.

Our indicators show that buyers are still strongly considering their options in this space, as our January [2024 Buyer’s Guide to AI Scribes](https://elion.health/resources/buyers-guide-to-ai-scribes) remains our most-visited resource.

## 3\. AI RCM felt like a “safer” bet for many healthcare leaders

While clinical-facing AI products are often talked about, revenue-focused AI products are seeing the most demand and uptake.

There seems to be much more apprehension around clinical use cases. According to [an in-depth analysis](https://flarecapitalpartners.medium.com/where-do-healthcare-budgets-match-ai-hype-a-10-year-lookback-of-funding-data-783d52010e29#6350) by Flare Capital, despite almost 50% of health system AI funding going toward clinical use cases, “ **clinical decision support solutions have yielded amongst the lowest maturity rates amongst all health system AI startups (6.8%)** while imaging AI solutions have fared slightly better with a 9.9% maturity rate.” Financial, patient engagement, and operations startups have had better returns, they found.

Within AI RCM, [AI denials management](https://elion.health/categories/ai-denials-management/products) and [AI prior auth](https://elion.health/categories/ai-prior-authorization-for-providers/products) are both the two largest categories and the most visited. Clinical documentation integrity (CDI) is also a big focus for folks browsing our database. [AI CDI](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinical-documentation-integrity/products) and [conventional CDI](https://elion.health/categories/clinical-documentation-integrity/products) both rank in our top 10 most-viewed categories. We also saw lots of buyer curiosity around Autonomous Coding, which we released a [buyer’s guide](https://elion.health/resources/ai-autonomous-coding-buyers-guide) and [webinar](https://elion.health/resources/ai-autonomous-coding-webinar) to cover.

Feel free to check out the report [here](https://elion.health/resources/2024-ai-in-rcm-report).
