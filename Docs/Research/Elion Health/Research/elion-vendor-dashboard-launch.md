## **How to Claim Your Profile**

If you have not yet claimed your profile, here’s how to get set up:

1. **Go to your public profile on Elion** (start at [elion.health](https://elion.health/) and search for your company or product).

2. **Look for the “Claim your profile” bar** at the top of the page and click through.

3. **Create your account** using your work-domain email address. This helps us verify faster.

4. **Submit your claim request.** Our team will review and grant access.

5. **Open the Vendor Dashboard** when you get your approval from us and start updating your profile.

_Note: We’re expecting a high volume of claims. Processing may take up to one week. Using your work-domain email will expedite approval._

## **Tips to Maximize Visibility**

A complete, high-quality profile is the single best way to show up to the right buyers. Once you’re in the dashboard:

- **Tighten your positioning.** You’ll be able to specify who you serve, what problem you solve, and measurable outcomes. We recommend getting tight on who your _best_ customers are and who sees the best outcomes.

- **Fill in structured fields.** These power Marketplace filters; the more you complete (integrations, security certifications, etc.), the more precisely you’ll match buyer intent.

- **Keep it current.** Update product modules, new capabilities, and go-live examples as they launch; we recommend checking in about monthly.

- **Show proof.** Add case study highlights, customers, and demo images or videos wherever possible.

- **Add your personalized CTA.** You’ll have an opportunity to provide either an email address or a link for customers to get in touch with you directly.

All vendors who claim their profile get access to the full Vendor Dashboard feature set for free. Our goal is simple: make it effortless for credible solutions to present accurate, up-to-date information to buyers who are actively evaluating options. There’s no pay-to-play to appear in our Marketplace or improve visibility (other than a robust profile that matches buyer searches).
