# AI Clinician Copilots: Technology, Market Trends, and Key Differentiators

**Date:** January 22, 2025  
**Author:** Patrick Wingo, Head of Research, Elion

**Categories:** AI Clinical Summaries, AI Ambient Scribes

---

EHRs were intended to enhance clinical efficiency and effectiveness.

Unfortunately, they have often made the job more complicated and tiresome for providers, compounded by a looming physician shortage and ever-increasing documentation burden.

The urgent need to reduce time spent inside the EHR has spawned one of the fastest-growing AI healthcare tech categories in the last decade: **AI clinician copilots**.

We include within “AI clinician copilots” both ambient scribe and clinical summary tools. As you’ll see in our report, **these two categories are rapidly converging, with significant overlap in the technology underpinning them, product features, and the vendors developing solutions in these spaces.**

Our newest report, _AI Clinician Copilots: Technology, Market Trends, and Key Differentiators_, breaks down:

- ​The tech under the hood, explaining how these solutions work today

- Key differentiators between vendors within these categories

- ​Where we see these markets going, so you can make informed decisions about where to invest now and whether to hold out for the next generation of solutions
