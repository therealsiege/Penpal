# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: August Edition

**Date:** September 4, 2025

---

Colin DuRant

In August 2025 we tracked 77 healthcare IT announcements across new product releases, system implementations, partnerships, and funding rounds. Epic’s UGM headlined with a push to turn the EHR into an AI operating system, while other health record vendors raced to embed native assistants of their own. Beyond the EHR, AI-driven contact center tools are starting to look “real” enough for enterprise rollouts, RCM agents are targeting specific financial bottlenecks, and health systems are taking a direct hand in shaping diagnostic AI research.

# **1\. Epic’s Betting on AI Agents to Anchor the EHR**

[Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic) [used its 2025 UGM](https://www.healthcareittoday.com/2025/08/21/a-deep-dive-into-the-announcements-at-epic-ugm-2025/) to make clear that it intends to become the AI operating system for healthcare. The centerpiece was a family of agents positioned to handle practical workflow tasks from prior authorization to patient education: Art for clinicians, Emmie for patients, and Penny for revenue cycle. The other major headline: Epic announced Comet, a generative AI system built on Epic’s Cosmos dataset trained on time-ordered sequences of diagnoses, labs, medications, and encounters from millions of de-identified patient records. Comet uses LLM-like architectures to simulate likely future patient trajectories, such as resolving or emerging conditions, complications, and shifting care needs. Epic plans to make Comet accessible to customers through its upcoming Cosmos AI Lab.

In addition to those announcements, Epic sketched an AI roadmap that could ripple far outside its 37% hospital market share. Its ambient scribe, built on [Microsoft Dragon](https://elion.health/products/microsoft-dragon-copilot), is expected in early 2026, signaling a deeper Azure partnership and creating uncertainty for buyers weighing third-party scribes in the near term. Other new features, like SMS appointment booking and AI-enhanced triage and billing, point to the company’s ambition to fold both engagement and automation directly into native Epic modules.

Toolbox integrations rounded out the story, with vendors like [CLEAR](https://elion.health/products/clear), [Caregility](https://elion.health/products/caregility-cloud), [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os), [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk), [NESA Solutions](https://nesasolutions.com/), [Imprivata](https://elion.health/products/imprivata), and [Five9](https://elion.health/products/five9) announcing new in-Epic pathways. For health systems, the implications are twofold: Epic users will need to judge whether to wait for native features or double down on specialist partners, while non-Epic systems face a marketplace increasingly reshaped by Epic’s gravitational pull.

# **2\. EHRs Race to Embed Native AI Assistants**

Epic wasn’t the only EHR vendor to lean into AI functionality and integrations this month. Across the market, competitors are embedding ambient documentation and assistant tools directly into their core platforms, blurring the line between standalone AI modules and native functionality.

[Altera](https://elion.health/products/altera-touchworks) [launched](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250814324693/en/Altera-Digital-Health-Delivers-Ambient-Listening-and-Note-Generation-to-Provider-Practices-with-TouchWorks-Note) TouchWorks Note+, a voice-enabled ambient tool built into its ambulatory EHR. [AvoMD](https://elion.health/products/avomd)’s [integration](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/838799063/avo-announces-meditech-alliance-partnership-to-bring-its-ai-powered-scribe-into-meditech-s-ehr) with [MEDITECH](https://elion.health/products/meditech-expanse) Expanse and [Scribematic](https://elion.health/products/scribematic)’s [“Matic Inside” for small and mid-sized EHRs](https://scribematic.ai/blog/H5VvXLf4S4mapSe6T50h/) both highlight how non-Epic platforms are likewise moving to ensure in-workflow scribing. [Oracle](https://elion.health/products/oracle-cerner) [unveiled](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-ushers-in-new-era-of-ai-driven-electronic-health-records-2025-08-13/) a voice-first AI-driven ambulatory EHR, with acute care functionality planned for 2026, and [athenahealth](https://elion.health/products/athenahealth) [rolled out](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250814198797/en/athenahealth-is-Ushering-in-the-Era-of-AI-enabled-Intelligent-Interoperability-for-Physician-Practices-Nationwide) “intelligent interoperability” to surface the most relevant external insights at the point of care. Primary care EHR [Elation Health](https://elion.health/products/elation) similarly [rolled out](https://www.elationhealth.com/resources/media-articles-elation-health-ehr/elation-health-announces-major-ai-expansion-of-clinical-first-platform/) an expansion of its own AI features across documentation, billing, and clinical insights.

The throughline: EHRs are consolidating capabilities that until recently belonged to third-party vendors, reinforcing that health systems should expect ambient scribing and assistant functionality to become table stakes inside the core record system itself.

# **3\. AI Contact Center Platforms Make Inroads**

AI-powered contact centers attracted some of the month’s largest venture investments, reflecting a maturing market where improving technology and tangible cost-saving opportunities, rather than just efficiency gains, are driving adoption.

[EliseAI](https://elion.health/products/elise-ai) [closed](https://www.eliseai.com/blog/eliseai-raises-250m-series-e) a $250M Series E, [Assort Health](https://elion.health/products/assort-health) [raised](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/26/assort-health-nabs-50m-to-automate-patient-phone-calls-sources-say/) about $50M in Series B, and [Hello Patient](https://elion.health/products/hello-patient) [raised](https://techcrunch.com/2025/08/26/assort-health-nabs-50m-to-automate-patient-phone-calls-sources-say/) a $20M Series A, providing fuel to scale enterprise deployments. On the product side, vendors are expanding scope well beyond call deflection. Innovaccer [introduced](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250729872336/en/Innovaccer-Launches-CometTM-Bringing-Always-on-Omnichannel-AI-Agents-to-the-Front-Line-of-Patient-Access) [Comet](https://elion.health/products/comet), an omnichannel AI suite (not to be confused with the aforementioned Epic AI product CoMET).

Health systems are buying in: Johns Hopkins [selected](https://www.talkdesk.com/news-and-press/press-releases/johns-hopkins-talkdesk-cxa/) [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk), citing Epic integration and AI self-service as key to modernizing patient access. Meanwhile, Intermountain Health [will pilot](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/29/3123221/0/en/Foundation-Health-to-Bring-AI-Powered-Patient-Communication-to-Intermountain-Health-s-Specialty-Pharmacy-Patients.html) [Foundation Health](https://elion.health/products/foundation-health-digital-pharmacy)’s PAIGE patient communication platform to automate refill reminders, onboarding, and benefits checks for specialty-pharmacy patients.

# **4\. RCM Agents Target Prior Auth and Eligibility**

Revenue cycle AI news kept pace in August with a focus on agentic tools tackling costly bottlenecks. Google Cloud and [Inovalon](https://elion.health/products/inovalon-claims-management-pro) [announced](https://www.forbes.com/sites/saibala/2025/08/26/google-cloud-and-inovalon-ink-deal-to-build-prior-authorization-agentic-system/) a partnership to build a prior authorization agent that pairs payer data depth with cloud AI infrastructure. Details of the product will be announced at Inovalon’s annual conference in November. [Innovaccer](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer) [launched](https://innovaccer.com/news/innovaccer-launches-flow-auth-to-automate-prior-authorizations-designed-to-deliver-5x-roi) Flow Auth, positioning it as an end-to-end PA tool that spans detection through appeals.

Capital continues to back solutions that automate revenue cycle functions, with [Arintra](https://elion.health/products/arintra) [raising](https://www.arintra.com/resource/arintra-raises-21m-series-a-to-expand-beyond-autonomous-medical-coding-and-build-the-future-of-healthcare-revenue-assurance) $21M Series A and [Infinx](https://elion.health/products/infinx-patient-access-plus) [investing](https://www.infinx.com/infinx-invests-maverick-ai-real-time-autonomous-medical-coding/) in [Maverick AI](https://elion.health/products/maverick-medical-ai) to strengthen its own automation stack. [Stedi](https://elion.health/products/stedi) [announced](https://www.stedi.com/blog/series-b) a $70M Series B and [released](https://www.stedi.com/blog/introducing-the-stedi-agent) Stedi Agent, an AI assistant that automates recovery of failed eligibility checks.

# **5\. Health Systems Invest in Imaging and Diagnostics Research**

August showed clear momentum in AI imaging and diagnostics, with health systems leading research while also partnering with vendors to accelerate progress. Mount Sinai researchers announced two major advances: [an AI method](https://www.mountsinai.org/about/newsroom/2025/mount-sinai-researchers-use-ai-and-lab-tests-to-predict-genetic-disease-risk) that uses routine lab and EHR data to evaluate the pathogenicity of rare genetic variants, and [a separate tool](https://www.mountsinai.org/about/newsroom/2025/mount-sinai-scientists-create-ai-powered-tool-to-improve-cancer-tissue-analysis) to improve cancer tissue analysis. Endeavor Health and Northwestern University likewise [announced](https://www.endeavorhealth.org/press-releases/endeavor-health-northwestern-university-partner-computer-program-diagnose-deadly-lung-condition) a computer program to identify acute respiratory distress syndrome.

Other health systems paired their research capacity with vendor platforms. Moffitt Cancer Center [entered a multi-year collaboration](https://www.pathai.com/news/pathai-and-moffitt-cancer-center-announce-strategic-collaboration) with [PathAI](https://elion.health/products/pathai) to scale AI pathology across its programs and co-develop new diagnostic approaches. Mayo Clinic [deployed](https://newsnetwork.mayoclinic.org/discussion/mayo-clinic-deploys-nvidia-blackwell-infrastructure-to-drive-generative-ai-solutions-in-medicine/) NVIDIA’s Blackwell infrastructure to support multimodal generative AI for imaging and pathology, an investment that enables systemwide experimentation and model development. Finally, on the implementation side, Tift Regional Medical Center [adopted](https://www.rapidai.com/press-release/tift-regional-medical-center-advances-stroke-care-with-rapidais-deep-clinical-ai) [RapidAI](https://elion.health/products/rapidai) for stroke decision support.
