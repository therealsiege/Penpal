# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: January Edition

**Date:** February 2, 2025  
**Author:** Patrick Wingo, Head of Research, Elion

**Categories:** AI clinician assistant, AI scribes, ACO enabler, VBC population health platforms, AI Ambient Scribes, Interoperability, Population Health Data and Analytics

---

In January 2025 we tracked 80 healthcare IT news stories, making it the most prolific news month out of the last six. [AI clinician assistant](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinician-assistant) news showed up most frequently this month, and just over half of all announcements we covered pertained to an AI product or feature. Here, we’re summarizing the month’s news—finding the signal in the noise—so you can stay abreast of the most important trends you may have missed.

## 1\. Mergers, Acquisitions, and Funding Were the Headlines

It was a slow start to the year, with fewer products and features released this month compared to the rash of investment and M&A announcements. We covered 31 such stories; a few trends we noticed:

- **Funding announcements tended to be later-stage**, on average, than those announced during the latter half of 2024. [Eleos](https://elion.health/products/eleos), [Rad AI](https://elion.health/products/rad-ai-reporting), and [SafelyYou](https://elion.health/products/safelyyou) made Series C announcements; [Qventus](https://elion.health/products/qventus-inpatient) announced a $105M Series D; and [Innovaccer](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer) announced a $275M Series F.

- **Providers’ focus on data infrastructure was reflected in investment activity.** Leaders have been telling us for months that getting data infrastructure in place to support AI implementations is a major priority. We saw that reflected in investments within the data and analytics categories. For example:

  - Intermountain Ventures [invested](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/qualified-health-launches-with-30m-seed-funding-to-develop-the-infrastructure-for-generative-ai-in-healthcare-302345625.html) in [Qualified Health](https://elion.health/products/qualified-health)’s $30M in seed funding.

  - Banner Health and Kaiser Permanente [participated](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250109241872/en/Innovaccer-Secures-275M-in-Series-F-to-Scale-its-Healthcare-Intelligence-Cloud) in [Innovaccer](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer)’s $275M Series F funding round.

  - Virtual care provider WellSpan Health [participated](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250109643850/en/Hippocratic-AI-Completes-141MM-Series-B-Financing-Round-Led-by-Kleiner-Perkins-Valuing-the-Company-at-1.64B) in [Hippocratic AI](https://elion.health/products/hippocratic-ai)’s $141M series B funding round.

  - [Percipio Health](https://elion.health/products/percipio-health) [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/percipio-health-raises-20m-and-unveils-ai-powered-population-health-monitoring-platform-302355568.html) a $20M Series A, including participation from UPMC and [Labcorp](https://elion.health/products/labcorp).
- [Neuroflow](https://elion.health/products/neuroflow-population-health) has been particularly busy. Following [the July announcement](https://www.neuroflow.com/intermountain-health-partners-with-neuroflow/) of their partnership with Intermountain Health, this month they [acquired](https://www.prweb.com/releases/neuroflow-announces-strategic-deal-to-incorporate-intermountain-healths-sophisticated-behavioral-health-risk-model-into-neuroflows-analytics-suite-302344074.html) Intermountain’s proprietary behavioral health analytics model. A mere few weeks later, Neuroflow [acquired](https://www.neuroflow.com/neuroflow-acquires-quartet-health-expanding-customer-base/) the VBC behavioral health care enablement company, Quartet Health (building on its [acquisition of Owl Health](https://www.neuroflow.com/neuroflow-acquires-owl-creating-the-largest-end-to-end-platform-for-behavioral-health-measurement-and-engagement-across-the-care-continuum/) back in June).

## 2\. AI Ambient Scribe Implementations Continue to Go Big

[Back in August](https://elion.health/resources/august-healthcare-it-news) we covered large health systems shifting beyond pilots toward enterprise-wide rollouts of [AI scribes](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes). We saw that continue, with [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) continuing to land major contracts with large health systems.

- [Mayo Clinic](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/mayo-clinic-announcement), [John Hopkins Medicine](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/hopkins-abridge), [Deaconess Health System](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/deaconess-health-system-announcement), and [Duke Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/duke-health-announcement), all expanded their [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) implementations either enterprise-wide or to a significantly larger number of physicians.

- CO safety net health system Denver Health [selected](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/safety-net-system-denver-health-selects-nablas-ambient-ai-technology-to-support-entire-clinical-staffs-wellbeing-302352481.html) [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla-copilot) following an 8-week pilot.

- [ACO enabler](https://elion.health/categories/aco-enablers-and-primary-care-aggregators) [Pearl Health](https://elion.health/products/pearl-health) selected [DeepScribe](https://elion.health/products/deepscribe) to roll out to its 3,500-plus primary care providers.

Of course, the numbers of physicians referenced in these announcements are not necessarily reflective of the number of providers actually using the tools. We look forward to seeing hard numbers from these implementations published in peer-reviewed journals.

## 3\. Interoperability Progress Continues Amidst Uncertainty

The jury is still out on just what the Trump administration might mean for TEFCA (though Elion advisor Brendan Keeler [did give us his best guesses](https://elion.health/resources/brendan-keeler-interoperability-2025)). In the meantime, though, we’re continuing to see large health tech vendors move forward with meeting interoperability standards regardless:

- [MEDITECH](https://elion.health/products/meditech-expanse) [announced](https://ehr.meditech.com/news/meditech-advances-interoperability-standards-with-its-full-support-of-uscdi-v4) full support of USCDI v4 interoperability standards.

- VBC platform [Wellsky](https://elion.health/products/wellsky-careport) [announced](https://wellsky.com/wellsky-solidifies-ongoing-commitment-to-nationwide-data-sharing-in-support-of-connected-care-delivery/) its participation in TEFCA and selected [Kno2](https://elion.health/products/kno2) as its QHIN.

- [eClinicalWorks](https://elion.health/products/eclinicalworks) announced its designation as a QHIN.

- On a related note, [Greenway Health](https://elion.health/products/greenway-health) [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/greenway-health-enhances-uds-reporting-with-uds-capability-for-fqhcs-302337377.html?tc=eml_cleartime) general availability of UDS+ reporting capability within Greenway Insights for FQHCs.

## 4\. Population Health Gets Personal

We’re continuing to monitor the ever-increasing capacity of AI to process and analyze patient records for the purposes of population health interventions. Many of the larger [VBC population health platforms](https://elion.health/categories/population-health-data-and-analytics) are already working to surface patients in need of care _before_ the patient presents with an issue. This month we saw a few newer, smaller startups enter specific segments of this market:

- [**C the Signs**](https://elion.health/products/c-the-signs) **,** [raised](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/c-the-signs-announces-investment-from-khosla-ventures-to-advance-ai-powered-early-cancer-detection) $8M to fund its expansion from the UK to the US. The company leverages AI to analyze data already available in patient electronic health records to “examine a wide number of personal and environmental data points” beyond regular cancer screening filters like age and sex.

- AI-powered radiology risk stratification platform for lung cancer screening [Oatmeal Health](https://elion.health/products/oatmeal-health) [markets](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/781062437/oatmeal-health-develops-ai-diagnostic-platform-to-transform-lung-cancer-screening-in-underserved-fqhc-communities) particularly to FQHCs, offering a solution that utilizes, “one of only 16 AI-specific CPT codes” for Medicare reimbursement, claiming to be a more affordable and equitable option than other outpatient diagnostic support platforms. ( [more](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/781062437/oatmeal-health-develops-ai-diagnostic-platform-to-transform-lung-cancer-screening-in-underserved-fqhc-communities))

- Population health management platform [Percipio Health](https://elion.health/products/percipio-health) [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/percipio-health-raises-20m-and-unveils-ai-powered-population-health-monitoring-platform-302355568.html) its launch, touting RPM functionality without wearables or medical devices. Per the release, “The platform works by using a single mobile app to collect multiple health signals daily … It uses vision-based AI biomarkers for vitals and medication monitoring, and vocal AI biomarkers for brain health assessments, among others.”
