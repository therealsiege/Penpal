According to a California Hospital Association [survey](https://calhospital.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/2022-CHA-DME-Survey-Update.pdf), up to 67% of hospitals report delaying a patient’s discharge due to durable medical equipment (DME) holdups: a bottleneck that reverberates across the care continuum.

Historically, ordering DME has been a manual, fax-based process. Some systems [report](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/strategy/solving-the-dme-ordering-puzzle-leading-health-systems-share-how-eprescribing-improves-dme-ordering-processes-increases-clinician-satisfaction-and-accelerates-discharges/) that just gathering the documentation required for DME can take a day or more of staff time. Delays can drive up costs by unnecessarily extending hospital stays, and in cases where the right medical equipment can’t be obtained, patients might face significant safety risk.

# What Are Durable Medical Equipment Order Management Platforms?

[DME Order Management](https://elion.health/categories/dme-order-management/products) platforms modernize the complex workflow that connects healthcare providers with DME suppliers. The capabilities supported encompass the end-to-end workflow for prescribing, documenting, securing authorization for, and tracking the fulfillment of medical equipment and supply orders destined for a patient’s home.

Solutions typically includes a number of key capabilities:

- **Electronic Order Entry**: Often integrated directly within a provider's Electronic Health Record (EHR).

- **Automated Documentation Prompts:** Guide clinicians to capture the specific information required by payers, such as Medicare's Standard Written Order elements, to create a "clean order" on the first attempt.

- **Electronic Signature (e-signature) Capture:** Eliminates the delays associated with chasing down physicians for wet signatures on paper forms.

- **Real-Time Order Status Tracking:** Provides full visibility from the moment an order is placed until the equipment is confirmed as delivered to the patient.

It is important to delineate this market's boundaries. We consider internal inventory management, delivery scheduling, and billing systems used by DME suppliers themselves outside the scope of this category. Also excluded are solutions focused exclusively on the [fulfillment](https://elion.health/categories/dme-fullfillment/products) side as well as the [e-prescribing](https://elion.health/categories/electronic-prescriptions/products) of traditional medications, which is a separate and more mature market.

# DME Order Management Market Landscape

While the market landscape of solutions supporting DME suppliers themselves is very robust, the market for health system solutions is comparatively small. There are a few solution archetypes serving the needs of DME prescribers.

## Multi-Supplier ePrescribing Networks for Ordering DME

These are centralized platforms designed to connect a wide range of healthcare providers, from large health systems to small clinics, with a broad network of DME suppliers. The platforms enable providers to use a single, streamlined workflow for all their DME orders, regardless of the fulfilling supplier. Their primary focus is on creating "clean orders" by embedding payer-specific rules and dynamic documentation prompts directly into the ordering workflow, ensuring all necessary clinical information is captured on the first pass. _Examples:_ [Parachute Health](https://elion.health/products/parachute-health), [DMEscripts](https://elion.health/products/dmescripts), [Tomorrow Health](https://elion.health/products/tomorrow-health)

## AI & Workflow Automation Overlays for DME Ordering

These are specialized tools that leverage AI and robotic process automation (RPA) to automate discrete, high-friction tasks within the broader DME ordering and fulfillment workflow, such as using LLMs to extract structured data from inbound faxes, compare clinical notes against payer medical necessity policies, and automate back-office processes like insurance re-verification and billing confirmations. They are often used to augment existing systems rather than replace them entirely. _Examples:_ [GenHealth.ai](http://genhealth.ai/), [DME Flow](https://elion.health/products/dme-flow), [Synthpop](https://elion.health/products/synthpop)

## DME Ordering Managed Services & Vendor Management Platforms

This segment consists of solutions that provide a combination of software and services to help specific provider types manage their portfolio of DME supplier relationships. This model is particularly suited for providers like hospices, which operate under a per diem model and must meticulously manage equipment costs. These platforms are not transactional ePrescribing networks but rather strategic partners that provide invoice consolidation, contract management, and performance analytics across a hospice's portfolio of DME suppliers, acting as an unbiased manager to optimize costs and service levels. _Example:_ [Qualis DME+](https://elion.health/products/qualis-dme)

# How Technology Will Continue to Change DME Ordering Software

There are several emerging trends of interest.

- The role of AI is set to advance from task automation to predictive decision support, with platforms beginning to anticipate patient equipment needs and flag potential care gaps based on clinical data.

- Concurrently, the maturation of interoperability standards continues to enable more direct and seamless connectivity between EHRs and supplier systems, further reducing friction.

- Finally, the very definition of a fulfillment network will expand, with retail pharmacies and other non-traditional players emerging as viable, last-mile delivery options integrated into these digital platforms.

## Frequently Asked Questions

## **1\. What is DME order management in healthcare?**

**Durable Medical Equipment (DME) order management** refers to the digital workflow that connects healthcare providers with DME suppliers to prescribe, document, authorize, and track the delivery of medical equipment for patients. These platforms replace manual fax-based ordering with integrated, electronic processes that improve speed, accuracy, and visibility from order to fulfillment.

## 2\. What is a DME order management platform?

A **DME order management platform** is specialized software that streamlines how providers order and track durable medical equipment. Core capabilities include:

- Electronic order entry from the EHR

- Automated documentation prompts to capture payer-required details

- E-signature capture for compliance

- Real-time order tracking from submission to delivery

These systems ensure “clean orders” on the first pass, reducing delays and extending patient discharge timelines.

## 3\. Why do hospitals and health systems need DME order management tools?

Without digital order management, hospitals can face **delayed discharges**—up to 67% of hospitals report bottlenecks tied to DME holdups. Automating these workflows helps reduce avoidable inpatient days, manage supply chain costs, and improve patient safety by ensuring timely access to medically necessary equipment.

## 4\. What types of DME order management solutions exist today?

The market currently includes three main solution types:

1. **Multi-Supplier ePrescribing Networks** – Centralized systems connecting providers to multiple DME suppliers through one workflow (e.g., Parachute Health, DMEscripts, Tomorrow Health).

2. **AI & Workflow Automation Overlays** – Tools using AI or RPA to automate tasks such as fax intake, payer policy matching, and insurance re-verification (e.g., [GenHealth.ai](http://genhealth.ai/), Synthpop).

3. **DME Managed Services & Vendor Management Platforms** – Software-plus-service models that manage supplier relationships, contracts, and cost optimization (e.g., Qualis DME+).

## 5\. How is AI changing DME order management?

AI is shifting from task automation to **predictive decision support**, anticipating patient equipment needs and identifying care gaps before discharge. Large language models can extract data from clinical notes, auto-populate required fields, and help maintain payer compliance—dramatically cutting manual administrative work.

## **6\. How do DME order management platforms improve patient outcomes?**

By ensuring faster, more accurate equipment delivery, these platforms reduce unnecessary hospital stays, lower readmission risk, and enhance home-care readiness—directly improving the patient discharge experience and continuity of care.
