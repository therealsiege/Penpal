In August 2024, we tracked nearly 80 healthcare IT news stories, with [value-based care](https://elion.health/categories/value-based-care/products), [provider and practice administration](https://elion.health/categories/provider-and-practice-administration/products), and [revenue cycle management](https://elion.health/categories/revenue-cycle-management/products) news showing up most frequently. Here, we’re summarizing the month’s news—finding the signal in the noise—so you can stay abreast of the most important trends you may have missed.

## 1\. Epic Was Everywhere in August

### **Support for TEFCA**

[Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic) made [news](https://www.cnbc.com/2024/08/01/epic-systems-will-support-the-governments-tefca-program-for-health-records.html) with [an update](https://www.epic.com/epic/post/your-health-records-are-coming-to-new-apps-heres-why/) allowing users to securely release their healthcare information to apps of their choice. The change represents a [major stride forward](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7224805440050606080/) for the government’s Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement (TEFCA) that Epic is helping to support.

While the update isn’t technologically earth-shattering for Epic, which already has fairly accessible API endpoints, it may help push other EHRs to take similar steps and push forward the adoption of the individual access services purpose of use, one of the major goals of TEFCA. (For more background on TEFCA, see our [primer](https://elion.health/resources/tefca-qhins-guide))

### **Integration Updates**

Six products announced new or improved Epic integrations in both [Epic’s Toolbox](https://showroom.epic.com/stage?id=35&t=31) and [Workshop](https://showroom.epic.com/stage?id=35&t=2). It’s interesting to see more vendors achieve these designations following the [launch](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/epic-launches-new-showroom-website-3rd-party-apps-services) of Showroom (its App Market successor) earlier this year.

- [Nym](https://elion.health/products/nym), [Codametrix](https://elion.health/products/codametrix), and [Solventum](https://elion.health/products/solventum-revenue-integrity) announced they were recognized as [Toolbox-designated in the fully autonomous coding category](https://fhir.epic.com/Documentation?docId=chlistingrequest&section=Embeddedchlistingrequest1Launch). ( [Fathom](https://elion.health/products/fathom) is the fourth and final vendor in this Epic Toolbox category, though we couldn’t find any news on when they were recognized.)

- [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk)’s Copilot [deepened its integration](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240819601443/en/Talkdesk-extends-Epic-Workshop-innovation-with-embedded-solutions-to-unlock-greater-patient-experiences) with the EHR vendor’s customer relationship management platform, Cheers, giving agents a unified experience.

- [Press Ganey](https://elion.health/products/press-ganey) and [Qualtrics](https://elion.health/products/qualtrics-patient-experience) both announced deeper integrations ( [here](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240816343791/en/Press-Ganey%E2%80%99s-PX-Connect-Suite-Delivers-Greater-AI-powered-Insights-with-Epic-Workshop-Integration) and [here](https://www.qualtrics.com/news/qualtrics-announces-new-capabilities-to-offer-a-personalized-healthcare-experience/), respectively) allowing provider organizations to both capture and act on patient surveys directly in Epic’s MyChart.

### **Users Group Meeting @ Epic HQ**

Of course, we can’t leave out that Epic HQ was also the place to be with [UGM](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/hashtag/?keywords=ugm2024) taking place. ## 2\. AI Ambient Scribes Are Going Mainstream

[AI ambient scribes](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) aren’t going anywhere any time soon; the big news, though, is that **we’re seeing health systems move beyond pilots into enterprise-wide implementations**.

- Kaiser Permanente [went live](https://about.kaiserpermanente.org/news/press-release-archive/kaiser-permanente-improves-member-experience-with-ai-enabled-clinical-technology) with [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) following a year of testing and implementation work.

- Reid Health [rolled out](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/reid-health-announcement) [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) to all clinicians following a pilot that found clinicians were able to spend 60 percent less time on after hours documentation.

- Ochsner Health [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ochsner-health-selects-deepscribe-to-bring-ambient-ai-to-their-4-700-clinicians-302209853.html) an enterprise-wide agreement with [DeepScribe](https://elion.health/products/deepscribe).

- Ascension Saint Thomas [announced](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240808480150/en/Ascension-Saint-Thomas-Integrates-Suki%E2%80%99s-Ambient-AI-Assistant-Into-Its-Residency-Program-as-a-Part-of-System-Wide-Rollout) [Suki](https://elion.health/products/suki) would be available to its 700-plus clinicians as well as second- and third-year residents with the goal of eventually rolling out to all residents.

- Northwestern Medicine [is launching](https://news.nuance.com/2024-08-15-Northwestern-Medicine-deploys-DAX-Copilot-embedded-in-Epic-within-its-enterprise-to-improve-patient-and-physician-experiences) [Nuance DAX](https://elion.health/products/nuance-dax) enterprise-wide.

We also saw one of the first examples of an [EHR](https://elion.health/categories/electronic-health-record/products) company building its own native AI ambient scribe, with [Elation Health](https://elion.health/products/elation) [releasing](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240827826984/en/Elation-Healths-Clinical-First-Approach-Continues-with-New-AI-Solutions-for-Primary-Care) its Note Assist product. We’ll be interested to see whether more EHRs go down the “build” path, rather than partnering with an existing vendor to deliver the capability.

## 3\. Digital Front Door Tools Keep Getting Smarter

Vendors are increasingly engaging patients directly with AI agents, despite the fact that [consumers are more comfortable](https://www.bain.com/insights/call-the-doctor-are-patients-ready-for-generative-ai-in-healthcare-snap-chart/) with clinical use-cases than AI call center tools. To whit:

- [Fair Square Medicare](https://fairsquaremedicare.com/) [released a suite](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/fair-square-medicare-launches-generative-ai-platform-that-transforms-customer-experience-for-medicare-entities-302221397.html) of AI contact center [intelligence](https://elion.health/categories/ai-contact-center-intelligence/products) and [agent assist](https://elion.health/categories/ai-contact-center-agent-assistants/products) solutions, called [Vox](https://elion.health/products/medicare-vox), for plan enrollment.

- MUSC Health [is leveraging](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240828064869/en/SoundHound-AI-Announces-Partnership-With-MUSC-Health-To-Transform-Patient-Access-With-AI-Agents) [SoundHound](https://www.soundhound.com/)’s [conversational chat](https://elion.health/categories/ai-conversational-chat/products) agent to manage appointments and answer patient questions.

- [Weave](https://elion.health/products/weave), the [patient engagement](https://elion.health/categories/patient-engagement/products) and [payments](https://elion.health/categories/patient-billing-and-collection/products) platform, [released an update](https://investors.getweave.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/189/weave-enhances-payment-product-suite-with-payment-reminders) that integrates patient payment reminders.

- [NRC Health](https://elion.health/products/nrc-health) [released a suite](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240822567645/en/AI-meets-empathy-NRC-Health-launches-first-AI-enabled-solutions-to-elevate-healthcare-experience) of new AI products for capturing feedback and improving responses.

Only time will tell whether the cost savings offered by AI will spell success for [AI contact center](https://elion.health/categories/ai-contact-center/products) and [patient engagement](https://elion.health/categories/patient-engagement/products) vendors, or if patient preferences will win out.

## 4\. The Front-End of the Rev Cycle is Going Automated

So far, we’ve seen AI-enabled coding make strides in adoption, but we’re excited to see more innovation on the front-end of the rev cycle. While benefits verification and prior auth tools can likewise help improve eventual payouts, they also have a beneficial impact on employee workflows and patient experience, allowing patients to be seen more quickly.

- [Infinitus](https://elion.health/products/infinitus), the AI-powered [benefits](https://elion.health/categories/eligibility-checking/products) and [prior auth](https://elion.health/categories/ai-prior-authorization-for-providers/products) solution, [released](https://www.pharmavoice.com/press-release/20240730-infinitus-announces-ai-ebv-for-patients-covered-by-commercial-insurance-1/) a new instant benefits verification tool for commercial insurance patients seeking specialty medication.

- [Opkit](https://elion.health/products/opkit) [released](https://www.accesswire.com/897167/opkit-rolls-out-back-of-office-generative-ai-call-center-for-medical-practices-with-a-human-in-the-loop-model) its human-in-the-loop GenAI calling platform, which handles [payer-facing calls](https://elion.health/categories/payer-facing-ai-phone-calls/products) for insurance verification, claims, and prior authorization.

- [Valer](https://elion.health/products/valer-health) and [Clearwave](https://elion.health/products/clearwave) [are partnering](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20240813828853/en/) to automate [prior authorization](https://elion.health/categories/prior-authorization/products) submission, status checking, and verification.

## 5\. Acquisitions Center On AI

According to [a Crunchbase survey](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/series-a-startups-more-time-series-b-funding-xai-quantum/), the time between series A and B rounds is currently at its highest point in over a decade. With investments slowing, AI-first startups look like a good (and potentially more affordable in this market) investment to legacy vendors. In that vein, [Stryker](https://www.stryker.com/) [is acquiring](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/08/12/2928353/0/en/Stryker-announces-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-care-ai-a-leading-virtual-care-and-ambient-intelligence-solutions-platform.html) [care.ai](https://elion.health/products/care-ai).

On the flip-side, in the race to become _the_ AI platform, some AI vendors are scooping up organizations with specific subject area expertise into which they can infuse their existing AI infrastructure. For example:

- [VitalHub](https://www.vitalhub.com/) [is acquiring](https://www.vitalhub.com/new-announcements/vitalhub-corp-to-acquire-medcurrent-corporation) [MedCurrent](https://elion.health/products/medcurrent-orderwise)

- [AvaSure](https://elion.health/products/avasure) [acquired](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/avasure-to-acquire-ouvas-ai-powered-smart-room-solutions-302207987.html) [Ouva](https://www.ouva.co/)

- [Health Catalyst](https://elion.health/products/health-catalyst) \[acquired\](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/health-catalyst-finalizes-acquisition-of-leading-care-management-company-lumeon-302217227.html#:~:text=(%22Health%20Catalyst%2C%22%20Nasdaq,United%20Kingdom%20dedicated%20to%20helping) [Lumeon](https://elion.health/products/lumeon)
