# Scaling AI Scribes: Lessons from the Front Lines of AI Scribe Adoption

**Type:** Webinars  
**Date:** June 24, 2025  
**Author:** Bobby Guelich, CEO, Elion

**Categories:** AI Ambient Scribes

---

​As ambient AI scribes move from pilot to enterprise rollout, health systems face a wave of complex decisions: How do you choose the right vendor? What’s the best way to structure a pilot? How do you prove ROI?

In this candid, tactical conversation, leaders from **Memorial Sloan Kettering, McLeod Health, and UCLA** share firsthand insights from their AI scribe evaluations and implementations. This roundtable goes beyond generic talking points to explore the _how_, _why_, and _what_ behind real-world rollouts.
