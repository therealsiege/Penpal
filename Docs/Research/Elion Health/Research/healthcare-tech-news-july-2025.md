# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: July Edition

**Date:** August 4, 2025

---

Colin DuRant

In July 2025 we tracked 84 healthcare IT announcements across new product releases, system implementations, partnerships, and funding rounds. While AI continues to dominate the conversation, the month’s biggest storylines weren’t just about flashy product launches—they reflected deepening investment in productivity‑enhancing platforms, shifting strategies in care delivery, and the quiet but steady build‑out of operational AI in areas that directly impact health system margins. Below, we’ve pulled out the most important themes to help you find the signal through the noise.

# 1\. Mega-Funding Rounds Continue for AI That Can Promise ROI

While the number of overall digital health deals in the first half of 2025 remains [below](https://rockhealth.com/insights/h1-2025-market-overview-proof-in-the-pudding/) historical averages, the money still flowed, concentrated in a number of large funding rounds reminiscent of the zeal of 2021. July brought a wave of nine-figure raises focused on categories where AI innovation seeks to directly impact health system efficiency and patient outcomes.

[Ambience Healthcare](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os) [closed](https://www.ambiencehealthcare.com/blog/ambience-healthcare-announces-243-million-series-c-to-scale-its-ai-platform-for-health-systems) a $243M Series C to scale its ambient AI assistant, which focuses heavily on leveraging ambient documentation to impact RCM bottom-line. With an emphasis on increased productivity and improved outcomes, [OpenEvidence](https://elion.health/products/openevidence) [raised](https://www.openevidence.com/announcements/openevidence-the-fastest-growing-application-for-physicians-in-history-announces-dollar210-million-round-at-dollar35-billion-valuation) $210M in Series B funding to expand its AI-powered clinical decision support platform and announced the wide release of “DeepConsult,” a free physician-facing agent. Similarly, [Aidoc](https://elion.health/products/aidoc), which promises to enable faster diagnosis and treatment, [secured](https://www.advocatehealth.org/news/advocate-health-deploys-ai-solution-to-redefine-diagnostic-excellence-through-agreement-with-aidoc) $150M to accelerate its CARE clinical foundation model, with participation from four U.S. health systems: Hartford HealthCare, Mercy, Sutter Health, and WellSpan Health.

The strong presence of provider investors is notable. For health systems, participation in these rounds is more than a financial play. It helps shape product roadmaps toward real-world workflows, ensures early access to emerging capabilities, and can solidify vendor relationships in increasingly consolidated markets. For the companies, deep provider partnerships accelerate validation and trust at a time when health system budgets remain tight, but productivity-enhancing AI remains a strategic priority.

# 2\. RCM Land-Grab: $1B+ Deals & Full-Stack Suites

If Q1-Q2 signaled growing platformization in RCM, July made it explicit with two billion-dollar buyouts. [Waystar](https://elion.health/products/waystar) [announced](https://investors.waystar.com/news-releases/news-release-details/waystar-acquire-iodine-software-accelerating-ai-powered) a $1.25B deal to acquire [Iodine Software](https://elion.health/products/iodine-aware-cdi), folding its CDI capabilities into Waystar’s end‑to‑end revenue cycle platform. Meanwhile, Blackstone [signed a definitive agreement](https://www.etnownews.com/companies/exclusive-blackstone-acquires-healthcare-tech-firm-ags-for-1-1-billion-details-article-152373213) to buy [AGS Health](https://elion.health/products/ags-cdi) for $1.1B; we wouldn’t be surprised if they roll-up multiple solutions, as [New Mountain did earlier this year](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/new-mountain-capital-brings-together-3-companies-form-ai-enabled-rcm-platform). While not as sizable, the trend was also visible in [VisiQuate](https://elion.health/products/visiquate-revenue-cycle-analytics)’s [acquisition](https://www.visiquate.com/news/visiquate-acquires-etyon-to-supercharge-insight-automation-and-denials-management-for-healthcare-providers) of [Etyon](https://elion.health/products/etyon) to integrate denials management and revenue intelligence into its automation suite. For health systems, the message is clear: The market is aligning with the prevailing preference for fewer, well‑known vendors that can cover the full revenue cycle—reducing the need to stitch together multiple point solutions while providing deeper integration and accountability.

# 3\. Hospital-at-Hospital Tech: Smart Rooms, Virtual Sitting & AI Triage

While the future of at‑home [programs remains uncertain](https://www.aha.org/fact-sheets/2024-08-06-fact-sheet-extending-hospital-home-program), health systems are finding “inside the walls” ways to capture similar efficiency gains through virtual observation, AI‑driven triage, and smart‑room technologies. Memorial Hospital Biloxi [implemented](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250709370739/en/CareView-Communications-Launches-Partnership-with-Memorial-Hospital-Biloxi-to-Enhance-Virtual-Sitting-Services) [CareView](https://elion.health/products/careview)’s virtual sitting solution to expand patient observation without adding headcount. OhioHealth Doctors Hospital [deployed](https://newsroom.ohiohealth.com/ohiohealth-doctors-hospital-advances-patient-care-with-expansion-of-smart-room-technology/) [Caregility](https://elion.health/products/caregility-cloud) smart rooms with digital whiteboards, telehealth, and virtual nursing support built in. Sutter Health [expanded](https://flosonicsmedical.com/sutter-health-hospitals-deploy-wearable-ultrasound-tech-to-personalize-fluid-management-for-sepsis/) use of [Flosonics Medical](https://elion.health/products/flopatch)’s wearable Doppler ultrasound patches for real‑time fluid status monitoring in sepsis patients, and [Connexall](https://elion.health/products/connexall) [integrated](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250708239003/en/Connexall-Now-Available-in-Epic-Toolbox-as-Alert-Manager-Solution) an alert‑management module into [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic) to cut alarm fatigue.

Taken together, these moves reflect a “hospital‑at‑hospital” renaissance: Systems are using AI‑enhanced monitoring and workflow tech to squeeze more efficiency from existing beds, protect clinical labor, and reduce costly adverse events—all while hedging against policy shifts that could curb at‑home program growth.

# 4\. AI Workforce & Staffing Tools Quietly Gain Ground

While ambient scribes and RCM automation get most of the headlines, vendors are steadily working to innovate in one of health systems’ biggest, most persistent cost drivers: labor. With margins still razor thin, staffing remains a top target for optimization—and the latest moves show a focus on tying together scheduling, recruiting, and budget control into unified platforms to drive both reduced operational overhead and better scheduling orchestration.

For example, [Symplr](https://elion.health/products/symplr-workforce) [acquired](https://www.symplr.com/press-releases/symplr-acquires-amn-healthcare-smart-square-scheduling-solution) the Smart Square scheduling platform from AMN to deepen its workforce management suite. [ShiftMed](https://elion.health/products/shiftmed-on-demand) [launched](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/shiftmed-launches-ai-powered-workforce-suite-to-drive-cost-savings-and-operational-efficiency-302505459.html) an AI workforce suite for auto‑shift fulfillment, aiming to reduce reliance on manual scheduling. [StrataJazz](https://elion.health/products/stratajazz) [added](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/24/3104388/0/en/Strata-Decision-Technology-Launches-StrataJazz-Position-Control-to-Streamline-Healthcare-Hiring-Processes-Optimize-Financial-Performance.html) position‑control capabilities so hiring approvals can be tied directly to budget constraints. [Intelliworx](https://elion.health/products/intelliworx-clinician-recruiting) [incorporated](https://intelliworxit.com/press-releases/healthcare-workforce-management-credentialing/) privileging data into its recruiting analytics to accelerate onboarding for credentialed providers.

# 5\. Language & Translation Tech Push Forward

This month [we spoke with Sofi Bergkvist](https://elion.health/resources/innovation-safety-net-sofi-bergkvist-center-for-care-innovations) of the Center for Care Innovations about how essential language support is for providers serving linguistically diverse communities, particularly in safety‑net and community health settings. It seems vendors are gradually beginning to respond to this type of feedback, embedding multilingual capabilities directly into the tools clinicians already use.

At Mount Sinai Medical Center, [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic-mychart-in-basket-automated-response-technology)’s GenAI-powered Spanish ART [is now live](https://www.msmc.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/spanish-language-art-press-release-07212025.pdf), automatically drafting MyChart replies in Spanish to help clinicians respond faster and more accurately to patient messages. In behavioral health, [Qualifacts](https://elion.health/products/qualifacts) [rolled out](https://www.qualifacts.com/resources/qualifacts-iq-assistant-launch-and-clinical-documentation-enhancements/) its iQ Assistant, which supports more than 120 languages. And in the inpatient setting, [Artisight](https://elion.health/products/artisight) [integrated](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/artisight-brings-voice-activated-interpreters-to-smart-hospital-platform-302512481.html) bedside interpreter services in 240 languages via voice activation—bringing instant translation to the point of care and helping fill the gap where live interpreter coverage falls short.

As these capabilities move from standalone services into core EHR and workflow platforms, health systems may find it easier to standardize language support across service lines—making it a routine part of care rather than an exception.
