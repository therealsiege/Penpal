# The Year in Health Tech News: A 2025 Digest

**Date:** January 5, 2026

---

Colin DuRant

In 2025, our team tracked over 1,000 health IT news stories. Here are the trends we saw emerge from the headlines and what they mean for you.

# 1\. Funding: Early Stage Bets and Mega-Rounds for Platforms

## **The Trend:**

As [other outlets have observed](https://www.wsj.com/articles/spurred-by-ai-healthtech-venture-funding-rebounds-b1e2450b), health tech funding did rebound in 2025, but in a more bifurcated manner than in previous eras. Capital concentrated primarily on mega-rounds for AI platforms positioned as foundational infrastructure. In parallel, investors still made numerous earlier-stage investments in automation tools, especially those promising to move margins in the near term, but “middle” rounds were notably missing.

## **Where It Showed Up:**

**The year’s biggest funding rounds were not investments based on incremental, land-and-expand growth, but instead reflect a belief in the potential for AI to fundamentally reshape healthcare’s digital infrastructure.** There’s a clear race to become the underlying “operating system” for provider technology, especially on the administrative side, and early ambient leaders raised significant funding on this premise: [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) raised a [$300M Series E](https://www.abridge.com/blog/series-e), [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os) [raised a $243M Series C](https://www.ambiencehealthcare.com/blog/ambience-healthcare-announces-243-million-series-c-to-scale-its-ai-platform-for-health-systems), and [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla) [raised a $70M Series C](https://www.nabla.com/blog/70m-series-c) this year. The canonical patient-encounter data that scribes own is a key entry point into many other downstream flows, giving scribes a data advantage that has the potential to unseat EHRs as the default foundational system.

But scribing isn’t the only entry point toward platform supremacy. [Commure](https://elion.health/products/commureos) secured [$200M in growth financing](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/06/19/3102223/0/en/Commure-Raises-200-Million-in-Growth-Financing-from-General-Catalyst-s-CVF-to-Accelerate-AI-Powered-RCM-Platform.html) to scale its AI-powered clinical and administrative operating system, leading with data collection, organization, and utilization first instead of aggregating data through tackling individual workflows. Workflow automation, now powered by AI agents as opposed to traditional RPA, presents another path, with [Tennr](https://elion.health/products/tennr)’s [$101M Series C](https://fortune.com/2025/06/18/tennr-health-tech-ai-patient-referral-ivp-a16z-lightspeed-iconiq-series-c/) to expand agentic automation across referral and intake workflows as a key example.

**At the same time, a long tail of smaller seed and Series A rounds clustered around tightly scoped, ROI-driven workflow automation.** Companies like [Ember](https://elion.health/products/ember) ( [$4.3M seed](https://www.embercopilot.ai/nexus-venture-partners-leads-4-3m-seed-funding-in-healthcare-ai-startup-ember) for AI medical coding), [Planbase](https://elion.health/products/planbase) ( [$2.1M seed](https://pulse2.com/planbase-2-1-million-raised-to-launch-ai-native-employee-management-platform-for-healthcare/) for workforce management), [VoiceCare AI](https://elion.health/products/voicecare-ai) ( [$4.54M seed](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/agentic-ai-startup-voicecare-ai-completes-successful-funding-round-302488165.html) for AI-powered prior auth and eligibility calls), and [Ascertain](https://elion.health/products/ascertain) ( [$10M Series A](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ascertain-raises-10-million-in-series-a-funding-to-scale-agentic-ai-platform-302433733.html) for utilization management) focused on narrow operational bottlenecks. Like their larger counterparts, we expect these vendors aim to prove ROI in a narrow wedge first, then expand horizontally as capital and customers allow in the future.

**Mid-sized growth rounds largely disappeared.** According to Rock Health’s [analysis](https://rockhealth.com/insights/q3-2025-market-overview-signals-out-of-sync/) of digital health funding as of the end-of-Q3, “Series B deal flow has thinned, with just 30 raises through Q3 2025, compared to an average of 63 annually over the past three years.” Instead, capital polarized toward either very large, conviction-driven platform bets or small, capital-efficient wedges designed to prove value quickly.

# 2\. RCM: Platformization and Agentic Automation

## **The Trend:**

In 2025, RCM continued to coalesce around the ideal of a single centralized platform, with capital, M&A, and product strategy all converging toward owning the full revenue workflow. Point solutions expanded into multi-point, service vendors pivoted to a more explicit technology focus, and the existing platforms aggressively adopted AI.

## **Where It Showed Up:**

**The clearest signal came from consolidation and large-scale rollups.** [New Mountain Capital combined](https://www.smarter-technologies.com/#news-smarter-launch) Access Healthcare, SmarterDx, and [Thoughtful.ai](http://thoughtful.ai/) into Smarter Technologies, which later also [acquired](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6838ba8411db86bb9b76b6c5/68db1ec6c31d6a70093b1197_Notes.pdf) [Pieces](https://elion.health/products/pieces), explicitly positioning the company as a full-stack AI RCM platform spanning CDI, coding, automation, and analytics. Similarly, [Waystar](https://elion.health/products/waystar)’s $1.25B [acquisition](https://investors.waystar.com/news-releases/news-release-details/waystar-acquire-iodine-software-accelerating-ai-powered) of Iodine Software folded clinical documentation integrity directly into an established end-to-end revenue cycle player.

Even within the last month, [news broke](https://www.reuters.com/business/holt-leaves-new-mountain-leads-30-billion-takeover-its-assets-sources-say-2025-12-19/) that Matt Holt, former managing director and president of private equity at New Mountain Capital, has left the firm and is in talks to lead consolidation of five of its health-tech assets across not just RCM but other areas as well (with New Mountain retaining a major equity stake).

While not as prominent, the trend was seen in smaller acquisition and partnership deals, too. [R1 RCM](https://elion.health/products/r1-rcm) [acquired](https://www.r1rcm.com/news-and-press/r1-to-acquire-phare-health-a-leading-ai-platform-for-automating-inpatient-coding-and-pre-bill-clinical-documentation-improvement/) Phare Health for autonomous coding and CDI, then [partnered](https://www.r1rcm.com/news-and-press/r1-announces-strategic-partnership-with-sierra-to-revolutionize-ai-driven-experiences-in-healthcare/) with Sierra to automate revenue cycle conversations via voice and chat, and [partnered](https://www.r1rcm.com/news-and-press/r1-launches-r37-ai-lab-in-partnership-with-palantir/) with Palantir on an “AI lab” to improve healthcare financial performance. [VisiQuate](https://elion.health/products/visiquate-revenue-cycle-analytics) [acquired](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/visiquate-acquires-rotera-to-advance-ai-driven-revenue-cycle-automation-302410866.html) Rotera [and later](https://www.visiquate.com/news/visiquate-acquires-etyon-to-supercharge-insight-automation-and-denials-management-for-healthcare-providers) [Etyon](https://elion.health/products/etyon) to extend its automation footprint into revenue intelligence and denials management. [Infinx](https://elion.health/products/infinx-patient-access-plus-platform) acquired i3 Verticals’ healthcare RCM business while simultaneously expanding Epic integrations to embed automation across prior auth, eligibility, and billing workflows.

**At the same time, RCM functionality crept upstream toward clinical workflows.** Abridge specifically publicized RCM gains following pilots at [Riverside](https://www.abridge.com/blog/riverside-data) and [Sharp](https://www.abridge.com/blog/sharp-abridge), while [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-cdi) expanded [inpatient CDI](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250930600029/en/Ambience-Healthcare-Becomes-First-Ambient-AI-Platform-to-Launch-Inpatient-CDI-at-the-Point-of-Care-Built-on-OpenAI) and point-of-care coding capabilities. [Cohere Health](https://elion.health/products/cohere-utilization-management)’s [integration](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cohere-health-improves-provider-experience-with-ai-powered-ambient-prior-authorization-at-the-point-of-care-302585634.html) with [Microsoft Dragon Copilot](https://elion.health/products/microsoft-dragon-copilot), and [Abridge’s](https://elion.health/products/abridge) earlier [announcement](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/highmark-health-ahn-abridge-prior-authorization) with Highmark, both illustrated how ambient clinical data could trigger utilization management and prior authorization logic in real time.

**And finally, 2025 felt like the “Year of the Agent” for RCM as agentic automation became a critical enabling technology across the revenue cycle.** Some notable examples include [Innovaccer's](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer) [launch](https://innovaccer.com/news/innovaccer-launches-flow-auth-to-automate-prior-authorizations-designed-to-deliver-5x-roi) of Flow Auth, an end-to-end PA automation solution, and [Stedi's](https://elion.health/products/stedi) [release of](https://www.stedi.com/blog/introducing-the-stedi-agent) Stedi Agent to automate failed eligibility checks. Infrastructure players like [Infinitus](https://elion.health/products/infinitus) [launched](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/infinitus-launches-model-context-protocol-mcp-server-to-bring-standardized-ai-interoperability-to-healthcare-302566243.html) a Model Context Protocol server to standardize interoperability between AI agents. Orchestration of agents remains a key aspect of AI value realization, and one not yet fully served by existing solutions.

# 3\. AI Scribes: Urgency Beats Roadmaps and the Market Keeps Moving

## **The Trend:**

AI scribes moved from a promising, but nascent technology to a common piece of foundational administrative technology for providers in 2025. Even Epic’s announcement of a forthcoming native ambient solution hasn’t slowed adoption of individual solutions. Health systems continued to make meaningful commitments to the major scribe solutions instead of waiting to see how the EHR-native solutions play out.

## **Where It Showed Up:**

**Enterprise-scale rollouts defined the AI scribe market.** [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) announced system-wide or significantly expanded implementations at [Mayo Clinic](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/mayo-clinic-announcement), [Duke Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/duke-health-announcement), [Deaconess Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/deaconess-health-system-announcement), [UNC Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/unc-health-abridge), [Inova Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/inova-health-abridge), [Akron Children’s](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/akron-childrens-abridge), [Memorial Sloan Kettering](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/msk-abridge), and others. [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os) similarly deepened its footprint with [Cleveland Clinic](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/cleveland-clinic-announces-the-rollout-of-ambience-healthcares-ai-plat-987308), [Houston Methodist](https://www.houstonmethodist.org/leading-medicine-blog/articles/2025/sep/ambient-tech-in-hospitals-lightening-the-note-taking-load/), [Onvida Health](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/onvida-health-and-ambience-healthcare-launch-frontier-ambient-ai-capa-1067943), and [John Muir Health](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/john-muir-health-announces-the-full-enterprise-rollout-of-ambience-hea-983017), highlighting its use not just for documentation, but for CDI and point-of-care coding.

**And adoption didn’t just come from the large academic systems.** Safety net health system, [Denver Health](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/safety-net-system-denver-health-selects-nablas-ambient-ai-technology-to-support-entire-clinical-staffs-wellbeing-302352481.html), and FQHC, [Neighborhood Healthcare](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/neighborhood-healthcare-integrates-nablas-ambient-ai-to-support-clinicians-and-improve-patient-experience-302437267.html) (among others), selected [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla-copilot), and [Altera](https://elion.health/products/paragon-denali) [announced](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250417519451/en/Paragon-Denali-Brings-Ambient-Listening-to-Rural-Critical-Access-and-Community-Hospitals-with-Nabla) that [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla) is now integrated with Paragon Denali, its EHR for rural, critical access, and community hospitals. Community hospitals and safety-net providers saw a noticeable uptick in scribe adoption over the course of the year.

**One of the year’s biggest AI scribe headlines came at Epic’s 2025 UGM, where the EHR announced a native ambient scribe, expected in early 2026.** However, this announcement and a similar one from [athenahealth](https://www.athenahealth.com/press-releases/ai-native-clinical-encounter-transforms-ehr) haven’t yet stalled the market. In the months following UGM, deals that were presumably well underway already, like Hartford HealthCare, Houston Methodist, Onvida Health, and others, continued rolling out. We’ll be interested to see the impact of Epic’s announced features as they reach the market in 2026.

# 4\. Patient Engagement & Voice AI: Capital and Innovation Outpace Adoption (For Now)

## **The Trend:**

AI-powered patient experience solutions, particularly voice AI agents, saw an explosion of product launches and funding in 2025, but adoption remains fragmented. Innovation outpaced implementation, and the market is beginning to look crowded well before it looks proven.

## **Where It Showed Up:**

**Across 2025, vendors moved quickly to add AI-driven patient engagement and voice capabilities.** [Innovaccer](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer-agents-of-care) [launched](https://innovaccer.com/resources/news/innovaccer-launches-agents-of-care-to-transform-healthcare-operations-enhance-care-delivery) a suite of AI agents spanning scheduling, intake, referrals, and prior authorization documentation. In a single month, [Cedar](https://elion.health/products/cedar-support) ( [Kora](https://www.cedar.com/all-press/cedar-unveils-agentic-ai-purpose-built-for-healthcare-billing-aiming-to-help-providers-cut-costs-with-a-30-reduction-in-patient-billing-calls/)), [Relatient](https://elion.health/products/relatient) ( [Dash Voice AI](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20250430853547/en/Relatient-Launches-Dash-Voice-AI-Agent-to-Automate-Patient-Appointment-Management-in-Call-Centers)), [Zo by Zocdoc](https://elion.health/products/zo), [SpinSci](https://elion.health/products/spinsci) \+ [Omilia](https://elion.health/products/omilia), and [OhMD](https://elion.health/products/ohmd) \+ [Twilio](https://elion.health/products/twilio) ( [Nia](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ohmd-launches-nia-a-human-in-the-loop-ai-voice-assistant-to-transform-healthcare-communication-powered-by-twilios-conversationrelay-302454920.html)) all released new voice AI agents for patient engagement tasks ranging from inbound scheduling and billing calls to general inquiry handling.

**Despite the volume of announcements, large, named health system implementations were limited.** A few examples of real-world implementation include Johns Hopkins Health System’s [selection](https://www.talkdesk.com/news-and-press/press-releases/johns-hopkins-talkdesk-cxa/) of [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk) to modernize patient access, and Intermountain Health’s [pilot](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/29/3123221/0/en/Foundation-Health-to-Bring-AI-Powered-Patient-Communication-to-Intermountain-Health-s-Specialty-Pharmacy-Patients.html) of [Foundation Health](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/07/29/3123221/0/en/Foundation-Health-to-Bring-AI-Powered-Patient-Communication-to-Intermountain-Health-s-Specialty-Pharmacy-Patients.html)’s PAIGE platform for pharmacy communications.

**Investor interest, however, was unmistakable.** AI contact center and voice engagement vendors raised some of the year’s largest rounds, including [EliseAI](https://elion.health/products/elise-ai)’s [$250M Series E](https://www.eliseai.com/blog/eliseai-raises-250m-series-e), [Assort Health](https://elion.health/products/assort-health)’s [$76M Series B](https://fortune.com/2025/09/30/exclusive-assort-health-raises-76-million-series-b-to-build-on-voice-ai-healthcare-platform/), and [Hello Patient](https://elion.health/products/hello-patient)’s [$22.5M Series A](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/hello-patient-secures-225m-investors-bet-ai-voice-agent-growth). These raises reflected confidence in the contact center as a cost-reduction wedge, even as broad-based provider adoption remained uneven.
