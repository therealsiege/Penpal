# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: May Edition

**Date:** June 2, 2025  
**Author:** Patrick Wingo, Head of Research, Elion

**Categories:** clinician assistants, RCM, AI imaging, AI RCM

---

In May 2025 we tracked 96 healthcare IT announcements across new product releases, system implementations, partnerships, and funding rounds. AI-powered tools accounted for 68% of the announcements we reviewed—comparable to the past two months and significantly higher than last year—driven by continued rollout of [clinician assistants](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinician-assistant), [RCM](https://elion.health/categories/ai-rcm) consolidation, and [AI imaging](https://elion.health/categories/ai-imaging) enhancements. Below, we’ve pulled out the most important themes to help you track what’s being built, bought, and implemented.

# 1\. Patient Engagement Vendors Rush to Add Voice AI

This month, at least five vendors— [Cedar](https://elion.health/products/cedar) ( [Kora](https://www.cedar.com/all-press/cedar-unveils-agentic-ai-purpose-built-for-healthcare-billing-aiming-to-help-providers-cut-costs-with-a-30-reduction-in-patient-billing-calls/)), [Relatient](https://elion.health/products/relatient) ( [Dash Voice AI](https://businesswire.com/news/home/20250430853547/en/Relatient-Launches-Dash-Voice-AI-Agent-to-Automate-Patient-Appointment-Management-in-Call-Centers)), [Zo by Zocdoc](https://elion.health/products/zo), [SpinSci](https://elion.health/products/spinsci) \+ [Omilia](https://elion.health/products/omilia), and [OhMD](https://elion.health/products/ohmd) \+ [Twilio](https://elion.health/products/twilio) ( [Nia](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/ohmd-launches-nia-a-human-in-the-loop-ai-voice-assistant-to-transform-healthcare-communication-powered-by-twilios-conversationrelay-302454920.html))—released new voice AI agents for [patient engagement](https://elion.health/categories/patient-engagement/products) tasks ranging from inbound scheduling and billing calls to general inquiry handling. While health system adoption is still slow (Allina Health was the only health system adoption this month, [partnering](https://investors.soundhound.com/news-releases/news-release-details/soundhound-ai-and-allina-health-launch-ai-agent-redefine-patient) with [SoundHound](https://elion.health/products/soundhound-amelia)), the volume and similarity of new releases suggest the category is moving rapidly toward commoditization. Most offerings now include basic conversational capabilities, self-service routing, and escalation to live staff. The question no longer seems to be can we build this, but which vendor has the reach, integrations, or go-to-market strength to win?

# 2\. AI Clinician Assistants Reach Pediatrics and Expand Across EHR Ecosystems

AI scribes and clinician copilots continued to scale across diverse care settings and EHR environments.

**Increased coverage of specialties and settings:** [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) [rolled out](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/abridge-rolls-out-customized-well-visit-note-pediatric-primary-care) a pediatric-specific well visit note template and was also [selected](https://www.childrensdayton.org/the-hub/dayton-childrens-partners-abridge-enhance-care-conversations) by Dayton Children’s Hospital to support [ambient documentation](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products). Meanwhile, [Office Practicum](https://elion.health/products/office-practicum) [integrated](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250409202198/en/Insight-Health-and-Office-Practicum-Announce-Strategic-Partnership-to-Transform-Pediatric-Documentation-with-AI) a white-labeled version of [InsightHealth’s ambient scribe](https://elion.health/products/insight-health%E2%80%99s-ambient-ai-scribe) for pediatric practices. [Videra Health](https://elion.health/products/videra-health) [launched](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/04/30/3071720/0/en/Videra-Health-Announces-the-Launch-of-Group-Notes-the-First-of-It-s-Kind-AI-Powered-Group-Therapy-Documentation-Solution-That-Automatically-Identifies-Patient-Voices-in-a-Group-Set.html) AI-powered Group Notes for behavioral health group therapy. McFarland Clinic, a multi-specialty provider, [expanded](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/mcfarland-clinic-taps-nabla-to-support-clinicians-and-streamline-documentation-in-epic-302459795.html) its deployment of [Nabla](https://elion.health/products/nabla-copilot) across 12 specialties, while Onvida Health [began testing](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/onvida-health-and-ambience-healthcare-partner-to-enhance-patient-care-1028377) [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os) across outpatient, inpatient, and emergency departments.

**EHR vendors integrate ambient and agentic capabilities natively:** [athenahealth](https://elion.health/products/athenahealth) made [Suki](https://elion.health/products/suki)’s [AI scribe](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) [generally available](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250501613704/en/Suki-and-athenahealth-Expand-Partnership-With-General-Availability-of-Ambient-Notes-Using-Sukis-Ambient-Technology) through its Ambient Notes solution, while [OmniMD](https://elion.health/products/omnimd-ai-medical-scribe) [launched](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/15/3082228/0/en/OmniMD-Announces-AI-Clinicians-Ushering-in-a-New-Era-of-Predictive-Personalized-and-Effortless-Healthcare-Delivery.html) AI Clinician, which combines ambient scribe, agentic task support, and clinical summaries. [TruBridge](https://elion.health/products/trubridge-electronic-health-record-and-health-information-systems) [announced](https://trubridge.com/resources/trubridge-announces-next-step-of-collaboration-with-microsoft-enhancing-community-healthcare-through-advanced-clinical-documentation-capabilities/) an integration with [Microsoft Dragon Copilot](https://elion.health/products/microsoft-dragon-copilot) to embed [clinical summaries](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinical-summaries/products) into its workflows.

**Knowledge-based assistants are also gaining traction:** [ASCO](https://elion.health/products/asco-guidelines-assistant) and Google Cloud [launched](https://ascopost.com/news/may-2025/asco-and-google-cloud-ai-powered-tool/) a genAI assistant that surfaces relevant clinical guideline excerpts in response to clinician queries, and White Plains Hospital [partnered](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/814071374/white-plains-hospital-teams-with-layer-health-to-streamline-clinical-registry-reporting) with [Layer Health](https://elion.health/products/layer-health-distill) to streamline clinical registry reporting.

# 3\. AI Imaging Sees Modality Expansion and Platform Integration

AI innovation in medical imaging accelerated this month, with vendors releasing new AI imaging enhancement modalities, expanding clinical decision support tools, and deepening integration into radiology workflows.

**New modalities and enhancement tools launched at an unprecedented clip.** GE HealthCare [unveiled](https://investor.gehealthcare.com/news-releases/news-release-details/estro-2025-congress-ge-healthcare-expand-its-radiation-oncology) CleaRecon DL, an AI enhancement solution for cone-beam CT, and separately [partnered](https://investor.gehealthcare.com/news-releases/news-release-details/estro-2025-congress-ge-healthcare-expand-its-radiation-oncology) with [Spectronic Medical](https://elion.health/products/spectronic-medical) to generate synthetic CT images from standard MR scans. [Viz.ai](https://elion.health/products/viz-ai) [released](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250430134731/en/Viz.ai-Launches-Viz-3D-CTA-to-Deliver-Enhanced-Imaging-and-Accelerate-Clinical-Decision-Making) Viz 3D CTA, which transforms CT angiography scans into 3D neurovascular visualizations, another leap toward richer, more actionable imaging data.

[**Imaging CDS**](https://elion.health/categories/ai-imaging-clinical-decision-support/products) **and workflow tools are consolidating into the platforms radiologists already use.** [RamSoft](https://elion.health/products/ramsoft-omegaai) [integrated](https://www.ramsoft.com/press-releases/therapixel-partnership-to-deliver-ai-powered-breast-imaging-solution) Therapixel’s MammoScreen AI for breast cancer detection into its PowerServer and OmegaAI platforms, and [iCAD’s ProFound Breast Health](https://elion.health/products/icad-profound-breast-health) solution [became available](https://www.icadmed.com/about/news-events/press-releases/icad-collaborates-with-microsoft-to-provide-access-to-its-mammography-solutions-in-microsofts-precision-imaging-network-pin/) through [Microsoft’s Powerscribe](https://elion.health/products/powerscribe) reporting tool. [CliniComp](https://elion.health/products/clinicomp) [added](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/clinicomp-launches-enterprise-pacs-fully-integrated-into-their-new-era-ehr-with-intrinsic-ai-302440066.html?tc=eml_cleartime) AI triage and transcription capabilities to its enterprise-wide PACS. Intelerad [partnered](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/05/20/3084931/0/en/Intelerad-Announces-Partnership-with-RADPAIR-Enhancing-Radiology-Reporting-with-AI.html) with [RADPAIR](https://elion.health/products/radpair) to bring generative and agent-based tools into radiologist workflows.

**Providers express a keen interest in AI imaging tools.** Meanwhile, TriHealth [selected](https://www.trihealth.com/news/trihealth-advancement-lung-cancer-ai) [RevealDx](https://elion.health/products/revealdx) for a lung nodule detection study, and Radiology Associates [adopted](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harrison-ai_radiology-clinicalai-healthcare-activity-7327656545603780608-su3i/) [Harrison.ai](https://elion.health/products/annalise-critical-care-ai)’s critical care AI to speed turnaround on high-priority reads. Cardiac Care Alliance [began deploying](https://cleerlyhealth.com/press/cleerly-and-cardiac-care-alliance-join-forces) [Cleerly](https://elion.health/products/cleerly)’s cardiac imaging AI across its VBC-focused network. On the investment front, [Rad AI](https://elion.health/products/rad-ai-continuity) [closed](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/rad-ai-announces-strategic-investments-from-four-leading-health-systems-to-advance-enterprise-ai-in-healthcare-302459908.html) an additional $8M strategic investment from Advocate Health, Memorial Hermann Health System, Corewell Health, and Atlantic Health System, bringing its Series C funding total to $68M

# 4\. AI RCM Continues to Move Toward Full-Stack Automation

As [covered last month](https://elion.health/resources/healthcare-it-news-april-2025), we’re continuing to see [AI RCM](https://elion.health/categories/ai-rcm) vendor consolidation. Nowhere is this more clearly demonstrated than in the [formation of Smarter Technologies](https://www.smarter-technologies.com/#news-smarter-launch), a newly unified automation and insights platform created through the roll-up of New Mountain Capital investments Access Healthcare, [SmarterDx](https://elion.health/products/smarterdx), and [Thoughtful.ai](https://elion.health/products/thoughtful-ai). Their consolidation under a single brand signals a bold bid to build a full-stack AI RCM platform that spans [CDI](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinical-documentation-integrity/products), [coding](https://elion.health/categories/autonomous-coding/products), [revenue cycle automation](https://elion.health/categories/revenue-cycle-automation/products), and [operational analytics](https://elion.health/categories/revenue-intelligence/products). In a similar vein, [Infinx](https://elion.health/products/infinx) acquired i3 Verticals’ healthcare RCM business to broaden its automation capabilities and also announced a new integration through [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic)’s Connection Hub, making it easier for health systems to embed Infinx tools across [prior auth](https://elion.health/categories/ai-prior-authorization-for-providers/products), [eligibility](https://elion.health/categories/eligibility-checking/products), and [billing](https://elion.health/categories/ai-patient-billing/products) workflows.

Several vendors also expanded core functionality. AKASA [released](https://akasa.com/press/akasa-debuts-cdi-optimizer/) [CDI Optimizer](https://elion.health/products/akasa-cdi-optimizer), a new tool that reviews 100% of inpatient encounters to flag documentation gaps, and [Iodine Software](https://elion.health/products/iodine-awareprebill) [launched](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250515954993/en/Iodine-Software-Launches-Next-Generation-Pre-Bill-Solution-to-Help-Health-Systems-Right-Size-Reimbursement) AwarePre-Bill, a “second-level CDI” solution aimed at pre-billing documentation integrity.

On the other hand, the RCM solutions to see the most actual traction in health systems this month were solutions for [patient billing](https://elion.health/categories/ai-patient-billing/products): [Cedar](https://elion.health/products/cedar), [adopted](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/harrison-ai_radiology-clinicalai-healthcare-activity-7327656545603780608-su3i/) by Sanford Health, and [PayZen](https://elion.health/products/payzen), [selected](https://payzen.com/payzen-partners-with-patterson-health-center-to-reinvent-its-collection-processes-better-servicing-rural-patients/) by Patterson Health Center.
