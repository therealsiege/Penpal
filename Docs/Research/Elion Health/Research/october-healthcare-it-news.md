# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: October Edition

**Date:** October 31, 2024  
**Author:** Bobby Guelich, CEO, Elion

**Categories:** revenue cycle management, AI contact center, RCM, provider administration, AI Ambient Scribes, Electronic Health Record (EHR)

---

In October 2024 we tracked 77 healthcare IT news stories, with [AI clinician assistant](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinician-assistant/products), [revenue cycle management](https://elion.health/categories/revenue-cycle-management), and [AI contact center](https://elion.health/categories/ai-contact-center) news showing up most frequently. It’s also worth noting that 71% of announcements we covered pertained to an AI product or feature, up from 43% in August and 51% in September. This suggests that AI adoption is either markedly increasing or vendors have caught on that referencing AI is all but imperative to create buzz.

Here, we’re summarizing the month’s news—finding the signal in the noise—so you can stay abreast of the most important trends you may have missed.

## 1\. Las Vegas Was the Place to Be for HLTH 2024

Based on our ongoing coverage of this space, it appears many vendors saved up their big funding and M&A announcements in hopes of making a bigger splash at HLTH. We saw at least double the usual number of announcements during the week of HLTH, making it even more remarkable if a vendor did make it into reporters’ post-event coverage.

_Fierce Healthcare_ did [a nice recap of the trends](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/health-tech/hlth-2024-recap-5-notable-trends-spotted-reporters-and-takeaways-executives) on the showroom floor, including the current temperature on AI in healthcare, “unsexy” data and interop solutions, pharmacy innovation, women’s health, and psychedelic medicine.

Our favorite demo of the week? [Anthropic](https://elion.health/products/anthropic-claude)’s computer use capability. You can see our Head of Research, Patrick Wingo’s take on [what it could mean for the future of AI in healthcare here](https://elion.health/resources/anthropic-computer-use-ai).

While HLTH may be over, if you’re ruminating on AI solutions you encountered there, you can still download our [Guide to Everything AI at HLTH 2024](https://elion.health/resources/guide-ai-hlth-2024).

## 2\. Oracle Rolls Out Major Product Updates

Shortly after HLTH, [Oracle](https://elion.health/products/oracle-cerner) hosted its Oracle Health Summit in Nashville October 28-30. As of press time, Oracle has:

- [Previewed](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-unveils-next-generation-ehr-2024-10-29/) its “next-generation EHR,” which promises to embed AI natively across clinical workflows and improve information exchange between payers and providers.

- [Release](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-health-makes-it-easier-for-patients-to-take-charge-of-their-care-2024-10-29/) of [Oracle Health Patient Portal](https://www.oracle.com/health/consumer-experience/digital-front-door/patient-portal/), which allows patients to securely review and share their health information.

- [Release](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-health-makes-it-easier-for-patients-to-take-charge-of-their-care-2024-10-29/) of [Oracle Health Patient Administration](https://www.oracle.com/health/revenue-cycle/patient-administration/), a front-office [patient intake](https://elion.health/categories/patient-intake/products), [scheduling](https://elion.health/categories/patient-scheduling/products), and registration solution.

- [Updates](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-clinical-ai-agent-2024-10-29/) to [Clinical AI Agent](https://www.oracle.com/health/clinical-suite/clinical-ai-agent/), formerly known as [Clinical Digital Assistant](https://elion.health/products/oracle-clinical-digital-assistant), a genAI agent for [ambient scribing](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) and [clinical summaries](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinical-summaries/products).

- [Announced plans](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-health-to-apply-for-tefca-qhin-status-2024-10-28/) to become a Trusted Exchange Framework and Common Agreement (TEFCA) Qualified Health Information Network (QHIN).

- [Introduced](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-helps-healthcare-organizations-address-critical-inventory-management-challenges-2024-10-28/) new [supply chain](https://elion.health/categories/supply-chain-management/products) capabilities around inventory management and procurement.

- [Added](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-helps-healthcare-organizations-find-the-right-talent-to-fill-mission-critical-roles-2024-10-28/) new talent acquisition tools to its [staffing](https://elion.health/categories/clinical-staffing/products) tools.

Prior to the summit, Oracle also announced:

- Two [new RCM products](https://www.oracle.com/news/announcement/oracle-helps-healthcare-facilities-simplify-patient-payments-while-reducing-transaction-fees-2024-10-16/)—a new [patient payments](https://elion.health/categories/patient-billing-and-collection/products) solution that includes gateway routing, processing, and acquiring under a single agreement, and Oracle Health Clinical Data Exchange, a new medical claims processing solution.

- [Integration with](https://finthrive.com/news/finthrive-achieves-claims-management-certification-for-oracle-health-patient-accounting) [FinThrive](https://elion.health/products/finthrive), an end-to-end RCM solution, via API, enabling improved functionality, performance, and a unified login experience.

It’s clear that much like [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic), Oracle is planning to compete by both broadening and deepening its native solutions across clinical workflows, [RCM](https://elion.health/categories/revenue-cycle-management), [provider administration](https://elion.health/categories/provider-and-practice-administration), and more.

## 4\. Players Are Grappling With Governance

The Coalition for Health AI (CHAI) announced draft frameworks this month for how it will:

1. Certify independent Health AI Assurance Labs, and

2. Help standardize the output of these labs in the form of CHAI Model Cards, a “nutrition label” for health AI solutions. CHAI then [previewed the first such card at HLTH](https://www.fiercehealthcare.com/ai-and-machine-learning/hlth24-heres-first-look-draft-nutritional-label-health-ai).

With AI being rapidly integrated into nearly every aspect of healthcare, we’re excited to see this progress. There is a pressing need for greater transparency into the performance, safety, and fairness of AI-driven solutions, and we’re encouraged by the collaborative approach CHAI has taken to develop an industry-wide evaluation infrastructure.

However, they’re far from the only game in town. [Andrew Hines](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andhines/), CTO at [Canvas](https://elion.health/products/canvas), [shared a breakdown](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/andhines_theres-a-secondary-ai-land-grab-happening-activity-7255632501384126464-Y0HA/?utm_source=share&utm_medium=member_desktop) (plus some hot takes) on all of the many entities currently vying for market share in the AI governance space. These include seemingly “neutral” players like CHAI, The Joint Commission, HIMSS, and Digital Medicine Society, as well as solutions either developed or co-developed by AI vendors themselves, such as Avanade SAIGE (which includes Microsoft and Accenture), Aidoc BRIDGE with NVIDIA, and Epic Seismometer.

This will undoubtedly be a space to watch over the coming months.

## 5\. Everyone Is (Still) Innovating Around AI Ambient Scribes

If you thought the [AI ambient scribe](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) space was fully saturated, you were mistaken. Despite being one of the largest categories in our taxonomy with 56 vendors as of press time, the market is far from set. Existing EHR and patient engagement vendors are still launching new integrations and native solutions in this space. For example:

- [Kipu Health](https://elion.health/products/kipu-health) [partnered](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/kipu-health-collaborates-with-aws-to-bring-cutting-edge-generative-ai-to-behavioral-health-industry-302268383.html?tc=eml_cleartime) with AWS to build a suite of AI-enabled solutions for behavioral health, starting with an [AI clinician assistant](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinician-assistant/products) that includes transcription and chart summarization.

- [Myriad Health](https://myriadsystems.ai/myriad-health) [released](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241016864881/en/Myriad-Systems-Launches-Powerful-AI-Charting-Capabilities) MyScribe AI for AI ambient scribing and coding suggestions.

- [Health Note](https://elion.health/products/health-note) [released](https://www.prweb.com/releases/health-note-adds-ambient-ai-scribing-to-its-clinical-platform-302279365.html) an AI ambient scribe.

- [Zoom](https://elion.health/products/zoom-healthcare) [partnered](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241022528459/en/Zoom-and-Suki-Plan-to-Provide-AI-generated-Clinical-Notes-to-Healthcare-Institutions) with [Suki](https://elion.health/products/suki) to generate clinical notes as well as other integrated AI features.

- [Net Health](https://elion.health/products/net-health) [announced](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/net-health-and-tali-to-launch-cutting-edge-integration-with-ai-technology-302284658.html?tc=eml_cleartime) it would integrate [Tali](https://elion.health/products/tali-ai).

- [athenahealth](https://elion.health/products/athenahealth) [released](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20241030050913/en/athenahealth-Launches-AI-Powered-Ambient-Notes-a-Unique-Fully-Integrated-Documentation-Solution-Designed-for-Ambulatory-Practices) a fully integrated AI ambient scribe for ambulatory practices.

Ultimately, we believe there will be a variety of scribes that focus on the myriad of different specialties and provider workflows, and perhaps ambient scribing will become just a standard feature of all clinical products, but it remains to be seen whether new vendors entering the market will be able to offer enough in the way of product features and usability or cost savings to make major headway as a standalone product.

One of the major ways that ambient scribe tools can differentiate currently is through their language models. With OpenAI’s Whisper transcription tool [in hot water](https://www.wired.com/story/hospitals-ai-transcription-tools-hallucination/), how and how much vendors train these foundation models is an essential component of differentiation for now.

## 6\. Speed Is the Name of the Game in AI for RCM

While accuracy seemed to be the catchphrase in previous months, lately we’re seeing speed in handling prior auths and denials as a top-selling point for AI RCM vendors. This month:

- [Infinx](https://elion.health/products/infinx) [launched](https://www.newswire.com/news/infinx-launches-next-generation-intelligent-automation-platform-for-22435933) a new solution that offers human-in-the-loop automation of RCM tasks, emphasizing its impact on claims processing speed and quicker reimbursements.

- Claimable [announced](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/10/02/2956879/0/en/Claimable-Launches-First-Ever-AI-Powered-Appeals-Platform-to-Fight-Unjust-Healthcare-Denials.html) the launch of its [AI denials appeal](https://elion.health/categories/ai-denials-management/products) solution, citing pilot results such as “most cases resolved in under 10 days (3x faster than average),” and “appeals submitted in minutes–not days.”

- [Cohere Health](https://elion.health/products/cohere-health) [is partnering](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/cohere-health-and-mcg-partner-to-deliver-best-in-class-prior-authorization-solution-302269952.html) with MCG to drive, “clinically appropriate auto-approvals \[on prior auths\], while reducing provider submission time and friction.”

- [Apprio](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/10/15/2963340/0/en/Apprio-Announces-Smart-Solution-To-Pro-Actively-Reduce-Claim-Denials.html) [released](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/10/15/2963340/0/en/Apprio-Announces-Smart-Solution-To-Pro-Actively-Reduce-Claim-Denials.html) Intelligent Denial Prevention, which aims to predict denials before claims are submitted.

- Blue Shield of California and [Salesforce](https://elion.health/products/salesforce-health-cloud) [are partnering](https://news.blueshieldca.com/2024/10/21/blue-shield-of-california-salesforce-making-prior-authorization-as-simple-as-using-a-credit-card) on a solution that promises to give patients prior auth responses within the course of their appointment and handle peer-to-peer clinical consultations within hours in the event of a rejection.

- [SmarterDx](https://elion.health/products/smarterdx) [released](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/smarterdx-launches-smarterdenials-ai-appeals-to-help-hospitals-fight-payer-denials-302288518.html) an AI denials solution that creates AI-generated appeals letters, promising to help providers, “get paid more accurately and get paid faster.”
