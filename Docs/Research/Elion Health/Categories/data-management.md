# Data Management

The Data Management category includes a variety of tools and platforms that help healthcare organizations collect, store, protect, and make the most of their data. By organizing data effectively and keeping it secure, these solutions enable providers and payers to use information more strategically to improve care, operations, and decision-making.

Key subcategories include Health Data Platforms that centralize clinical, financial, and operational data to power analytics and support better care delivery. Patient Data Tokenization protects privacy by converting sensitive details into secure tokens for safe sharing and integration. Customer Data Platforms bring together patient and consumer data from different sources to create unified profiles for more personalized engagement and outreach. Data Factoring tools refine and enrich raw or unstructured data, transforming it into clear, actionable information. Together, these tools help healthcare organizations unlock the full value of their data to drive efficiency and better patient outcomes.

## Subcategories

### Customer Data Platforms (CDP) (19 products)

Customer Data Platforms help healthcare organizations collect, unify, and manage patient and user data from many sources, such as website visits, email campaigns, call centers, and clinical systems like EHRs. By bringing this information together, these platforms build a single, comprehensive profile for each patient, giving teams a complete view of patient interactions, preferences, and needs in one place.
With unified data, healthcare organizations can personalize outreach and tailor engagement strategies for specific patient segments, improving how they communicate through digital marketing, appointment reminders, or educational content. Many CDPs also include tools for audience segmentation, real-time analytics, and data privacy management to keep patient information secure and compliant. By turning fragmented data into actionable insights, these platforms help healthcare providers strengthen relationships with patients, increase loyalty, and deliver more seamless, patient-centered experiences across every touchpoint.

### Health Data Platforms (55 products)

Health Data Platforms help healthcare organizations securely gather, manage, and analyze large volumes of data from many different sources, including EHRs, pharmacies, labs, imaging systems, claims data, and patient-generated information from wearables and remote monitoring devices. By centralizing this diverse data into a single platform, these solutions break down data silos and make information more usable for care teams, researchers, payers, and other stakeholders.
These platforms support advanced analytics that can identify trends, measure outcomes, and uncover opportunities to improve care quality and reduce costs. They are also vital for supporting insurance underwriting, real-world evidence generation, and life sciences research by providing clean, structured, and compliant datasets. Many platforms offer built-in tools for data governance, de-identification, interoperability, and regulatory compliance to protect patient privacy and maintain trust. By enabling better data integration and analysis, Health Data Platforms help healthcare organizations operate more efficiently, deliver more personalized care, and drive innovation across the industry.

### Patient Data Archiving (6 products)

Patient Data Archiving is the secure storage and management of historical healthcare data, typically from legacy systems, to meet data retention requirements and support operational efficiency. By consolidating information from outdated EHRs, EMRs, ERP systems, and billing platforms into a centralized archive, healthcare organizations can retire old systems without losing access to vital clinical, financial, and administrative records.
These solutions ensure HIPAA compliance by protecting sensitive data and offer tools that make it easy for providers to search, retrieve, and share historical information as needed. Modern archives integrate with current health IT systems to create a seamless “one patient, one record” experience and can be deployed on-premises or in the cloud to fit different infrastructure needs. This approach preserves data accessibility while reducing the costs and complexity of maintaining legacy platforms.

### Patient Data Tokenization (11 products)

The Patient Data Tokenization category focuses on technologies that enhance data privacy and security by converting sensitive patient information into tokens that cannot be traced back to individuals without proper authorization. These solutions enable healthcare organizations to securely link and share patient-level data from diverse sources—such as medical records, imaging systems, and health networks—while ensuring compliance with privacy regulations like HIPAA.
Tokenization facilitates the secure exchange and aggregation of data for purposes like population health management, research, and clinical development without exposing protected health information (PHI). Advanced features in this category include automated de-identification of medical images and robust data matching capabilities, ensuring seamless data retrieval and integration across platforms. By balancing data accessibility with strict privacy controls, patient data tokenization supports innovation in healthcare while safeguarding patient confidentiality.

### Provider Data Management (8 products)

Provider Data Management (PDM) is the enterprise mastering and governance layer for provider, organization, location, and affiliation data. It resolves identities, removes duplicates, applies survivorship rules, and maintains lineage so titles, specialties, privileges, locations, and identifiers stay consistent across the enterprise. The benefit for providers is fewer scheduling/referral and billing/credentialing errors, faster onboarding/changes, and analytics provider leadership can actually rely on.
Most solutions feature multi-source ingestion and normalization; probabilistic/deterministic matching with merge/unmerge; stewardship queues and policy-driven governance; versioning and audit trails; and publication via APIs/event streams with continuous data-quality monitoring. Solutions within this category include healthcare-oriented MDM platforms, identity-resolution/matching engines, provider-graph services that expose mastered entities, address/identifier normalization utilities, and stewardship & data-quality workbenches.

### Synthetic Data (14 products)

Synthetic data tools create artificial patient-level data that statistically reflects real clinical information but contains no identifiable patient details. This means healthcare organizations can safely use realistic data to develop and test new technologies while staying compliant with privacy laws like HIPAA. By mimicking real-world data patterns, synthetic datasets help teams uncover insights and validate solutions without the legal and ethical concerns tied to using actual patient records.
These tools are widely used by clinical informatics, data science, IT, and digital innovation teams to build machine learning models, test algorithms, train staff, and simulate workflows in a secure environment. Synthetic data supports faster development cycles by removing access barriers and reducing reliance on restricted or siloed real data. Ultimately, these solutions help healthcare organizations innovate more confidently while protecting patient privacy.
