# Analytics

The Analytics category brings together advanced data tools that help healthcare organizations make smarter decisions, improve patient outcomes, and operate more efficiently. These technologies analyze both structured and unstructured healthcare data to uncover insights that guide clinical care, financial planning, and operational strategies. From predicting health risks to identifying treatment opportunities, analytics tools empower providers, payers, and researchers to act on data quickly and accurately.

Subcategories include Machine Learning, which covers models and infrastructure used for prediction, diagnosis, personalization, and workflow optimization. Risk Modeling products focus on forecasting costs and health trajectories to support budgeting, care planning, and value-based care. Large Language Models (LLMs) extract meaning from clinical text to automate documentation, summarize records, and streamline communication. Together, these tools help healthcare organizations harness data at scale to deliver more informed, efficient, and proactive care.

## Subcategories

### Foundation Model Platforms and APIs (19 products)

Foundation Model Platforms and APIs provide health systems with secure access to large language and multimodal models, plus the tools needed to build, deploy, and operate generative AI applications. These platforms typically offer managed model endpoints, enterprise controls for identity and access, and options for customizing outputs through retrieval, fine-tuning, and workflow templates. Provider organizations use them to power internal copilots and embedded AI features across clinical, operational, and administrative workflows while maintaining governance over sensitive data.
This category includes both “API-first” model platforms and enterprise workspaces designed to support clinician and staff use at scale, including governance, auditability, and integration with approved institutional knowledge sources.

### Machine Learning (18 products)

Machine Learning in healthcare covers a broad set of technologies and tools, from Large Language Models to classifiers and other advanced algorithms, along with the APIs and infrastructure that support them. These solutions help organizations unlock insights from complex healthcare and life sciences data that would be hard to detect with traditional methods.
By applying machine learning, providers, researchers, and health systems can make better predictions, assist with patient diagnosis, personalize treatment plans, and accelerate research and development. These technologies also help streamline operations by automating tasks and finding efficiencies, ultimately supporting more informed decisions and better patient outcomes.

### Risk Modeling (7 products)

Risk Modeling products help healthcare organizations use data and predictive analytics to assess and manage risks tied to patient outcomes and healthcare costs. By analyzing large sets of clinical, claims, and demographic data, these tools forecast future healthcare expenses, predict patient health trajectories, and estimate the impact of various interventions, supporting smarter, data-driven decisions.
These solutions often use machine learning and advanced statistical methods to generate accurate forecasts and risk assessments. They can project costs and benefits for different care strategies and evaluate risks for self-funded plans, helping organizations budget effectively and build necessary reserves. By making complex risk factors more understandable, these tools empower providers, insurers, and policymakers to plan proactively and improve care and financial performance.
