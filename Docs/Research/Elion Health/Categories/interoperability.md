# Interoperability

The Interoperability category includes solutions that make it possible for healthcare data to flow securely and efficiently between systems, so providers, payers, and patients can access up-to-date information when they need it. These tools help break down data silos and support seamless communication across different parts of the healthcare ecosystem.

Key subcategories include Clinical Data Access tools that pull patient information from EHRs, health information exchanges, and claims systems to support informed care. Encounter Notification Services send real-time alerts when patients are admitted, discharged, or visit emergency departments, improving coordination and follow-up. EHR Integration solutions connect various systems to streamline workflows and ensure clinical data moves smoothly between platforms. Together, these tools help reduce duplicate tests, speed up care decisions, and improve continuity, leading to better outcomes and greater operational efficiency.

## Subcategories

### Clinical Data Access (36 products)

Clinical Data Access solutions help healthcare organizations connect and share patient information across different systems to improve how care is delivered. By making it easier to access and integrate clinical data from various sources, these tools ensure that providers have a complete and up-to-date view of a patient’s health history when making care decisions.
These products either serve as health information exchanges themselves or act as connectors that link providers to other exchanges while adding features like record location and data summarization. They integrate this information directly into EHR systems, giving clinicians timely access to comprehensive patient data and supporting more informed, coordinated, and efficient care.

### EHR Integration (35 products)

EHR Integration products and services enable healthcare organizations to connect other digital systems with electronic health records, ensuring that patient data can flow seamlessly where it’s needed. These solutions allow external tools to securely read from and write to the EHR, which helps maintain a complete, up-to-date view of a patient’s health information without the need for duplicative manual entry or fragmented workflows.
These integrations can be direct, with the vendor managing secure connections and data exchange with EHRs, or indirect by providing robust APIs, developer tools, or integration frameworks that other systems use to connect. This capability is critical for powering applications like clinical decision support, billing, analytics, and patient engagement tools that rely on timely, accurate data. Strong EHR Integration helps organizations meet interoperability standards, comply with data sharing requirements, and reduce administrative burden while giving clinicians and care teams the real-time information they need to deliver better, more coordinated care.

### Encounter Notification Services (6 products)

Encounter Notification Services products keep care teams informed by sending real-time alerts when patients are admitted, discharged, or transferred between healthcare facilities. These notifications include detailed visit information, giving providers timely insight into their patients’ status and helping them stay connected to what’s happening across different care settings.
These services are especially important for managing patients with complex conditions or those moving between hospitals, rehab, or home care. By receiving immediate updates, care teams can coordinate follow-up more effectively, reduce the risk of unnecessary readmissions, and ensure patients get the right care at the right time to support better outcomes.
