# AI Products

The AI Products category includes a broad set of artificial intelligence solutions that help healthcare organizations deliver better care, operate more efficiently, and engage patients more effectively. These tools bring AI into clinical care, administrative workflows, patient communication, and everyday operations, supporting providers and patients alike with advanced automation and real-time insights.

Key subcategories include AI Clinician Assistants that automate tasks like documentation, clinical summaries, and decision support at the point of care. AI Facility Management tools keep healthcare environments running smoothly through predictive maintenance and resource optimization. AI Remote Patient Monitoring and Triage solutions track patient health outside the clinic, spotting early issues and enabling timely intervention. AI Imaging tools support accurate diagnoses, while AI RCM automates financial tasks like billing and coding. AI Patient Companions offer reminders, medication guidance, and emotional support, and AI Contact Center solutions use chatbots and automated calls to streamline communication with patients and payers. Together, these AI tools help providers raise care quality, strengthen operations, and create a better patient experience.

## Subcategories

### AI Patient Companions (10 products)

AI Patient Companions refers to autonomous bots that use natural language processing, generative AI, and machine learning to offer users conversational companionship through text and sometimes voice interactions. These bots are designed to simulate supportive, human-like conversations that can help people feel heard and less alone, often acting as a first line of emotional support or a safe outlet for sharing thoughts and feelings any time of day.
While early companion AI products focused mainly on casual conversation and mood support, many companies in this category are now working to demonstrate clinical value through evidence-based practices and real-world outcomes. Some bots are beginning to integrate therapeutic frameworks, guided exercises, or mental wellness check-ins to complement traditional mental health care. By combining friendly interaction with proven support methods, AI Patient Companions aim to make mental health resources more accessible, reduce loneliness, and extend everyday emotional care to people who might otherwise go without it.
