# Provider and Practice Administration

The Provider and Practice Administration category includes a wide range of tools and services designed to keep healthcare organizations running smoothly behind the scenes. These solutions help manage essential tasks like maintaining accurate provider directories, handling credentialing, licensing, and enrollment to ensure compliance, and streamlining staff scheduling with workforce management systems.

To keep daily operations efficient, ticketing and help desk tools help resolve issues quickly, and supply chain management systems ensure that medical supplies and equipment are always available when needed. Secure fax services protect the transmission of sensitive patient information. Digital adoption and training platforms make it easier for staff to learn and use new technologies and processes. Together, these solutions strengthen administrative operations, boost efficiency, and support providers in delivering high-quality, compliant patient care.

## Subcategories

### Clinician Recruiting (3 products)

Clinician Recruiting software helps healthcare organizations simplify and modernize how they find, attract, and hire qualified medical professionals. These tools are designed to make it easier to fill both in-person and virtual care roles by streamlining the entire recruitment process.
These platforms typically offer features like applicant tracking, credential verification, and integration with major job boards to widen the talent pool and reach the right candidates. By automating recruitment workflows and keeping all candidate information in one place, these solutions help organizations stay compliant, create a smoother experience for applicants, and speed up hiring timelines so hospitals and clinics can maintain the staff they need to provide quality care.

### Credentialing, Licensing, and Enrollment (31 products)

Credentialing, Licensing, and Enrollment solutions help healthcare organizations verify that their providers have the proper qualifications, secure the right licenses, and enroll in insurance networks and health plans. These tools are critical for ensuring that clinicians meet all regulatory, payer, and industry requirements so they can deliver care and receive timely reimbursement for their services.
These solutions automate the detailed tasks of validating education history, professional training, board certifications, and legal authorizations to practice in specific states or specialties. They often include workflows for renewing licenses, managing expirations, and tracking changes in credentialing status. Many platforms also help organizations manage payer enrollment applications, monitor application statuses, and keep provider directories up to date. By streamlining these time-consuming processes, these tools reduce administrative overhead, shorten onboarding timelines, and lower the risk of compliance gaps that could disrupt care delivery or payments.

### Digital Adoption and Training (6 products)

Digital Adoption and Training platforms help healthcare organizations make the most of their technology by guiding staff to learn, adopt, and confidently use digital tools. These in-app solutions provide interactive training, step-by-step walkthroughs, and real-time tips to help new and existing users master workflows and understand best practices without needing to leave the system they are working in.
These platforms integrate directly with EHR systems, practice management software, and other clinical or administrative tools to deliver training within the user’s daily environment. Many also include features like progress tracking, customized learning paths, and analytics that show where users may need extra support. By speeding up onboarding, reducing errors, and helping staff stay current with system updates or new features, Digital Adoption and Training platforms ensure technology is used to its full potential while minimizing disruptions to patient care.

### Facility and Procedure Scheduling (13 products)

**Facility and Procedure Scheduling** solutions help healthcare organizations plan and coordinate the use of constrained procedural capacity such as operating rooms, procedure suites, infusion chairs, and other shared procedural resources (e.g., endoscopy, cath/EP labs, interventional radiology). These platforms typically integrate with, extend, or sit on top of the EHR’s procedural scheduling tools to reduce friction in booking, ensure complete case information, and improve visibility into available time across rooms, sites, and service lines. “Facility” in this category refers to procedural rooms/chairs/devices—not routine ambulatory exam room assignment and not hospital-wide inpatient bed management.
Beyond booking, these solutions improve throughput by forecasting case duration, identifying underutilized capacity, optimizing block and open time, and supporting real-time adjustments when cancellations, delays, or resource constraints arise. Many also include operational schedule boards and dashboards that enable perioperative and procedural leaders to improve on-time starts, reduce overtime, and increase case volume without adding new rooms.

### Provider Directory Management (5 products)

Provider Directory Management solutions help healthcare organizations maintain complete and accurate information about the providers in their networks. These tools make it easier to keep details like contact information, specialties, and affiliations up to date so that patients and referring providers can find the right care when they need it.
Some solutions provide ready-made provider databases compiled from multiple sources, while others offer tools that help organizations manage and update their own directories. By streamlining the process of keeping provider information current, these products support smoother patient referrals, help meet regulatory requirements, and strengthen care coordination across health systems and networks.

### Safety Reporting (14 products)

Safety Reporting solutions are used by healthcare organizations to document, track, and manage patient safety incidents such as adverse events, near misses, medication errors, and procedural complications. These systems provide structured forms and configurable workflows that support frontline staff in submitting detailed reports, while enabling automated routing for triage, follow-up, and resolution. Many platforms include built-in tools for categorizing events by severity, location, and contributing factors, and are designed to ensure compliance with internal protocols and external reporting requirements.
Beyond incident capture, Safety Reporting solutions offer analytics capabilities that help organizations identify trends, recurring risks, and systemic issues. Features such as root cause analysis, heat mapping, and dashboards support safety committees and quality teams in prioritizing interventions. Integration with EHRs and other care management systems ensures access to relevant clinical context, while audit trails and role-based access controls maintain data integrity and accountability. These tools support a continuous improvement cycle aimed at reducing preventable harm and strengthening organizational safety practices.

### Supply Chain Management (9 products)

Healthcare Supply Chain Management platforms are critical in ensuring the efficient procurement, distribution, and management of medical supplies and equipment within healthcare organizations. These platforms provide end-to-end visibility and control over the supply chain, enabling healthcare providers to optimize inventory levels, reduce costs, and minimize waste. By leveraging advanced analytics and automation, these platforms help organizations anticipate demand, streamline purchasing processes, and ensure that the right products are available when and where they are needed.
In addition to core supply chain functions, many platforms offer integration with existing enterprise resource planning (ERP) and electronic health record (EHR) systems, facilitating seamless data exchange and enhancing decision-making capabilities. They often include features like real-time tracking, supplier performance management, and compliance monitoring.
