# Patient Administration

The Patient Administration category includes tools that help healthcare organizations manage patient interactions and handle the administrative tasks that keep care delivery running smoothly. These solutions simplify how information is collected, appointments are managed, patients are verified, and relationships are maintained, all while improving the overall patient experience.

Key subcategories include Form Builders and Patient Intake tools that digitize paperwork and streamline registration. Patient Scheduling systems make it easier to book and manage appointments with real-time availability and reminders. Identity Verification platforms ensure patients are securely onboarded and protected against fraud. Patient CRMs track interactions to personalize care and communication, while Patient Portals give patients easy access to their records and test results. Marketing Automation tools help maintain engagement and outreach, and Patient Engagement and Feedback Platforms invite patients to share their experiences, supporting continuous improvement. Together, these tools increase operational efficiency and strengthen patient satisfaction at every touchpoint.

## Subcategories

### Autonomous Patient Assessment (14 products)

Autonomous Patient Assessment solutions leverage AI-driven tools and interactive platforms to gather patient information. By automatically collecting data before, during, or after a clinical encounter, these tools capture a broad range of details—from the history of present illness and family history to behavioral health and social determinants of health. Many platforms employ natural language processing, facial or voice analysis, and adaptive questionnaires to evaluate patient conditions, helping identify potential risks and facilitating timely, evidence-based care.
Beyond data collection, these solutions improve the accuracy of clinical documentation and provide actionable insights that aid clinicians in focusing on higher-level decision-making rather than routine administrative tasks. Additionally, by automatically tracking patient progress throughout treatment, autonomous patient assessment tools empower healthcare teams to monitor outcomes and adjust care plans proactively, leading to more personalized and effective patient care.

### Digital Intake, Registration, and Check-In (65 products)

**Digital Intake, Registration & Check-In** solutions help health systems complete the pre-visit and arrival workflow digitally, ensuring the information needed to register the encounter and prepare the care team is captured accurately and on time. These tools collect and confirm demographics, guarantor and insurance details, visit-specific questionnaires, and required consents, then write structured data back to the EHR and surface exceptions for staff follow-up. The result is faster throughput at the front desk, fewer registration errors that create downstream denials or clinical friction, and a more consistent patient experience.
Solutions in this category typically include configurable intake and registration pathways, mobile-first form and document capture, e-signature and consent workflows, validation and deduplication controls, staff workqueues for incomplete or conflicting information, and digital check-in for arrival confirmation and day-of attestation. You can expect to find both end-to-end platforms that span intake through check-in and more focused products that specialize in high-friction steps such as document capture, registration data quality, or check-in workflow orchestration, with tight EHR integration as a defining capability.

### Form Builder (8 products)

Form Builders are digital tools that make it easy to create and manage forms without needing advanced technical skills. These solutions provide no-code, low-code, or developer-friendly options, so users can design forms that fit their exact needs using drag-and-drop interfaces, templates, and customizable fields.
Many Form Builders are built to handle sensitive healthcare information securely, offering HIPAA-compliant features for collecting and managing protected health data. Others focus on more general operational uses, helping organizations streamline tasks like intake forms, feedback surveys, or internal workflows. By making form creation simple and flexible, these tools help teams collect information efficiently and stay compliant when needed.

### Patient CRM (23 products)

Patient CRM products help healthcare providers strengthen and manage their relationships with patients by organizing every interaction in one place. These platforms give care teams a clear, complete view of each patient’s history, preferences, and communications, supporting more personalized and connected care.
Unlike general CRM tools, Patient CRMs include features tailored to healthcare, such as appointment scheduling, secure messaging, treatment plan tracking, and integration with EHRs. By combining these capabilities, these systems make it easier to engage patients, keep communication clear and consistent, and coordinate care more effectively across providers and touchpoints.

### Patient Engagement (111 products)

Patient Engagement solutions help healthcare organizations keep patients informed and connected throughout their care journey by managing essential tasks like scheduling, intake, payments, and administrative communication. These tools make it easier for patients to interact with their providers and stay on top of their appointments and financial responsibilities.
Key features often include automated appointment and payment reminders that help patients prepare for visits and settle bills on time. While these tools mainly handle administrative workflows, their broader goal is to ensure patients have easy access to care, from booking an appointment to completing payment after a visit, ultimately improving satisfaction and reducing gaps in care.

### Patient Feedback Platforms (19 products)

Patient survey platforms help healthcare providers gather and understand feedback to improve the patient experience and deliver better care. These tools make it easy to collect input from patients at different stages of their care journey, giving organizations valuable insight into how patients feel about their services.
Tools typically offer customizable survey templates, robust analytics, and clear reporting features so healthcare teams can track satisfaction trends, pinpoint areas for improvement, and measure progress over time. By acting on this feedback, providers can make informed changes that enhance care quality and strengthen patient trust and loyalty.

### Patient Marketing Automation (19 products)

Patient marketing automation platforms automate targeted outreach to prospective and existing patients across multiple channels—email, SMS, search engines, and social media. They aim to drive patient acquisition, retention, and engagement by delivering personalized health information, screening reminders, and promotional campaigns for relevant services.
Unlike traditional appointment reminders or scheduling systems, these platforms focus on patient acquisition or net-new appointments and services. They are also distinct from push-message or email marketing platforms; to be included within our category, vendors must use personalization and automated targeting, as opposed to mass messaging across an entire patient population or basic transactional emails.

### Patient Scheduling (92 products)

Patient Scheduling software makes booking, rescheduling, and managing appointments much simpler for both patients and healthcare providers. These tools give patients the convenience of selecting appointment times that work for them, often through easy-to-use online portals or mobile apps. On the provider side, scheduling platforms help manage daily calendars more efficiently, balance patient flow, and fill gaps to make the best use of staff and resources.
These solutions typically include features like automated reminders via text or email to help reduce no-shows and last-minute cancellations. Real-time updates ensure that available time slots are accurate, helping practices maximize appointment capacity. Many systems integrate directly with EHRs, so schedules automatically align with patient records and provider availability. Some platforms also support waitlists, telehealth booking, and patient self-check-in, further streamlining front desk workflows.

### Symptom Checkers (13 products)

Symptom checkers are digital tools that help patients understand what might be causing their symptoms and decide what kind of care they may need. By analyzing information like symptoms, medical history, and demographics, these tools provide patients with quick, personalized guidance that can help them make more informed decisions about their health.
Using natural language processing and machine learning, AI symptom checkers compare patient information against large medical databases to generate possible explanations and recommend next steps. They can direct people to self-care, telehealth appointments, or in-person visits depending on the urgency of their condition. Often integrated with patient portals and telehealth platforms, these tools make it easier for patients to move smoothly from checking symptoms to getting the right level of care.
