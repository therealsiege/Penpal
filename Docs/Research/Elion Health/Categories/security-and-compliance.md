# Security and Compliance

The Security and Compliance category includes tools and services that help healthcare organizations protect sensitive data, stay aligned with regulatory requirements, and build trust with patients and partners. As healthcare data becomes increasingly digital and distributed, the need for strong security practices and streamlined compliance processes has never been greater. Solutions in this category help reduce the risks of data breaches, support adherence to frameworks like HIPAA, SOC 2, HITRUST, and GDPR, and simplify the path to achieving and maintaining certification.

Subcategories include Compliance Certification services that guide organizations through regulatory audits and help implement policies, training, and monitoring systems to achieve and retain certifications. Compliance Infrastructure platforms offer automated tools to manage security controls, track compliance status, and scale with organizational needs. Cybersecurity solutions protect healthcare systems from breaches and cyberattacks through features like threat detection, encryption, identity and access management, and secure data sharing. Together, these tools provide the foundation healthcare organizations need to secure health information, meet legal requirements, and operate with confidence in a complex regulatory landscape.

## Subcategories

### AI Governance Platforms (22 products)

AI governance platforms are products designed for end-to-end AI lifecycle management within health systems. These platforms provide the centralized governance infrastructure necessary to manage the escalating volume and complexity of AI systems, ranging from predictive models to vendor-supplied generative AI solutions. Their core utility is establishing visibility and control over all internal and third-party AI initiatives by serving as a single AI system of record or comprehensive AI inventory. These platforms are explicitly built to accelerate safe AI adoption by streamlining workflows, reducing manual overhead, and integrating securely with existing clinical systems such as EHRs.
The critical function of this product category is to operationalize trust by ensuring continuous compliance, clinical safety, and ethical standards. These tools automate governance checks across the AI lifecycle, including project intake and risk assessments. They enforce policies aligned with foundational frameworks like the NIST AI Risk Management Framework (RMF) and healthcare-specific standards such as the Coalition for Health AI (CHAI) guidelines and ONC HTI-1. Furthermore, they provide essential capabilities for continuous monitoring and validation of deployed AI models, enabling real-time oversight to detect issues like drift, bias, and performance degradation that could compromise patient care.

### Compliance Certification (14 products)

Compliance Certification services help organizations meet and maintain regulatory standards such as SOC 2, HIPAA, HITRUST, and GDPR. These services simplify the complex process of achieving certification, ensuring that companies can prove they meet industry requirements for data security and privacy.
Solutions in this category typically offer gap assessments, help create required policies, deliver employee training, and provide continuous monitoring to keep organizations compliant over time. They also guide companies through the certification process itself, helping strengthen overall data protection practices and reduce risks related to privacy and security breaches.

### Compliance Infrastructure (22 products)

Compliance Infrastructure solutions provide cloud-based platforms that help healthcare organizations keep health data secure and compliant with standards like HIPAA, SOC 2, and GDPR. These platforms take care of the complex requirements needed to protect sensitive information and meet strict regulatory obligations.
They offer features such as continuous security monitoring, automated compliance checks, and scalable infrastructure that can grow with a healthcare organization’s needs. By managing these technical and regulatory demands, Compliance Infrastructure providers allow healthcare teams to focus more on delivering care and less on navigating compliance challenges.
