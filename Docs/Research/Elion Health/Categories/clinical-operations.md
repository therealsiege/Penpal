# Clinical Operations

The Clinical Operations category includes tools and systems that help healthcare organizations deliver care more efficiently by improving coordination, workflows, and data management. These solutions support care teams in providing high-quality, consistent care while reducing unnecessary complexity and administrative burden.

Key solutions include Clinical Decision Support platforms that deliver evidence-based insights during patient encounters and Clinical Workflow Management tools that help standardize and streamline daily processes. Care Team Coordination tools improve communication among multidisciplinary teams to support smooth care transitions. Headless EHR platforms offer modular, customizable ways to manage health data while ensuring interoperability, while traditional EHRs centralize patient information for documentation, billing, and care delivery. Clinical Data Abstraction tools pull essential patient information for reporting and research, and Home Care Documentation solutions help remote providers capture and manage patient data to maintain continuity of care outside traditional settings. Together, these technologies enable organizations to operate more effectively, deliver better outcomes, and adapt to new models of care.

## Subcategories

### Care Team Coordination (36 products)

Care Team Coordination solutions facilitate HIPAA-compliant collaboration and communication across the clinical and care management team. For example, care team members might use coordination software to develop and manage care plans, assign tasks, take notes, share content with patients, and track patient progress. ‍ Care team coordination solutions frequently have features that overlap with those found in the Clinical Workflow Management, Complex Care Management, and Secure Patient Communications categories. However, vendors in the care team coordination category tend to have a greater focus on supporting care teams internally.

### Clinical Data Abstraction (16 products)

Clinical data abstraction platforms help healthcare organizations efficiently extract and manage important clinical information from sources like medical records, pathology reports, and registries. By automating and organizing this process, these tools ensure that data is accurate, complete, and compliant with regulatory requirements, including for use in clinical registries.
These platforms integrate with EHR systems and other health IT tools to enable smooth data exchange and support better patient care coordination. They commonly include features like real-time data validation, workflow automation, and compliance tracking, helping organizations maintain high-quality clinical documentation and meet reporting standards more easily.

### Clinical Workflow Management (28 products)

Clinical workflow management software enables providers to create, implement, and continually improve various care pathways and care management workflows. Solutions in this category are distinguished by their ability to seamlessly create workflows and enable automation through low / no code tools.
In addition to their core workflow development capabilities, these solutions also facilitate efficient patient communication and care team collaboration, sharing many features with vendors found in Care Team Coordination.

### Electronic Health Record (EHR) (102 products)

Electronic Health Records are digital systems that capture, store, and organize a patient’s complete medical history in one secure place. By replacing paper charts, EHRs make it easier for healthcare providers to access up-to-date information about a patient’s diagnoses, lab results, medications, treatment plans, and previous encounters, supporting safer, more informed clinical decisions.
These systems are designed to support the entire care journey by enabling detailed documentation of medical visits, treatment updates, and care coordination across multiple providers and settings. EHRs also help automate tasks like ordering tests, prescribing medications, and sending reminders for preventive care. In addition to improving clinical workflows, EHRs support the financial side of healthcare by creating clear records that facilitate accurate billing and reimbursement, while helping providers meet compliance and reporting requirements under standards like HIPAA and value-based care programs.

### Headless EHR (10 products)

Headless EHRs are modern, API-driven systems that handle core patient data management and provider workflows without being tied to a single, traditional user interface. Instead of forcing organizations to use a fixed frontend, these solutions expose robust, well-documented APIs that let developers build custom applications on top of the EHR’s data and capabilities. This gives healthcare organizations more freedom to design user experiences that fit their unique workflows, branding, or patient engagement goals while still leveraging secure, centralized health records in the background.
This flexible approach helps digital health companies, health systems, and care providers innovate faster, integrate with other health IT systems, and adapt to new care models or consumer-facing applications. However, the tradeoff is that organizations may need more internal development resources to build, maintain, and support their own frontends or user interfaces. Some headless EHR products are entirely API-first with no built-in UI, while others offer a hybrid model with both full API access and an optional frontend, giving organizations the choice to use the out-of-the-box interface or develop their own for a fully customized solution.

### Home Care Documentation (10 products)

The Home Care Documentation category focuses on tools designed to streamline and enhance the documentation processes for in-home healthcare providers. These solutions leverage AI to assist with the completion of essential assessments, such as OASIS, while reducing documentation time and improving accuracy. By automating key tasks—like ingesting referral information, validating chart data, and checking for documentation errors—these platforms enable clinicians to focus more on patient care instead of administrative burdens.
A major focus of these tools is ensuring compliance with clinical protocols and optimizing revenue cycle processes. Integrated directly into electronic health records (EHR) systems, they support clinicians by providing real-time guidance, automated error checking, and coding assistance. These features reduce the likelihood of missed diagnoses and ensure accurate reporting, which improves both patient outcomes and reimbursement. Home care documentation platforms also aid in clinician onboarding and education, helping healthcare agencies maintain high standards of care across their operations.

### Post Acute Transitional Care (27 products)

Post-Acute Transitional Care solutions help healthcare providers manage the crucial period after a patient leaves an acute care setting, making sure they safely transition to home, skilled nursing facilities, or other post-acute care environments. These tools aim to reduce readmissions, close care gaps, and improve outcomes through coordinated and proactive follow-up. Discharge planners, care managers, and transitional care teams use them to monitor recovery and ensure patients stick to their discharge plans.
These solutions support smooth transitions by coordinating follow-up care after hospital stays or surgeries, monitoring high-risk patients, and facilitating communication between hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, home health providers, and primary care teams. Features often include automated outreach, clear delivery of care plan instructions, risk stratification to identify patients needing extra support, and tools to manage tasks and appointments. By extending care beyond hospital walls, they help maintain continuity, prevent complications, and support value-based care goals.

### Referral Management (29 products)

Referral Management solutions help provider organizations execute and track referrals end-to-end so patients reach the right next step of care. These products support the operational work required to route a referral to the appropriate destination, ensure the receiving team has complete clinical and administrative information, and close the loop with visibility into whether the referral was accepted, scheduled, completed, and documented.
Most EHRs support basic referral orders, but purpose-built referral platforms improve performance at scale by standardizing intake, digitizing document exchange, orchestrating outreach and scheduling, and providing reporting on referral turnaround times, specialist access, and network leakage. Many also streamline high-friction intake channels such as fax and unstructured documents, and integrate with EHR scheduling, provider directories, and insurance workflows to reduce delays and avoid lost referrals.

### Social Care (23 products)

Social Care products and services help healthcare organizations address social determinants of health by identifying, tracking, and reducing barriers that can affect a patient’s ability to access care. These solutions tackle issues like food insecurity, financial challenges, lack of transportation, and other systemic obstacles that can impact health outcomes.
They use data to flag potential barriers and bring this information to providers and care managers at the point of care, making it easier to tailor support to each patient’s situation. These tools also track patients over time and across groups to ensure consistent quality of care and connect patients to community resources, referral networks, and service programs that help meet their social needs alongside their medical care.
