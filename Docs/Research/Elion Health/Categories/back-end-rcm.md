# Back-End

Back-End Revenue Cycle converts adjudications into cash and closes the books. It includes Payment Reconciliation (ingesting ERA/EOB, posting payments/adjustments, linking deposits, reconciling to bank and GL), Denials Management (classifying, routing, and appealing payer denials and underpayments), and Patient Billing & Collection (statements, payment plans, refunds, and agency handoffs). The emphasis is accurate posting, fast resolution, and a complete audit trail.

Back-End consumes clean claims and supporting documentation from Mid-Cycle and the expected allowables from Contract Management to identify variances while it posts and balances cash. It does not create claims or assign codes. Processes during back-end revenue cycle ensure every dollar received is matched, reconciled, and either cleared or escalated, and it returns performance signals (payer behavior, cash-flow, denial trends) to Revenue Intelligence for continuous improvement.

## Subcategories

### Denials Management (45 products)

Denials Management solutions identify, classify, and resolve payer claim denials and underpayments. They ingest remittances and claim status (e.g., 835/277CA), normalize CARC/RARC codes, distinguish hard vs. soft denials, and generate an auditable cause for each event. From there, they drive resolution—correcting data, adding documentation, building appeal packages, tracking timely-filing/appeal windows, and coordinating follow-up across teams.
Modern platforms add analytics and automation to reduce rework at scale: trend root causes by payer and service line, prioritize worklists by recoverability and value, auto-assemble appeal content with criteria citations, and log payer communications. Outputs feed prevention efforts upstream (e.g., status/medical-necessity insights to UR/CM, coding edits to Mid-Cycle), but the core job here is post-adjudication recovery and control of avoidable denials.

### Patient Billing and Collection (59 products)

Patient Billing and Collection products help healthcare providers simplify and manage the entire billing process, ensuring that patients receive clear and accurate bills through the communication channels they prefer. By making it easier to issue and track bills, these tools reduce administrative workload and support a smoother payment experience for patients.
Many of these products also offer flexible payment options and financing plans that help patients manage their healthcare costs and pay their bills on time. By improving how payments are collected and providing a more patient-friendly billing experience, these solutions boost collection rates, reduce bad debt, and strengthen patient satisfaction and trust.

### Payment Reconciliation (19 products)

Payment Reconciliation tools help healthcare providers match every incoming payment, regardless of channel, with the corresponding electronic remittance advice (ERA) and posts the result to the correct patient and service-line accounts. By ingesting ERA files, converting paper EOBs, linking deposits to remits, and reconciling actual payments against payer contracts, these solutions eliminate manual work, shrink cash that’s been received but not yet matched, and surface denials or underpayments in real-time.
Beyond posting, many platforms track variances and route to appropriate teams, generate audit-ready trails for compliance, and power analytics dashboards that track payer performance and cash-flow. Some of these solutions include native lockbox solutions while others integrate with bank lockboxes. Most integrate with EHR and billing systems, clearinghouses, and general-ledger workflows ensures every dollar is captured, balanced and viable.
