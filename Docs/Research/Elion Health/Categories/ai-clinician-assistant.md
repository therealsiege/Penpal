# AI Clinician Assistant

The AI Clinician Assistant category includes a variety of AI-powered tools designed to make clinicians’ work more efficient, support better decision-making, and reduce time spent on routine administrative tasks. By automating and streamlining parts of clinical workflows, these solutions help providers spend more time focused on patient care and less on documentation and system navigation.

Key tools in this category include AI Ambient Scribes that capture conversations and automatically create clinical notes, and AI Clinical Summaries that pull and condense important patient information for tasks like pre-charting and handoffs. AI Inbox Management automates sorting and responding to clinical messages, while Smart EHR UI tools make electronic health records more intuitive and user-friendly. Other solutions provide real-time decision support at the point of care, structured clinical pathways and reference materials to guide treatment choices, and AI tools that enhance surgical procedures by analyzing data and delivering insights during operations. Together, these capabilities strengthen clinical workflows and help providers deliver safer, more effective care.

## Subcategories

### AI Ambient Scribes (111 products)

AI Ambient Scribes are companies that use AI to passively capture clinical encounters and produce notes or transcripts. They go beyond simple dictation tools or remote scribes by combining speech-to-text technology with large language models trained for clinical contexts. These platforms capture both patient conversations and clinicians’ dictations, generate transcripts, and synthesize them into clinical notes.
For most scribes, these notes will then be automatically integrated with into the EHR. Some scribes may include an additional review layer that verifies the AI’s output. Increasingly, AI Ambient Scribes add features like prompts or coding suggestions to close care gaps or increase documentation completeness and accuracy.

### AI Clinical Summaries (43 products)

AI Clinical Summary solutions use artificial intelligence to gather and distill clinical information into clear, concise summaries. These tools help providers quickly understand complex patient data, saving time and supporting more informed care decisions.
They serve many purposes, from pre-charting and referral intake to identifying care or diagnosis gaps, tracking quality measures, curating real-world evidence, matching patients to clinical trials, or preparing documentation for registries, insurance reviews, or legal needs. Because summaries are so versatile, these solutions often overlap with clinical decision support, care gap analytics, patient intake, and clinical documentation improvement tools, pairing the power of AI with specific workflow needs.

### AI Inbox Management (12 products)

The AI Inbox Management category focuses on solutions that automate and streamline the management of clinical inboxes within healthcare settings, reducing administrative burdens on providers and improving communication workflows. These tools integrate directly with EHR systems to process various types of patient communications, documents, and inquiries. By using AI to categorize and prioritize tasks—such as e-faxes, lab results, referrals, and patient messages—these platforms help clinicians efficiently manage their in-baskets and ensure timely responses to urgent needs.
A key advantage of AI inbox management is its ability to reduce physician burnout by minimizing repetitive manual tasks and enabling faster triage of patient messages. These tools often include advanced features like sentiment analysis to assess patient satisfaction and context-aware response generation to help clinicians draft personalized messages. Automating the routing of messages and updates to patient charts also enhances care coordination and ensures that critical information is promptly addressed, contributing to better patient outcomes and more efficient healthcare operations.

### AI Surgical Clinical Decision Support (8 products)

The AI Surgical Clinical Decision Support category encompasses tools designed to enhance surgical procedures through advanced data analysis and real-time insights. These platforms integrate video monitoring and data streams from surgical tools, life support systems, and other instruments to provide surgeons and clinical teams with valuable feedback during procedures. By analyzing various data inputs, these tools can offer actionable insights that improve surgical precision, patient safety, and overall outcomes. They assist in real-time decision-making, allowing providers to make adjustments during operations based on up-to-the-minute data.
A significant benefit of these systems is their potential to reduce variability in surgical practices and improve outcomes across a wide range of procedures. By providing clinical teams with AI-driven insights, they can better predict complications, identify optimal strategies, and ensure that surgical workflows align with best practices.

### Outpatient Diagnostic Support (14 products)

Outpatient Diagnostic Support products use advanced technologies like AI and machine learning to help healthcare providers diagnose and manage medical conditions in outpatient settings. These tools improve diagnostic accuracy, support earlier detection of diseases, and streamline clinical workflows, which leads to better patient outcomes and greater operational efficiency.
These solutions integrate with EHRs, pathology systems, and imaging devices, making them easy to use within existing care environments. They analyze data from clinical records, lab results, images, or even patient speech to deliver actionable insights for conditions across specialties such as oncology, ophthalmology, chronic disease management, mental health, and nutrition. Some products offer autonomous diagnostic capabilities for on-the-spot decision-making, while others support continuous monitoring and personalized prevention. By identifying disease markers, managing incidental findings, and providing real-time risk assessments, these tools help close care gaps, expand access to specialist-level insights, and strengthen outpatient care through earlier and more accurate interventions.

### Smart EHR UI (4 products)

Smart EHR UI products enhance the way clinicians interact with existing EHR systems by sitting on top of them and organizing the most relevant information for specific workflows. These tools pull in data from the EHR and other connected sources, then surface and summarize what clinicians need to see to make faster, more informed decisions during patient care.
Using AI, machine learning, natural language processing, and advanced search, Smart EHR UI solutions create a more intuitive and streamlined experience than the standard EHR interface alone. They not only display information clearly but also allow clinicians to update records and write data back into the EHR seamlessly, helping to reduce clicks, save time, and make clinical workflows more efficient.
