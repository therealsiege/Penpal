# AI Contact Center

The AI Contact Center category uses artificial intelligence to make communication, support, and operations more efficient within healthcare contact centers. By automating repetitive tasks and improving how staff interact with patients and payers, these solutions help organizations deliver faster, more reliable service while reducing costs and boosting patient and customer satisfaction.

Key tools include Patient-Facing AI Phone Calls that handle reminders, prescription refills, and common questions, and Payer-Facing AI Phone Calls that automate tasks like prior authorizations and claims checks. AI Conversational Chatbots provide instant answers to routine questions, while AI Agent Assistants help contact center staff by offering real-time suggestions, retrieving information, and generating documentation during calls. AI Contact Center Intelligence tools analyze conversations to spot trends, optimize workflows, and support better service decisions. Together, these solutions make healthcare contact centers more responsive, cost-effective, and capable of delivering a better experience for patients and partners.

## Subcategories

### AI Contact Center Agent Assistants (24 products)

AI Contact Center Assistants support contact center agents, managers, and roles like nurse triage by enhancing human-led workflows rather than replacing them. These tools are designed to help people handling calls or messages work more efficiently and effectively. Core capabilities include generating call summaries or transcripts, managing follow-up tasks, highlighting care gaps and next steps during interactions, and surfacing relevant policies or procedures in real time.
They can also deliver live quality assurance and feedback to agents while they work. Many companies in this space pair these assistants with fully autonomous solutions and often bundle them with AI-powered analytics and intelligence tools to provide deeper insights and performance improvements.

### AI Contact Center Intelligence (29 products)

AI Contact Center Analytics and Intelligence solutions use AI to assess contact center performance and deliver insights to boost efficiency and effectiveness. These tools focus on analyzing large volumes of voice and text data from customer interactions to uncover patterns and improvement areas. Core use cases include evaluating agent performance, generating feedback, analyzing conversation content to suggest ways to meet customer needs, and measuring customer sentiment.
By turning complex contact center data into actionable recommendations, these solutions help managers and teams understand what’s working, where gaps exist, and how to deliver better experiences and outcomes at scale.

### Patient-Facing AI Phone Calls (68 products)

Patient-Facing AI Phone Calls include products that use advanced artificial intelligence to interact with patients over the phone for a range of administrative and support tasks. While most tools handle inbound calls, many also offer outbound capabilities for things like appointment reminders. Some solutions extend these interactions to web and chat, using technologies like natural language processing, generative AI, and machine learning to enable smooth, human-like conversations.
These AI phone systems can help patients schedule appointments, get answers to common questions, request prescription refills, reach IT helpdesks, find providers or departments, and handle billing inquiries. What sets vendors apart is their ability to handle real-world complications like background noise, different accents, and unexpected conversation shifts while sounding natural and fluent. Strong solutions support multiple languages, integrate securely with EHRs, and can hand calls off to human agents when needed. They also offer analytics to monitor call success rates, track common issues, and maintain high standards of privacy and security.

### Payer-Facing AI Phone Calls (16 products)

Payer-Facing AI Phone Calls involve products that use AI to manage phone interactions with health insurers, primarily through outbound calls but sometimes with inbound capabilities as well. These tools help automate tasks such as verifying eligibility and benefits, securing prior authorizations, checking claim statuses, managing denied claims appeals, and supporting credentialing and provider management processes.
Since payer interactions occur in a more structured environment than patient calls, these products stand out based on their ability to integrate securely with EHRs, protect data privacy, and provide seamless fallback to human agents when needed. Many include dashboards for scheduling and tracking calls, payer-specific modules for navigating IVR systems and scripts, and low-code tools for customizing workflows. Strong analytics features help organizations monitor call outcomes, spot errors, and optimize performance.
