# Administrative

The Administrative category encompasses solutions designed to manage the operations and logistical aspects of healthcare organizations, ensuring efficient and compliant practices.

Patient Administration platforms handle key touchpoints along the patient journey, such as intake, scheduling, identity verification, and engagement, improving the patient experience while reducing administrative burdens.

Provider and Practice Administration solutions focus on managing the operational needs of healthcare practices, including workforce management, credentialing, and secure communications. These tools ensure that healthcare providers can maintain compliance, manage staff effectively, and streamline practice workflows. Together, these administrative tools and platforms enable healthcare organizations to operate efficiently, ensuring smooth coordination between financial, operational, and patient-facing activities.
