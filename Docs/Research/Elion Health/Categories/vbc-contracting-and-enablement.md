# VBC Contracting and Enablement

VBC Contracting and Enablement solutions help healthcare organizations design, negotiate, and manage contracts that tie payment to the quality and efficiency of care rather than the volume of services delivered. These tools make it easier for providers and payers to align on performance metrics, payment terms, and care standards needed to succeed under value-based care models.

In addition to supporting negotiations, these solutions guide the setup of reimbursement structures like bundled payments or capitation, define patient cohorts and networks, and help establish risk adjustment methods. By managing the financial and strategic aspects of value-based contracts, they give organizations the frameworks and tools they need to build sustainable arrangements that reward better patient outcomes and cost control.

## Subcategories

### ACO Enablers and Primary Care Aggregators (26 products)

The ACO Enablers and Primary Care Aggregators category includes organizations that help healthcare providers shift to and succeed in value-based care models. These partners play an important role for primary care practices and health systems by making it easier to participate in Medicare programs like the Shared Savings Program and ACO REACH, as well as Medicare Advantage and commercial value-based contracts. They provide the expertise and support needed to manage care more effectively and meet the goals of these programs.
These enablers offer technology platforms that bring together data, deliver actionable insights at the point of care, and provide population health analytics to identify gaps and manage patient outcomes. They also help with financial management, compliance, and regulatory reporting so that providers can operate sustainably while taking on financial risk. By supporting care coordination, chronic disease management, and patient engagement, these organizations help providers deliver better care, encourage healthy behaviors, and strengthen performance in risk-sharing arrangements.

### Network Design (12 products)

Network Design helps risk-bearing organizations identify and build partnerships with high-quality providers to form strong, efficient care networks. This capability is essential for value-based contracting, as larger, well-structured networks improve negotiation leverage and give organizations more control over costs and clinical outcomes. Companies offering Network Design services often also support Care Navigation, helping patients connect with the right providers within these optimized networks.
These services rely on robust analytics to assess provider quality and costs, ensuring that only high-performing providers are included. They also evaluate whether a network meets service and geographic requirements, advising on how to maintain adequate coverage and performance. By strengthening provider networks, these solutions help organizations achieve better results under value-based care models.

### Value-Based Contracting Consultants (13 products)

Value-Based Contracting Consultants help healthcare providers and payers design and negotiate contracts that tie payments to quality and outcomes instead of volume. These firms bring deep strategic and actuarial expertise to guide complex negotiations, especially for commercial risk arrangements with health plans, as well as with payers like Managed Medicaid and employers that may not use standardized contracting models.
Their services typically include designing reimbursement structures such as bundled payments or capitation, defining patient cohorts, building networks, and setting risk adjustment strategies. These consultants focus on the financial and strategic elements of value-based care contracts rather than day-to-day operations, offering actuarial analysis and expert guidance to ensure fair, sustainable agreements that align financial incentives with better patient outcomes.
