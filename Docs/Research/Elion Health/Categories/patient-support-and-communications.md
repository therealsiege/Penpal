# Patient Support and Communications

The Patient Support and Communications category includes tools that help healthcare providers strengthen patient engagement, improve communication, and maintain continuity of care. These solutions make it easier for patients to stay informed, connected, and active participants in their care journey, which ultimately leads to better experiences and outcomes.

Key subcategories include Care Plan Management tools that help patients and providers build and follow personalized treatment plans, and Secure Patient Communications platforms that enable safe, direct interactions between patients and care teams. Patient Education tools give patients clear information about their conditions and treatments, while Patient-Reported Outcomes systems gather feedback to inform clinical decisions. Bedside Engagement solutions keep patients involved during hospital stays, and Remote or AI Language Interpretation tools help bridge language gaps so all patients receive clear, equitable care. Together, these tools ensure patients feel supported, heard, and confident in managing their health.

## Subcategories

### Bedside Patient Engagement (16 products)

Bedside Patient Engagement covers the hardware and platforms designed to support patients during their hospital stay and equip clinicians and staff with tools to deliver better in-room care. These solutions usually include devices like tablets, control panels, and televisions that patients and staff can use to manage information and communication while in the hospital.
These platforms deliver educational content and care plan information alongside hardware to help patients understand their conditions or procedures. They are also typically integrated with the hospital’s electronic health record system, ensuring that patient data stays connected and up to date throughout the hospital stay.

### Care Plan Management (25 products)

Care Plan Management tools help providers create and deliver personalized care plans for needs such as maternal health, surgical preparation and recovery, and transitions between care settings. These platforms include customizable plan templates, patient education materials like guides and videos, and features for direct virtual communication with patients. They enable routine check-ins, deploy surveys to track adherence, and escalate issues to the broader care team when necessary, improving patient engagement and follow-through.
Although these tools are purpose-built for designing and managing individual care plans within clinical workflows, they often overlap with related solutions for care team coordination, patient education, secure communication, remote monitoring, and medication adherence. Unlike broader care management platforms, which support longitudinal oversight and population-level dashboards, care plan management tools focus on building, assigning, and maintaining detailed, patient-specific plans to guide day-to-day care delivery.

### Digital Patient Education (30 products)

Digital Patient Education tools help patients better understand their health conditions, treatment choices, and care plans by delivering information in clear, engaging formats. These solutions use videos, easy-to-read materials, and interactive content to explain medical diagnoses, procedures, and recommendations in ways that patients can easily absorb and act on.
These tools are typically digital and can be tailored or branded to match a specific healthcare provider or system. They are often embedded into patient portals, apps, or other engagement tools to fit naturally into the care experience. By making information more accessible and personalized, Digital Patient Education tools support stronger patient involvement, better adherence to treatment plans, and improved health outcomes.

### Medication Adherence (11 products)

The Medication Adherence category includes tools and platforms designed to help patients follow prescribed medication regimens, improving outcomes and reducing healthcare costs. These solutions leverage technologies like real-time notifications, predictive analytics, and automated workflows to minimize prescription abandonment and ensure timely medication use. They often integrate with pharmacies, medical offices, and health systems to streamline operations, offering features such as prescription tracking, automated dispensing, and home delivery.
Many of these platforms also support telepharmacy services and advanced monitoring tools, such as smart devices that track medication use, providing feedback to both patients and providers. This level of monitoring helps prevent adverse drug events and reduces readmission rates, especially for high-risk populations. By facilitating early interventions and supporting compliance with value-based care initiatives, medication adherence platforms play a vital role in enhancing patient outcomes and optimizing health system performance.

### Remote and AI-Enabled Language Interpretation (19 products)

Remote AI-Enabled Interpretation services help healthcare providers overcome language barriers by offering real-time medical translation using AI, human interpreters, or a combination of both. These services are designed specifically for clinical settings where accurate communication and secure handling of sensitive health information are critical.
Unlike general consumer translation tools, these solutions are built to meet HIPAA compliance, enterprise-level security standards, and the specialized vocabulary of medical conversations. They provide on-demand access to interpreters and AI translation tools 24/7, ensuring that patients and providers can communicate clearly at any time, which supports safer care and a better patient experience.

### Secure Patient Communications (26 products)

Secure Patient Communications includes digital tools that enable healthcare providers and patients to communicate safely and easily while meeting strict HIPAA requirements. These solutions cover secure messaging, calls, and emails that protect patient information and ensure that conversations stay confidential and trustworthy.
By providing a secure way to share updates and answer questions, these tools help patients better understand their conditions, follow treatment plans, and stay engaged in their care. They also support chronic care management and allow for convenient asynchronous interactions, making it easier for providers to respond to patient needs without requiring an in-person visit every time.
