# VBC Operations

The Value-Based Care Operations category includes solutions that help healthcare organizations deliver care efficiently and meet the goals of value-based payment models. These tools are designed to ensure patients receive the right care at the right time, supporting better outcomes and helping providers achieve performance benchmarks tied to quality and cost-effectiveness.

Key solutions include Care Management platforms that coordinate services for patients with chronic conditions and support preventive care throughout the patient journey. Care Navigation tools guide patients through complex healthcare systems, connecting them with referrals, specialized services, or community resources. Care Gap Closure and Service Extenders identify missing care and deliver timely interventions, often using community health workers or digital tools to expand the reach of care teams. Together, these solutions strengthen care delivery, improve patient engagement, and help organizations succeed under value-based care models.

## Subcategories

### Care Gap Closure and Service Extenders (26 products)

Care Gap Closure and Service Extenders are companies that deliver direct care services to help close care gaps, capture coding opportunities, and perform other clinical activities that enhance financial outcomes under value-based care contracts. These organizations expand the reach of a healthcare provider’s direct care delivery, such as by offering in-home annual wellness visits to ensure patients receive essential preventive care.
Solutions in this category are often viewed as last-mile care providers with specialized expertise in value-based care delivery and specific use cases. They combine hands-on clinical services with deep knowledge of the activities that directly impact the success of value-based contracts, helping organizations meet performance targets and improve patient outcomes.

### Care Management (34 products)

Care Management focuses on coordinating and overseeing patient care to improve health outcomes and meet value-based care goals. Companies in this category offer solutions that help providers manage the care of their patient populations more effectively, using tools that support structured care planning and oversight.
These solutions range from provider-facing enablement software to patient engagement tools that motivate patients to take an active role in their care. Many companies also deliver patient-facing services through professionals skilled in care management, care navigation, or care coordination. This category often overlaps with Care Plan Management and Care Navigation, as many vendors provide integrated capabilities that bridge these related functions.

### Care Navigation (15 products)

Care Navigation focuses on guiding patients through high-value care networks to ensure they receive the right services from the right providers for the best outcomes at the lowest cost. This often includes managing referral processes to connect patients with appropriate specialists or services while avoiding unnecessary spending.
