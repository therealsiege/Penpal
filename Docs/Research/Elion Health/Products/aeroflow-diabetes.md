# Aeroflow Diabetes

Aeroflow Health’s Diabetes Management Solutions is a population-level program for health plans and providers that identifies members with diabetes, equips them with eligible monitoring devices, and supports their self-management through education and clinical coaching. The platform offers a personalized benefits-verification storefront where patients can access covered items such as CGMs and blood pressure monitors, along with structured diabetes self-management education addressing medication use, lifestyle modification, and glucose monitoring.
