# Collibra

Collibra Platform is a unified data-intelligence and governance solution designed to help organizations discover, catalog, manage, govern and protect their data and AI assets. It enables enterprises to create a “single source of truth” for data and metadata, supports data quality, lineage, policy enforcement, collaboration, and AI-use case governance.
