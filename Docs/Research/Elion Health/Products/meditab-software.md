# Meditab Software

Meditab offers a comprehensive, all-in-one EHR + practice management ecosystem aimed at ambulatory and specialty medical practices. Its features include a fully customizable EMR for over 40 specialties, mobile and cloud access, patient portal and app, telemedicine, integrated lab and diagnostic orders, billing and payment processing, practice analytics, scheduling, patient engagement, and other administrative tools (appointment booking, lead management, etc.).
