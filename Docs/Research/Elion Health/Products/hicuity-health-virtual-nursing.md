# Hicuity Health Virtual Nursing

Hicuity Health's Virtual Nursing is a telehealth solution that supports inpatient care by providing remote nursing services for activities such as patient admissions, discharge processes, medication reconciliation, and patient monitoring. It allows bedside teams to focus on critical in-person tasks by handling routine functions remotely, reducing the need for travel nurses.
