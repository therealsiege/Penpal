# Syntrillo

Syntrillo is developing an AI-enabled telehealth platform aimed at preventing strokes by integrating data from wearable devices and clinical records to monitor and manage patients' stroke risk. The platform features an automated disease monitoring system that models patient risk and behavior, generates personalized prevention plans, and includes an AI virtual assistant providing 24/7 support to patients and providers.
