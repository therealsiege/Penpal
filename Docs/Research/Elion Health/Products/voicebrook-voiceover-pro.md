# Voicebrook VoiceOver PRO

Voicebrook VoiceOver PRO is a voice-driven pathology reporting platform designed specifically for anatomic pathology labs. It combines speech recognition with structured, rules-based reporting to produce standardized pathology reports while reducing manual data entry, corrections, and rework.
