# Reveleer

Reveleer is an AI-powered platform for value-based healthcare, offering solutions for risk adjustment, quality improvement, and member management. It automates medical record retrieval, coding, and clinical data analysis to help health plans and provider organizations improve reimbursement accuracy and care quality.
