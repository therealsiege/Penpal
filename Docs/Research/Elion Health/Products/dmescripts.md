# DMEscripts

DMEscripts is an electronic prescribing platform designed for durable medical equipment (DME) orders. It guides healthcare providers through a step-by-step process to generate complete and accurate equipment orders, verifies patient insurance/qualification rules, integrates with an EHR, sends electronic prescriptions to suppliers, and allows tracking of the order from submission to delivery.
