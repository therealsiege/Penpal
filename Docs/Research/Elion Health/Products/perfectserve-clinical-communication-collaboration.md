# PerfectServe Clinical Communication Collaboration

PerfectServe Clinical Communication Collaboration is a secure platform designed to improve real-time communication and coordination among healthcare teams. It offers role-based messaging, voice calling, and alert notifications while integrating with EHRs, scheduling systems, and nurse call solutions.
