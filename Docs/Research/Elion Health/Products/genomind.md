# Genomind

Genomind uses pharmacogenetic testing to tailor medication choices based on an individual's genetic profile. Their services include precision medicine software to identify gene-drug interaction risks, adjustment of treatment plans, and consultations with PharmD and PhD experts for healthcare providers.
