# WellBeam

WellBeam is a platform for bridging the care continuum between acute and post-acute (home health, hospice) settings. It allows MDs and advanced practice providers (APPs) to electronically sign post-acute orders, receive clinician messages about changes in patient status (e.g. wound photos), and handle urgent clinician communications from anywhere—directly within their EMR workflows.
