# Gesund.ai

Gesund.ai is a platform that helps developers of medical AI models conduct independent validation studies to meet regulatory standards such as FDA requirements. It offers tools for testing AI models on diverse, real-world datasets and supports compliance, transparency, and scalability for healthcare AI validation.
