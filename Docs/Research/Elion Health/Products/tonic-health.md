# Tonic Health

Tonic Health is a real-time, mobile-first patient intake and contactless check-in platform that enables patients to complete forms, screenings, eConsents, surveys, and payments from any device, anywhere. It automates the full intake workflow by delivering customizable digital forms, pre-visit intake, point-of-care check-in, risk assessments, PROs, patient satisfaction surveys, and secure payment capture.
