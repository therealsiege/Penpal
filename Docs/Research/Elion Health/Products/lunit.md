# Lunit

Lunit is a medical imaging AI suite used in radiology reading workflows for chest X‑ray and mammography screening and triage. It provides automated abnormality detection and scoring, identifying thoracic findings on chest X‑ray and localizing lesions with malignancy likelihood on mammography, and prioritizes urgent cases with triage and worklist features.
