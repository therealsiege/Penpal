# Bot MD Care

Bot MD Care is a patient engagement platform that automates various clinical workflows using popular chat apps like WhatsApp, Messenger, and iMessage. It allows providers to customize and automate tasks such like scheduling, medication reminders, and patient education, without requiring patients to download additional apps.
