# healow

[Contact](https://plus.healow.com/demo-request/)

Healow is a patient engagement platform offering online appointment booking, telehealth visits, and secure messaging between patients and healthcare providers. It supports eCheck-In, appointment reminders, health records access, and bill payments, integrating with the EHR to streamline patient-provider interactions.
