# Prognos Health

Prognos Health operates a real-world data marketplace that integrates de-identified clinical and genomic laboratory data from over 325 million patients. This platform enables healthcare organizations to license harmonized data, facilitating analyses such as patient journey mapping, healthcare provider targeting, and commercial analytics across various therapeutic areas.
