# Pivotal Analytics

Pivotal Analytics provides healthcare market intelligence and geospatial analytics tools designed for healthcare organizations, including hospitals, health systems, and real estate developers. Its platform offers data visualization, competitive analysis, and demographic insights to help users make informed decisions about facility planning, service expansion, and patient access.
