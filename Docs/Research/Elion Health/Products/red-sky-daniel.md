# Red Sky Daniel

Red Sky Health offers an AI platform called Daniel that automates the denial management process for healthcare providers. Daniel uses machine learning and generative AI to analyze 100% of denied claims, identify errors based on historical billing and denial patterns, recommend corrections, and automate resubmission to maximize the likelihood of payment.
