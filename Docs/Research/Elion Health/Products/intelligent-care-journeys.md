# Intelligent Care Journeys

Intelligent Care Journeys by Diagnostic Robotics is an AI-powered solution designed to assist care teams in preventing adverse health events, such as hospitalizations, emergency department visits, and the worsening of chronic conditions. It employs predictive models to manage conditions like COPD, diabetes, musculoskeletal issues, and heart failure.
