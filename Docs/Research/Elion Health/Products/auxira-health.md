# Auxira Health

Auxira Health is a virtual care extension platform purpose-built for cardiology practices, focused on improving access, provider efficiency, and patient engagement. The company offers a dedicated, remotely staffed Advanced Practice Provider (APP)-led care team that integrates directly into a practice’s workflows and EHR
