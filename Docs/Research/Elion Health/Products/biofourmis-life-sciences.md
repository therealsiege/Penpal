# Biofourmis Life Sciences

Biofourmis offers life sciences solutions through a platform that supports decentralized clinical trials. Their system includes tools for remote patient management, eConsent, ePRO, remote data capture, and virtual visits. It also provides device-agnostic integration for continuous and episodic data collection and AI-driven analytics for developing digital biomarkers.
