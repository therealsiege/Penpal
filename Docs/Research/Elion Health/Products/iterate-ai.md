# Iterate.ai

Iterate.ai Generate is an on-prem, privacy-first AI platform that allows healthcare organizations to run large or small language models locally to analyze documents, claims, and operational data without sending data to the cloud. In healthcare use cases, it is applied to revenue integrity and recovery by analyzing EMR, billing, and EDI data to identify underpaid, denied, or misfiled claims and surface root causes before deadlines.
