# Keywell AI

Keywell’s AI Enablement Platform is a HIPAA-compliant, Databricks-powered system designed for healthcare organizations to deploy and govern enterprise AI securely. Preloaded with healthcare ontologies and data models such as SNOMED and CMS, it enables analytics, policy research, and operational automation for providers, payers, and public health agencies.
