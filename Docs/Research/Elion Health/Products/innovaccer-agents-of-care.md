# Innovaccer Agents of Care

Innovaccer Agents of Care is an AI-powered virtual assistant designed to support care teams by automating administrative tasks and providing real-time clinical insights. It integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) and other healthcare systems to assist with patient outreach, care coordination, and documentation.
