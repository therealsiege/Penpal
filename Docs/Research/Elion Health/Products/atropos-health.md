# Atropos Health

[Contact](https://www.atroposhealth.com/contact-us/)

Atropos Health provides evidence generation solutions that support clinical decision-making by utilizing real-world data (RWD). It offers tools for health systems, research institutions, and graduate medical education (GME) programs to derive insights from clinical and population health data. These insights can improve patient outcomes, enhance value-based care, and guide quality initiatives. The platform allows organizations to monitor interventions in real-time and leverage comparative effectiveness research to optimize clinical operations, care pathways, and drug formularies.
