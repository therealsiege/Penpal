# Get Well 360 Platform

Get Well 360 is a digital patient engagement platform that supports the entire patient journey — pre-visit, inpatient, outpatient, transitions of care, and long-term engagement. It offers modules for activation & growth, care navigation, point-of-care engagement, ED interaction, health equity, and personalized digital care plans.
