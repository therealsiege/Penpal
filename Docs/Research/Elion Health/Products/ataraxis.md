# Ataraxis

Ataraxis AI develops AI-powered diagnostic tools to enhance cancer prognosis and treatment selection. Their flagship product, Ataraxis Breast, is an AI-native test that analyzes multi-modal patient data, including standard pathology slides, to predict breast cancer recurrence risk and assist in personalizing treatment decisions.
