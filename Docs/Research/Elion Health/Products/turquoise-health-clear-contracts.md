# Turquoise Health Clear Contracts

Turquoise Health Clear Contracts is an AI-powered contract intelligence platform designed for healthcare organizations to manage, analyze, and compare payer contracts. It centralizes contract documents and amendments, allowing users to search for terms across agreements, identify rate trends, and prepare for negotiations using a master matrix view.
