# OmniMD AI Front Desk

OmniMD AI Front Desk is an AI medical receptionist that fits in the front-desk and patient-access workflow to automate patient intake, scheduling, check-in, and follow-up. It provides real-time insurance eligibility and referral verification, performs automated appointment scheduling and reminders, and supports downstream billing tasks such as auto-coding, invoice generation, and copay application.
