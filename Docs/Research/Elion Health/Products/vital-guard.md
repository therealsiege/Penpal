# Vital Guard

Vital Guard is an AI-based, EHR-connected solution that analyzes radiology reports and clinical documentation using natural language processing to identify incidental findings detected during imaging studies ordered for unrelated reasons. The system generates prioritized worklists of findings that were not communicated during the clinical encounter and facilitates follow-up through clinician outreach and patient text messaging.
