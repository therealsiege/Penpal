# InstantScripts

InstantScripts is an Australian online healthcare platform providing convenient access to medical services such as prescriptions, telehealth consultations, medical certificates, and specialist referrals. Founded in 2018, it allows users to request prescriptions for over 300 medications, with options for eScripts sent to their phone or home delivery.
