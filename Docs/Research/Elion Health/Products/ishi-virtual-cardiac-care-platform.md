# ISHI Virtual Cardiac Care Platform

ISHI Virtual Cardiac Care Platform supports cardiovascular care through AI-driven risk stratification, personalized care pathways, and clinician collaboration tools. The platform enables remote monitoring, virtual consultations, and multidisciplinary care coordination for patients with heart failure and related conditions, helping health systems extend specialist access and manage population-level cardiac risk more effectively.
