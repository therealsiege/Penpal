# Sentact OnDemand

Sentact OnDemand is a real-time issue reporting and resolution platform designed for healthcare facilities to support service recovery, safety, and operational responsiveness. It allows staff to report problems immediately, either during or outside of structured rounding, and routes tasks to the appropriate departments based on hospital protocols.
