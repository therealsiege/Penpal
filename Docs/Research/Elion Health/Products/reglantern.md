# RegLantern

RegLantern provides tools and services to help healthcare centers maintain compliance with the Health Resources and Services Administration (HRSA) requirements. It offers features such as mock site surveys, continuous compliance updates, and the RegPathway suite of web-based tools to simplify meeting HRSA standards
