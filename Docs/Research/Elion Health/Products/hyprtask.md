# hyprtask

Hyprtask is an AI-powered automation platform built to manage and execute administrative and revenue cycle workflows for healthcare practices. It automates tasks such as insurance eligibility verification, prior authorizations, patient registration, claims submission, payment posting, AR follow-up, scheduling, and fax or medical records submissions. The system integrates directly with existing EHRs and payer portals without requiring IT setup, coding, or new hardware
