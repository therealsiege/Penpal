# Calvient Practice Engagement

Practice Engagement is a patient engagement module in Calvient’s practice operations platform that supports pre-visit, visit, and post-visit workflows to manage appointment attendance and follow-up. It provides automated multi-channel appointment reminders and smart follow-up sequences via SMS, email, and phone, supports pre-visit preparation such as forms and insurance verification, and offers a patient education library tailored by diagnosis or specialty.
