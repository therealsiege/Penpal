# Academy of Interpretation Interpreter Training

The Academy of Interpretation offers training courses for individuals seeking certification as professional interpreters, with a focus on medical, legal, and community interpretation. Their programs cover essential skills such as medical terminology, ethics, cultural competency, and real-world interpreting scenarios.
