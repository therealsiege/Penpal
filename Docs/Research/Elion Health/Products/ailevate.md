# Ailevate

Ailevate is an on-premises AI platform designed to autonomously resolve denied healthcare claims by identifying and correcting coding errors without changing existing workflows. It integrates with EHRs, practice management systems, and clearinghouses to fix denial issues in real time, enabling immediate resubmission and improving cash flow.
