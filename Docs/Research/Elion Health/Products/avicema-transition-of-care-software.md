# AviTracks-TC

AviTracks-TC is a configurable transition of care management software designed to automate patient care transitions and support CMS compliance for Transitional Care Management (TCM) services. It enables healthcare organizations to manage patient enrollment, create electronic care plans, assign team tasks, configure workflows, monitor patient risks, and document time for Medicare billing codes (99495 and 99496).
