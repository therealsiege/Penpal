# Accuity

Accuity is a CDI solution that combines physician-led reviews with technology to enhance the accuracy and compliance of hospital documentation. This pre-bill review system helps health systems capture more accurate diagnoses and procedures, leading to optimized reimbursements and improved clinical outcomes.
