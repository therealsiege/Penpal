# Sword Health

Sword Health is a digital platform offering virtual musculoskeletal (MSK) care, combining artificial intelligence with clinical expertise to provide personalized physical therapy programs. Users engage in tailored exercise routines guided by licensed physical therapists and receive real-time feedback through FDA-listed wearable technology.
