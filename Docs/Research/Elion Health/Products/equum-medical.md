# Equum Medical

Equum Medical is a telehealth services provider focused on augmenting acute care delivery through virtual clinical staff, particularly in hospitals facing workforce shortages and rising patient acuity. Equum offers a range of remote care services—including Virtual Nursing, Tele-Critical Care, Tele-ED, Tele-Transfer, Remote Patient Monitoring, and Virtual Sitters—to improve patient flow, staffing efficiency, and clinical outcomes.
