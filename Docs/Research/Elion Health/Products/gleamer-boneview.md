# Gleamer BoneView

Gleamer BoneView is an AI-driven X‑ray interpretation tool used in radiology reading workflows for preliminary reads and second reads to support fracture and bone-trauma detection in emergency and musculoskeletal care. It detects fractures, effusions, dislocations, and focal bone lesions and highlights suspected regions with bounding boxes and confidence labels.
