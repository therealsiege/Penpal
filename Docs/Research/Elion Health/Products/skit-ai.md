# Skit.ai

Skit.ai is a generative AI platform designed for healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM) providers, focusing on early-out collections and patient financial engagement. It automates omnichannel communication through voice, SMS, email, and chat to support both inbound and outbound interactions.
