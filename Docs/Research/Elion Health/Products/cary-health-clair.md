# Cary Health Clair

Cary Health Clair is a clinical reference platform used at the point of care and within clinical decision-support workflows. It provides natural-language question-and-answer search and concise clinical summaries, and it supports medication decision tools including drug interaction checks and stewardship guidance on treatment, dose, and duration.
