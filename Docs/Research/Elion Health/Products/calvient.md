# Calvient

Contact

Calvient's unified platform combines patient engagement tools with AI-powered document management to enhance healthcare operations. It offers online scheduling, automated appointment reminders, digital registration, and secure messaging to facilitate patient interactions. The system also includes an AI-driven fax inbox that automates document processing, integrating with electronic health records to reduce administrative tasks.
