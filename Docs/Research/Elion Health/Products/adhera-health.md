# Adhera Health

Adhera Health offers a digital health platform called the AI-Precision Digital Companion, which provides personalized support and interventions for families managing pediatric chronic conditions. It focuses on enhancing the wellbeing of both patients and caregivers through digital programs, expert coaching, and mental health support.
