# House Rx

House Rx offers a technology-enabled specialty pharmacy platform that works with community oncology and specialty care practices to manage medication dispensing and improve patient outcomes. Their solution supports in-practice pharmacy enablement, prior authorization support, medication access assistance, and patient engagement through care coordination.
