# Glooko

Glooko is a diabetes management platform that enables healthcare providers to remotely monitor patients' glucose levels, insulin usage, and other health metrics. It integrates with a wide range of diabetes devices, including glucose meters, insulin pumps, and continuous glucose monitors (CGMs), to collect and analyze patient data.
