# PharmaTools.AI Patiently AI

Patiently AI is a patient-facing language-simplification tool that converts medical notes, test results, and clinical documents into plain, readable explanations. It allows users to paste, scan, upload, or record medical text and receive patient-friendly output with adjustable reading level and tone.
