# FinThrive Patient Access

FinThrive Patient Access is designed to enhance the patient financial experience by improving price transparency, streamlining digital check-ins, and increasing efficiency in revenue collection. It enables patient scheduling and intake, eligibility checking, payment collection, and price estimates.
