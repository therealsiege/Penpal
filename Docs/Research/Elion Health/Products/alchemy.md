# Alchemy

Alchemy Health provides infrastructure for clinics to establish in-house pharmacies, focusing on 340B programs and public health entities like FQHCs, HIV clinics, and STD testing centers. They offer comprehensive support, including physical setup, staffing, and digital tools to manage operations, helping clinics maintain pharmacy ownership while outsourcing operations. Their platform also includes clinical programs to expand patient outreach, such as mobile testing services.
