# Net-Rx RecRx

Net-Rx RecRx is a pharmacy claims reconciliation platform designed to help pharmacies verify third-party payer reimbursements against adjudicated claims. It automates the process of matching electronic remittance advice (835 files) to claims data, identifies short-paid or missing payments, and provides tools for tracking expected funds.
