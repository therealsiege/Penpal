# Medicom

Medicom is an imaging-specific health information network that offers tools for locating, accessing, and sharing clinical records and medical images. Key products include Medicom Connect for seamless information exchange, Medicom Intellect for research insights, and Patient Link for patients to share their medical records electronically.
