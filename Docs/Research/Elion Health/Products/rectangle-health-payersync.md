# Rectangle Health PayerSync

Rectangle Health offers PayerSync as part of its Practice Management Bridge platform, focusing on automating and simplifying payer enrollment and credentialing for healthcare providers. PayerSync is used to track, manage, and update payer contracts and credentialing status, reducing manual workflows and administrative delays.
