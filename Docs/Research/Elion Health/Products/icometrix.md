# Icometrix

Icometrix icobrain provides AI-based quantitative analysis of brain MRI and fits into neurology workflows for longitudinal monitoring of multiple sclerosis, dementia, traumatic brain injury, stroke, and epilepsy. It quantifies FLAIR and T1 lesion volumes and measures global and regional brain volumes. It compares those measurements to age- and sex-matched normative data and provides color-coded visualizations and structured longitudinal reports.
