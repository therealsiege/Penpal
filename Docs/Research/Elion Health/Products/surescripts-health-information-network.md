# Surescripts Health Information Network

The Surescripts Health Information Network is a nationwide interoperability platform that enables the exchange of clinical data between healthcare providers, pharmacies, payers, and health systems. It supports use cases such as electronic prescribing, medication history sharing, clinical messaging, and record locator services.
