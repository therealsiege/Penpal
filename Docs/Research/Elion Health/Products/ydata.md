# YData

YData provides a data-centric AI development platform tailored for industries like healthcare and pharma, with a strong emphasis on synthetic data generation and profiling. Its platform, YData Fabric, helps healthcare organizations safely access and share patient data by generating privacy-preserving synthetic datasets that maintain the statistical integrity of the original data.
