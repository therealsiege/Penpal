# Medlytix

Medlytix is a revenue enhancement partner specializing in advanced analytics and intelligence to help healthcare providers uncover and recover hidden third-party payments. Their Rev iQ® suite includes several contingency-based solutions—with no setup fees—that operate on a “you get paid, we get paid” mode.
