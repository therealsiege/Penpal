# 1-800 Notify

1-800 Notify offers HIPAA-compliant communication services for healthcare providers, including automated appointment reminders, billing notifications, and payment processing solutions. These services aim to enhance patient engagement and streamline administrative workflows by delivering timely messages through phone calls, text messages, and emails.
