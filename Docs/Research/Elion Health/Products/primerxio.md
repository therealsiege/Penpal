# PrimeRx.io

PrimeRx is a pharmacy-management software platform designed for retail, specialty, and long-term care pharmacies. It offers features such as automatic refill management, patient engagement messaging, inventory and dispensing management, compounding workflows, multi-store visibility, point-of-sale, document management, and analytics concerning pharmacy operations.
