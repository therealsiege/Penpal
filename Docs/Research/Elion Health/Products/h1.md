# H1

H1 provides a healthcare data platform that aggregates and analyzes information to support medical, clinical, and commercial decision-making. The platform combines public data (e.g., government databases, hospital websites, clinical trial registries, and publications), private data (e.g., medical and pharmacy claims, social media activity, HCP and patient demographics), and proprietary data (e.g., client CRM systems, clinical trial management systems, and HCP insights from H1 Connect).
