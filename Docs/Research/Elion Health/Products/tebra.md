# Tebra

Tebra is a healthcare technology platform that provides electronic health records (EHR), practice management, billing, and patient engagement solutions for medical practices. Formed from the merger of Kareo and PatientPop, it offers tools for appointment scheduling, revenue cycle management, telehealth, and digital marketing to help independent practices manage operations and grow their patient base.
