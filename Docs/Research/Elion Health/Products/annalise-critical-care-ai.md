# Harrison.ai Comprehensive Care

Harrison.ai Comprehensive Care is an imaging AI triage solution for chest X-ray and non-contrast head CT used in radiology workflows for study prioritization and early detection. It detects 12 findings (seven head CT, five chest X-ray) and returns results rapidly to surface emergent and incidental findings.
