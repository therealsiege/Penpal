# AITRICS VitalCare

AITRICS Vital Care predicts the likelihood of patient deterioration within a hospital using data from EHRs including six vital signs, 11 blood test results, the patient's state of consciousness, and age to provide early warnings when a patient's condition could lead to sepsis, death, or cardiac arrest.
