# Omega Healthcare CodeIQ

CodeIQ is an AI-powered medical coding platform designed to assist in the automation and accuracy of clinical coding processes. It uses natural language processing and machine learning to extract and interpret clinical data from patient records, enabling the generation of medical codes that align with regulatory and payer requirements. CodeIQ is intended to support inpatient, outpatient, and specialty coding workflows, and can be used alongside human coders to increase productivity and reduce errors.
