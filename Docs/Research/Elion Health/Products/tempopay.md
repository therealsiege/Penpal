# TempoPay

TempoPay is a healthcare payment platform designed to help employees manage out-of-pocket medical expenses through flexible, no-interest repayment plans. TempoPay allows users to pay for healthcare services using a virtual or physical card, with repayment options that include payroll deductions or payments from personal accounts like HSAs.
