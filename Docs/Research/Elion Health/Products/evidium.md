# Evidium

Evidium provides a computational medical reasoning platform that transforms fragmented clinical guidelines, real-world data, and medical evidence into actionable, precise intelligence for healthcare organizations. The platform uses neurosymbolic AI and patented reasoning technology to understand each patient’s clinical journey, project likely outcomes, improve risk stratification, augment LLMs with trustworthy logic, and surface insights directly within workflows.
