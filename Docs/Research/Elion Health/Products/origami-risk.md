# Origami Risk

Origami Risk offers a cloud-based, software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform aimed at healthcare organizations for unified risk, safety, claims, insurance, quality, and compliance management. It enables tracking of events and hazards, incident and claim management, policy and provider credentialing, reporting, analytics, and workflows to meet requirements of regulatory & accrediting bodies like CMS, Joint Commission, OSHA, HIPAA. The platform helps centralize risk data, reduce manual overhead, spot trends to prevent harm to patients, employees, and facilities, and improve operational resilience.
