# Clarify Atlas

Clarify Health's Atlas platform is an enterprise analytics solution that leverages big data to provide comprehensive insights into healthcare performance, trends, and benchmarks. It maps over 4 billion patient care journeys, analyzing 15 billion claims to deliver AI-powered predictions and actionable insights for healthcare providers, payers, and life sciences companies.
