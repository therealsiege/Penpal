# Kinometrix

Kinometrix provides a healthcare analytics platform focused on preventing hospital-acquired conditions, with an emphasis on inpatient fall-risk assessment. Its machine learning solution, K-FRAS, analyzes patient data from EHRs to deliver real-time fall-risk predictions and actionable insights directly to clinicians. The platform integrates into existing workflows, enabling healthcare providers to intervene early based on individualized patient risk profiles.
