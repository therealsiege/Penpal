# Careview

CareView provides video-based patient monitoring systems designed to support hospital staff in reducing falls, enhancing patient safety, and improving visibility in inpatient settings. Its core offerings include continuous video monitoring, virtual nursing, and a virtual observer platform that allows staff to monitor multiple patients remotely.
