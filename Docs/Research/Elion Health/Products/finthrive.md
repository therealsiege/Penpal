# FinThrive

FinThrive provides a comprehensive end-to-end revenue management platform for healthcare organizations, including capabilities for patient access, revenue integrity, revenue collection, and recovery solutions. It supports claims submission to payers, integrating with EHR systems to ensure high first-pass payment rates and reduce denials through advanced claim edits and scrubbing.
