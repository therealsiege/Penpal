# MCG Indicia

MCG Indicia provides evidence-based care guidelines to optimize care delivery and clinical documentation to support reimbursement, case management, and care management objectives. It integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to offer real-time guidance based on MCG care guidelines, helping providers make informed decisions at critical points such as hospital admission and care transitions.
