# Brim Analytics

Contact

Brim Analytics is a software platform that uses AI to automate and accelerate the process of chart abstraction from electronic medical records. It is designed for use in medical research, clinical trials, tumor registries, and other healthcare applications where structured data must be extracted from unstructured clinical notes
