# 98point6 Technologies

98point6 is a virtual care platform used in clinical workflows to deliver asynchronous and real-time primary care and low-acuity services. It automates patient intake with intelligent question sets and photo capture, and supports text, audio, and video visits with escalation from asynchronous to real-time care.
