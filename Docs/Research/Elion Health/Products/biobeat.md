# Biobeat

Biobeat offers wearable monitoring devices, such as wrist and chest monitors, that provide continuous, real-time tracking of 13 vital signs using proprietary PPG sensor technology. The platform is used for in-hospital, ambulatory, and remote patient monitoring, supporting applications like early deterioration detection, personalized care, and clinical research.
