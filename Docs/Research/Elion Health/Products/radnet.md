# Radnet

RadNet operates a network of outpatient diagnostic imaging centers across the United States, offering services such as MRI, ultrasound, and 3D mammography. RadNet also partners with hospitals and health systems, engages in quality assurance programs, and offers specialized imaging for conditions like breast, lung, prostate, and brain health.
