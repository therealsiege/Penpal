# Unity AI

Unity AI is a conversational AI platform used in ambulatory and outpatient healthcare workflows for patient access, scheduling, intake, confirmations, and follow-up. It provides distinct AI agents that book and reschedule appointments, send multi-touch reminders with preparation instructions, and collect intake data such as demographics, medical history, and insurance.
