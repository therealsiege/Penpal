# Oneview Healthcare Care Experience Platform

The Oneview Care Experience Platform is a bedside and mobile experience system used by hospitals to give patients access to education, entertainment, care-team communication, and non-clinical requests through a unified interface. It integrates with hospital EMRs and other operational systems to display personalized care plans, deliver tailored educational content, support digital meal ordering, enable service requests, and facilitate virtual care. The platform also provides workflow tools for staff, including digital rounding, room control, and coordination of patient services. Hospitals use it to reduce non-urgent call-light workload, improve HCAHPS-related experience measures, and streamline communication between patients and care teams.
