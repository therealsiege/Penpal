# SaveLife.AI

SaveLife.AI is a healthcare technology company offering AI-driven solutions for radiology, clinical documentation, and diagnostic support. Its products include RadioView.AI and RadReport for automated radiology reporting, AizaMD for ambient clinical intelligence that transcribes patient encounters into structured notes, and tools such as NeuroICH, DenseViewAI, MammoSightAI, and CXRDetectAI for brain hemorrhage, breast density, mammography, and chest X-ray analysis.
