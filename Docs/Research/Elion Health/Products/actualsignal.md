# ActualSignal

ActualSignal is a digital neurology care platform that uses patients’ smartphones (no additional hardware required) to collect continuous neurological data—such as movement, tapping tests, mood/symptom surveys—and shares these insights real-time with care teams and payers to support proactive interventions, especially in conditions like Parkinson’s disease.
