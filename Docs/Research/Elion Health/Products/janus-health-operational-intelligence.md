# Janus Health Operational Intelligence

[Contact](https://info.janus-ai.com/janus-health-elion)

Janus Health’s Operational Intelligence platform is designed to analyze and enhance healthcare revenue cycle operations by providing deep, actionable insights into workforce efficiency and process performance. The solution collects task-level data through a lightweight desktop agent that monitors user interactions across relevant applications, including those not covered by traditional EHR reporting.
