# Zedmed

Zedmed is an Australian-based medical practice management software designed for general practices, specialists, and allied health providers. It offers both cloud-based and on-premise solutions with features that cover clinical management, billing, e-prescribing, document handling, and patient communications. Its key functionalities include integrated e-prescribing, customizable reporting, online appointment scheduling, two-way SMS for patient reminders, and results management.
