# Nsight Health

Nsight Health offers remote patient monitoring (RPM), chronic care management, and transitional care solutions. The platform uses AI to automate care workflows, improve patient engagement, and support 24/7 care coordination. It integrates with over 50 EMR systems and uses devices like glucose meters and pulse oximeters for real-time monitoring.
