# Silna

Silna Health focuses on helping specialty healthcare providers manage prior authorizations, benefits, and eligibility checks. It is designed for use in physical therapy, speech therapy, and behavioral health. Silna automates the authorization process using AI, provides regular benefit and eligibility checks, and sends reminders for expiring authorizations to prevent lapses in care.
