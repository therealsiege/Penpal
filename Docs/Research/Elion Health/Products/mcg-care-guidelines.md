# MCG Care Guidelines

MCG Care Guidelines is an evidence-based clinical decision support product that provides standardized care guidance across inpatient, ambulatory, behavioral health, post-acute, and home care settings. It synthesizes peer-reviewed clinical literature into condition-specific criteria used to inform medical necessity, level of care decisions, care planning, and documentation.
