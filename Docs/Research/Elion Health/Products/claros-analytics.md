# Claros Analytics

Claros Analytics offers advanced predictive analytics for the health benefits industry, enabling users to assess and manage risk in healthcare plans effectively. Some of their solutions include projecting plan costs, evaluating self-funding risk and rewards, budgeting, and building IBNR reserves.
