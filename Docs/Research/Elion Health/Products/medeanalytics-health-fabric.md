# MedeAnalytics Health Fabric

MedeAnalytics Health Fabric is a healthcare data platform that unifies and curates data from multiple clinical, financial, and operational systems to support analytics and decision-making. It offers real-time data integration, cleansing, and enrichment, and allows users to conduct ad-hoc analysis via third-party tools like Tableau or Amazon QuickSight.
