# CompuGroup Medical Aprima

CGM Aprima is an EHR and practice management system designed to adapt to individual provider workflows, improving efficiency and patient care. It integrates with health information exchanges like Carequality and CommonWell, and offers options for practice management, telehealth, and revenue cycle services.
