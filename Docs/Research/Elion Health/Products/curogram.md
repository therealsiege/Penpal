# Curogram

Curogram is a patient communication and telemedicine platform used in front‑office and clinical workflows for patient intake and appointment management. It provides secure two‑way texting with document links, automated customizable appointment reminders via SMS, email, and phone, and virtual visit features including virtual waiting rooms and video visits.
