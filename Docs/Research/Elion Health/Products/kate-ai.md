# KATE AI

KATE AI is a machine-learning clinical platform that supports emergency department triage by providing real-time risk and acuity guidance. It continuously reads structured and free-text electronic health record data, identifies under- and over-triage and high-risk presentations, and provides modules such as KATE Triage and KATE Sepsis along with dashboards and cohorting tools for quality improvement.
