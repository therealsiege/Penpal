# ASAM Criteria

The ASAM Criteria is an evidence-based clinical guideline product that provides standardized criteria for assessing, placing, and transitioning patients with substance use disorders and co-occurring conditions across levels of care. It defines a structured framework using multidimensional clinical assessments to determine appropriate treatment intensity, setting, and ongoing care decisions.
