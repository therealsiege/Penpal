# Manta Health

Manta Health is an AI-powered coverage and prior authorization platform designed for ambulatory medical practices. It automates real-time eligibility checks and end-to-end prior authorization workflows across a wide range of payers, using EHR integrations to pull required clinical and demographic data without changing existing staff workflows.
