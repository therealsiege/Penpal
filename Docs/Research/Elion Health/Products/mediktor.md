# Mediktor

Mediktor is an AI-powered symptom checker and triage tool designed for healthcare providers to assess patients' symptoms and recommend the appropriate level of care. It uses NLP and a medical knowledge base to analyze patient-reported symptoms and provide potential conditions, urgency levels, and next steps. The platform can integrate with telemedicine services, EHRs, and hospital systems to support clinical decision-making.
