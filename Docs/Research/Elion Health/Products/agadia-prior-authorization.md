# Agadia Prior Authorization

Agadia’s Prior Authorization solution (PAHub) is a cloud-based platform that enables health plans, PBMs, and managed care organizations to automate and manage electronic prior authorization workflows. It provides configurable decision support, electronic submission and review of requests, integration with ePA networks such as Surescripts, and compliance with CMS and state mandates.
