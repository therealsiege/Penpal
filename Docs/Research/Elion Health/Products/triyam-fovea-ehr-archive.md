# Triyam Fovea EHR Archive

Fovea EHR Archive is a secure, multi-tenant SaaS application to archive data from legacy systems, including EHR, EMR, ERP, financial, and billing systems. It stores historical patient data in a vendor-neutral format, allowing healthcare providers to search, view, download, or transmit information, thereby ensuring continuity of care and simplifying the release of information processes.
