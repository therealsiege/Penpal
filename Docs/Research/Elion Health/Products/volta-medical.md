# Volta Medical

Volta Medical’s AF-Xplorer is an AI-assisted mapping and decision-support system used during electrophysiology (EP) ablation procedures for atrial fibrillation (AF) and atrial tachycardia (AT). It detects dispersed electrograms (EGMs) and annotates regions of interest in near real-time, integrating with existing EP recording systems, 3D mapping/navigation systems, and mapping catheters.
