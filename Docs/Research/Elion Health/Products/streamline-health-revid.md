# Streamline Health RevID

Streamline RevID is a cloud-based charge reconciliation tool designed to identify discrepancies between clinical activity and charge data before claims are submitted for billing. It automates daily charge reconciliation by analyzing clinical documentation against billing data to capture missed or late charges and reduce rebilling.
