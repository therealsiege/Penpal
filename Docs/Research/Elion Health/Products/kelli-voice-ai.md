# Kelli Voice AI

Kelli Voice AI is an AI-powered patient engagement platform that automates phone and text outreach for healthcare organizations to improve care coordination and visit completion. It conducts natural-language patient conversations for appointment confirmations, care gap closure, and pre-visit assessments, generating structured, EHR-ready reports for clinical teams.
