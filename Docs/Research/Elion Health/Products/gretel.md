# Gretel

Gretel is a synthetic data platform—now part of NVIDIA—that enables users to generate artificial datasets that preserve the statistical properties of real data without including any actual sensitive information. The platform supports training generative models, validating output with privacy and quality metrics, and producing scalable synthetic data on demand.
