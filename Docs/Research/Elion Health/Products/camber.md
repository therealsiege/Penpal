# Camber

Camber is an automated revenue-cycle management platform used in recurring care workflows such as pre-bill and claims submission to validate, submit, and track insurance claims. It validates claims with pre-submission error detection and payer-specific rules, runs daily batch claim submissions, and provides session-level financial reporting along with live payment tracking and forecasting.
