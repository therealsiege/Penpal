# DocGo

DocGo is a healthcare services company that provides mobile medical care, medical transportation, and remote patient monitoring. Their mobile health services deliver urgent and preventive care directly to patients' homes, addressing conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, and chronic obstructive pulmonary disease.
