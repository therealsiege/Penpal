# Salvo Health

Salvo Health offers a virtual care platform that supports medical practices in delivering continuous care for patients managing obesity, GLP-1 therapy, and chronic gastrointestinal conditions such as IBS. It provides asynchronous in-app messaging, monthly virtual visits, and daily tracking tools for symptoms and quality of life.
