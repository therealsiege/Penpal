# Carematix

Carematix offers RPM solutions that help improve patient outcomes and reduce healthcare costs. Their system utilizes advanced telehealth devices such as blood pressure monitors, glucometers, and weight scales that integrate with electronic medical records (EMRs) to provide real-time health data.
