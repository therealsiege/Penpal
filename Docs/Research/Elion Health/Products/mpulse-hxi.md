# Health Experience & Insights Platform (HXI)

Health Experience & Insights (HXI) is a cloud-based platform used in front-end patient and member engagement and navigation workflows for payers and health systems. It ingests and normalizes claims, clinical, pharmacy, lab, and digital behavior data to produce unified member 360 profiles and risk scores.
