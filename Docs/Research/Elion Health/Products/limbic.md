# Limbic

Limbic provides clinical AI solutions for mental healthcare, aiming to enhance efficiency and patient outcomes. Their offerings include Limbic Access, which supports clinical assessments and decision-making, and Limbic Care, an AI companion for ongoing patient support between sessions.
