# Fuse Insight

Fuse is an automation platform for healthcare providers that focuses on patient intake, insurance verification, and price transparency. It integrates with existing EHR systems to automate the collection of insurance and payment details, complete HIPAA-compliant forms, and perform real-time insurance verification at the CPT code level.
