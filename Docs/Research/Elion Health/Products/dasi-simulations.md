# PrecisionTAVI

DASI Simulations provides patient-specific computational modeling for preprocedural planning of structural heart procedures by converting routine CT imaging into 4D predictive simulations. It generates predictive outputs that estimate complication risks such as coronary obstruction and leaflet thrombosis, simulates device–anatomy interactions, and produces comparative visualizations to evaluate device options.
