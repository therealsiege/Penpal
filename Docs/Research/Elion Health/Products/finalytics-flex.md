# Finalytics Flex

Finalytics Flex is a healthcare fintech solution that accelerates insurance claim payment—delivering payments in just three days to providers—by monetizing claims through its automated remittance management platform. It integrates smoothly with existing practice systems and provides a centralized dashboard for real-time monitoring of payments and claim statuses, reducing administrative burden and enhancing visibility.
