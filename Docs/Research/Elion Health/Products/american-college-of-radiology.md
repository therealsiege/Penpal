# ACR Appropriateness Criteria

ACR Appropriateness Criteria is an evidence-based clinical guideline product that supports appropriate imaging and interventional radiology decisions. It provides condition-specific recommendations and ratings for diagnostic imaging and treatments across thousands of clinical scenarios, helping clinicians select the most appropriate study based on patient presentation.
