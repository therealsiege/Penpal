# Manifest MedEx

Manifest MedEx is California’s designated nonprofit health information exchange (HIE) that provides providers with real-time access to longitudinal patient data across medical, behavioral health, and social services. Its services include ADT event notifications, consolidated health records, and analytics tools that help clinicians identify high-risk patients, close care gaps, support risk-based contracts, and coordinate follow-up after hospital or ED encounters.
