# Bot MD Hospital

Bot MD Hospital is a healthcare application designed to enhance hospital productivity by integrating various hospital information systems into a single, accessible platform. It allows doctors and nurses to quickly search for call rosters, hospital guidelines, drug and lab information, and HR policies from their phones.
