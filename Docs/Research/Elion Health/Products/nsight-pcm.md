# Nsight Principal Care Management

Nsight Principal Care Management is a remote care service that operates alongside chronic care models to manage a single complex, high‑risk chronic condition over time. Core capabilities include telehealth delivery, remote specialist case management, and development of individualized care plans with ongoing condition stabilization.
