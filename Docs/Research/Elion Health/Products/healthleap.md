# Healthleap

HealthLeap is an AI-driven platform focused on improving patient care by continuously screening for malnutrition using real-time data from patient charts, including lab results and clinical notes. By identifying malnutrition earlier, HealthLeap helps clinicians intervene sooner, reducing hospital length of stay and readmission rates, while also improving billing accuracy for hospitals.
