# Lyrebird Health

Lyrebird Health is a medical scribe used during clinician–patient consultations to generate documentation for entry into the electronic medical record. It listens to in-person, phone, and telehealth conversations and generates clinical notes, letters, and other documentation for electronic medical record transfer.
