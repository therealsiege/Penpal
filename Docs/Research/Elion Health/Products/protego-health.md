# Protego Health

Protego Health is a platform that automates the process of preventing and, when necessary, appealing denied medical claims for healthcare providers. By integrating payer policies and medical records, it focuses on high-value claims, utilizing AI to identify appropriate codes, rectify denials, and generate appeal letters.
