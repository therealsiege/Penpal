# iCAD ProFound Breast Health

iCAD's ProFound Breast Health Suite is an AI-powered platform that enhances breast cancer detection, density assessment, and personalized risk evaluation. It integrates with digital breast tomosynthesis (3D mammography) and 2D mammography systems to provide radiologists with precise analysis, aiding in early cancer detection and improving diagnostic accuracy.
