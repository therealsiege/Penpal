# PayerPrice

PayerPrice is a healthcare data analytics platform that provides comprehensive insights into negotiated rates between payers and providers across the United States. By aggregating and analyzing data from all 50 states, specialties, and practice sizes, PayerPrice enables healthcare organizations to benchmark commercial rates, optimize managed care contracting, and enhance revenue cycle integrity.
