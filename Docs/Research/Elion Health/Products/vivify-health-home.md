# Vivify Health +Home

Vivify Health's +Home is a turnkey remote patient monitoring solution, designed for patients to use at home with a pre-configured tablet and medical devices such as blood pressure monitors, pulse oximeters, and thermometers. The system allows healthcare providers to track biometric data, conduct virtual visits, and provide condition-specific care pathways for chronic conditions like hypertension, CHF, and COPD. It integrates cellular connectivity and offers technical support, along with data encryption for secure transmission.
