# Smartlink  TCM

Smartlink TCM is a transitional care management platform that automates the full TCM workflow to help healthcare providers efficiently manage post-discharge care and meet Medicare requirements. It offers real-time patient tracking, integrated billing support, automatic ADT (Admission, Discharge, Transfer) notifications from HIEs, and full EHR integration with over 90 systems.
