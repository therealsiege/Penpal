# Judi Health

Judi Health provides a unified claims processing platform that supports both medical and pharmacy claims on a single system. It is designed for use by payers, third-party administrators (TPAs), employers, and providers to manage core administrative functions such as eligibility, enrollment, prior authorizations, payment adjudication, accumulators, and appeals.
