# DART Health

[Contact](https://cal.com/darthealth/45min?overlayCalendar=true)

DART Health is a healthcare-specific AI deployment platform that integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to identify clinical gaps, risks, and revenue opportunities using a combination of structured and unstructured data. It supports over 100 clinical AI use cases, including malnutrition detection, readmission risk prediction, and documentation improvement for coding accuracy and compliance.
