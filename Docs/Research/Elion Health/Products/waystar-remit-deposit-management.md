# Waystar Remit + Deposit Management

Waystar’s Remit + Deposit Management is a payment reconciliation solution designed to automate the matching of remittance advices (ERAs) with electronic funds transfers (EFTs) and checks. It consolidates data from multiple banking institutions, identifies missing or inaccurate remits and deposits, and auto-posts payments once confirmed.
