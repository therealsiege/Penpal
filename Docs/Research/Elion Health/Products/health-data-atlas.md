# Health Data Atlas

Health Data Atlas provides a comprehensive healthcare market intelligence solution that simplifies healthcare data and links claims to healthcare organizations (HCOs), facilities, and parent Integrated Delivery Networks (IDNs). Health Data Atlas integrates data from various sources, including NPPES, CMS, state licenses, IRS filings, and claims datasets to build accurate organizational hierarchies and provider profiles.
