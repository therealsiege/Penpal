# EvidenceCare CareInsights

[Contact](https://evidence.care/schedule-a-demo)

EvidenceCare CareInsights is a mobile platform that delivers peer-comparison feedback to physicians to promote self-driven improvements in clinical care. Instead of relying on traditional dashboards or top-down mandates, CareInsights sends physicians personalized, process-focused metrics via text messages, helping reduce unwarranted clinical variation.
