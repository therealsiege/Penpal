# Sift Patient Payments

Sift Healthcare's Patient Payments Management system uses AI to optimize the revenue cycle by predicting patient payment behaviors. It offers advanced analytics to determine who will pay, when, and the best methods and frequency for contact. This includes structuring payment plans and identifying charity qualifications.
