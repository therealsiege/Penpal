# Premier

Premier Inc. is a healthcare improvement company that provides data analytics, supply chain services, consulting, and technology solutions to hospitals and healthcare providers. Its platform supports clinical decision-making, cost management, and operational performance through tools that analyze clinical, financial, and operational data.
