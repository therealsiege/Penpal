# Flatiron OncoEMR

Flatiron OncoEMR is a cloud-based EHR designed for oncology care, with features like customizable templates, integrated clinical content, and support for molecular profiling and clinical trials. Additionally, OncoEMR leverages machine learning to optimize cancer treatment plans and match patients with appropriate clinical trials.
