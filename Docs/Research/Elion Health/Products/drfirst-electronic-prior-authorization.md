# DrFirst Electronic Prior Authorization

DrFirst Electronic Prior Authorization (ePA) is a tool that automates the prior authorization process for prescriptions (especially specialty medications). It integrates with prescriber workflows and EHRs to check benefits in real-time, determine whether a PA is needed, submit PA requests, track their status, and reduce manual steps.
