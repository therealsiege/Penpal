# Passage Health

Passage Health is an all-in-one clinical and practice management platform designed for autism care and applied behavior analysis (ABA) practices. It streamlines operations with features such as efficient scheduling workflows, claim submission and billing, HIPAA-compliant document management, customizable session notes, real-time data collection via mobile and web apps, and advanced reporting with automated graphing and insights.
