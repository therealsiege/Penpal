# CytoVale Intellisep

The IntelliSep sepsis test by CytoVale is an FDA-cleared diagnostic tool designed for rapid sepsis detection in emergency department patients exhibiting signs of infection. Utilizing a small blood sample, the test assesses the biophysical properties of white blood cells to evaluate the body's immune response to infection. It provides results within approximately 8 minutes, categorizing patients into three risk bands that indicate their probability of developing sepsis within the next three days.
