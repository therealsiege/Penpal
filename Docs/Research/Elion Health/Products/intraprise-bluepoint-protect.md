# Intraprise BluePoint Protect

BluePrint Protect is an Integrated Risk Management platform developed by Intraprise Health to assist healthcare organizations in identifying, assessing, and prioritizing both internal and vendor-related cybersecurity risks. The platform consolidates risk data from various sources into a unified risk register, enhancing visibility and enabling efficient remediation planning. It automates processes such as stakeholder interviews and third-party risk assessments, aligning with industry frameworks like NIST and HITRUST to support compliance efforts.
