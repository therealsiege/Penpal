# Ufonia

Ufonia provides an automated clinical assistant to handle routine healthcare conversations such as triaging referrals, pre-operative assessments, post-operative follow-ups, and long-term outcome monitoring. It conducts patient interactions via phone without requiring internet access, supporting unlimited patient capacity and ensuring a consistent, high-quality experience.
