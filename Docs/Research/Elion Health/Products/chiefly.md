# Chiefly

Chiefly is a scheduling automation platform aimed at medical residency / graduate medical education (GME) programs. It automates resident scheduling (e.g. block scheduling, shift assignments), tracks compliance in real time (e.g. accreditation rules, duty hours), and provides visibility and collaboration tools for chiefs, coordinators, and program directors. It replaces manual spreadsheet workflows, reduces errors and administrative overhead, and accelerates the scheduling process.
