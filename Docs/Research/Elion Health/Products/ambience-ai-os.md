# Ambience Scribe

Ambience Healthcare offers a suite of AI-driven tools designed to enhance clinical documentation and workflow. AutoScribe serves as an AI medical scribe that generates comprehensive documentation during patient visits across various specialties. Other tools include AutoCDI for real-time coding and compliance, AutoAVS for sending patients personalized after-visit summaries to reduce in-basket messages, and AutoRefer for managing specialist referrals.
