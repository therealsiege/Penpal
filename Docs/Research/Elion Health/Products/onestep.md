# OneStep

OneStep is a digital physical therapy platform that leverages smartphone-based motion analysis to provide remote patient monitoring and treatment. It offers tools for clinicians to assess gait, fall risk, and mobility without the need for additional hardware, relying solely on the smartphone’s sensors.
