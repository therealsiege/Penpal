# MammoScreen

Therapixel develops AI-powered software for radiology, with a focus on assisting breast cancer screening through mammography. Its primary product, MammoScreen, analyzes 2D and 3D mammograms to highlight suspicious regions and provide a confidence score that supports radiologists in decision-making.
