# Clinia

Clinia is an AI-powered search and data infrastructure platform designed for healthcare applications, enabling accurate and contextually relevant search experiences within electronic medical records (EMRs), patient portals, and clinical workflows. It offers semantic search, generative Q&A capabilities, and a health data repository to help providers, patients, and organizations quickly retrieve critical health information.
