# FIFTH Eye

FIFTH Eye's AHI System is an FDA-cleared medical device software that monitors and predicts hemodynamic instability using Lead II ECG signals. It offers a 48-minute lead time ahead of vital signs, helping clinicians detect hemodynamic changes early.
