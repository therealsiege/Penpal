# Ada

Ada is a health application that assists users in evaluating their symptoms and obtaining reliable medical guidance within minutes. Developed by a team of doctors and scientists, Ada utilizes artificial intelligence and clinical evidence to provide personalized health assessments. This information can also be made available to clinicians through the EHR, improving the efficiency of patient interviews.
