# GenHealth.ai  LMM

GenHealth.ai’s Large Medical Model (LMM) is designed to predict future healthcare events by analyzing sequences of medical events such as diagnoses, treatments, and medications. Instead of using traditional language models, it operates on clinical data like ICD, CPT, RxNorm, and LOINC codes, which represent patient conditions, procedures, and medications.
