# Availity Fusion

Availity Fusion is a clinical data quality platform designed for payers to evaluate, standardize, and enrich clinical data for strategic use. It offers a Data Dashboard with over 400 rules to assess data usability and identify documentation issues, inconsistencies, and errors.
