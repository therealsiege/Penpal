# TherigySTM

TherigySTM

is a clinical software platform designed to support specialty pharmacies, PBMs, infusion centers, oncology clinics, and hospital systems in managing complex pharmacy operations and improving patient outcomes. It facilitates therapy management through features like disease-specific care plans, secure mobile messaging, referral and financial assistance tracking, and configurable workflows.
