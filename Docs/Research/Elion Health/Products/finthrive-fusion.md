# FinThrive Fusion

FinThrive Fusion is a data intelligence platform designed to unify and optimize healthcare revenue cycle management (RCM). It consolidates data from electronic health records, billing systems, and payer portals into a centralized data lake, enabling enterprise-wide analytics and automation.
