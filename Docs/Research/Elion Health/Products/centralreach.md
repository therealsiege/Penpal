# CentralReach

CentralReach is a cloud-based practice management and clinical data collection platform designed for applied behavior analysis (ABA) therapy and related healthcare services. It includes tools for electronic medical records (EMR), billing, scheduling, and data-driven treatment planning to support therapy providers working with individuals with autism and other developmental disabilities.
