# Patients.App

[Contact](https://calendar.google.com/calendar/u/0/appointments/schedules/AcZssZ0OqobnK8oA07AId5oD-BaHEH9htSYpxtXexWq6Zoya7tDSq8LSiBvZ7RBA221N_AlwzYe8r9Wv)

Patients.App is an AI-powered clinical summarization and documentation platform that compiles and organizes medical records from various sources (e.g. PDFs, faxes, EMRs) into clean, structured formats tailored to clinician needs. It allows providers to filter by diagnostic focus—such as transplant criteria or perioperative checklists—and receive autogenerated drafts of clinical notes or reports.
