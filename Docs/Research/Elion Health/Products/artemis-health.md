# Artemis Health

Artemis Health is a cloud-based healthcare analytics and benefits optimization platform that helps self-insured employers, benefits advisors, and health plans analyze and act on complex benefits data to improve outcomes and control costs. The platform connects and normalizes multiple data sources, offers customizable analytics, pre-built and custom reporting, interactive dashboards, trend and cost-saving insights, population health metrics, and benchmarking to support decision-making on plan design, utilization, gaps in care, and program effectiveness.
