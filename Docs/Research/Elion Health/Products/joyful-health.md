# Joyful Health

Joyful Health is a financial operating system tailored for healthcare practices, integrating data from various sources to identify missed revenue, automate collection processes, and streamline financial operations. It assists billing and collections teams in pinpointing and recovering lost revenue, offering insights into areas where funds are uncollected and providing actionable steps for recovery.
