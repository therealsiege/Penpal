# EHNOTE

EHNOTE is an integrated Electronic Health Record (EHR) system specifically designed for ophthalmology practices. It consolidates EHR, practice management, ambulatory surgery center (ASC) charting, patient engagement, revenue cycle management, optical sales, and inventory control into a single platform.
