# NicoBoard

NicoBoard is a patient engagement platform designed to help families and caregivers better understand and manage complex medical information, particularly in neonatal and pediatric care. It provides personalized data visualizations, educational resources, and real-time health updates to improve communication between healthcare providers and patients’ families.
