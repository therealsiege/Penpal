# Edwin Health

Contact

Edwin Health offers a patient engagement platform that transforms care plans into personalized, actionable “missions” for patients, tracks completion and evidence of outcomes, and enriches provider systems with behavioral, clinical, and outcomes data. It integrates with care teams’ workflows by interpreting clinical notes or care instructions and turning them into step-by-step patient tasks, verifying completion, and adjusting over time.
