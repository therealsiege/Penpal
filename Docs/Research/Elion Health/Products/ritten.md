# Ritten

Ritten is an electronic medical record (EMR) and operations platform built specifically for behavioral-health organizations across detox, residential treatment, PHP/IOP, outpatient, and coaching programs. It supports scheduling for individual and group sessions, AI-assisted clinical documentation, treatment planning, e-prescribing, integrated labs, and workflow compliance tracking.
