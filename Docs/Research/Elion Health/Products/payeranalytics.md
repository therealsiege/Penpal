# PayerAnalytics

Payer Analytics is a health data analytics platform focused on CMS Price Transparency compliance and payer contract evaluation. It allows hospitals and health systems to upload and analyze machine-readable files required by CMS regulations, visualize negotiated payer rates, and compare pricing across regions, services, and organizations.
