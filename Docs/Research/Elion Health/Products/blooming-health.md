# Blooming Health

Blooming One is an AI-assisted engagement platform developed by Blooming Health to help organizations, such as aging service providers and community-based organizations, connect effectively with their communities. The platform supports communication across multiple channels—including text, voice calls, and emails—in over 80 languages, ensuring accessibility for diverse populations.
