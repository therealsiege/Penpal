# Harrison.rad.1

Harrison.rad.1 is a radiology multimodal vision‑language foundation model used in imaging research and product development workflows; it is not cleared for clinical use and is intended for research access. It detects and localizes radiological findings, generates draft radiology reports, and conducts open‑ended image question‑answering with longitudinal reasoning using clinical history and patient context.
