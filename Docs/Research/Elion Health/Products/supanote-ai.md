# Supanote AI

Supanote is an AI-powered clinical documentation tool built for mental health professionals that automates the creation of therapy session notes. It allows users to upload audio recordings, dictate or record directly during sessions, and then generates structured clinical notes in formats such as SOAP, DAP, or treatment plans.
