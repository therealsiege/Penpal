# MedBridge Care

Medbridge Care is a digital musculoskeletal (MSK) and movement-based care platform designed to support hybrid and virtual care delivery. Built for physical therapy, occupational therapy, and related disciplines, it includes tools for home exercise programs, virtual triage with motion capture, remote therapeutic monitoring (RTM), and patient-reported outcomes.
