# HealPros

HealPros provides in-home and mobile preventive care services targeted at individuals with chronic health conditions, working primarily with Medicare Advantage and other health plans. Their services include diabetic retinopathy screenings, osteoporosis and bone density assessments, diabetic foot exams, and basic lab tests—all delivered outside of traditional care settings to improve patient engagement and close care gaps.
