# RADNAV

[Contact](https://imagineteam.com/landing/radnav/)

RADNAV is a radiology workflow optimization tool designed to assist radiologists with structured reporting and clinical decision support. It integrates directly with existing radiology systems (e.g., PACS and dictation tools) to provide real-time prompts, checklists, and best practice guidance during the interpretation process.
