# Medisafe Digital Drug Companion

Medisafe’s Digital Drug Companion is a mobile-based platform designed to support medication adherence for patients using prescription therapies. It provides personalized reminders, education, and guidance based on the user’s medication regimen, with features that can include refill alerts, drug interaction warnings, and condition-specific information.
