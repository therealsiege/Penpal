# Wisedocs

Wisedocs is a medical record and claims document processing platform used in claims and medical-record-review workflows to convert unstructured files into structured, defensible outputs. It generates customizable medical summaries and chronologies, performs automated deduplication and handwriting detection, and provides interfaces for case creation, document upload, and real-time insight retrieval.
