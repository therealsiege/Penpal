# Oasis Primary Care VBC Enablement

Oasis Health Partners is a provider-enablement platform designed for independent primary care practices that serve senior populations in rural communities with value-based models. It provides infrastructure, data insights, and care team support to help providers manage chronic conditions, enhance patient engagement, and reduce avoidable hospitalizations.
