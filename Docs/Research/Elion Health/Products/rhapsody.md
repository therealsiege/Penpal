# Rhapsody

[Contact](https://rhapsody.health/get-a-demo/)

Rhapsody is a healthcare interoperability and data-exchange platform that connects healthcare with trusted data so organizations can exchange, enrich, and act on information inside and outside of their enterprises. Rhapsody supports every major format, including FHIR, JSON, custom APIs and HL7,  provides identity data resolution via a purpose-built Enterprise Master Patient Index; and delivers semantic terminology management. Healthcare IT teams deploy Rhapsody their way: customer-hosted, SaaS, or iPaaS.
