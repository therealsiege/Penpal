# ImagineCo-Pilot

[Contact](https://imagineteam.com/imagineone/imagineco-pilot/)

ImagineCo-Pilot is an AI-driven revenue cycle management solution integrated within ImagineSoftware’s ImagineOne® platform. It automates high-volume billing workflows such as denial resolution, eligibility verification, ERA posting, claim submission, and patient financial communications, achieving over 90% automation in tasks like denials and claims correction.
