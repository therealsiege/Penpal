# Qventus Perioperative Care Coordination Solution

Contact

Qventus Perioperative Care Coordination is an AI-powered solution designed to improve surgical scheduling, pre-admission testing (PAT), and post-surgical follow-up. It automates administrative tasks such as risk flagging, patient outreach via voice, text, and email, medical record processing, and EHR documentation. The system integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to identify patient-specific risk factors, coordinate additional care, and reduce last-minute surgery cancellations.
