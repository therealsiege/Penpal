# Cured

Cured is a healthcare digital marketing and CRM platform that operates in patient engagement and outreach workflows for health systems, payers, and digital health companies. It provides prebuilt campaign templates called Curations, automates multi-step patient and member journeys, and uses analytics and machine learning, including propensity scoring, to segment and target audiences.
