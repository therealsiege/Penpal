# InpharmD

InpharmD is a literature-search and drug-information platform used in medication-review and evidence-review workflows to provide clinicians rapid, curated answers to clinical questions. It performs customized literature searches, synthesizes and summarizes referenced evidence, and provides concise, referenced responses to support medication decisions.
