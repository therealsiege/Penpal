# CareNostics

Carenostics offers AI-powered clinical decision support tools designed to enhance the management of chronic diseases like diabetes, hypertension, and chronic kidney disease. Their solutions integrate with EHRs to analyze patient data, providing early identification and actionable insights to healthcare providers.
