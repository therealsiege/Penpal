# Commure Engage

Commure Engage is a clinical care coordination platform that automates patient engagement through over 500 evidence-based digital care pathways. It enables healthcare organizations to activate, engage, and monitor patients across various service lines by providing automated appointment reminders, educational content, and real-time messaging.
