# Clarity Group Healthcare SafetyZone

Healthcare SafetyZone is a risk management and incident reporting platform for healthcare organizations. It allows providers to capture, track, analyze, and learn from safety events (patient incidents, environmental or employee safety, HIPAA breaches, equipment issues, regulatory/state reporting, etc.) using customizable templates and real-time data dashboards. It supports workflow automation (alerts, reviewer assignments, traceable follow-ups), data surveillance to spot risk trends, and cross-department or system-wide access via web or mobile.
