# Healthedge Prospective Payment Integrity

HealthEdge Prospective Payment Integrity is a payment accuracy solution designed to help healthcare payers identify and prevent improper claims payments before they are processed. The platform uses real-time analytics, configurable business rules, and automation to detect billing errors, fraud, and non-compliant claims.
