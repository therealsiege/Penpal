# Wysa Copilot

Wysa Copilot is a hybrid AI–human mental health therapy platform used in clinical workflows for between-session support and patient monitoring, including collaborative care, outpatient therapy, and NHS Talking Therapies. It provides AI-guided interactive tools, an evidence-based care library clinicians assign to patients, regular automated assessments tracking progress and generating engagement insights to adjust care, and secure real-time/asynchronous messaging with AI-driven crisis detection and escalation, logging high-risk incidents for clinician follow-up.
