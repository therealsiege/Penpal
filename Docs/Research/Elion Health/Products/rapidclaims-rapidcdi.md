# RapidClaims RapidCDI

Contact

RapidCDI by RapidClaims is an AI-powered clinical documentation improvement (CDI) and risk adjustment platform designed to support value-based care. It captures and codes patient complexity at the point of care using natural language processing and machine learning, improving Hierarchical Condition Category (HCC) coding accuracy and Risk Adjustment Factor (RAF) scores.
