# Nym

[Contact](https://nym.health/contact-us/)

Nym is an AI medical coding product that deciphers clinical language in patient charts and assigns ICD-10 and CPT codes to them using advanced machine learning and semantic technologies. They offer services for outpatient coding for both professional and facility charges, as well as inpatient coding for professional charges.
