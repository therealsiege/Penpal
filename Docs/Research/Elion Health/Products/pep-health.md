# PEP Health

PEP Health is a technology platform that uses AI to analyze unsolicited online patient feedback and convert it into metrics such as the PEP Score (patient experience) and Trust Score (confidence and loyalty). These daily, real-time measures provide an early indicator of quality outcomes like HCAHPS, with research showing up to 99% accuracy.
