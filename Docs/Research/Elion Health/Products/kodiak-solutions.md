# Kodiak Solutions

Kodiak Solutions is a cloud-based revenue cycle and healthcare finance analytics platform used by hospitals and health systems to analyze net revenue, reimbursement risk, and payer performance. Its core Revenue Cycle Analytics (RCA) software aggregates large volumes of claims, payment, and financial data to support net revenue reporting, benchmarking, denials intelligence, charge capture analysis, and cash reconciliation.
