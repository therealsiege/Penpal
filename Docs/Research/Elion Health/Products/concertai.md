# ConcertAI

ConcertAI offers a suite of real-world data products designed to support various research and clinical needs. Their RWD360 platform provides a comprehensive repository of pan-tumor clinical data, enriched by validated AI models and linked with claims data to offer detailed insights into patient journeys. This platform facilitates analyses such as comparative effectiveness studies, treatment pattern evaluations, and adverse event assessments.
