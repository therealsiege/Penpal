# Sematic Health

Contact

Sematic Health provides an automation platform for Advanced Primary Care Management (APCM) integrated with EHR systems such as athenaOne. The system automates key APCM workflows—including patient enrollment, care plan creation, care gap detection, and billing—while maintaining compliance with CMS requirements.
