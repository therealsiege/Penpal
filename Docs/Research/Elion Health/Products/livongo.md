# Livongo

Livongo, now part of Teladoc Health, offers digital health solutions for managing chronic conditions such as diabetes, hypertension, weight management, and behavioral health. The platform provides connected devices like blood glucose meters and blood pressure monitors that automatically upload readings to a secure account, delivering real-time insights and personalized feedback.
