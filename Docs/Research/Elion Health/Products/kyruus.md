# Kyruus

Kyruus is an enterprise care-access platform used in patient access workflows to manage provider data, power provider search and matching, and enable online scheduling and booking. It consolidates and normalizes provider information into a single source of truth, surfaces clinicians by specialty, availability, insurance, and patient preferences, and books appointments into scheduling systems.
