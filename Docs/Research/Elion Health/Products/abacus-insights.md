# Abacus Insights

Abacus Insights provides data management and integration solutions for healthcare payers, enabling them to access and utilize comprehensive, accurate, and timely healthcare data. Their platform consolidates various data sources into a unified view, ensuring interoperability and usability across multiple applications.
