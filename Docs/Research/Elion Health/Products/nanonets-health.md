# Nanonets Health

Nanonets Health is an AI-powered automation platform designed to optimize healthcare administrative processes, focusing on patient access and operational efficiency. It enables organizations to extract data from various document sources, integrate with platforms like Gmail and Salesforce, and automate claims processing to reduce errors. The platform includes AI agents for patient intake, eligibility verification, prior authorization automation, and scheduling, offering customizable solutions for patient access workflows.
