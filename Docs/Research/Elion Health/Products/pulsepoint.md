# PulsePoint

PulsePoint is a healthcare marketing data activation platform used in campaign planning and omnichannel activation for audience targeting, patient intake, and media execution. It provides deterministic NPI-level HCP identity matching (HCP DirectMatch), supports omnichannel activation across display, video, CTV, audio, native, social, and search, and offers contextual consumer targeting based on health pages and keywords.
