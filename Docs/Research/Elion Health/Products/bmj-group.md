# BMJ Best Practice

BMJ Best Practice is an evidence-based clinical decision support platform that provides healthcare professionals with structured guidance for diagnosis, treatment, and prevention of medical conditions at the point of care. The system compiles and continuously updates clinical recommendations using peer-reviewed research, clinical guidelines, and expert review.
