# Sonio

Sonio is a cloud-based prenatal ultrasound reporting platform used during ultrasound exams and reporting workflows in OB-GYN and maternal-fetal medicine clinics. It provides real-time AI visual checklists that detect target views and verify image quality, auto-populates report fields such as fetal anatomy and placental location, and manages images and video by auto-organizing studies and extracting best frames.
