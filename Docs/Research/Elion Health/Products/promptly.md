# Promptly

Promptly is a patient engagement and intake platform designed for specialty medical practices to streamline administrative workflows, communications, and payment collections. It offers digital scheduling, automated appointment reminders, mobile-friendly intake forms, real-time insurance eligibility verification, text-to-pay billing, and post-visit communication tools.
