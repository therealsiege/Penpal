# Parakeet Health

[Contact](https://parakeethealth.com/contact)

Parakeet Health is a patient engagement platform used in healthcare contact center workflows for scheduling, triage, and referral management. It provides always-on voice agents and two-way SMS for inbound and outbound communications. It records and transcribes conversations, supports multilingual interactions, and stores conversations in a client portal for monitoring.
