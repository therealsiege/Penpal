# Subflow Health

Contact

Subflow Health is a healthcare workflow automation platform designed to optimize administrative and operational processes for healthcare providers. It offers tools for automating tasks such as patient intake, billing, appointment scheduling, and document management. The platform integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) and other healthcare systems to improve efficiency and reduce manual workload.
