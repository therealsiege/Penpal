# VectorCare SoFaaS

VectorCare SoFaaS™ (SMART on FHIR as a Service) is a managed EHR-integration infrastructure platform that lets healthcare technology vendors, suppliers, and service providers deploy SMART on FHIR-compliant applications inside major electronic health records without building their own integration stack.
