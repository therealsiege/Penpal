# Commure Agents

[Contact](https://www.commure.com/contact?utm_source=elion&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=elion&utm_content=agents)

Commure Agents is an AI-powered assistant platform designed to support healthcare operations by automating routine workflows across clinical, operational, and administrative domains. The agents are modular, purpose-built tools that can answer staff questions, complete tasks like prior auth or discharge planning, collect patient payments, summarize patient histories, and flag clinical risks in real time.
