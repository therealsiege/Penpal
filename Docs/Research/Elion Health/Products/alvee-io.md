# Alvee.io

Alvee helps identifying and measuring health disparities, predicting and tracking social needs, and providing real-time insights to promote health equity. It is an AI-driven platform that integrates with existing CRM and EHR systems, pulling relevant data and helping surface important SDoH information to clinicians at the point of care, as well as offering tools for demographic data collection, health equity dashboards, and compliance with regulatory requirements.
