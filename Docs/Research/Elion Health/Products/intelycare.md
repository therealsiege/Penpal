# IntelyCare

IntelyCare is a healthcare staffing platform that connects nursing professionals with per diem shifts, contract assignments, and permanent positions across various care settings. Through its mobile app, nurses and nursing assistants can browse and accept shifts that align with their schedules and preferences.
