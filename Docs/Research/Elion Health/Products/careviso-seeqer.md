# Careviso SeeQer

CareViso's seeQer platform provides tools for verifying patient insurance coverage, confirming benefits, and estimating out-of-pocket costs before treatment. It helps providers maintain price transparency compliance by informing patients about their financial responsibilities, including deductible, copay, and coverage details, before care begins.
