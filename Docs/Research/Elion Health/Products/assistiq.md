# AssistIQ

AssistIQ provides an AI-powered platform for capturing and analyzing supply and implant usage in operating rooms and procedural areas. Using computer vision, AIQ Capture automatically records item use without barcodes, RFID, or manual input, significantly improving charge capture and reducing documentation time. The platform includes AIQ Supply Cloud and AIQ Insights, offering inventory visibility and cost analytics, and integrates with EHR and ERP systems.
