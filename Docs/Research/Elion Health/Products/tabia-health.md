# Tabia Health

Tabia Health is a care coordination solution that integrates with EHR systems to capture and analyze patient data, helping organizations reduce no-shows, predict patient behavior, and enhance financial performance. It offers automated task management and insights to improve back-office efficiency while supporting better coordination between healthcare providers and payors.
