# Ours Privacy

Ours Privacy provides a privacy-first performance marketing solution for healthcare organizations, ensuring compliance with HIPAA and other regulations. The platform replaces non-compliant ad trackers with HIPAA-compliant alternatives, stripping PHI before sending data to platforms like Meta and Google
