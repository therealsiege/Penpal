# LastMinute

LastMinute is a healthcare workforce management platform that uses AI to optimize scheduling and staffing. It automates shift planning by considering staff availability, skills, and demand, helping healthcare providers manage last-minute changes like call-outs and shift swaps. The system includes intelligent forecasting tools to predict staffing needs, reducing risks of under- or over-staffing.
