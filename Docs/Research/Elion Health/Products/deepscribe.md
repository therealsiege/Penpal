# DeepScribe

DeepScribe is an AI-powered medical scribe platform that automates clinical documentation by converting patient-clinician conversations into structured chart notes. Utilizing advanced speech recognition and natural language processing, it captures and transcribes medical dialogues in real-time, integrating seamlessly with Electronic Health Record (EHR) systems.
