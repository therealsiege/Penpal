# Innovaccer Pharmacy Quality

Innovaccer Pharmacy Quality is a data-driven platform that supports performance improvement and quality measurement for pharmacies, with a focus on Medicare Star Ratings and value-based care metrics. The platform enables pharmacies to track adherence to medication therapies, identify at-risk patients, and intervene with targeted outreach programs. It integrates with pharmacy dispensing systems and payer data sources to provide performance dashboards and actionable insights.
