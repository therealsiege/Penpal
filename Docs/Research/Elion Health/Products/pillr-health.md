# Pillr Health

Pillr Health provides technology and services to optimize pharmacy operations, with a focus on supporting 340B programs for covered entities and pharmacies. Its platform includes solutions for split billing, contract and entity-owned pharmacy administration, audit support, compliance, and specialty pharmacy development. Additional offerings include analytics, program management, and consulting services to improve operational efficiency and regulatory adherence.
