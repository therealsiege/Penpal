# Apollo Managed Care Guidelines

Apollo Managed Care Guidelines is an evidence-based clinical guidance product that provides medical review criteria and care management guidelines used to support utilization management and medical necessity determinations. It includes more than 2,000 condition- and service-specific guidelines covering inpatient care, ambulatory services, behavioral health, therapy and rehabilitation, post-acute care, disease state management, and case management.
