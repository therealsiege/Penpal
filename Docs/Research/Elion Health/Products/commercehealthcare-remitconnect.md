# CommerceHealthcare RemitConnect

RemitConnect a receivables management platform that automates the reconciliation and posting of healthcare payments from all sources, including EFTs, ERAs, checks, and paper EOBs. It uses an exception-based workflow to streamline claim-to-payment processes, reduce manual posting, and improve accuracy across revenue systems
