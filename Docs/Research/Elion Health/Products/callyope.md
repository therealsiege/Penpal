# Callyope

Callyope is an AI-driven mental health monitoring platform that analyzes speech patterns to assess psychiatric conditions such as depression, bipolar disorder, and cognitive deficits. Patients complete a one-minute voice test via smartphone, either at home or in a clinical setting, and the system evaluates speech features like vocabulary, speech rate, and prosody to predict clinical scores.
