# Long Tail Health Solutions

Long Tail Health Solutions offers AI-powered tools and services to help healthcare providers manage clinical justification requirements imposed by payers, which often lead to claim denials and revenue loss. Their platform, built on a generative AI engine, analyzes clinical guidelines, payer contracts, and historical denial patterns to define necessary documentation and reduce preventable denials.
