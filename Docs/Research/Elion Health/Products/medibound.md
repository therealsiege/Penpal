# medibound

Medibound is a platform for clinics and care providers that automates patient follow-ups, remote care monitoring, and virtual check-ins by integrating with EHRs and medical devices/wearables. It uses “agents” (AI / rule-based workflows) to trigger care next steps from patient vitals or other signals, personalize care plans, manage patient data in one place, and reduce manual work on post-operative or chronic care follow-ups.
