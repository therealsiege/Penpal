# Solventum 360 Encompass

The 3M 360 Encompass System integrates computer-assisted coding (CAC) and CDI into a single platform. It uses NLP and AI to automatically suggest medical codes based on patient documentation, improving the accuracy and efficiency of the coding process. The system prioritizes high-risk cases for review, tracks quality metrics like Hospital Acquired Conditions (HACs), and supports real-time clinical intelligence reporting.
