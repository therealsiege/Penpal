# Droidal Insurance Verification AI Agent

[Contact](https://droidal.com/free-ai-agent-trial/)

Droidal's Insurance Verification AI Agent automates patient insurance eligibility checks by connecting directly to payer portals and EHR systems. It retrieves and verifies coverage details such as co-pays, deductibles, and policy status in real time, flags discrepancies, and updates patient records automatically. The system supports all major payers, provides audit trails, and uses pattern recognition to detect fraud and prevent claim denials.
