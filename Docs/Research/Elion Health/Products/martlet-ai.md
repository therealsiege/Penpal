# Martlet.ai

Martlet.ai is an AI-powered platform focused on real-time HCC coding and risk adjustment for providers, payers, and auditors. It integrates directly with EHRs to offer smart, in-the-moment prompts during patient encounters, using large language models to extract structured HCC codes from unstructured clinical notes.
