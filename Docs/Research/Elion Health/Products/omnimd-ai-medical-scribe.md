# OmniMD AI Medical Scribe

OmniMD AI Medical Scribe is an ambient AI documentation tool designed to convert patient-provider conversations into structured medical charts in real time. It includes advanced medical speech recognition, speaker diarization, and multilingual support, and can operate as a standalone product or fully integrated with OmniMD’s EHR system.
