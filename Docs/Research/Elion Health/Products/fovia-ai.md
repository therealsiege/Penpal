# Fovia AI

Fovia AI provides an advanced imaging-visualization layer that integrates AI results from multiple vendors directly into radiology workflows, PACS systems, and reporting tools. Its aiCockpit viewer consolidates algorithm outputs in a single interface where radiologists can review, navigate, annotate, validate, accept/reject, and modify AI findings in both 2D and 3D. The F.A.S.T. AI SDK allows PACS and imaging-software developers to embed AI visualization and workflow components without building their own rendering or integration stack.
