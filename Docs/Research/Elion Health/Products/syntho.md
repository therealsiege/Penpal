# Syntho

Syntho offers a synthetic data generation platform that is used in healthcare to create privacy-preserving, statistically accurate data for analytics, testing, AI model training, and research. It supports various healthcare data types, including EHRs, clinical trials, claims, and registries, and is designed to work with time-series and longitudinal data common in medical contexts.
