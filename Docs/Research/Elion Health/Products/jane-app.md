# Jane App

Jane App is a comprehensive practice management software designed for health and wellness practitioners, integrating features such as online booking, charting, scheduling, billing, and telehealth services into a single platform. It supports secure video appointments compliant with HIPAA, PIPEDA, and GDPR standards, ensuring the protection of patient information.
