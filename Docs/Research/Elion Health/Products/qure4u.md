# Qure4u

Qure4u is a digital health platform used in outpatient and population health workflows for patient intake, virtual care, and remote patient monitoring. It provides remote monitoring device connectivity with one-click patient enrollment, collects vitals from Bluetooth and home devices, and routes data to clinician dashboards with analytics and alerts for out-of-range values.
