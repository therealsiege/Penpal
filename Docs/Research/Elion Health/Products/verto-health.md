# Verto Health

Verto Health provides a “digital twin” data platform (via Verto Connect) that aggregates and normalizes health data from across many sources — legacy EMRs, modern cloud EMRs, wearables, lab systems — whether structured or unstructured. It does not force organizations to build or maintain a new data repository; instead it continually updates the normalized patient-record view so clinical workflows, population health analytics, and care interventions can be powered in near real-time.
