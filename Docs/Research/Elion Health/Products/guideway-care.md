# Guideway Care Evidence-Based Patient Activation

Guideway Care Evidence-Based Patient Activation is a service and technology program designed to improve patient adherence and care coordination through structured outreach and behavioral engagement strategies. The solution combines patient activation technology with trained Patient Activation Agents and Guides who conduct outreach, address non-clinical barriers, coordinate follow-up care, and support appointment adherence.
