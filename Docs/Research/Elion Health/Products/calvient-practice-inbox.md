# Calvient Practice Inbox

Calvient Practice Inbox is a document intake and routing solution used at the front of clinical workflows for patient intake and pre-bill document triage. It consolidates faxes, emails, referrals, lab results, and scanned mail into a single inbox, applies AI Assist to classify documents and extract structured data, and automatically routes tasks to appropriate staff.
