# Brado

Brado’s Consumer Experience Practice (CEP) for Healthcare offers data-driven marketing and customer engagement solutions for healthcare organizations. It focuses on helping providers, payers, and life sciences companies enhance patient experiences through customer insights, brand strategy, and omnichannel marketing.
