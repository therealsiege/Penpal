# InteleShare

Intelerad InteleShare is a cloud-based medical image exchange platform that enables healthcare organizations to securely share, access, and view imaging studies without physical media. It integrates with EHRs and RIS systems to allow clinicians and patients to retrieve imaging directly within existing workflows, including patient portals, and supports DICOM and HL7 data routing.
