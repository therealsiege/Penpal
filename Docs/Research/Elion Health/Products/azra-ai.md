# Azra AI

Azra AI develops AI-powered solutions aimed at optimizing clinical workflows in healthcare, particularly in oncology and radiology. Their technology is used to automate the identification and prioritization of cancer patients from pathology reports, manage incidental findings in radiology, and streamline tumor registry processes.
