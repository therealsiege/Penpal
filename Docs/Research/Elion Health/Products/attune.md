# Attune

Attune is an AI-powered conversational voice platform designed specifically for healthcare organizations to automate non-clinical patient and member communications. It uses deterministic, rules-based voice agents to handle inbound and outbound calls for scheduling, reminders, referrals, transitions of care, benefits education, and other administrative workflows that require accuracy and auditability.
