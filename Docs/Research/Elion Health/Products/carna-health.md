# Carna Health

Carna Health is a digital health platform for early identification, monitoring, and care management of chronic kidney disease that fits into screening and ongoing disease-management workflows. It provides capture of point-of-care test results such as eGFR and creatinine. It delivers predictive analytics and clinical decision support to stratify patient risk, recommend screening cadence, and generate personalized care pathways.
