# Neuroglee

Neuroglee is a virtual-first neurology care provider that combines clinical services with digital tools to support patients with Alzheimer’s disease, mild cognitive impairment, and other neurological conditions. Care is delivered by board-certified neurologists through telemedicine visits, supported by care navigators and digital cognitive therapy programs. The model emphasizes ongoing monitoring, cognitive assessments, and personalized treatment plans, with the option for in-person care when needed.
