# Maverick Medical AI

Maverick Medical AI offers autonomous medical coding through its mCoder platform, focusing on high accuracy and a fast Direct-to-Bill process. The platform supports ICD and CPT coding with deep learning algorithms and real-time error correction. It integrates with existing systems to automate a large portion of coding workflows, aiming to minimize reliance on human coders while maintaining compliance with payer-specific policies.
