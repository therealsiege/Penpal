# NCCN Guidelines

NCCN Guidelines are evidence-based clinical practice guidelines that provide standardized recommendations for cancer prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and supportive care across nearly all cancer types. They present consensus-driven algorithms, supporting evidence, and references to guide clinical decision-making throughout the oncology care continuum.
