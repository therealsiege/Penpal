# Sword Intelligence

[Contact](https://intelligence.sword.com/book-a-meeting)

Sword Intelligence is a modular AI-driven healthcare operations platform where you can spin up agnets to automate and manage non-clinical workflows. The product uses AI Care Managers that engage with patients across voice, text, chat, and email, and integrate with existing systems. It automates high-volume operational work such as patient enrollment, eligibility and benefits checks, triage and intake, scheduling and follow-up, high-risk outreach, document processing, and back-office data entry, with real-time dashboards, audit trails, and compliance controls for human oversight.
