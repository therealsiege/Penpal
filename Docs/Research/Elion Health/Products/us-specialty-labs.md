# US Specialty Labs

US Specialty Labs is a high-complexity clinical laboratory based in San Diego, California, offering a wide range of testing services. They specialize in processing both traditional venipuncture samples and non-traditional micro-samples such as capillary finger-stick, saliva, and menstrual blood.
