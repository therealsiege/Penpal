# TeleVox

TeleVox is an omnichannel patient relationship management platform used in patient communication and scheduling workflows across hospitals, health systems, physician practices, and community health centers. It provides automated appointment reminders, two-way messaging across SMS, phone, email, and voice that supports pre-visit instructions and post-visit care programs, and a conversational AI virtual assistant that enables self-service scheduling and prescription refill requests.
