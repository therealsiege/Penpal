# Vibrant Practice

[Contact](https://b2w0hdg5ybd.typeform.com/vibrant?typeform-source=www.vibrantpractice.com&_gl=1*1c1lkhr*_gcl_au*MjE4OTMxODkzLjE3NTg2OTMyNTc.)

Vibrant Practice is a digital health platform designed for clinicians in functional, integrative, and precision medicine. It provides an AI-powered EHR with telehealth, labs, e-prescribing, intake, and scheduling tools, along with an AI Clinical Co-Pilot that assists with chart summarization, note generation, and documentation.
