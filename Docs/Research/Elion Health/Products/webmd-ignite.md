# WebMD Ignite

WebMD Ignite is WebMD's integrated growth and engagement platform that supports patient intake, point-of-care education, marketing, and referral growth workflows for health systems, payers, and clinical leaders. It provides a large online clinical education library, delivers branded video content for facility displays, and offers programmatic audience targeting with analytics to run and measure marketing and referral campaigns.
