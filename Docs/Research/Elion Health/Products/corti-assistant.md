# Corti Assistant

Corti Assistant is an AI-powered medical scribe that automatically documents patient visits in real time by capturing conversations and generating structured clinical notes. It supports SOAP note formats and can integrate with EHR systems, allowing clinicians to focus on patient care rather than manual documentation. The assistant provides multi-language support, specialty-specific templates, and secure, encrypted data handling.
