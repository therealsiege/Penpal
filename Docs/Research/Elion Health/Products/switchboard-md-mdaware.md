# MDAware: Inbox Management

MDAware is an AI-powered communication tool designed to enhance efficiency in healthcare settings by automating the triage and routing of patient messages. The platform categorizes incoming communications and directs them to the appropriate healthcare providers, reducing unnecessary manual handling and improving response times.
