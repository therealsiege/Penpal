# ALIGNMT AI

ALIGNMT AI is a compliance and risk management platform designed for healthcare organizations deploying AI systems. It automates compliance with ONC, ISO, and NIST standards, supports continuous monitoring of AI models in real-world use, and provides tools for governance, auditing, and transparency reporting.
