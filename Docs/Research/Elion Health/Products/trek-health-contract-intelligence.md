# Trek Health Contract Intelligence

Trek Health’s Contract Intelligence is an AI-powered contract management platform designed for healthcare providers to centralize payer agreements, streamline negotiation workflows, and surface reimbursement opportunities. It enables providers to store and organize contracts securely, automate policy monitoring, and connect agreements with analytics for better financial performance.
