# Reimagine Care

Reimagine Care offers a virtual and at-home care platform for cancer treatment, aimed at supporting health systems and oncology providers. The platform provides 24/7 access to symptom management via chat, voice, and video, and offers in-home supportive care, including administration of subcutaneous, intravenous, and oral medications.
