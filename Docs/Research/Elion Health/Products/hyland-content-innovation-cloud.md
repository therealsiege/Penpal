# Hyland Content Innovation Cloud

Hyland Content Innovation Cloud is a cloud-based enterprise content platform that unifies documents, data, and workflows across organizational systems. The platform federates content from multiple repositories, structures unstructured data, and applies AI-driven tools for content intelligence, document processing, process automation, and knowledge discovery.
