# Nsight Chronic Care Management

Nsight Chronic Care Management is a remote care solution used in monthly chronic care management workflows for patients with two or more chronic conditions. It provides 24/7 clinical monitoring by a team of RNs and MAs with English and Spanish support, structured initial risk assessments, and monthly RN phone visits.
