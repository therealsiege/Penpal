# Birth Model OB Platform

Birth Model is a technology-enabled maternity care platform designed to help obstetric providers manage risk, improve outcomes, and succeed in value-based payment models. The platform aggregates EHR data and other clinical inputs to identify risk factors, recommend interventions, and support clinical decision-making throughout the prenatal and postpartum care journey.
