# Steer Health

Steer Health is a healthcare automation platform that fits in the digital front door and access-to-care workflow, supporting patient acquisition, scheduling, intake, and pre- and post-visit automation. It provides real-time scheduling across care venues, automated voice and messaging agents (Luna) for call deflection, and an intelligent scribe (SteerNotes) that generates clinical documentation.
