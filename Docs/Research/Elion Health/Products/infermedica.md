# Infermedica

Infermedica is an AI-based clinical inference platform used for patient self-assessment and virtual triage in digital and telehealth care pathways. It constructs dynamic medical interviews by asking adaptive questions, analyzes symptoms and risk factors, and returns triage recommendations such as self-care, teleconsultation, physician visit, or emergency.
