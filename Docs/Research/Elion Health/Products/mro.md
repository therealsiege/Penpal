# MRO

MRO is a clinical data exchange and release-of-information platform used in healthcare workflows for ROI, HEDIS, risk adjustment, and continuity of care. It provides automated retrieval of medical records, curates and normalizes clinical data from disparate electronic health records, and digitizes and tags payer data requests.
