# OutcomesAI

OutcomesAI combines AI voice agents with licensed nurses to provide scalable, safe, and human-centered care services for health systems, virtual care providers, and pharmaceutical / specialty therapy support. It automates tasks such as nurse triage, scheduling, referrals, post-acute and transition-care coordination, while allowing human clinicians to handle higher-complexity work.
