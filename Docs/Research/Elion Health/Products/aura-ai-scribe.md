# Aura AI Scribe

[Contact](https://www.insighthealth.ai/contact)

Aura AI Scribe by Insight Health is a voice-based clinical documentation tool that uses AI to generate notes from provider-patient conversations. It supports multiple specialties and is designed to work within the clinical workflow. The platform uses automatic speech recognition with speaker diarization, and includes specialty-specific adaptations such as recognition of medical terminology, drugs, and clinical guidelines.
