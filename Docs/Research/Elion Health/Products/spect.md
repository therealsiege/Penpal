# Spect

Spect is a healthcare technology company specializing in portable retinal imaging solutions aimed at early detection of eye diseases such as diabetic retinopathy, glaucoma, and age-related macular degeneration. Their platform includes a handheld retinal camera that enables medical staff to perform retinal exams in various settings, including clinics and patients' homes.
