# OpenDoctor

OpenDoctor is a patient self-scheduling and access platform used in appointment scheduling and intake workflows for radiology and primary care. It provides advanced scheduling logic for complex exams, offers online smart registration forms and patient reminders, and performs real-time insurance verification.
