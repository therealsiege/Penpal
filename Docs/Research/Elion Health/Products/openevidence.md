# OpenEvidence

OpenEvidence is a medical information and search platform used at the point of care for clinical decision support and administrative workflows. It provides evidence-based answers, drafts prior-authorization letters and patient handouts, and transcribes encounters while surfacing guideline-based evidence within clinical documentation.
