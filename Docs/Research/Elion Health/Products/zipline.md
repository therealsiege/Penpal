# Zipline

Zipline uses autonomous drones to deliver medical supplies, such as blood, vaccines, and medications, to remote and hard-to-reach areas. These drones ensure timely deliveries, often within 30 minutes, overcoming challenges like poor infrastructure and geographical barriers.
