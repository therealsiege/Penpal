# Celery

[Contact](https://www.celeryway.com/?utm_source=Webaite&utm_medium=marketplace&utm_campaign=elion)

Celery is a financial oversight platform that uses AI to detect and prevent costly errors in payroll and revenue operations for healthcare organizations. Its two primary tools—Payroll Guard and Profit Shield—automatically review uploaded data to catch payroll irregularities like excessive overtime or noncompliance with policy, and identify unprofitable clients or transactions.
