# RelyMD

RelyMD's EMS Collaboration platform provides emergency medical services (EMS) teams with 24/7 access to emergency-trained providers via a fully integrated telemedicine system. This service enables EMS personnel to consult with physicians in real-time, facilitating in-place treatment plans for patients who may not require hospital transport, reducing unnecessary emergency room visits and allowing EMS resources to remain available for more critical cases.
