# Jiq AI

IQ AI provides AI-driven voice agents specifically designed for healthcare settings. These virtual assistants handle tasks such as scheduling and confirming appointments, answering frequently asked questions, sending reminders for upcoming visits, and routing calls without requiring additional staff.
