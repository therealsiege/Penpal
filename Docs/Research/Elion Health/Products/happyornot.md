# HappyOrNot

HappyOrNot is a technology platform that offers intuitive, smiley-face terminals (Smiley Terminal™, Smiley Touch™, and Smiley Digital™) that collect real-time patient satisfaction feedback across digital and physical touchpoints. The system ensures anonymous data collection, applies encryption and anonymization, and complies with GDPR for data privacy and security.
