# Janus Health Notice of Admissions

[Contact](https://info.janus-ai.com/janus-health-elion)

Janus Health's Notice of Admission automation is a tool designed to streamline the admission notification process for hospitals and health systems. It integrates directly with electronic health records (EHRs) to automatically submit admission notices to payers, validate data for completeness, and manage payer responses.
