# Delphyr

Delphyr is a Europe-based AI clinical assistant that integrates within the EHR to automate administrative and documentation work for patient intake, note generation, and case preparation. It compiles structured patient summaries, retrieves lab and imaging results, and surfaces clinical data with source links, providing confidence scores, source locations, and continuous monitoring.
