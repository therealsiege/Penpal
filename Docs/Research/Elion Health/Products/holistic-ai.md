# Holistic AI

Holistic AI is an enterprise-grade AI governance platform that provides full-lifecycle oversight of all types of AI systems — from internal models to vendor solutions and embedded tools. It enables organizations to discover and catalogue “shadow” and formal AI assets, assess risk dimensions (such as bias, robustness, privacy, efficacy, transparency), apply policy-based controls, monitor ongoing deployment and performance, and generate audit-ready reports aligned to frameworks like the NIST AI Risk Management Framework, the EU AI Act, and ISO/IEC 42001.
