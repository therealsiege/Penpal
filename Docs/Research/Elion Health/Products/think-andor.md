# ThinkAndor

ThinkAndor is a configurable virtual health platform that integrates with EHRs to provide real-time actionable intelligence to clinicians, enabling faster decision-making and improved patient outcomes. The platform supports virtual visits, virtual sitting and nursing, remote patient monitoring, virtual team collaboration, and post-acute communications and notifications.
