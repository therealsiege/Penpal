# InteliChart

InteliChart is a patient engagement platform used across pre-visit to post-visit workflows in ambulatory and post-acute settings. It provides a web and mobile patient portal giving patients access to medical records, lab results, intake forms, scheduling, secure messaging, bill pay, medication refills, online self-scheduling, and waitlists.
