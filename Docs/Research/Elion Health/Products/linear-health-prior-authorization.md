# Linear Health Prior Authorization

Linear Health Prior Authorization Management is an AI-powered product that automates the end-to-end prior authorization process for healthcare providers. It submits prior authorizations directly into payer portals, adapts submissions to payer-specific requirements, tracks approval status in real time, and flags denials or missing information before submission.
