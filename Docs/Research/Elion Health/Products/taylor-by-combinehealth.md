# Taylor by CombineHealth

[Contact](https://www.combinehealth.ai/demo)

Taylor is an AI-powered revenue cycle optimization platform that provides real-time dashboards, predictive insights, and natural language querying to help healthcare organizations manage financial performance. It tracks key areas such as claims, charge capture, accounts receivable, payer trends, and staff productivity while forecasting revenue cycle risks before they affect cash flow.
