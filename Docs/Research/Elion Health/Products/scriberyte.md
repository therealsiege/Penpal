# ScribeRyte AI

ScribeRyte (by ScribeEMR) is a medical scribing product used at the point of encounter to capture and document clinical notes during and after visits. It captures provider–patient conversations with ambient speech recognition, applies clinical natural language processing to generate SOAP-format notes, and provides two models—ScribeRyte AI Instant (real-time generated notes) and ScribeRyte AI Plus (generated notes reviewed and refined by remote scribes).
