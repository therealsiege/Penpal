# Radial Care

Radial Care is a cloud-based AI/ML decision-support platform used in value-based care workflows, particularly during transitions of care and post-acute placement. It analyzes clinical, claims, and social-risk data and provides real-time, individualized recommendations for discharge disposition, readmission risk, and prioritized interventions beginning on the first day of a hospitalization.
