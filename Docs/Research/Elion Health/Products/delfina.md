# Delfina

Delfina offers a digital pregnancy care platform designed to provide personalized, data-driven support throughout pregnancy. The platform integrates with healthcare providers to automate routine tasks and enhance care plans, offering continuous support through features like telehealth, at-home monitoring, and doula services.
