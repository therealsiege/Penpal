# Fabric

Fabric Health is a care enablement platform that includes the Digital Front Door, automating care navigation through an AI-enabled symptom checker, as well as patient intake, triage, and routing processes. The platform supports hybrid care, combining virtual and in-person visits, and automates clinical documentation to enhance clinical capacity.
