# Pieces

Pieces Technologies provides AI-driven tools that enhance clinical workflows by automating documentation and generating actionable insights within the EHR. Its solutions support multidisciplinary care teams by creating summaries, daily progress notes, and discharge reports, helping reduce clinician workload and errors.
