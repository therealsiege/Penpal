# Substrate

Substrate is an AI-driven revenue cycle automation platform that performs manual billing and payer tasks directly within EMRs, practice management systems, and payer portals. Its AI agents check claim status, verify eligibility, convert EOBs to electronic remittance files, and automatically submit prior authorizations and appeals with supporting medical records.
