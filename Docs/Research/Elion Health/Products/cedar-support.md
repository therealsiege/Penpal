# Cedar Support

[Contact](https://www.cedar.com/contact-us/?utm_source=elion&utm_medium=webpage&utm_campaign=vendorprofile&utm_content=cedarsupport)

Cedar Support is a patient billing support platform that combines AI technology with live agent services to manage healthcare financial interactions. The system provides inbound and outbound call center support, early-out collections, and predictive outreach capabilities, helping health systems improve response efficiency, reduce operational costs, and enhance patient billing experiences through integrated service workflows.
