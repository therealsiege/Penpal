# Medically Home

Medically Home offers a decentralized healthcare model that provides high-acuity medical care to patients in their homes. It includes a 24/7 Medical Command Center, personalized care protocols, and a network of specialists via telemedicine. The platform enables care orchestration, real-time monitoring, and integrated acute and post-acute care.
