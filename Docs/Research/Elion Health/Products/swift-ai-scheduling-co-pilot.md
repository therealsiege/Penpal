# Swift AI Scheduling Co-Pilot

Swift is a scheduling assistant tool designed to help nurses and nurse managers reduce the burden of shift scheduling. It automates tasks like swap and coverage requests, validates staffing rules and qualifications, communicates changes among staff and managers via SMS or chat, and updates the enterprise scheduling system automatically.
