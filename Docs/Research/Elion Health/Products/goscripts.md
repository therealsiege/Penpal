# GoScripts

GoScripts is a web-based documentation and prescription platform used by HME/DME suppliers and medical providers to generate, validate, and track DME/HME prescription documentation. It supports electronic completion of orders, renewals, recertifications, and insurance verification using rules tied to HCPCS codes to reduce incomplete or inaccurate documents.
