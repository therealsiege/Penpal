# Symplr Safety

Symplr Safety is a quality and safety management platform used by healthcare organizations to monitor, manage, and improve patient and staff safety outcomes. It centralizes incident reporting, risk assessments, and safety event tracking across clinical and operational settings. The system supports real-time data capture and workflow automation for safety investigations, root cause analysis, and regulatory reporting.
