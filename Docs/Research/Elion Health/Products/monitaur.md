# Monitaur

Monitaur is an AI governance and risk management platform designed to help regulated industries—particularly insurance and financial services—adopt and monitor AI responsibly. It provides tools for defining enterprise-wide governance frameworks, managing compliance, and automating model validation and reporting across the full AI lifecycle. The platform enables organizations to establish policies, track model performance, demonstrate fairness and accuracy, and maintain audit-ready documentation for regulators.
