# Linear Health Outbound Referral Coordination

Linear Health Outbound Referral Coordination is an AI-powered product that automates the outbound referral workflow for primary care and other referring providers. It detects referral orders from the EHR, submits prior authorizations to payer portals, matches patients to in-network specialists, and conducts multi-channel patient outreach until appointments are scheduled.
