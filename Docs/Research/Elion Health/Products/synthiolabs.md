# SynthioLabs

SynthioLabs Patient Engagement is an AI-powered patient support platform designed to extend care beyond prescriptions through empathetic, compliant, and scalable engagement. The solution features a Voice AI Nurse companion, validated through over 1,000 nurse-led evaluations, that educates patients, triages symptoms, and guides them along personalized care journeys
