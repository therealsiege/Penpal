# WELL AI Inbox Admin

WELL AI Inbox Admin is a tool designed to automate management of clinic inboxes, particularly for handling e-faxes and documents. It uses AI to categorize and sort incoming documents by understanding their content. The tool integrates with EHRs to centralize document management, automate tasks like patient chart updates and referral creation, and ensure that urgent faxes are prioritized.
