# Signal1

Signal 1 offers a healthcare-specific AI governance and deployment platform designed for hospitals and health systems. It enables organizations to inventory, assess, approve, deploy, monitor and manage AI models—both internally developed and vendor provided—through a single system. Features include rapid model validation for accuracy, risk and compliance; centralized workflows for approvals; real-time monitoring of models for performance drift or risk; dashboarding for ROI and impact; and integration into clinical workflows (e.g., discharge planning, deterioration prediction).
