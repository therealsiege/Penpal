# Global Healthcare Exchange

Global Healthcare Exchange (GHX) is a healthcare supply chain technology and services company that provides cloud-based solutions to hospitals, suppliers, and distributors. The GHX Platform connects over 1.3 million trading partners and supports automation for order processing, invoicing, inventory management, and vendor credentialing.
