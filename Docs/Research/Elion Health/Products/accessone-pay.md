# AccessOne Pay

AccessOne Pay is a patient payment and financing platform for healthcare providers and health systems. It enables patients to pay medical bills instantly via mobile, without requiring logins, apps, or paper statements, and supports flexible financing options, including 0% interest repayment plans. The system integrates with major HIS/EHR revenue cycle platforms (like Epic) to automate payment workflows, reduce staff workload, and improve patient collections.
