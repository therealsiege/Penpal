# Osigu

Osigu offers a cloud-based platform that provides healthcare organizations with end-to-end revenue cycle management (RCM) solutions. Its features include real-time patient eligibility verification, automated billing, prior authorization management, and claims submission, all designed to reduce administrative burdens and improve financial stability.
