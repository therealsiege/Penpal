# ThoroughCare

ThoroughCare provides a digital platform for healthcare providers to enhance care delivery through comprehensive care coordination. The platform supports various programs, including Chronic Care Management, Remote Patient Monitoring, Behavioral Health Integration, Transitional Care Management, Annual Wellness Visits, and Advance Care Planning.
