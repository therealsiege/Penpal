# Avanade SAIGE

SAIGE is a healthcare-specific AI governance platform that provides a centralized registry for AI models, automates risk monitoring, and facilitates vendor collaboration in one interface. It lets healthcare organizations register, track and manage all deployed AI systems (internal and vendor-supplied) from one “system of record”, automate governance workflows (submission, approval, monitoring), and generate audit-ready metrics.
