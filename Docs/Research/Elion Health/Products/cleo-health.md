# Cleo Health

Cleo Health is a documentation and real-time feedback platform used in acute care workflows from triage through discharge. It captures ambient audio, transcribes encounters, generates specialty-specific, time-stamped clinical notes and supplemental documents (handoffs, progress notes, discharge summaries), and updates medical decision-making entries as new context arrives.
