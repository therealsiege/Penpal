# Prosperity EHR

Prosperity EHR is an electronic health record platform designed specifically for behavioral health and human services providers. It offers tools for clinical documentation, treatment planning, scheduling, billing, and outcomes tracking, with a focus on supporting organizations that provide services like therapy, substance use treatment, and community-based care.
