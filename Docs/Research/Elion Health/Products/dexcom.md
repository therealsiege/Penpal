# Dexcom

Dexcom specializes in continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems, providing real-time glucose level tracking for people with diabetes. Their CGM devices use small sensors worn on the body to measure glucose levels continuously, helping users manage their condition by providing alerts for high and low glucose levels.
