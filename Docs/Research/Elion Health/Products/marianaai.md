# MarianaAI

MarianaAI's CARE platform automates clinical workflows with AI tools like the AI Medical Scribe, which transcribes multi-lingual medical encounters in real-time and generates clinical notes and codes. The platform also creates detailed Patient Portraits, summarizes patient histories to improve care and optimize HCC/RAF scores, and automates medical coding. Additionally, it auto-drafts responses and triaging messages for patient communication.
