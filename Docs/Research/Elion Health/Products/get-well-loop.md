# Get Well Loop

GetWell Loop is a digital, mobile-enabled care plan and transitions of care tool (episode care) that allows patients to engage with their care teams through automated guidance, monitoring, education, and check-ins across their surgical or disease episodes. It enables patients to follow a personalized care plan, submit responses, and helps care teams track adherence and intervene when needed — all without the patient needing to call in.
