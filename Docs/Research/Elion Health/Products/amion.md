# Amion

Amion is a healthcare scheduling platform designed for hospitals and healthcare systems to manage and share on-call and shift schedules for physicians and other medical staff. The platform allows users to create and distribute schedules through a web interface or mobile app, providing access to current schedules and facilitating communication via integrated messaging features.
