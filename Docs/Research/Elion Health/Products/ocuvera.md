# Ocuvera

Ocuvera provides AI-powered monitoring systems to help hospitals reduce patient falls and optimize nursing workflows. Using a 3D camera and advanced computer vision algorithms, the system can predict when a patient is likely to exit their bed or chair without assistance, sending alerts to nursing staff via mobile devices.
