# Hinge Health

Hinge Health is a digital clinic specializing in musculoskeletal (MSK) care, offering personalized programs to address chronic back and joint pain. Their services include virtual physical therapy, behavioral health support, and educational resources, all accessible through a user-friendly app. Patients receive tailored exercise plans demonstrated by physical therapists, with real-time feedback provided via advanced wearable technology.
