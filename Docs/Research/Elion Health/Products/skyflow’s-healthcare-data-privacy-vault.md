# Skyflow's Healthcare Data Privacy Vault

Skyflow's Healthcare Data Privacy Vault is a platform designed to isolate, protect, and govern sensitive health data. It uses polymorphic encryption and tokenization to de-identify protected health information (PHI), ensuring compliance with HIPAA and other data privacy regulations.
