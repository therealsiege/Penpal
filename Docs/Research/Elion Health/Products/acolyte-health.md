# Acolyte Health

Acolyte Health is a healthcare technology company that specializes in creating interactive video content to enhance patient engagement and health literacy. Their platform leverages AI to generate personalized videos featuring digital twins of healthcare professionals, providing a more relatable and effective means of communication.
