# Qualifacts

Qualifacts provides behavioral health-focused EHR solutions designed to streamline clinical workflows, billing, care management, and virtual care services. It offers features like customizable treatment plans, AI-assisted therapy notes, and patient portals to improve care coordination and client engagement. The platform supports diverse healthcare needs, including addiction treatment, mental health services, and CCBHCs.
