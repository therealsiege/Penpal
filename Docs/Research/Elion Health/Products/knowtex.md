# Knowtex

Knowtex is a clinical documentation platform used in pre-bill and documentation workflows that captures clinician–patient conversations and produces structured, billable medical notes and orders. It provides real-time transcription of natural dialogue, extracts billing codes such as ICD-10, CPT, E/M, and HCC, and captures orders and referrals while surfacing clinical intelligence like clinical-trial matches and guideline references.
