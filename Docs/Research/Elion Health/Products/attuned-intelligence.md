# Attuned Intelligence

Attuned Intelligence provides an AI voice agent system for healthcare call centers that answers patient calls 24/7, routes them, and automates tasks like appointment booking, medication refills, and telephone encounters. It can integrate with EHRs to automate call handling workflows, escalates to humans when needed, requires minimal IT lift to deploy (just forwarding a phone line), and supports multilingual conversations.
