# Nsight Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM)

Nsight Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) is a web-based remote care platform used after visits for ongoing monitoring in RPM, CCM, and RTM programs. It provisions cellular monitoring devices (blood pressure, glucose, pulse-ox, weight, spirometer, ECG-capable options) and provides device consumables and logistics.
