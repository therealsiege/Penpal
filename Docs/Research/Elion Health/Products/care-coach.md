# Care.coach

Care.coach offers a digital health platform that combines human interaction with AI through a 24/7 avatar interface, providing companionship and health coaching to users. This service supports individuals in managing chronic conditions, medication adherence, and reducing social isolation by engaging users in daily conversations and health-related activities.
