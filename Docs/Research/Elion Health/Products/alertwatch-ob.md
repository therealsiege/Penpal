# AlertWatch OB

AlertWatch:OB is an FDA 510(k)-cleared maternal surveillance system designed to enhance safety in labor and delivery units. AlertWatcht integrates data from electronic medical records to provide real-time Modified Early Warning Scores (MEWS) and alerts, assisting clinicians in monitoring maternal health from triage through postpartum, including operating rooms and post-anesthesia care units.
