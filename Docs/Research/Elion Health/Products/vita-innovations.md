# Vita Innovations

Vita Innovations' EmerGenAI supports emergency department patient intake and triage workflows. Core capabilities include collecting patient-reported data during wait times and extracting medication details from images; evaluating acuity, including Emergency Severity Index scoring, and generating patient summaries and protocol guidance; and matching patients to social services.
