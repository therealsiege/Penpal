# Optum LYNX Outpatient Charge Capture

Optum LYNX Outpatient Charge Capture is a software solution designed to help hospitals and clinics ensure accurate and compliant capture of charges for outpatient services. It uses proprietary algorithms plus regulatory guidelines to determine facility visit levels, resource utilization, and patient care complexity for various outpatient settings such as emergency departments, clinics, oncology, observation, and infusion services
