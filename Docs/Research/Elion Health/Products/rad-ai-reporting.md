# Rad AI Reporting

Rad AI Reporting is a radiology reporting platform used during the interpretation and reporting stage of imaging workflows to replace or augment existing reporting tools. It uses generative AI to provide structured report content and impressions from dictated findings, has customizable templates with consensus-guideline insertion, and provides real-time report updates.
