# CoreCare

CoreCare is an AI-powered workflow platform designed specifically for long-term care providers, including skilled nursing facilities (SNFs). It consolidates critical revenue cycle and compliance tasks—such as admissions, billing, Medicaid eligibility tracking, and clinical documentation—into a single collaborative interface.
