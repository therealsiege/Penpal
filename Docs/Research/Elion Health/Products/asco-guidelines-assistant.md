# ASCO Guidelines Assistant

ASCO Guidelines Assistant is a generative AI tool developed by the American Society of Clinical Oncology (ASCO) in partnership with Google Cloud. Designed for oncology professionals, it provides interactive, chat-based access to ASCO’s library of evidence-based clinical practice guidelines.
