# Synapse Medicine

Synapse Medicine is a medication-focused clinical decision support platform used in prescribing and medication-management workflows. It provides real-time drug search and prescribing suggestions, detects drug–drug interactions with severity visualization, and issues patient-specific alerts based on age, weight, pregnancy status, allergies, and diagnoses.
