# Janus Health Teleport

[Contact](https://info.janus-ai.com/janus-health-elion)

Janus Health Teleport is a browser extension designed to automate the navigation of payer portals for healthcare revenue cycle teams. By integrating with electronic health records (EHRs), it allows users to access specific claim information within payer portals directly from the EHR interface.
