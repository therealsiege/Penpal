# OpenLoop Licensing and Credentialing

OpenLoop Licensing and Credentialing provides services to expedite and manage the licensing and credentialing process for healthcare providers. They handle state board and DEA licenses, as well as hospital, healthcare facility, and payer plan credentialing, including Medicare and Medicaid.
