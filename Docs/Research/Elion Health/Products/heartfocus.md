# HeartFocus

HeartFocus is an AI-guided cardiac ultrasound application and software development kit used at the point of care in primary care, emergency and acute care, and outpatient echocardiography acquisition workflows. It provides real-time, on-image AI guidance to acquire the ten standard echocardiographic views, automatically records clips when diagnostic quality is reached, and saves captured clips for archiving.
