# MedeAnalytics Cost Insights

MedeAnalytics Cost Insights is an analytics platform that helps hospitals and health systems analyze and manage the true cost of care. It combines clinical, financial, and claims data to identify high-cost variations in procedures, supplies, and service lines, allowing organizations to compare performance against peers and benchmarks.
