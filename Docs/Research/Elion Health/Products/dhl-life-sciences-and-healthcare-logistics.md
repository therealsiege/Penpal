# DHL Life Sciences and Healthcare Logistics

DHL Life Sciences and Healthcare Logistics provides specialized supply chain services for pharmaceutical, biotech, medical device, and clinical research organizations. Its offerings include temperature-controlled transportation, clinical trial logistics, direct-to-patient delivery, inventory management, and regulatory-compliant warehousing.
