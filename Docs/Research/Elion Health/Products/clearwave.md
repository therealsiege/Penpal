# Clearwave

Clearwave is a patient engagement and registration platform used in ambulatory and specialty workflows for scheduling, pre-registration, check-in, and point-of-service collections. It provides self-service scheduling and registration across mobile, tablet, and kiosk; runs automated Multi-Factor Eligibility insurance verification at multiple patient touchpoints; and accepts payments via card swipe, tap-to-pay, and card-on-file while capturing consents and scanning IDs.
