# Paragon Denali

Paragon Denali is a cloud-native electronic health record (EHR) system from Altera Health, designed for rural, critical access, and community hospitals. It supports both acute and ambulatory care, featuring integrated clinical workflows and decision-support tools that enhance patient care and streamline data access at the point of care. The system is hosted on Microsoft Azure, enabling regular, low-downtime updates, and includes functionalities for emergency, radiology, operating room, pharmacy, and revenue cycle management.
