# Paymentus

Paymentus is a cloud-based billing and payment platform for healthcare organizations that enables electronic bill presentment and payments across multiple channels including web, mobile, text, IVR, kiosks, and digital wallets. It integrates with patient portals and major EHR systems to support real-time payment posting, flexible payment plans, and automated billing communications such as reminders and notifications.
