# Ellipsis Health

Ellipsis Health offers an AI-powered care management platform centered on its virtual agent, Sage. Sage is a multilingual, emotionally intelligent AI that conducts fully autonomous care management phone calls, designed to reduce administrative workload, improve patient engagement, and accelerate program enrollments.
