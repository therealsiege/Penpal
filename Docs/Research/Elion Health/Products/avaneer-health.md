# Avaneer Health

Avaneer Health operates the Avaneer Network, a secure, permissioned decentralized data platform that sits in healthcare administrative workflows such as eligibility verification, prior authorization, payer-to-payer exchange, and pre-adjudication claims processing. It provides private SparkZone environments for participants, Collaboration Services for person-centric identity and data authorization, and a Solution Exchange to discover and subscribe to network services; the platform records immutable audit trails and supports decentralized control of permissioned data.
