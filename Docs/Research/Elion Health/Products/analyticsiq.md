# AnalyticsIQ

AnalyticsIQ provides data and analytics services to healthcare organizations, focusing on social determinants of health (SDoH) data. Their platform offers insights into patient demographics, behaviors, and health factors, including physical and mental health metrics, lifestyle choices, and access to medical care.
