# SoundHound Amelia

SoundHound's Amelia is a voice AI solution integrated with Epic to provide personalized self-service for healthcare systems. The AI agent, named Emily, is used by MUSC Health to handle patient interactions, including appointment management and answering non-clinical queries, available 24/7.
