# ActiveCampaign

ActiveCampaign is an email, SMS, and CRM marketing automation platform used in pre- and post-encounter workflows for patient outreach, appointment reminders, and patient marketing. It provides an automation builder, CRM pipelines, and messaging across email, SMS, WhatsApp, and web to create and route leads, apply lead scoring, and send personalized or bulk messages.
