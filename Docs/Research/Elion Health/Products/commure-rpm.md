# Commure RPM

Commure RPM PillTrack is a remote patient monitoring (RPM) solution designed to track medication adherence and patient health data. It integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) and uses automation to monitor prescription fulfillment and patient compliance. The system helps healthcare providers identify potential adherence issues and intervene as needed.
