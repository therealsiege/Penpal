# Vizium360 Patient Surveying and Data Analytics

Vizium360 Patient Surveying is a platform designed to help healthcare practices collect, analyze, and act on patient feedback. It delivers automated outreach sending surveys after visits, dashboards with metrics like NPS and OSAT, benchmarking across specialties/locations, and online reputation management by publishing positive patient ratings
