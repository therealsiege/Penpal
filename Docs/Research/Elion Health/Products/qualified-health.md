# Qualified Health

Qualified Health provides a comprehensive infrastructure platform designed to facilitate the safe and effective adoption of generative AI in healthcare settings. Their technology offers enforceable governance through role-based access controls, risk alerts, data privacy protections, and safeguards against AI inaccuracies. Additionally, the platform enables rapid development and deployment of AI agents for workflow automation, enhancing operational efficiency.
