# NexusConnexions

NexusConnexions by Nexus Health Resources is transitional care management software aimed at hospitals and skilled nursing facilities to improve patient outcomes after discharge. It automatically generates patient-specific agendas for follow-up interactions based on diagnoses, chronic conditions, and payer information.
