# AccurKardia

AccurKardia offers AccurECG, a cloud-based, device-agnostic software platform for fully automated electrocardiogram (ECG) interpretation. The AccurECG system is FDA 510(k) cleared and can detect up to 13 arrhythmias, including atrial fibrillation and ventricular ectopic beats.
