# DecisionRx

DecisionRx is a precision medicine platform that provides pharmacogenomics (PGx) testing and clinical decision support to help healthcare providers optimize medication therapy based on a patient's genetic profile. The platform combines genetic test results with clinical data to deliver medication recommendations, aiming to reduce adverse drug reactions and improve treatment efficacy.
