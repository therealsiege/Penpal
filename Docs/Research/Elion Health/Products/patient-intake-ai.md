# Patient Intake AI

Patient Intake AI is a healthcare technology platform designed to automate and manage front office operations in medical practices using AI tools. The product suite includes AI-powered features for patient scheduling, insurance card capture through smartphone images, automated insurance verification across 20,000+ plans, and denial management aimed at preventing claims errors caused by incorrect patient information.
