# Notable HCC Chart Review

Notable HCC Chart Review uses AI to analyze patient histories – including clinical notes, claims, and other data sources – to identify new and recapture hierarchical condition category (HCC) coding opportunities and documentation discrepancies, helping improve the accuracy of risk adjustment workflows.
