# AI Medica AI Calc

AI Medica AI Calc is a clinical decision support and risk‑scoring platform used inside or alongside the electronic health record to automate evidence‑based calculations at the point of care. It computes a library of validated clinical scores such as CHA2DS2‑VASc, CURB‑65, MELD, APACHE II, and NEWS2, and maps coded data elements like LOINC, SNOMED‑CT, and ICD‑10 to populate inputs.
