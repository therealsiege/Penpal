# DocuSign

DocuSign is an electronic signature and agreement-management platform used in healthcare for patient intake, consent, authorization, staffing, and vendor agreement workflows. It provides device-agnostic eSignatures, records automated audit trails with timestamps, IP addresses, and signer identity, and protects data in transit and at rest with AES-256 encryption.
