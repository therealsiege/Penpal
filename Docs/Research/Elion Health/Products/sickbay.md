# Sickbay

Sickbay Core is a clinical virtualization platform that aggregates real-time patient monitoring data from bedside devices into a single, vendor-agnostic system. It captures and stores high-fidelity waveforms and vital signs, making them available for remote monitoring, secondary analytics, and AI applications.
