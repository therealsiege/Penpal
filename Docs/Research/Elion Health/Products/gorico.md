# GoRICO

GoRICO is a governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform that helps organizations manage, automate, and sustain their security and compliance efforts across various environments—cloud, hybrid, and on-premises. It provides a customizable workspace tailored to each organization’s policies and tech stack, allowing for centralized, year-round evidence collection, risk tracking, and real-time compliance scoring.
