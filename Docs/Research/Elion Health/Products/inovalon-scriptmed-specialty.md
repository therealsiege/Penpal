# Inovalon ScriptMed Specialty

Inovalon ScriptMed Specialty is a specialty pharmacy workflow and patient management platform that supports the full lifecycle of specialty prescriptions, from referral intake through dispensing and ongoing patient care. It provides configurable workflows, patient portals, and reporting tools to manage coverage requirements, documentation, and care coordination while reducing manual tasks for pharmacy staff.
