# Inovalon  Safety Management

Inovalon Safety Management is a software suite that supports the reporting, tracking, and analysis of patient safety events in healthcare facilities. It offers three product tiers: Safety Management Starter for post-acute care, VigiLanz Safety Management for acute and ambulatory settings, and VigiLanz Safety Management Pro, which includes automated event detection and advanced analytics.
