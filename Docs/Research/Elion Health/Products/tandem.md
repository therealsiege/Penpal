# Tandem

Tandem is an AI-powered platform that integrates directly into provider EHRs and workflows to automate the end-to-end medication access process. The platform conducts benefits verification, generates and submits PAs and appeals, facilitates enrollment in manufacturer programs, and routes prescriptions to the optimal pharmacy for the patient.
