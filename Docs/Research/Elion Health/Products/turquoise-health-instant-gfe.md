# Turquoise Health Instant GFE

Turquoise Health's Instant GFE (Good Faith Estimate) tool helps providers generate accurate cost estimates for patients in compliance with the No Surprises Act. It allows for instant creation, calculation, and sharing of estimates, incorporating facility and physician fees.
