# PVerify Enhanced Patient Coverage Insights

pVerify's Enhanced Patient Coverage Insights solution allows healthcare providers to generate real-time estimates of patient financial responsibility by using verified insurance benefits and custom fee schedules. The estimator calculates costs such as copays, coinsurance, and deductibles based on eligibility results and Medicare pricing or unique fee schedules.
