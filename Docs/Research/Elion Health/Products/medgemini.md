# MedGemini

Med-Gemini is an AI model family from Google, optimized for healthcare tasks like medical imaging, pathology, and genomics. It builds on Google’s Gemini models, using multimodal data and long-context reasoning to improve clinical workflows, such as generating radiology reports and predicting health outcomes from genomic data.
