# ShiftWizard

ShiftWizard is a healthcare workforce management software that focuses on improving staff scheduling and communication within healthcare organizations. The platform offers comprehensive scheduling solutions, including real-time tracking of productivity metrics and integrating with EHR and Time & Attendance systems to provide accurate staffing data. This integration allows healthcare providers to make informed staffing decisions based on historical and real-time data through it’s Acuity module, which can be used for assigning patients to nurses based on complexity.
