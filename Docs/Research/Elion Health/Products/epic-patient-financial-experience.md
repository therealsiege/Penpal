# Epic Patient Financial Experience

Epic Patient Financial Experience is a set of tools embedded in Epic’s EHR and MyChart that provide patients with accurate cost estimates before care, personalized payment plans or financial assistance options, and the ability to pay digitally via wallet, credit, e‑check, or SMS. It allows patients to view their expected charges, consent to auto-payment, or set up a payment plan during digital check-in. It also consolidates hospital and professional balances into one statement.
