# InfluxMD

InfluxMD is a medical practice CRM and patient acquisition platform that operates in front-end workflows such as lead capture, patient intake, and appointment scheduling. It provides AI-driven features that engage patients 24/7 with an Intelligent Marketing Agent, extract insurance card information, and run automated multi-channel follow-up sequences across email, SMS, voice, and web chat.
