# Amigo AI

Amigo AI is a platform that builds, validates, and operates patient‑facing clinical AI agents that sit in front of care workflows such as triage, patient engagement, and care coordination. It provides a cognitive agent architecture with context graphs, functional memory, and dynamic behaviors, and offers RESTful APIs plus Python and TypeScript SDKs.
