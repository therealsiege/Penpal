# Longevity AI

Longevity AI provides a digital health platform that combines AI and data analytics to assess biological age and optimize healthspan. The platform offers tools for clinicians and patients, including real-time health monitoring, personalized recommendations, and predictive analytics to support preventive care and lifestyle modifications.
