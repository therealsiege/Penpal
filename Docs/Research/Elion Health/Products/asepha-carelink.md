# Asepha CareLink

Asepha offers AI-driven document processing and workflow automation tools for pharmacies (both retail and specialty). Its prescription OCR extracts information from faxes, handwritten scripts, and Sig codes, triages missing documentation, and populates pharmacy management or EHR systems.
