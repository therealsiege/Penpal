# Iodine AwarePreBill

Iodine AwarePreBill is an AI-powered solution that targets revenue leakage in the post-discharge, pre-bill phase of the revenue cycle. It uses Iodine’s proprietary CognitiveML™ platform to identify high-value inpatient records with potential documentation or coding issues that could impact reimbursement.
