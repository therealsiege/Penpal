# MedMor Patient Onboarding

MedMor Patient Onboarding is a service-based healthcare logistics offering that provides hands-on patient setup, training, and education for remote care programs and telehealth platforms. The service includes IPV screening implementation, telehealth platform activation, device onboarding for remote monitoring tools, and patient education to ensure proper use and adherence.
