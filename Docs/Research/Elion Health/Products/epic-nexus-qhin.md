# Epic Nexus QHIN

Epic Nexus QHIN is Epic's Qualified Health Information Network that connects Epic provider organizations to the TEFCA national exchange, supporting treatment workflows, individual access services, and public health reporting. It onboards Epic community members, routes individual access service queries to responding nodes via standardized APIs, and locates patient records across the Nexus community.
