# Epic Sepsis Model

The Epic Sepsis Model (ESM) is a predictive tool embedded in Epic’s EHR platform, designed to identify patients at risk of developing sepsis. It generates risk estimates every 20 minutes based on patient data, aiming to support early interventions by alerting clinicians before sepsis progresses.
