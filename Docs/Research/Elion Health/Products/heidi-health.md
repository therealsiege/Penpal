# Heidi Health

[Contact](https://www.heidihealth.com/contact-sales)

Heidi Health is an AI medical scribe and care partner platform that automates clinical documentation, evidence search, and follow-up communication for clinicians. It transcribes consultations in over 115 languages, generates formatted clinical notes, referrals, and patient handouts, and integrates outputs into the EHR. The system assists providers before, during, and after visits by organizing patient histories, applying clinical codes, and managing administrative tasks while maintaining compliance with HIPAA, GDPR, and SOC 2 standards.
