# TPP SystemOne

TPP's SystmOne is a comprehensive electronic health record (EHR) platform used across various healthcare settings, including primary care, hospitals, mental health, and social care. It offers a unified EHR that ensures all patient data is accessible and shared across different care providers, improving care coordination and decision-making.
