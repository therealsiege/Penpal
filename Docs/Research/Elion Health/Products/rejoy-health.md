# Rejoy Health

Rejoy Health is a medical search engine and digital clinical assistant used at the point of care. It provides evidence-backed answers with cited sources, generates concise, sourced summaries for clinical research, maintenance of certification exam preparation, patient handouts, and laboratory test consideration, and provides 24/7 chatbot access for on-demand queries.
