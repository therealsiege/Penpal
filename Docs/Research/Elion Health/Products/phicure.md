# PHIcure

[Contact](https://imagineteam.com/landing/phicure/)

PHIcure provides clearinghouse and banking reconciliation solutions that connect healthcare organizations with payers, providers, and financial institutions. Its EDIQ Clearinghouse supports claim submission and eligibility verification, while the BankingQ™ suite (Balance, Parse, Post) automates reconciliation of payments, eliminating manual balancing and spreadsheet use.
