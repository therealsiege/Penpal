# RapidClaims RapidScrub

Contact

RapidScrub by RapidClaims is an AI-based pre-submission claim scrubber designed to prevent denials and optimize billing accuracy. It leverages a continuously updated library of over 119 million payer-specific edits to identify issues such as coding errors, missing data, and eligibility mismatches before claims are submitted.
