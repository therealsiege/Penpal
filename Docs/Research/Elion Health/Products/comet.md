# Comet

Comet is an AI-powered virtual workforce platform designed to support healthcare access centers. It automates routine scheduling, referral conversions, and patient inquiries across channels such as voice, chat, and SMS. Comet uses AI agents to resolve simple requests autonomously and AI copilots to assist staff with more complex issues.
