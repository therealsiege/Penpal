# OWKIN

Owkin is a biotechnology and AI company that applies agentic AI and multimodal data to accelerate drug discovery, development, and diagnostics. Its flagship platform, K Pro, combines large biological datasets with a network of intelligent agents to generate interpretable insights for biopharma teams. K Pro integrates multimodal foundation models, reinforcement learning, and biological reasoning to identify biomarkers, predict patient outcomes, and guide precision therapy development.
