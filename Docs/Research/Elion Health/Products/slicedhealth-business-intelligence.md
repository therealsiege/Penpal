# SlicedHealth Business Intelligence

Contact

SlicedHealth BI is a platform that provides dynamic, automated internal reporting and analytics (claims, payer behavior, denials, profitability) for hospitals, health systems, group practices, and ambulatory surgery centers. It surfaces actionable insights, uncovers underpayments, tracks payer trends, identifies denial risks, and helps with forecasting and strategic planning.
