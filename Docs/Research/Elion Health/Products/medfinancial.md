# Medfinancial

Medfinancial provides patient financing solutions through its We Care® Patient Payment Program, which helps healthcare providers manage self-pay accounts while giving patients affordable, non-credit–based payment options. The program accepts 100% of applicants, offers 0% APR or low-interest installment plans with no late fees or prepayment penalties, and covers balances typically between $500–$15,000.
