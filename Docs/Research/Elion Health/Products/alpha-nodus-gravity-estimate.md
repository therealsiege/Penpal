# Alpha Nodus Gravity Estimate

Gravity Estimate (part of the Alpha Nodus Gravity platform) generates real-time, accurate cost estimates for patients before care, by combining payer data, fee schedules, and insurance benefit info to produce transparent cost projections. It automates benefit extraction, handles complex fee schedule logic, and delivers estimates securely through digital channels to reduce billing surprises and boost upfront collections.
