# AdhereHealth

AdhereHealth provides healthcare solutions focused on medication adherence, primarily targeting high-risk populations for improved health outcomes and cost reduction. Its Adhere Platform combines predictive analytics, clinical workflows, and telepharmacy services to help healthcare plans enhance Medicare and Medicaid performance on key measures like CMS Star Ratings.
