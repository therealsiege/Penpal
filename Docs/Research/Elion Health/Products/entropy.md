# Entropy

Entropy is an AI platform used in specialty healthcare operations to automate patient intake and revenue cycle workflows. It provides document classification and referral triage, verifies insurance eligibility, and submits and manages prior authorization requests and denial follow-up while supporting human-in-the-loop review for clinical accuracy.
