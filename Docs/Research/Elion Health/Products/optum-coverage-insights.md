# Optum Coverage Insight

Optum Coverage Insight is an insurance discovery solution that uses data mining, machine learning, and predictive analytics to identify undisclosed coverage for patients classified as self-pay or underinsured. The platform integrates with payer networks to validate insurance and demographic information, helping providers recover revenue that might otherwise be lost.
