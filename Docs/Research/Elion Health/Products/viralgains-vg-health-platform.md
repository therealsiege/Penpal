# Viralgains VG Health Platform

ViralGains VG Health is an advertising-platform feature used in healthcare and pharmaceutical digital campaigns to collect zero-party data via optional short surveys embedded in display and video ads. It provides VoiceAlike AI to predict responses from non-responders, offers real-time reporting, and activates audience segments for targeting and sequencing.
