# Red Rover Health

Red Rover Health offers a healthcare integration platform that utilizes RESTful APIs to connect third-party software applications with electronic health record (EHR) systems. This platform enables healthcare providers to incorporate solutions into their existing EHRs, facilitating real-time data exchange and enhancing patient care workflows.
