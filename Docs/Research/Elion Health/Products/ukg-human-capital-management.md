# UKG Human Capital Management

Formerly known as Kronos, UKG's Human Capital Management solutions offer scheduling tools to help organizations manage workforce deployment. These tools enable the creation of flexible schedules that align staffing with business demands, with features including real-time staffing visibility, compliance management, and employee self-service options for shift swaps and time-off requests.
