# eInsights

Contact

eInsights offers hospital-focused insurance discovery software designed to identify unknown or missed commercial, Medicare, and Medicaid coverage. The platform scans all patient accounts—not just self-pay—and continuously reevaluates them as demographics or statuses change, helping recover lost revenue before timely filing deadlines.
