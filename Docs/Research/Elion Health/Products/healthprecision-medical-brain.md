# Medical Brain by healthPrecision

Medical Brain is an AI-powered clinical decision support and care management platform that monitors patients continuously, aggregates comprehensive health data, and provides real-time actionable insights to both patients and providers. It aims to identify emerging health risks early, guide necessary actions to improve outcomes, and prevent costly interventions.
