# Koios DS

Koios DS is an ultrasound decision support tool used in diagnostic imaging workflows to assist classification of breast lesions and thyroid nodules. It analyzes two orthogonal ultrasound views and provides malignancy risk assessments and standardized lesion descriptors aligned to BI-RADS for breast and TI-RADS/ATA for thyroid.
