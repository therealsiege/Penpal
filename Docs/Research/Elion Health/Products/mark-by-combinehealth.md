# Mark by CombineHealth

[Contact](https://www.combinehealth.ai/contact-us)

Mark is an AI-powered medical billing assistant designed to automate key aspects of revenue cycle management (RCM). It can fill out insurance claim forms, write denial letters, and ensure compliance with billing rules such as NCCI edits and medical necessity guidelines. Mark is built to integrate with any Electronic Health Record (EHR) system, including transcribing hand-written notes into digital formats.
