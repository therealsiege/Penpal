# Altair

Altair is an AI-based prior authorization assistant that connects patient records with payer medical policies to automate the creation of prior-auth submissions. It consolidates insurance benefits, clinical history, and documentation requirements into one workspace, reducing the need for staff to switch systems or manually copy information.
