# QuicksortRx

QuicksortRx provides a real-time analytics and intelligence platform focused on pharmacy procurement and supply chain management within health systems. It helps pharmacy leaders identify cost-saving opportunities such as NDC substitutions, contract anomalies, overspending alerts (e.g., WAC premiums, 340B variances), streamline purchasing across multiple facilities, and present actionable dashboards and reports.
