# ValidMind

ValidMind is a model and AI risk management platform designed for regulated industries such as financial services, life sciences, and healthcare. It automates model testing, validation, documentation, and governance across traditional, statistical, and generative AI models. ValidMind provides a shared, auditable environment where risk teams can perform stress testing, manage model lifecycle documentation, version control artifacts, and monitor compliance and performance in real time.
