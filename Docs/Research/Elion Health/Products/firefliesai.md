# Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai is an AI meeting assistant adapted for healthcare settings to transcribe clinical conversations, generate structured clinical notes, extract patient insights (symptoms, history, action items), and surface follow-ups or tasks. It helps reduce clinician documentation burden by turning dialogues into SOAP, DAP, BIRP, or other note formats, highlights key patient concerns, and integrates with EHR or telehealth platforms.
