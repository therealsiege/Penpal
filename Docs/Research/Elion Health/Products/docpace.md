# DOCPACE®

DOCPACE® is an AI-driven appointment management platform used in day-of scheduling and patient-flow workflows for ambulatory practices. It performs real-time appointment stacking, updates schedules as the day changes, sends automated personalized patient reminders and alerts, and provides a Patient Flow Report that analyzes schedule capacity and drivers.
