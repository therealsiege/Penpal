# Confido Health

Contact

Confido Health provides AI-driven automation tools for healthcare operations, aimed at reducing staff workload and enhancing patient engagement. Their virtual agents—Sara, Ryan, and Lilly—are used to automate tasks such as appointment scheduling, payment collections, pre/post-operative engagement, closing care gaps, and managing care transitions.
