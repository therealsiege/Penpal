# Altera Sunrise

Altera Sunrise is an electronic health record (EHR) platform designed for hospitals and health systems, offering a unified patient record across inpatient and outpatient settings. Built with cloud-capable architecture on Microsoft Azure, Sunrise includes tools for clinical documentation, order entry, medication management, and financial operations
