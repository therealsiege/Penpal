# Trellis AI

Trellis AI is an automation platform for specialty providers and diagnostic labs that handles intake, prior authorizations, and appeals using an AI agent trained on clinical and insurance policy data. It extracts and routes information from unstructured documents, performs eligibility checks, and proactively gathers missing data from patients or providers to prevent delays.
