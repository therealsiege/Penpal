# Stedi Eligibility Checks

[Contact](https://www.stedi.com/contact)

Stedi Eligibility Checks is an API-based service that enables healthcare providers and software platforms to automatically verify a patient’s insurance coverage and benefits with payers. It converts simple JSON requests into HIPAA-compliant X12 270/271 eligibility transactions, sends them to payers, and returns structured responses showing whether coverage is active, what services are included, and patient cost responsibilities.
