# Vibe Health

Vibe Health by eVideon is a comprehensive smart room platform designed to enhance the healthcare experience for patients, families, and clinicians. It integrates with hospital EHR systems and other installed technologies to provide real-time access to critical health information via in-room Smart TVs, digital whiteboards, digital door signs, and bedside tablets.
