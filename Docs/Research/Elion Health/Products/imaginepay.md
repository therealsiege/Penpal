# ImaginePay

[Contact](https://imagineteam.com/imagineone/patient-payments-engagement/)

ImaginePay is a patient payment portal designed to simplify the process of paying medical bills, either at home or at the point of service. The platform allows patients to manage their payments through a secure, HIPAA- and PCI-certified system that supports multiple payment methods, including ACH, cash, check, and credit/debit cards.
