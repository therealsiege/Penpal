# K2view data Masking and Privacy

K2View's data masking and privacy solution focuses on protecting sensitive information, such as Personally Identifiable Information (PII), through a variety of static and dynamic masking techniques. It supports both structured and unstructured data, making it suitable for use across multiple industries, including healthcare and financial services.
