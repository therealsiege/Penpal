# Macro Helix

Macro Helix provides software and services for managing 340B drug pricing programs, with solutions designed for covered entities and contract pharmacies. Its core platform, 340B Architect, supports functions such as split billing, contract pharmacy administration, referral capture, audit preparation, and compliance monitoring. Additional tools include analytics for program performance, order optimization for central pharmacies, and management of manufacturer restrictions.
