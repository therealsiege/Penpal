# Neon Health

Neon Health provides an AI-powered platform that automates complex patient access workflows for healthcare organizations, including benefit verification, prior authorization, financial assistance, patient onboarding, and adherence support. Their system includes modular AI agents capable of navigating payer phone trees, filling out forms, extracting data from portals, and engaging with patients across multiple channels like voice, SMS, fax, and email.
