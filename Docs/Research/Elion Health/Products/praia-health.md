# Praia Health

[Contact](https://www.praiahealth.com/contact)

Praia Health helps healthcare organizations integrate and personalize patient experiences across systems, offering capabilities such as data interoperability, patient engagement tools, and analytics to provide personalized health recommendations and communication. The platform is designed to connect disparate healthcare data sources, including EHRs, CRM systems, and other digital tools to create a unified patient experience.
