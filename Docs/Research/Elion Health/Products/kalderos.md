# Kalderos

Kalderos provides a drug discount management platform that helps stakeholders across the pharmaceutical ecosystem—such as manufacturers, covered entities, and state payers—monitor and resolve issues related to noncompliant drug discount claims. The platform uses AI and machine learning to analyze large volumes of claims data from Medicaid, commercial payers, and 340B programs to detect overlapping discounts and streamline compliance workflows.
