# HITEKS CAPD360 Insight

HITEKS CAPD360 Insight is a real-time clinical documentation integrity (CDI) solution used at the point of care and in pre-bill and quality reporting workflows. It detects documentation opportunities with clinical natural language processing, generates configurable real-time queries and guidance, and captures ICD-10 concepts along with present-on-admission and peri-operative events.
