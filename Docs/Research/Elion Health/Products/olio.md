# Olio

[Contact](https://www.olio.health/contact-support)

Olio is a care coordination platform designed for organizations engaged in value-based care, including payers, health systems, primary care groups at risk, and post-acute and behavioral health providers. It enables real-time collaboration across healthcare teams through shared workflows, patient status updates, messaging, event notifications, and case management tools.
