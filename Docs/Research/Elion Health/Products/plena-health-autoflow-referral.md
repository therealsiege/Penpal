# Plena Health Autoflow Referral

Plena Health Autoflow Referral is a front-office automation tool that manages incoming referrals for healthcare practices. It extracts information from faxed or electronic referral documents, validates key details such as patient demographics, insurance, and clinical information, and prepares them for scheduling and intake. The system integrates with practice management and EHR systems to reduce manual data entry and ensure referrals are processed quickly and accurately.
