# Jessica by CombineHealth

[Contact](https://www.combinehealth.ai/demo)

Jessica is an AI-powered medical scribe that automates clinical documentation by listening to provider–patient conversations in real time. It generates structured notes—including SOAP notes, progress notes, and visit summaries—formatted for direct entry into EHRs. Jessica flags missing information with smart prompts, follows specialty-specific documentation guidelines, and integrates with all major EHR systems.
