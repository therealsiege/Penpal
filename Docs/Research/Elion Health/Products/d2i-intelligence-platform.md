# d2i Intelligence Platform

d2i is a healthcare performance analytics company that helps provider organizations turn raw clinical, operational, and financial data into trusted, actionable insights. Its solutions focus on specialty-specific analytics—most notably emergency medicine—to support physician performance management, quality improvement, compensation alignment, and value-based care initiatives. d2i aggregates and normalizes data from multiple sources, including EHRs and billing systems, to deliver timely dashboards, scorecards, and benchmarking that physicians and leaders can use to understand outcomes, productivity, quality metrics, and compliance.
