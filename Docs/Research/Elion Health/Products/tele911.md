# Tele911

Tele911 is a telehealth platform that integrates emergency medicine services into 911 and Emergency Medical Services (EMS) systems. It enables EMS personnel to connect non-emergent patients with emergency physicians via telehealth consultations, allowing for treatment in place or diversion to appropriate care settings, thereby reducing unnecessary emergency department visits.
