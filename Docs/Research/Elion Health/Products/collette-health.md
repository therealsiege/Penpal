# Collette Health

Collette Health provides a virtual care platform focused on virtual observation, virtual nursing, and workforce augmentation. Their solutions use AI and human oversight to monitor patients remotely (e.g. preventing falls, detecting contraband, reducing social isolation), support virtual nursing workflows (admissions, rounding, discharge teaching, documentation), and integrate virtually with hospital systems.
