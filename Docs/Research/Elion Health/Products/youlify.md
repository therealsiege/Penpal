# Youlify

[Contact](https://www.youlify.ai/demo)

Youlify is an AI-powered revenue cycle management platform focused on medical billing and claims optimization for healthcare providers. It uses machine learning trained on large billing datasets to detect missed claims, undercoding, and denial patterns, aiming to improve reimbursement rates and reduce revenue loss.
