# Symplr Workforce

Symplr Workforce is a healthcare-specific workforce management platform that centralizes timekeeping, staffing, and scheduling processes to help hospitals and health systems maintain productivity and optimize staff deployment. It includes tools for employee attendance, payroll, and workforce analytics to manage complex healthcare scheduling demands.
