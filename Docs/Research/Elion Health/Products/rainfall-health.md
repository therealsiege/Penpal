# Rainfall Health

Rainfall Health is a compliance and reimbursement modeling platform focused on helping hospitals respond to CMS’s TEAM (Transforming Episode Accountability Model) payment program. It provides financial modeling tools to estimate potential Medicare reimbursement impact over the five-year TEAM performance period and identify revenue at risk or opportunity.
