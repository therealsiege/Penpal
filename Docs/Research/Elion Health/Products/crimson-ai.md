# Crimson AI

Crimson AI is an analytics and performance management platform that helps healthcare organizations evaluate clinical, financial, and operational outcomes. It aggregates data from EHRs, claims, and other systems to support provider performance measurement, care variation analysis, and population health management. The platform is often used for physician alignment, quality reporting, and value-based care initiatives by delivering actionable insights through dashboards and benchmarking tools.
