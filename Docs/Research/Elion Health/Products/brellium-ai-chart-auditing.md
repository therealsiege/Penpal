# Brellium AI Chart Auditing

[Contact](https://brellium.com/demo?utm_medium=review-sites&utm_campaign=Elion&utm_source=Elion)

Brellium automates the medical chart review process. It audits clinical documentation and billing notes according to custom criteria, identifying discrepancies, errors, and gaps. The platform connects with EHR systems to provide real-time reporting, helps improve documentation quality, and allows for same-day billing validation.
