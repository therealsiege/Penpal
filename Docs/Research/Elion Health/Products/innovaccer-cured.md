# Innovaccer Cured

Cured is a healthcare experience platform that combines a consumer data platform, CRM, and digital marketing tools to help providers and health systems engage patients across the care journey. It integrates healthcare data to build targeted patient audiences, supports automated outreach campaigns for acquisition, activation, and retention, and provides analytics to measure engagement and conversion.
