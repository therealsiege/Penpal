# Amenities Health

Amenities Health is a white-labeled digital front door and patient loyalty platform used at pre-visit and access points to enable membership-based concierge and virtual care. It provides rapid patient registration and account creation, consolidates provider search and next-available appointment scheduling across virtual, urgent, and primary care, and offers managed membership services such as concierge primary care, 24/7 virtual urgent care, and specialty e-consults.
