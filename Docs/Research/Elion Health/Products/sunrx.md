# SunRx

SUNRx provides a comprehensive suite of 340B program management services for hospitals, health centers, and pharmacies. Its platform includes tools for split billing, contract pharmacy management, compliance monitoring, audit support, and advanced claims capture, helping covered entities optimize savings while meeting federal requirements. SUNRx also offers specialty pharmacy support and technology solutions to streamline inventory and claims processing.
