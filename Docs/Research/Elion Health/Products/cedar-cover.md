# Cedar Cover

Cedar Cover is a tool that helps healthcare providers identify, enroll, and manage patient coverage through Medicaid, ACA plans, and pharmaceutical assistance programs. It automates eligibility screening and enrollment to convert self-pay or uninsured patients into covered individuals, reducing write-offs and administrative costs.
