# Lexi AI

Lexi is an AI healthcare medical interpreter that fits into intake, consultation, pharmacy, and discharge workflows to provide real-time multilingual interpretation across the patient journey. It supports more than 20 languages, provides on-demand voice interpretation for clinical encounters, and is medically fine-tuned for clinical terminology.
