# Aegis

Aegis Health is a claims denial management platform designed for healthcare providers, hospitals, and billing teams to automate the insurance appeal process. It identifies denied claims through a centralized dashboard, prioritizes them based on urgency and value, and generates payer-specific appeal letters by pulling data from EOBs, patient files, and EHR systems.
