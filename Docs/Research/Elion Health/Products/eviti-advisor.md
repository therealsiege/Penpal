# Eviti Advisor

Eviti Advisor is a web-based, evidence-based oncology decision-support application used at the point of care to present curated cancer treatment regimens and protocols. It surfaces chemotherapy, radiation, and supportive drug regimens, lets clinicians filter and compare options side-by-side, and presents evidence summaries, side-effect profiles, and cost information.
