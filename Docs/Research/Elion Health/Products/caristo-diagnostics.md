# CaRi-Heart

CaRi-Heart is a medical imaging analysis solution used in the coronary CT angiography (CCTA) diagnostic and risk-prediction workflow for coronary artery disease. It quantifies coronary inflammation with an FAI-Score™, characterizes coronary plaque as CaRi-Plaque™, and generates an individualized CaRi-Heart® risk score to inform clinical decision making.
