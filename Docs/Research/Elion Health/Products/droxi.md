# Droxi

Droxi is an AI-driven platform designed to reduce the administrative burden on healthcare providers by streamlining EHR inbox management. It centralizes patient information, automates routine tasks such as prescription refills and lab result reviews, and displays relevant patient data to minimize redundant clicking and screen switching.
