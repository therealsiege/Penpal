# CommerceHealthcare Patient Financing

CommerceHealthcare’s Health Services Financing (HSF®) is a patient financing / lending program designed to make healthcare payments more manageable for patients and less burdensome for providers’ revenue cycle teams. It offers patients a line of credit (often low-interest or interest-free) that can be used for estimated out-of-pocket charges either before services (pre-service) or during the billing cycle, with simple enrollment (no credit check in many cases) and flexible repayment.
