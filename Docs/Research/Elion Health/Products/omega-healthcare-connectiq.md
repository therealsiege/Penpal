# Omega Healthcare ConnectIQ

ConnectIQ is an analytics and workflow automation platform designed to support revenue cycle management operations. It provides real-time data monitoring, performance dashboards, and predictive analytics to help healthcare organizations identify revenue leakage, manage denials, and improve collections. ConnectIQ integrates with EHR and billing systems to provide insights across the RCM lifecycle, including coding accuracy, claim status, and payer trends.
