# Curae

Curae offers a Patient Financial Access Platform that helps health systems improve affordability for patients and financial performance for providers. It includes tools for identifying insurance coverage at the time of service, enrolling patients in Medicaid or ACA plans, providing non-recourse patient financing (including 0% interest lines of credit for some), recovering underpayments (e.g. from Medicare), reducing bad debt, and automating billing and collections workflows.
