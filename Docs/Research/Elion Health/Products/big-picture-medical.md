# Big Picture Medical

Big Picture Medical offers a configurable, interoperable software platform designed to connect healthcare providers and streamline patient pathways. Using a modular system called "Blocks," the platform supports a wide range of clinical and research use cases, enabling healthcare organizations to create customizable, interconnected care pathways.
