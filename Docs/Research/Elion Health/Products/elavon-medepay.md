# Elavon MedEpay

Elavon MedEpay is a healthcare-focused payments and billing solution for providers and patients. It offers payment acceptance via multiple channels (online payments, IVR, patient portal, text or email prompts, contactless payments, mobile wallets, etc.), supports payment plans (including financing options), stores payment methods (“card on file”), automates posting to patient ledgers, sends statements and reminders, and provides tools for reconciliation and reporting
