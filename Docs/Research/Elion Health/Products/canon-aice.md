# Canon AiCE

Canon Medical Advanced intelligent Clear-IQ Engine (AiCE) is a deep learning–based CT image reconstruction technology that reduces noise while preserving signal and spatial resolution. It uses a convolutional neural network trained on model-based iterative reconstruction data to produce sharper images with natural texture at faster reconstruction speeds.
