# Oncora Medical Clinical AI

Oncora Medical Clinical AI is a clinical documentation tool that uses a large language model (LLM) to extract oncology-specific data from medical documents and generate structured data and documentation. It connects to electronic health records via FHIR APIs and pulls pathology, radiology, and clinical notes.
