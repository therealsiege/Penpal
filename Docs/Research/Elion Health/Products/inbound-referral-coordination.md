# Linear Health Inbound Referral Coordination

Linear Health Inbound Referral Coordination is an AI-powered solution designed to automate the entire inbound referral workflow for specialty clinics. The platform processes incoming referrals—primarily via fax—by extracting patient, insurance, and clinical data, creating or updating EHR charts, and initiating immediate multi-channel patient outreach through SMS, email, and voice AI.
