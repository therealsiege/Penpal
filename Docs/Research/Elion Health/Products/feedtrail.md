# Feedtrail

Feedtrail is a patient experience platform that enables healthcare organizations to capture real-time patient feedback and translate it into actionable insights for improving care quality and outcomes. It supports customizable surveys, automated outreach via text, email, and phone, and provides analytics to identify trends, track performance, and guide service recovery efforts.
