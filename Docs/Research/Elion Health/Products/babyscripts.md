# BabyScripts

Babyscripts' Maternity Care Program is a digital health solution designed to enhance prenatal and postpartum care through remote monitoring and patient engagement tools. It includes features such as myJourney for education and self-care, myBloodPressure for remote blood pressure monitoring, and myMentalHealth for mental health screenings.
