# docology

docology is a document intake and clinical information processing platform designed for private specialty clinics to reduce time spent reviewing charts. It captures incoming patient records, parses and organizes documents, extracts structured data (such as demographics and insurance), and integrates the results directly into the existing EHR without changing clinician workflows.
