# Digital Workforce

Digital Workforce Services Plc provides business process automation (BPA) and AI agent solutions to industries such as healthcare and financial services. Its offerings include “Outsmart,” a comprehensive automation platform that supports end-to-end redesign of workflows around human decision points, rather than automating existing inefficient processes.
