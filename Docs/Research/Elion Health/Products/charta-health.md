# Charta Health

[Contact](https://www.chartahealth.com/contact)

Charta provides an AI-driven platform that conducts autonomous pre-bill reviews of medical charts to identify revenue opportunities and coding errors. By integrating with existing electronic health record (EHR) systems, it aims to enhance revenue integrity, prevent claim denials, and ensure compliance with billing standards.
