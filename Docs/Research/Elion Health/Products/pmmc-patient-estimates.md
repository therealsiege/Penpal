# PMMC Patient Estimates

PMMC's Patient Estimates tool allows providers to generate accurate cost estimates based on contract data. The web-based tool integrates with contract management systems to deliver real-time estimates of patient financial responsibility, enabling front-end staff to provide these details quickly and collect payments at the time of service, supporting compliance with requirements like the No Surprises Act.
