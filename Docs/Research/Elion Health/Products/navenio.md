# Navenio

Navenio provides location-based intelligent workflow solutions for healthcare settings, using indoor positioning technology to optimize the movement and coordination of healthcare staff and resources within facilities. This technology aims to improve efficiency and patient care by ensuring the right person is in the right place at the right time.
