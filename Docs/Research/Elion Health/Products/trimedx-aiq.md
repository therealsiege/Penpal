# TRIMEDX-AIQ

TRIMEDX-AIQ is an AI-native clinical asset management intelligence platform used by health systems to manage the full lifecycle of medical equipment. It combines a large multi-vendor medical device dataset with predictive analytics to validate inventories, anticipate equipment failures, automate work order preparation, and coordinate maintenance and parts procurement.
