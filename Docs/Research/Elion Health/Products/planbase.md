# Planbase

Planbase is an AI-powered workforce management platform purpose-built for healthcare organizations. It automates administrative workflows such as provider onboarding, credentialing, scheduling, payroll, and compliance monitoring through generative AI agents that adapt to each organization’s operations. The platform includes AI forecasting for patient demand, automated credential verification across state databases, and real-time workforce analytics for utilization, coverage, and performance insights.
