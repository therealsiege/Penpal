# Prosper Marketplace

Prosper offers healthcare financing through personal loans to patients, helping them pay for elective and non-covered medical expenses. Providers can partner with Prosper at no cost, giving patients access to fast online loan applications, instant pre-qualification without impacting credit scores, and flexible repayment terms
