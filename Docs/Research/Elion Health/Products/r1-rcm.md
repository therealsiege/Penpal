# R1 RCM

R1 RCM provides end-to-end, technology-enabled outsourced revenue cycle management services as well as a modular technology platform for in-house revenue cycle augmentation. Its outsourced services manage $80B in net patient revenue across thousands of hospitals and physician groups, while its technology platform supports functions such as accounts receivable and denials recovery, complex claims, prior authorization, charge capture, coding and CDI, underpayment recovery, insurance discovery, and regulatory compliance.
