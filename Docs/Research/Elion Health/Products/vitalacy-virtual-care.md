# Vitalacy Virtual Care

Vitalacy provides virtual care and patient monitoring solutions that focus on improving safety and compliance in healthcare settings. Its platform offers features such as remote patient monitoring, hand hygiene compliance tracking, and automated risk assessments to reduce hospital-acquired infections and enhance patient outcomes.
