# Glendor

Glendor specializes in the de-identification of Protected Health Information (PHI) to facilitate secure medical data protection, sharing, and aggregation. PHI Image Sanitizer automatically removes identifiable information from various medical images such as CT, MRI, Ultrasound, X-Ray, and PET scans.
