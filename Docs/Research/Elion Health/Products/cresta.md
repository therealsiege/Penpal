# Cresta

Cresta offers an AI-powered platform for contact centers that assists agents in real time by analyzing conversations and providing coaching, guidance, and automation. Its capabilities include real-time speech and text analysis, suggested responses, automated workflows, quality assurance scoring, and performance analytics.
