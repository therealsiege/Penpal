# MD Live

MDLIVE is a telehealth platform that provides patients with 24/7 access to board-certified doctors and licensed therapists via phone, video, or app. Patients can consult healthcare professionals for various non-emergency medical conditions, receive diagnoses, and obtain prescriptions sent to their preferred pharmacy.
