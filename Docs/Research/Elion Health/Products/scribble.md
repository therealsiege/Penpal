# Scribble

Scribble is a clinical documentation suite from IKS Health that offers multiple workflows combining AI, speech recognition, and human scribes. Its modules include AI-only scribing (Scribble Now), AI plus human review (Scribble Swift), AI plus clinician scribe and coding (Scribble Pro), transcription services (Scribble Transcribe), and live virtual scribes (Scribble Live).
