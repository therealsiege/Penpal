# Xealth

Xealth is a digital health orchestration platform embedded in the electronic health record that health systems use at the point of care to prescribe, enroll, and monitor third-party digital tools. It provides a digital care app inside clinician workflows, a rules engine to automate distribution, and a command center for reporting and analytics.
