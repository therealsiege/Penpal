# Q Connect

Q Connect is a real-time patient experience management solution designed for healthcare providers and payers. Unlike traditional post-visit surveys that suffer from low response rates and delays, Q Connect uses real-time SMS text-based surveys with branching logic to capture feedback instantly at multiple points in the patient journey (clinical and non-clinical).
