# VectorCare

VectorCare is a cloud-based patient logistics and care coordination platform that centralizes transport, home health, and durable medical equipment (DME) management. The platform combines workflow automation, credentialing, analytics, and payments into one system to improve coordination between hospitals, providers, and vendors.
