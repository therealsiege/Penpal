# Netsmart Care Coordination

Netsmart’s Care Coordination solutions provide healthcare organizations with tools to coordinate patient care across different providers and settings. The platform enables real-time data sharing, care plan management, and secure communication between care teams, including behavioral health, primary care, and social services. It integrates with EHRs, health information exchanges, and other systems to ensure a comprehensive view of patient data.
