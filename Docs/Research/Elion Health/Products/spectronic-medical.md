# Spectronic Medical

Spectronic Medical offers an AI-driven solution called MRI Planner, designed to enable MRI-only radiotherapy planning by generating high-quality synthetic CT (sCT) images from standard MRI scans. The platform uses deep learning to produce sCTs for brain, head-neck, and pelvis regions, and includes automated organ-at-risk (OAR) delineations for prostate cases.
