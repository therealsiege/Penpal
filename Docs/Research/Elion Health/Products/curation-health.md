# Reveleer Clinical Intelligence

Reveleer Clinical Intelligence is a clinical decision support platform used in value-based care workflows, providing visit-aligned patient insights at pre-visit, point-of-care, and post-visit review. It ingests electronic health record, claims, and third-party data and applies rules-based logic, natural language processing, and predictive models to surface care gaps, suspected conditions, and risk-adjustment opportunities.
