# Keikku

Keikku is a smart medical device that combines a digital stethoscope with ambient-listening AI to transcribe clinician-patient interactions, generate SOAP notes (including ICD-10 and CPT codes), and upload auscultation audio directly into the EHR. It supports both in-person and remote consultations, performs heart and lung sound detection (e.g., murmurs or crackles) and integrates via APIs with existing systems.
