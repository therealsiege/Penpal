# Parathon

Parathon offers a revenue intelligence software suite designed to centralize and enhance healthcare revenue cycle management using its proprietary Parallel Database (PDB™). The platform enables full integration of claims data across all payers and supports a range of financial tools including contract modeling, denial management, patient responsibility estimators, and real-time worklist automation.
