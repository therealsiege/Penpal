# Century Health

Century Health is a real-world evidence (RWE) platform that uses AI to transform fragmented, unstructured clinical data (from EHRs) into research-ready datasets. It curates, enriches, and normalizes clinical data across providers, enabling faster analytics and insights for researchers, biotech, and providers without imposing heavy burden on clinical teams.
