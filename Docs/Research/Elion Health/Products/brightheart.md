# BrightHeart

BrightHeart is an AI-powered prenatal ultrasound platform designed to support standardized, expert-level assessment of fetal anatomy during routine pregnancy scans, with a focus on congenital heart defect CHD detection. The platform analyzes ultrasound images and cine clips in real time to assess exam completeness and evaluate fetal heart structures, flagging abnormal findings or confirming normality during image acquisition and review.
