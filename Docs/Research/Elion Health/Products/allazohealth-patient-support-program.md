# AllazoHealth  Patient Support Programs

AllazoHealth’s Patient Support Programs use AI to personalize outreach and interventions aimed at improving medication adherence. The platform analyzes patient data to predict which individuals are at risk of non-adherence and determines the most effective communication method and timing for each person
