# EnterpriseRx

EnterpriseRx is a host-based pharmacy management system that supports full pharmacy workflows including dispensing, clinical services programs, refill/home delivery, intake tools, inventory, load balancing across locations, reporting and analytics, and integrated patient engagement (automated refill reminders, web/mobile ordering).
