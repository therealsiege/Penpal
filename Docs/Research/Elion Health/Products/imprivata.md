# Imprivata Identity Governance and Administration (IGA)

Imprivata Identity Governance and Administration (IGA) is a solution for managing the entire identity lifecycle in healthcare organizations. It automates provisioning and de-provisioning of user access, supports role-based access controls, and provides visibility into who has access to what resources.
