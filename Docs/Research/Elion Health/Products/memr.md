# Predoc

Predoc is a health information management platform used in pre-visit intake and clinical documentation workflows. It automates medical record retrieval from U.S. providers. The platform aggregates and structures data from digital sources and PDFs and indexes and surfaces the most relevant clinical information, including specialty-tailored facesheets.
