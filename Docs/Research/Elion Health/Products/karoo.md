# Karoo

Karoo Health is a healthcare company specializing in value-based cardiovascular care. They offer a hybrid care model that combines dedicated on-site and virtual care teams with proprietary digital technology to assist cardiology networks, health systems, and health plans in transitioning to and succeeding in value-based care initiatives.
