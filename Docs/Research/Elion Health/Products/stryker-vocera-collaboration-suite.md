# Stryker Vocera Collaboration Suite

The Stryker Vocera Collaboration Suite is a secure communication platform designed for healthcare teams to improve real-time collaboration and patient care coordination. It enables voice calls, secure text messaging, and alert notifications through mobile devices and wearable Vocera badges.
