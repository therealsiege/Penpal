# EasyPA

EasyPA is an AI platform that automates and accelerates the prior authorization (PA) workflow for medical practices. It fills authorization forms automatically, tracks status in real time, predicts approval likelihood, manages denials and appeals, and integrates with major EHRs (Epic, Athena, eClinicalWorks) in as little as 48 hours.
