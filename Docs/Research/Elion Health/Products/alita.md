# Alita

Alita Smart Intake is an AI-driven patient and family intake tool built for post-acute care providers and related facilities (skilled nursing, assisted living, home health, independent living etc.). It automates early contact workflows: captures data (insurance, care needs, documents), offers real-time calendar bookings, gives 24/7 responses, supports many languages, validates information like licenses, and shows conversion analytics so providers can see where inquiries turn into care recipients
