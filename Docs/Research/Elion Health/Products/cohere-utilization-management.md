# Cohere Utilization Management

Cohere Health’s Utilization Management Suite is an AI-driven platform that automates prior authorization and utilization management processes for health plans and providers. The suite offers in-house, delegated, and API-based deployment options to digitize authorization workflows, reduce administrative costs, and enhance care coordination. Its analytics and clinical intelligence capabilities aim to improve review efficiency, provider experience, and patient access to appropriate care.
