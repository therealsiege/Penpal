# Hallmark Workforce Intelligence and Enablement Platform

Hallmark Health Care Solutions offers a SaaS platform that lets healthcare organizations manage their clinical workforce more strategically. It provides tools for sourcing and deploying contingent labor (via an Internal Resource Pool and a Vendor Management Solution), automates provider compensation and contracting, tracks workforce data and analytics, and aims to help reduce labor costs while improving team engagement and performance.
