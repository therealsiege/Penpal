# Inbox Health

[Contact](https://www.inboxhealth.com/contact/)

Inbox Health is a patient billing platform designed to automate and personalize the billing process for medical practices. It offers features like AI-optimized billing cycles and customizable messaging via email, text, paper mail, and voice calls.
