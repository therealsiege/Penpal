# Decoded Health

Decoded Health offers Fusion Intake and Virtual Front Door. Fusion Intake automates the patient intake process using AI to gather comprehensive patient information, including medical history and medication reviews, and integrates this data into EHR systems. Virtual Front Door streamlines scheduling, registration, and financial processes, optimizing patient access and resource allocation.
