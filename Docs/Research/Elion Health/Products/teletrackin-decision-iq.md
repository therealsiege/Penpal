# TeleTracking Decision IQ

TeleTracking Decision IQ is an AI-enabled operational intelligence tool that prioritizes hospital discharges and coordinates systemwide patient flow. It analyzes throughput data to generate a computational model of the facility, forecasting demand and identifying the “next best discharge” to reduce ED boarding, overstays, and bottlenecks. The platform sends clear, unit-level task priorities to frontline and ancillary departments and embeds them into existing workflows so staff don’t have to switch systems.
