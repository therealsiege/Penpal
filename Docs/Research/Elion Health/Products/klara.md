# Klara

Klara is a conversational patient engagement platform used in front‑office and patient intake workflows to centralize multi‑channel patient communications (text, web chat, call‑to‑text, and voicemails) into a single inbox. It provides automated appointment reminders and broadcast messaging, transcribes voicemails, and offers secure two‑way messaging.
