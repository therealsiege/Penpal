# Nurse Co-Pilot

Hippocratic AI's Nurse Co-Pilot is a generative AI assistant designed to support nurses by handling non–hands-on clinical tasks such as patient education, admission and discharge communication, follow-up calls, and documentation capture. The tool integrates into nursing workflows to reduce administrative burden while maintaining accuracy and clinical safety. It enables nurses to offload routine interactions and data entry, helping them focus on direct patient care and communication.
