# Keebler Health

Keebler Health provides an AI-driven risk adjustment platform designed for accuracy in clinical decision support and coding workflows. It helps risk-bearing entities, such as healthcare providers and payers, by integrating diverse data sources—including faxes, FHIR, and health information exchanges (HIEs)—to identify risk adjustment opportunities with transparency.
