# InsightHealth Aura AI Scribe

InsightHealth Aura AI Scribe is an ambient AI scribing tool designed to automate clinical documentation during patient encounters. It listens to conversations and converts them into customizable medical notes in formats such as SOAP, APSO, and H&P. The platform also offers integrated ICD-10 and CPT coding (in Pro versions), and supports both iOS and Chrome for mobile and desktop use.
