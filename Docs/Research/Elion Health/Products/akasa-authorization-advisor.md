# AKASA Authorization Advisor

AKASA Authorization Advisor is an AI-enabled prior authorization tool for streamlining the submission, tracking, and follow-up of medical authorization requests for procedures. It is integrated with Epic and Cerner, and focuses on health system-specific use cases.
