# Shields Health Solutions

Shields Health Solutions partners with health systems to build and manage integrated specialty pharmacy programs, focusing on improving patient access, payer relationships, and outcomes for complex medications. Their services include access to limited distribution drugs, support for prior authorizations, patient financial assistance, and ongoing care coordination through embedded pharmacists and technicians.
