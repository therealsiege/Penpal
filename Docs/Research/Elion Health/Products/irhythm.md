# iRhythm

iRhythm is a digital healthcare company specializing in cardiac monitoring solutions, best known for its Zio ambulatory cardiac patch. The Zio system records continuous ECG data for up to 14 days, which is then analyzed using proprietary algorithms and reviewed by certified technicians to detect arrhythmias such as atrial fibrillation. Reports are delivered to ordering clinicians through secure portals or EHR integration.
