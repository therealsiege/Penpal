# Springbuk

Springbuk is a cloud-native health data analytics and health intelligence platform that helps self-insured employers, HR leaders, and benefits advisors aggregate, normalize, and analyze multi-source employee health and benefits data to identify cost drivers, predict risk, uncover gaps in care, and inform benefits strategy decisions.
