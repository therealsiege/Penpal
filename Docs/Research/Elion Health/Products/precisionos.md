# PrecisionOS

PrecisionOS provides virtual reality (VR) surgical training solutions designed to enhance the skills of medical professionals, particularly in orthopedics. Their platform offers realistic, immersive simulations that allow surgeons to practice procedures in a controlled environment, improving decision-making and technical proficiency.
