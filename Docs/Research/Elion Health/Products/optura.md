# Optura

Optura is an AI orchestration platform built specifically for healthcare enterprises, designed to turn fragmented pilot initiatives into a unified, enterprise-scale AI program. It includes an “AI Enterprise Operating System” (AIEOS) that enables organisations to define value-use cases, deploy AI agents, monitor performance/ROI, maintain governance and security, and scale AI across operations.
