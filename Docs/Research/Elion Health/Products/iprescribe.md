# iPrescribe

iPrescribe is a mobile e-prescribing application, designed for healthcare providers to electronically prescribe medications, including controlled substances. The app integrates with EHR systems and state prescription drug monitoring programs (PDMPs) to check for potential drug interactions and medication history.
