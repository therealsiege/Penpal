# EmergConnect

EmergConnect is a hospital-focused emergency department workflow platform that automates patient intake, triage documentation, and patient communication to improve ED flow and operational performance. It captures patient-reported information digitally at arrival, structures triage data, and integrates it directly into existing EMRs without requiring custom builds.
