# Corti

Corti offers an AI-powered platform designed to assist healthcare providers with speech-to-text transcription, live note-taking, documentation, coding, and contextual search. The platform provides real-time transcription for consultations, captures essential clinical details, and automates medical documentation such as referral letters and notes. Corti also supports automated diagnosis and procedure coding to enhance billing accuracy, and its contextual search allows access to internal and external knowledge sources directly within clinical workflows.
