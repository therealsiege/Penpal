# ClaraPrice Good Faith Estimates

ClaraPrice Good Faith Estimates tool helps healthcare providers comply with the No Surprises Act by offering a platform to create, manage, and deliver accurate cost estimates for patients. It features workflow automation, collaborative tools, and pre-configured templates to streamline the estimation process across multiple locations.
