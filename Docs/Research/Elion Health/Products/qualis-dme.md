# Qualis DME+

Qualis DME+ is hospice-focused software that manages the full workflow of durable medical equipment (DME) procurement and oversight. It centralizes contracting, pricing negotiation, vendor evaluation, invoice consolidation, and order-to-delivery tracking across multiple DME suppliers. The platform provides daily operational dashboards, financial reporting, and tools to identify DME cost drivers, along with clinical education resources to support hospice staff.
