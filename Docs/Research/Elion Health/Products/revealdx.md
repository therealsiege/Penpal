# Reveal-Dx

Reveal-Dx is a Seattle-based software company developing AI-enabled diagnostic tools to support non-invasive evaluation of pulmonary nodules. Its core product, RevealAI-Lung, uses radiomics and proprietary malignancy scoring (mSI) to help clinicians assess the likelihood that a lung nodule is cancerous, aiming to reduce unnecessary invasive procedures like biopsies and surgeries
