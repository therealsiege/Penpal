# HealthSnap

HealthSnap is virtual care management and remote patient monitoring platform includes remote monitoring of vital signs, predictive analytics for early intervention, and integration with existing healthcare systems. HealthSnap also facilitates patient engagement to decrease the need for frequent in-person visits.
