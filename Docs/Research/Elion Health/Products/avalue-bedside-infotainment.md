# Avalue  Bedside Infotainment

Avalue Bedside Infotainment is designed for hospitals and healthcare facilities to enhance patient engagement and comfort. These systems integrate touch-screen displays with access to TV, internet, video calls, and hospital information, as well as features like electronic health record (EHR) access for clinical staff.
