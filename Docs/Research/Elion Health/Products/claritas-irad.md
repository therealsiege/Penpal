# Claritas Healthtech iRAD

Claritas Healthtech iRAD is a cloud-based radiology image enhancement software that improves contrast, sharpness, and edge definition while reducing noise, without altering the original image or clinical workflow. It works across multiple imaging modalities, including X-ray, CT, MRI, and ultrasound, and is file-type agnostic, allowing it to integrate into existing PACS and radiology systems.
