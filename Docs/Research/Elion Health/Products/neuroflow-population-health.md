# Neuroflow Population Health

NeuroFlow's Population Health solution focuses on identifying and addressing behavioral health needs within a population. The platform collects data through assessments and screenings, stratifies risk, and engages individuals with tailored digital cognitive behavioral therapy (dCBT) and resources.
