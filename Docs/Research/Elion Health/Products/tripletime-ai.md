# Tripletime AI

Tripletime AI is an ambient clinical documentation tool designed for healthcare providers to speed up note-taking and reduce time spent on administrative tasks. It uses AI to capture voice notes, transcribe conversations, and extract structured data from documents, images, and speech during patient encounters.
