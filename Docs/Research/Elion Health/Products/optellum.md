# Optellum

Optellum offers the Virtual Nodule Clinic (VNC), the only FDA-cleared and Medicare-reimbursable AI solution for managing lung nodules to support earlier lung cancer diagnosis. The platform combines three key components: Patient Discovery AI (automated detection of lung nodules in radiology reports across hospital systems), workflow management dashboards for triage and tracking, and Lung Cancer Prediction AI, which uses CT imaging and radiomics to generate a cancer risk score validated against standard-of-care models.
