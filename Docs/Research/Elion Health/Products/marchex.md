# Marchex

Marchex Engage is a conversation intelligence platform used in healthcare to analyze patient phone and text interactions within patient intake, marketing attribution, and patient experience workflows. It converts unstructured conversation data into structured insights and post-call metrics such as appointment conversion and fallout, and links calls to campaigns for marketing attribution.
