# Ferrum Health

Ferrum Health offers an platform designed for securely deploying, managing, and scaling AI applications across various clinical service lines. The platform enables hospitals to integrate multiple AI applications for tasks like lung nodule detection, fracture identification, and liver lesion analysis.
