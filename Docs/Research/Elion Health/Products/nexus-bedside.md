# Nexus Bedside

Nexus Bedside is a mobile point-of-care nursing application designed to give clinicians real-time access to patient information and clinical workflows at the bedside. It integrates with hospital EHR systems to provide features such as patient charts, medication administration, vitals entry, care task lists, and clinical documentation.
