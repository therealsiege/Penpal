# SpendRule

SpendRule is a contract intelligence platform designed for healthcare organizations to validate purchased services invoices against contract terms before payment. It uses AI to extract pricing terms, rebates, tiered discounts, and other conditions from complex service agreements and performs line-by-line invoice matching to detect discrepancies prior to payment approval.
