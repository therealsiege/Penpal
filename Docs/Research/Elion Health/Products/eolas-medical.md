# Eolas Medical

Eolas Medical provides a customizable platform that turns healthcare organizations’ clinical knowledge—such as guidelines, protocols, and educational materials—into searchable, AI-assisted digital resources for frontline use. It enables healthcare teams to create centralized “spaces” where staff can instantly access up-to-date information at the point of care via desktop or mobile
