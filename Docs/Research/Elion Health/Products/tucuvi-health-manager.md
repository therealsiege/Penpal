# Tucuvi Health Manager

Tucuvi is an phone-based AI agent trained in evidence-based clinical protocols to automate clinical calls for care plans and chronic conditions, such as follow-ups for COPD, heart failure, and maternity. It guides conversations based on clinical guidelines to ensure meaningful and medically informed interactions, and is integrated with EHR systems for appropriate data access and writeback.
