# Ease Health

Ease Health is an AI-native platform for behavioral health organizations that combines EHR, CRM, and revenue cycle management in a single system. It supports referral intake, eligibility checks, admissions, clinical documentation, scheduling, and billing workflows across settings including outpatient, intensive outpatient, residential, detox, medication-assisted treatment, and opioid treatment programs.
