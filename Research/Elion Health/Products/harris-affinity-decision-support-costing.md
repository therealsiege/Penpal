# Harris Affinity Decision Support Costing

Harris ADS Costing (Affinity Decision Support Costing) is a decision support platform that integrates financial, clinical, and patient activity data to help hospitals understand the true cost of care. It provides detailed activity-based costing across labor, medications, and high-cost devices, enabling hospital administrators to track utilization, verify cost accuracy, and analyze patient-level expenses.
