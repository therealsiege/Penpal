# Care.ai

Care.ai is an AI-powered solution that allows remote nurses to assist bedside teams by managing non-physical care tasks such as documentation, education, routine monitoring, and compliance reporting. It utilizes in-room sensors and autonomous AI to reduce the burden on on-site nurses, enabling them to focus on hands-on patient care.
