# CheckinAsyst

CheckinAsyst is a digital patient intake and payment solution that streamlines front-office workflows for healthcare practices. It offers automated eligibility and benefit verification, self-service and point-of-service payments (including card, card-on-file, check, and online), payment plan options, and customizable SMS/email reminders.
