# LifeLinkr

LifeLinkr is a cloud-based IVF clinic management software designed for fertility clinics and individual doctors. It provides tools for patient management, appointment scheduling, billing, inventory and pharmacy tracking, lab reporting, KPI dashboards, and in-built CRM for communication.
