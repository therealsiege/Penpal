# Ksana Vira

Ksana Vira is a mobile-based mental health platform that supports clinicians and patients with continuous behavioral monitoring and personalized intervention. Using smartphone sensor data and daily self-reports, Vira’s AI clinical assistant provides practitioners with real-time insights into mood, habits, and patient adherence between sessions.
