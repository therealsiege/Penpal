# Pulsara

Pulsara's platform enables EMS and hospital staff to communicate event-based patient information seamlessly, including one-tap team notifications, image sharing, audio/video calls, and ETA updates, directly through the Pulsara app. This integration allows EMS personnel to start a case, share critical details like ECGs or field images, and receive immediate feedback upon case closure, effectively replacing traditional radio reports and connecting EMS as an integral part of every patient case.
