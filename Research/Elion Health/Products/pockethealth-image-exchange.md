# PocketHealth Image Exchange

PocketHealth Image Exchange is a cloud-based medical imaging interoperability platform that automates the secure exchange of diagnostic images across healthcare organizations. It centralizes inbound and outbound image-sharing workflows, replacing CDs and manual processes with automated ingestion, patient matching, and data normalization.
