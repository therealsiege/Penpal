# Carta Healthcare

Atlas by Carta Healthcare uses AI to automate the abstraction of clinical data from medical records for registries. This tool combines AI with human expertise to streamline data extraction, allowing healthcare providers to focus more on patient care. Atlas supports a variety of registries and ensures data quality through a verification process.
