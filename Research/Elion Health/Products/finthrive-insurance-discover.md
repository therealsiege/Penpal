# FinThrive Insurance Discover

FinThrive Insurance Discover is a solution that identifies unknown or inactive third-party insurance coverage to support revenue recovery in healthcare organizations. It uses automated data mining and analytics to scan historical accounts and uncover coverage that was missed at the time of service.
