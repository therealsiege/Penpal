# HorusCDI

HorusCDI is a GenAI-powered clinical documentation integrity (CDI) platform that automates the review of clinical documentation and streamlines CDI and billing audit workflows. It identifies documentation gaps, suggests improvements, generates AI-driven CDI queries, and provides real-time tracking of physician responses.
