# Avasure

AvaSure provides a virtual care platform designed to enhance patient safety and reduce staffing costs in healthcare settings. The platform features AI-powered virtual sitting and virtual nursing, allowing clinicians to monitor multiple patients simultaneously and respond to alerts for falls and other high-risk events.
