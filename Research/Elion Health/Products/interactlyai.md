# Interactly.ai

Interactly is a healthcare-focused AI automation platform that provides “AI teammates” to manage front- and back-office workflows such as scheduling, lab follow-up, prescription refills, and prior authorization paperwork. It supports multimodal communication across voice, SMS, chat, email, and fax, with integrations into major EHRs (Epic, Cerner, Athena, NextGen) and call center systems.
