# Evolv Health

Evolv Health provides transitional care management services and technology for hospitals, health plans, MSOs, ACOs, and skilled nursing facilities. Their offering combines a platform called Sensys with dedicated care management teams to centralize post-acute care operations, automate discharge planning workflows, manage patient length of stay, and enhance collaboration with community providers
