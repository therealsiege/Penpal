# Yosi Health

Yosi Health is a patient intake and engagement platform used in pre-visit and front-desk workflows to provide contactless registration and check-in. It enables remote pre-arrival registration where patients complete customized intake forms, upload insurance and ID photos, and e-sign documents with completed PDFs added to the patient record; it also provides self-scheduling, two-way text messaging, telehealth links, and card-on-file payment collection.
