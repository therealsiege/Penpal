# Protecto Data Privacy Vault

Protecto's Data Privacy Vault helps organizations manage and secure sensitive data like Personally Identifiable Information (PII) and Protected Health Information (PHI) by using tokenization and masking techniques. The vault ensures data remains usable for analytics and AI without exposing its original form.
