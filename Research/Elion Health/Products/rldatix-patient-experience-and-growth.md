# RLDatix Patient Experience & Growth

RLDatix offers Patient Experience & Growth section that allows healthcare organizations to collect feedback (surveys like HCAHPS, CAHPS, PROs, NPS), analyze satisfaction trends, benchmark performance vs peers or internal history, target patient acquisition/marketing campaigns using predictive analytics, and manage reputational risk.
