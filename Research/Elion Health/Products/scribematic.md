# Matic

Matic is an embedded platform that sits inside EHRs and health‑tech platforms to support pre-bill and claims-ready documentation in the care-to-collection workflow. It captures clinical encounters, assigns CPT/ICD/HCC codes, and generates longitudinal summaries.
