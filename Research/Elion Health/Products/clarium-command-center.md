# Clarium Command Center

[Contact](https://www.clariumhealth.com/demo?utm_campaign=19669672-Elion&utm_source=elion-profile)

Clarium Health is an AI-powered data platform designed to optimize healthcare supply chains by integrating systems like the ERP, EHR, and WMS. It provides real-time collaboration between hospitals and suppliers. It includes disruption monitoring, substitute management, procedure card optimization, demand planning, inventory optimization, and an AI assistant.
