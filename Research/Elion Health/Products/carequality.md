# Carequality

Carequality is a vendor-neutral, national interoperability framework that fits in health information exchange workflows to enable query-based and directed exchange of clinical records. It provides a common set of legal, technical, and policy rules, supports query/retrieve of clinical documents, and enables implementers to connect without negotiating one-off agreements.
