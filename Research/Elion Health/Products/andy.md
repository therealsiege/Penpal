# Andy

Andy is an AI-powered ambient scribe tool designed to help clinicians complete OASIS assessments quickly and accurately, integrating directly into leading EHR systems. It captures patient data during home health visits, improves accuracy by identifying conditions often missed by clinicians, and reduces documentation time.
