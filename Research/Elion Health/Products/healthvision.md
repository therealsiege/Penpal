# HealthVision

HealthVision, developed by the Health Data Analytics Institute (HDAI), is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to support personalized care and optimize clinical workflows. The platform aggregates data from multiple sources and provides real-time insights, helping clinicians make data-driven decisions.
