# RLDatix Risk Management

RLDatix Risk Management is designed to enhance patient safety and manage risk in healthcare settings, and provides tools for incident reporting, event analysis, risk register management, and clinical surveillance. The software allows organizations to capture and analyze data from adverse events, feedback, and claims, enabling them to identify high-risk areas and implement mitigation strategies.
