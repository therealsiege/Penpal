# Suki Assistant

Suki Assistant is an AI-powered clinical assistant designed to help healthcare providers with tasks such as documentation, coding, patient summaries, and clinical Q&A. The assistant includes features like pre-charting, summarizing lab results, and answering both patient-specific and general medical questions, such as drug interactions.
