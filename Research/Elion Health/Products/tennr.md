# Tennr

[Contact](https://www.tennr.com/book-a-demo)

Tennr is an automation platform designed for healthcare operations, using its proprietary RaeLM™ machine learning model to process medical documents and optimize administrative workflows. The platform automates tasks such as processing referrals, patient intake, eligibility checks, and prior authorizations by classifying and extracting data from incoming documents like faxes
