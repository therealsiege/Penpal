# HeartFlow

HeartFlow offers a non-invasive diagnostic tool for coronary artery disease (CAD) called the HeartFlow FFRCT Analysis. This technology utilizes data from coronary computed tomography angiography (CCTA) scans to create a personalized, color-coded 3D model of a patient's coronary arteries.
