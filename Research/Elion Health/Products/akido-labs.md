# Akido Labs

Akido Labs is a healthcare technology company offering AI-driven solutions aimed at expanding access to high-quality, in-person care. Its platform, Scope AI, is designed to dramatically increase healthcare system capacity—enabling faster appointment availability, eliminating copays, and supporting services like house calls.
