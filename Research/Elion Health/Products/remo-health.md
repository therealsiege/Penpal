# Remo Health

Remo Health provides virtual dementia care through a platform that combines clinical services, educational resources, and community support. Users can access remote diagnosis and management from dementia specialists, explore expert-reviewed articles and tools for care planning, and engage with a 24/7 online caregiver community.
