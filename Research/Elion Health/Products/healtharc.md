# HealthArc

HealthArc provides a comprehensive remote patient monitoring (RPM) platform that supports chronic care and transitional care management. The platform integrates with cellular and Bluetooth medical devices for tracking vital signs like blood pressure and glucose levels. HealthArc offers AI-powered tools for clinical workflow automation, helping care teams prioritize high-risk patients and optimize care.
