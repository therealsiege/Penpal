# Zingage

Zingage is an AI-driven operations platform for home care agencies. Its main modules—Operator and Perform—automate scheduling, staffing, intake, back-office tasks, compliance, and caregiver engagement. Operator handles 24/7 operations like filling call-outs, managing urgent changes, and documentation, while Perform helps reduce turnover by incentivizing and engaging caregivers.
