# Bamboo Bridge

Bamboo Bridge is a behavioral-health care-coordination solution that combines real-time patient intelligence with tech-enabled care navigators to identify, engage, and connect high-need individuals to appropriate behavioral health services. It integrates directly into EHR workflows using ADT notifications, enabling providers to initiate navigation without switching systems, while offering real-time visibility into patient transitions.
