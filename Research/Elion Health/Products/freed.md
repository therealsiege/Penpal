# Freed

Freed is an AI-powered medical scribe tool that creates clinical documentation from recorded patient encounters across specialties such as family medicine, psychiatry, internal medicine, pediatrics, and mental health. Clinicians can use any device to record in-person or telehealth visits, after which Freed generates structured notes that can be reviewed, edited, and pasted into any EHR.
