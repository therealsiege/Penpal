# Commure RCM

[Contact](https://www.commure.com/contact?utm_source=elion&utm_medium=referral&utm_campaign=elion&utm_content=rcm)

Commure RCM is a technology-enabled revenue cycle management solution that combines software and services to support billing, claims processing, and payment collection for mid-market and enterprise healthcare providers. It integrates with over 50 EHRs via API, HL7, and Selenium, pulling clinical data to automate key revenue cycle tasks.
