# MDClarity Revfind

RevFind, MD Clarity's post-remittance underpayment and denial-management platform, reads contracts and remittances to calculate allowed amounts, surface true variances, assemble appeal packets, and track recoveries.It also supports dashboards and payer-performance analytics that help payment posting, revenue integrity, and managed care teams prioritize appeals, model rate changes, and report recoveries.
