# Navina

Navina is an AI-powered platform designed to assist healthcare providers by transforming complex patient data into actionable insights at the point of care. It integrates information from various sources, including electronic health records (EHRs), health information exchanges (HIEs), and insurance claims, to create a comprehensive patient profile.
