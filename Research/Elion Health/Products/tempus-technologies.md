# Tempus Technologies

Tempus Technologies specializes in payment-processing solutions, offering tools for point-of-sale (POS), mobile, desktop, and e-commerce transactions. Their solutions support payment gateways with Point-to-Point Encryption (P2PE) 3.0, which ensures secure, end-to-end encryption and tokenization across different devices and platforms.
