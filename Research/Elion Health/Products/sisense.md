# Sisense

Sisense is an analytics and embedded business intelligence platform that sits in application and operational analytics workflows to model, visualize, and deliver data experiences. It provides data connectivity and modeling, runs large-scale in-memory analytics, and supports conversational queries, automated narratives, forecasting, and anomaly detection.
