# Cliniko

Cliniko is a cloud-based practice management software designed for healthcare professionals, particularly in allied health. It provides features such as appointment scheduling, medical record management, billing, and telehealth. Cliniko ensures the security of patient data with end-to-end encryption both in transit and at rest, and it offers daily backups and secure storage in regional data centers based on user location
