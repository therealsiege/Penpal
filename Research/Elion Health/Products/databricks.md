# Databricks

Databricks offers a comprehensive data and AI platform tailored for healthcare and life sciences, known as the Databricks Lakehouse for Healthcare. This platform enables organizations to unify diverse healthcare data, such as electronic health records (EHRs), genomic data, and real-world evidence.
