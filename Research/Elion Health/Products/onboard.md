# Onboard

Onboard AI is a healthcare-focused AI governance and compliance platform that helps organizations evaluate, validate, and monitor AI tools before and after deployment. It provides automated workflows for vendor assessments, risk evaluation, approval tracking, and ongoing model performance monitoring, all built around established frameworks such as CHAI and NIST. The system enables compliance, legal, IT, and clinical teams to collaborate through a single centralized review process—ensuring AI systems meet safety, privacy, and performance standards prior to integration into clinical workflows.
