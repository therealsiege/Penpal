# Syllable Agentic Platform

Syllable is an agentic platform for building and deploying AI agents tailored for enterprise call centers. The system includes an Agent Builder with a prompt editor supporting major LLMs, pre-built templates, and multilingual capabilities across nine languages. Syllable’s agents can handle calls over SIP, PSTN, or WebRTC, with all interactions recorded and transcribed.
