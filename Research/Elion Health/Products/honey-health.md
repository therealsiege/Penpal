# Honey Health

Honey Health offers an AI-driven back-office automation platform for healthcare organizations. It provides “AI staff” (agents) that operate inside existing EHRs to handle workflows such as data fetching/interoperability, pre-chart and note preparation, orders and refills, fax triage and extraction, and prior authorizations.
