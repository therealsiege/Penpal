# Opmed

Opmed is an AI-powered platform designed to optimize scheduling and resource allocation in healthcare settings, with a focus on operating rooms. The platform improves OR performance by analyzing complex variables such as surgeon preferences, case durations, and equipment availability to generate schedules. Its capabilities include daily scheduling, mid-term and long-term planning, real-time predictive analytics, and adaptive block management.
