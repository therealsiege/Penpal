# Clearway Health

Clearway Health provides specialty pharmacy services tailored to hospitals and health systems, focusing on program design, medication access, network expansion, and embedded clinical staff. Originating from Boston Medical Center Health System, the organization helps partners develop or enhance in-house specialty pharmacies, aiming to improve medication adherence, access for complex patients, and financial sustainability. Services include hands-on operational support, 340B program optimization, and integration with care teams to reduce barriers for vulnerable populations.
