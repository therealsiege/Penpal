# Roger

Roger is a home-health documentation assistant that integrates with agency EMRs to automate OASIS and routine visit charting at the point of care. It records clinician–patient conversations during in-home visits, provides real-time prompts for missing documentation, and generates completed charts that sync to the EHR in near real time.
