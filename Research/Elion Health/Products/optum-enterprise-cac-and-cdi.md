# Optum Enterprise CAC and CDI 3D

Optum Enterprise CAC (Computer-Assisted Coding) and CDI 3D is an AI-powered integrated platform designed to enhance clinical documentation and coding accuracy through the use of Optum’s Clinical Language Intelligence (CLI). The system reviews cases, identifying opportunities for documentation improvement and coding specificity, and automating workflows to reduce administrative burden.
