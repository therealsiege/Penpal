# Glytec

Glytec is a cloud-based platform designed to assist healthcare providers in managing insulin therapy across various care settings. It features Glucommander™, an FDA-cleared insulin dosing decision support tool that analyzes real-time and historical patient data to deliver personalized dosing recommendations.
