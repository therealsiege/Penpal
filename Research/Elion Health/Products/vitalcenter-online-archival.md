# VitalCenter Online Archival

VitalCenter Online Archival is a cloud-based solution that enables healthcare organizations to access data from legacy systems through a unified interface. It supports various electronic medical record (EMR) source systems, including Allscripts, MEDITECH, and Epic, facilitating the management of both clinical and revenue cycle data.
