# OnCall AI

Contact

OnCall AI is a mobile-first clinical documentation and billing tool that captures physician-patient phone conversations and automatically generates billable medical notes. It uses ambient voice AI to transcribe calls and produce documentation that includes structured elements like ICD-10 and CPT codes.
