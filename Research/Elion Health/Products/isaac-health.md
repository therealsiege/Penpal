# Isaac Health

Isaac Health is a virtual and hybrid care provider focused on neurocognitive and dementia care, offering health systems on-demand access to neurologists, neuropsychologists, psychiatrists, cognitive therapists, and care navigators. The platform supports comprehensive evaluation, diagnosis, treatment planning, and ongoing management for patients with memory and cognitive concerns, including caregiver education and wraparound services.
