# SmarterDx SmarterDenials

SmarterDenials by SmarterDx is an AI-powered tool designed to help hospitals efficiently appeal denied insurance claims by analyzing patient records and generating evidence-backed appeal letters. The system extracts key data from denial documents, identifies discrepancies between the denial and clinical documentation, and provides supporting evidence with coding references. It automates the creation of comprehensive appeal letters within minutes, allowing hospital staff to review, edit, and finalize them before submission.
