# Teladoc Virtual Sitting

Teladoc Health's Virtual Sitting solution enables a single remote staff member to monitor multiple patients through one-way video channels, allowing them to redirect patients or alert bedside staff when intervention is needed. This approach helps reduce the reliance on high-cost, typically unreimbursed sitter positions, thereby lowering staffing costs.
