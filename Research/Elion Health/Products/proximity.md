# Proximity

Proximity is a SaaS platform that centralizes and automates 340B revenue operations for hospitals and health centers in post‑adjudication and revenue recovery workflows. It ingests claims, eligibility, replenishment, wholesaler/TPA data, normalizes into a unified 340B dataset, detects missed savings, eligibility gaps and compliance risks with real‑time analytics and alerts, and provides a rules engine with no‑code workflows to remediate/reprocess claims.
