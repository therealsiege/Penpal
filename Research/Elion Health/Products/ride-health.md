# Ride Health

Ride Health offers a transportation coordination platform designed to assist healthcare organizations and transportation providers in managing transportation benefits and enhancing access to care for complex populations. The platform assesses each patient's specific needs and preferences to coordinate appropriate transportation options, including public transit, rideshare, taxi, and non-emergency medical transportation services.
