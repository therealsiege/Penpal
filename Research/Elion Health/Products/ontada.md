# Ontada

Ontada provides oncology-focused real-world data (RWD), research services, and point-of-care technologies. It supports life sciences companies and oncology providers by offering access to data from over 2.4 million cancer patients and 2,400 oncology providers, largely sourced from its proprietary EHR, iKnowMed, used within The US Oncology Network.
