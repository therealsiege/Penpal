# Predoc

Predoc is a health information management platform that automates the retrieval, structuring, and analysis of patient medical records across U.S. healthcare providers. It retrieves complete health records quickly through automated digital requests, indexes unstructured data from PDFs and other sources, and uses clinically trained AI to highlight the most relevant patient information for care delivery or research.
