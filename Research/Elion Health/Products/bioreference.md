# BioReference

BioReference Laboratories provides a wide array of diagnostic testing services. They specialize in areas such as oncology, urology, and women’s health. They offer routine clinical tests, complex genomic testing, and pathology services, with secure access to test orders and results through a HIPAA-compliant provider portal and a patient portal for managing lab reports and billing.
