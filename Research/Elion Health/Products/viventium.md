# viventium

Viventium is a cloud-based human capital management (HCM) platform tailored for post-acute care and home-based healthcare providers. It offers payroll, HR, time and attendance, talent acquisition, benefits administration, and business intelligence tools, all designed to meet the compliance and workforce needs of healthcare organizations.
