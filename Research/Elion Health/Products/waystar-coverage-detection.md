# Waystar Coverage Detection

Waystar Coverage Detection is a revenue cycle tool that automatically identifies active, billable insurance coverage that may have been missed during patient registration or billing. It integrates with eligibility and claims workflows to detect coverage at various stages of the revenue cycle—from pre-service to denial follow-up—helping reduce write-offs and accelerate reimbursement.
