# PPMP Better Clinics

Better Clinics is an online practice management software designed for allied health professionals, including physiotherapists, chiropractors, massage therapists, and personal trainers. It offers tools for appointment scheduling, clinical notes, invoicing, and client management, all accessible via a secure, cloud-based platform.
