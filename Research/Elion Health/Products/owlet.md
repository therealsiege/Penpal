# Owlet

Owlet’s BabySat® is a home-monitoring system for infants (1-18 months old, 6-30 lbs) who may have chronic or acute conditions. It uses a fabric sock with a wireless sensor placed on the baby’s foot to measure pulse rate and oxygen saturation (SpO₂) in real time, transmits these readings to a base station and a mobile app, and alerts caregivers if values fall outside physician-set ranges.
