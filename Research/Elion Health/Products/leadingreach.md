# LeadingReach

LeadingReach is a referral management and network coordination platform that digitally connects providers across healthcare networks to manage inbound and outbound referrals. The system supports provider matching based on network participation and care setting, tracks referral status and appointment conversion in real time, and enables secure messaging and file sharing between referring and receiving offices.
