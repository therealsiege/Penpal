# LiveData PeriOp Manager

Contact

LiveData PeriOp Manager is a surgical operations platform designed to optimize perioperative workflows and increase operating room efficiency in hospitals and surgical centers. It integrates with EHRs and hospital systems to automate case scheduling, monitor patient flow, manage pre-op and intra-op milestones, and improve communication across perioperative teams.
