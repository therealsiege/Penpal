# Lilee

[Contact](https://calendly.com/lilee-ai/discovery-call-lilee-ai)

Lilee is an agentic automation platform built for utilization management (UM) and health plan operations, enabling AI agents to validate requests, analyze documents, and execute end-to-end administrative workflows. It connects directly to existing systems such as Epic, Facets, and other core admin platforms through FHIR, HL7, and X12 bridges, without requiring replatforming. Lilee’s Policy Vault codifies payer rules with versioning and audit trails, and its agents perform real-time review of PDFs, faxes, and EDI attachments with staff handling only a small set of exceptions.
