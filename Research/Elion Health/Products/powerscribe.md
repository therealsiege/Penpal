# PowerScribe

PowerScribe is a radiology reporting platform designed to streamline diagnostic imaging workflows through advanced speech recognition and clinical language understanding. It allows radiologists to dictate reports using voice commands, insert structured data, and apply standardized templates and guidelines for consistent documentation.
