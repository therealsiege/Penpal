# Barti

Barti is a cloud-based electronic health record (EHR) platform built specifically for eye care practices, integrating clinical documentation, scheduling, billing, inventory, optical ordering, and patient communication into a single system. It includes an AI-powered scribe for exam documentation, patient intake forms, text and voice messaging, and tools for managing contact lens and optical sales.
