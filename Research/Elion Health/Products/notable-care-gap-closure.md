# Notable Care Gap Closure

Notable's Care Gap Closure uses AI to analyze patient records via direct EHR and other data source integrations to identify care gaps. Patients with open care gaps are engaged through personalized texts and emails to schedule needed care, and provider notes are updated ahead of visits to support care gap closure. This improves quality metrics while reducing manual workflows for healthcare staff through automation.
