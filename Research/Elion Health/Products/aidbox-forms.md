# Aidbox Forms

[Contact](https://calendar.app.google/aTLDjXWJZkPknGn1A)

Aidbox Forms offers a form builder that allows providers to create, manage, and use FHIR-compliant forms without requiring coding. Features include a drag-and-drop interface, customization options, and integration capabilities with existing health IT systems.
