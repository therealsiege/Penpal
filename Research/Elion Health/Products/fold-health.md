# Fold Health

Fold Health is an outcomes-focused primary care platform used within value-based and direct primary care workflows to automate administrative and care-management tasks. It includes a Clin-Ops Automation Engine that automates longitudinal care journeys and repetitive administrative workflows, and a Care Team Enablement Suite that provides scheduling, tasking, and interdisciplinary collaboration tools.
