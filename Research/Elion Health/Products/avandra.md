# Avandra

Avandra Imaging provides a data platform for healthcare and research, focusing on the aggregation and management of medical imaging and multimodal data. Their platform offers access to anonymized datasets enhanced with tokenization techniques, facilitating integration with third-party data to support medical research and innovation.
