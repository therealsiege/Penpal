# Credo

Credo is a SaaS platform used in value-based care workflows to retrieve longitudinal medical records and produce pre-encounter clinical histories for providers, MSOs, ACOs, and health plans. It provides automated medical record retrieval and consolidation, generates pre-encounter clinical summaries, and supports performance analytics and lifecycle tracking for RAF and HCC activity.
