# Medinsight.ai

Medinsight.ai is an AI-powered incident reporting and patient safety platform that leverages generative AI to automatically detect, classify, and analyze adverse events and near misses in clinical environments. The system offers "zero-touch" incident reporting—minimizing manual input—to increase reporting rates, reduce staff workload, and identify safety trends in real time.
