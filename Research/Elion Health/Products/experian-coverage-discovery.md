# Experian Health Coverage Discovery

Experian Health’s Coverage Discovery is an automated insurance discovery tool that identifies previously unknown billable insurance coverage for patients listed as self-pay. It searches for Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial plans by cross-referencing proprietary databases that include employer data, historical search records, and demographic details.
