# Iguana Integration Engine

[Contact](https://www.interfaceware.com/contact)

Interfaceware’s primary product is the
Iguana Integration Engine
, which supports complex data integration between various healthcare systems, databases, and devices. Iguana is capable of handling multiple data formats, including HL7, FHIR, X12, and JSON, and utilizes a wide range of transfer protocols such as HTTP/S, TCP/IP, and FTP.
