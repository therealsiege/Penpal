# CancerIQ Reach

CancerIQ Reach is a campaign-based marketing outreach solution designed to help healthcare providers identify individuals in their communities at elevated risk for cancer and motivate them to engage in preventive care. The platform offers population segmentation tools and marketing toolkits that facilitate targeted outreach through various channels, such as patient portals and email.
