# Global Payments

Global Payments offers integrated payment solutions tailored for the healthcare industry, enabling healthcare providers—from solo practices to large facilities—to accept payments seamlessly in person, online, or via mobile. Their features include support for credit/debit cards (including FSA/HSA), mobile wallets, card-on-file storage, recurring billing, payment links via text or email, and secure online portals.
