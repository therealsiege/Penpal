# Valerie Health

Valerie Health is an AI-driven healthcare platform specializing in referral management. It automates key processes, such as extracting and verifying referral data, contacting referring providers, and following up on missing information. The platform also facilitates patient engagement by scheduling appointments, coordinating intake forms, and tracking upcoming visits, enhancing referral workflow efficiency for healthcare providers.
