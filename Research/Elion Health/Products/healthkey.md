# HealthKey

HealthKey is an AI-enabled clinical summarization platform that aggregates data from multiple sources, extracts relevant information, and summarizes it, while also citing the source material for any conclusions. Its main focus is chart prep, with summary reports that can be synced with provider schedules and emailed to them ahead of time.
