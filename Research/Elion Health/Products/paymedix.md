# PayMedix

[Contact](https://paymedix.com/contact/)

PayMedix is a healthcare payment platform designed to help providers receive full payment for in-network services, regardless of a patient's ability to pay. It consolidates billing by issuing a single monthly statement to patients, covering all services from multiple providers. PayMedix also handles patient collections, removing financial risks for providers and eliminating the need for them to pursue unpaid balances.
