# Weave

Weave is an all-in-one patient communication, scheduling, and payments platform used at the front desk and in revenue workflows of small and medium-sized healthcare practices such as medical, dental, optometry, physical therapy, and specialty clinics. It provides a VoIP phone system with call pop, voicemail transcription, a message-drafting assistant, and automated call categorization, and supports two-way texting, missed-call texts, appointment reminders, and online scheduling.
