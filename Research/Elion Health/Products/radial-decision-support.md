# Radial Decision Support

Radial Decision Support is a decision-support platform used in transitions-of-care and value-based care workflows to prioritize patients for intervention and guide placement, readmission prevention, and end-of-life planning. It provides real-time insights and longitudinal patient histories, generates automated clinical justifications and prioritization flags, and supports screening for social needs, readmission risk assessment, and post-acute placement decisions.
