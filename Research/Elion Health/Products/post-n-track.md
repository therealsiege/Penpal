# Post-n-Track

Post-n-Track is a managed data-exchange service geared to healthcare providers and payers, supporting electronic healthcare transactions (EDI) such as claims submission, eligibility, claim status and other files, via a secure platform that emphasizes data governance and minimal retention of PHI/PII.
