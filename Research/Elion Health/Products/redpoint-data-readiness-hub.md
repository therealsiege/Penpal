# Redpoint Data Readiness Hub

Redpoint Data Readiness Hub is a data management platform that prepares customer data for AI and customer experience initiatives by cleansing, standardizing, and unifying records into accurate “Golden Records.” It automates data quality checks, applies deterministic and probabilistic identity resolution, and continuously updates unified profiles with real-time changes.
