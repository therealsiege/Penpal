# LookDeep Health

LookDeep Health offers AI-powered virtual care solutions like Virtual Sitting, Virtual Nursing, and Virtual Medicine, using computer vision and AI (VisionAI) to monitor patient movements and enhance safety. Its platform supports continuous patient observation, reduces nurse workload, and enables remote admissions and discharges.
