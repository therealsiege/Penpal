# Rovicare

Rovicare is a transitional care management platform that helps healthcare organizations automate care coordination and discharge processes. It connects hospitals, skilled nursing facilities, behavioral health providers, outpatient clinics, and social care services to improve transitions, track referrals, and manage patient placements electronically.
