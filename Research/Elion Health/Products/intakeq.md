# intakeQ / practiceQ

IntakeQ (forms-only) and PracticeQ (practice management) are web-based tools used by healthcare practices for patient intake and ongoing administrative workflows. IntakeQ collects patient information via customizable online intake questionnaires that accept file attachments and e-signatures.
