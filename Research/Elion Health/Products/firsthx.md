# FirstHx

FirstHx is a cloud-based patient intake application used in the pre-visit workflow to capture structured medical histories before clinical encounters. It provides adaptive, AI-guided medical interviews and specialty modules (EyeHx, HeartHx, PedsHx) that collect history of present illness, past medical history, medications, allergies, social determinants of health, and calculator/risk-score data.
