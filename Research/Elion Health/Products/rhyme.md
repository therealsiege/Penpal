# Rhyme

Rhyme, formerly PriorAuthNow, is a platform that unites payers and providers to streamline the prior authorization process. It integrates with EHR systems for data exchange, supports communication methods including faxes, and uses intelligent workflows to optimize authorization success rates. The platform provides real-time status updates and facilitates collaboration among healthcare teams through built-in communication tools.
