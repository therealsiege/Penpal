# Stedi Claims

[Contact](https://www.stedi.com/contact)

Stedi Claims is an API-based platform for automating healthcare claim workflows, enabling providers and developers to submit, track, and reconcile claims electronically. Stedi integrates with thousands of payers and delivers claim data in JSON for easy system interoperability and monitoring across payer and provider systems.
