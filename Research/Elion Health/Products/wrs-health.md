# WRS Health

WRS Health provides a cloud-based EHR and practice management platform tailored to the specific needs of medical practices across various specialties, including internal medicine, psychiatry, cardiology, and more. It offers tools for clinical charting, patient scheduling, telehealth, and revenue cycle management, with specialty-specific templates and workflows designed to reduce administrative burdens.
