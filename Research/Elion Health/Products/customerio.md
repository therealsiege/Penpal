# Customer.io

Customer.io is a customer engagement platform used in healthcare workflows to support patient engagement, onboarding, appointment reminders, and retention. It provides a visual Journeys workflow builder to orchestrate multi‑step automations and supports channel delivery across email, SMS/MMS (including WhatsApp), push, and in‑app messages.
