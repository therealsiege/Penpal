# Eko Health Sensora

Eko Health’s SENSORA® platform is an FDA-cleared AI system that integrates with Eko digital stethoscopes to analyze heart sounds and ECG data during routine physical exams. With a 15-second recording, it assesses risk for structural heart murmurs, low ejection fraction, and atrial fibrillation (with CORE 500™), supporting early identification of valvular heart disease and systolic dysfunction in primary care.
