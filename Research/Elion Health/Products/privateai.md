# PrivateAI

Private AI offers solutions that identify, redact, and replace personally identifiable information (PII) across various data formats, including text, audio, and images. Their technology supports over 50 PII entities in more than 50 languages, enabling organizations to process data while maintaining privacy and complying with regulations such as GDPR, HIPAA, and PCI DSS.
