# Spectral AI Deepview

Spectral AI's DeepView Wound Imaging System is a non-invasive diagnostic tool that employs multispectral imaging and artificial intelligence to provide immediate predictions on wound healing potential. It assists clinicians in making informed treatment decisions for acute and chronic wounds, including burns and diabetic foot ulcers, by offering objective assessments that are not visible to the human eye.
