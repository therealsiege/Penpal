# BillionToOne

BillionToOne is a molecular diagnostics company that develops and runs single-molecule blood tests using its proprietary Quantitative Counting Templates (QCT) technology. Its products include non-invasive prenatal testing for fetal risk assessment and liquid biopsy tests for cancer therapy selection and treatment response monitoring, all performed from a single blood draw.
