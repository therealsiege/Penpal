# Lifelink Systems

Lifelink Systems provides a conversational AI platform that supports patient-facing workflows across the care continuum, including the digital front door, pre-visit, in-visit, and post-visit phases. It provides configurable text-based mobile chatbots and conversational flows that find and schedule appointments and send reminders, collect clinical intake and consent, and coordinate discharges and referrals.
