# Empathia AI

Empathia AI is a medical documentation and ambient scribe tool that automatically transcribes patient encounters and generates structured medical charts, such as SOAP notes, which can be copied to or integrated with EHRs. Its core features include automatic transcription, chart generation, multi-language support, and the ability to edit the generated notes before finalizing them.
