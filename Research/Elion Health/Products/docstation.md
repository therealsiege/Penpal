# DocStation

DocStation is a unified pharmacy platform combining clinical, administrative, billing, and documentation tools tailored for pharmacists. It helps pharmacies launch and scale clinical services (e.g. MTM, vaccines, DME) by handling insurance eligibility, claim submission, payer credentialing, scheduling, documentation, population health tracking, and interoperability with dispensing systems.
