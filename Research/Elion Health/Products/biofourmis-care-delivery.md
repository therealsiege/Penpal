# Biofourmis Care Delivery

Biofourmis provides technology-enabled care delivery solutions through a cloud-based platform that integrates continuous and episodic data collection, AI-driven analytics, and in-home service coordination. Their offerings include remote patient management, hospital-at-home programs, and support for transitions in care.
