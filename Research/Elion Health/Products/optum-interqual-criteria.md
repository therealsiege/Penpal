# Optum InterQual Criteria

Optum InterQual Criteria is an evidence-based clinical criteria data product that provides standardized medical necessity and level-of-care criteria across the continuum of care, including medical, behavioral health, and ambulatory services. It is used by utilization management, care management, and clinical review teams to assess appropriateness of admissions, continued stays, procedures, and treatment settings.
