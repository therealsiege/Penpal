# ModelOp

ModelOp is an enterprise AI lifecycle management and governance platform designed to operationalize responsible AI at scale across machine learning, generative AI, agentic AI, and third-party systems. It provides a centralized AI system of record that inventories all AI assets, automates policy enforcement, and manages model risk, documentation, and performance monitoring throughout the lifecycle.
