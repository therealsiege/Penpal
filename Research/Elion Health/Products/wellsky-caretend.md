# Wellsky CareTend

CareTend is a specialty pharmacy software solution designed to support complex workflows including specialty drug dispensing, infusion/home-care services, payer interactions, claims processing, delivery management, and clinical engagement. It offers features such as inventory and order management, claims automation, patient engagement modules, e-prescribing integrations, APIs for interoperability, and analytics for performance monitoring.
