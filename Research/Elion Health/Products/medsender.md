# Medsender

Medsender is a healthcare communication platform that enables secure faxing, document management, and AI-powered data extraction for medical practices, hospitals, and pharmacies. It allows users to send and receive faxes digitally, extract structured data from documents using AI, and integrate with electronic health records (EHRs) to automate administrative workflows.
