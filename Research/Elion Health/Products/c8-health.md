# C8 Health

C8 Health offers a clinical resource management platform that centralizes and disseminates clinical guidelines, knowledge bases, and protocols for hospitals. It provides features like AI-powered search, workflow integration, collaboration tools, and data analytics to help medical teams access, update, and share critical information efficiently.
