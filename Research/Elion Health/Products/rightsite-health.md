# Rightsite Health

RightSite Health collaborates with Emergency Medical Services (EMS) to address the frequent use of 911 for non-emergent situations. By integrating telehealth consultations with ER doctors and patient navigators during dispatch and on-scene responses, they guide patients to appropriate care settings, reducing unnecessary emergency room visits.
