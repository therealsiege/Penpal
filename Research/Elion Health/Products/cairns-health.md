# Cairns Health

Cairns Health offers a voice-driven, AI-powered platform for continuous patient engagement and ambient health monitoring, particularly for aging adults and patients in home or senior care settings. It combines intelligent sensing, voice interaction, medication reminders, behavioral health check-ins, and remote care coordination into a single system.
