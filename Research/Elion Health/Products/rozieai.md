# RozieAI

RozieAI provides AI-powered solutions designed to improve customer service in healthcare and other service-oriented industries. Its primary platform, Rozie Care, enables organizations to handle high volumes of personalized interactions by leveraging AI to access institutional knowledge and offer guided workflows.
