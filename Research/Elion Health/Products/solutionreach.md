# Solutionreach

Solutionreach is a patient engagement and patient relationship management platform used in healthcare front-office workflows for appointment and patient communication. It provides automated appointment reminders via text, email, and voice, supports two-way consent-based texting for confirmations, rescheduling, image collection, and payment requests, and handles digital intake and payments.
