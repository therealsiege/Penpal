# Corro Utilization Management

CorroHealth offers utilization management services aimed at helping healthcare providers manage the medical necessity, appropriateness, and efficiency of care. Their solutions include prior authorization support, concurrent and retrospective medical necessity reviews, and denial management. CorroHealth uses a combination of clinical staff and technology, including natural language processing and analytics, to assist with these reviews across various care settings.
