# Kyron Medical Denial Intelligence

[Contact](https://www.kyronmedical.com/)

Kyron Medical provides an AI-powered denial management platform designed for revenue cycle management (RCM) teams. Its agents automate denial triage, draft appeal letters in under 10 seconds, reconcile claim statuses, and manage eligibility and prior authorization tasks. The platform integrates with major EHRs, clearinghouses, and payer portals, while offering a full audit trail, real-time queue management, and operational dashboards.
