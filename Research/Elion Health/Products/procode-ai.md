# Procode AI

Procode AI provides an AI coding copilot that integrates directly with EHR systems to automate medical coding and charge submission for surgical and procedural specialties. The system extracts clinical documentation, generates CPT, diagnosis, and modifier codes, checks coding rules such as NCCI, MUE, and LCD edits, and prepares charges for human review before submission.
