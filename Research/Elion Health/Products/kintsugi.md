# Kintsugi

Kintsugi develops AI-driven voice biomarker technology to detect signs of depression and anxiety from brief, free-form speech samples. Their primary offering, Kintsugi Voice, is an API-first platform that integrates with call centers, telehealth systems, and remote patient monitoring applications to identify mental health challenges in real time.
