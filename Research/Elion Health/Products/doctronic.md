# Doctronic

Doctronic is an AI-powered clinical consultation platform that provides users with automated health assessments and personalized management plans based on medical data and physician-trained algorithms. It offers 24/7 AI consultations that generate summaries and SOAP notes for patients to share with clinicians. Users can escalate to video visits with licensed U.S. doctors for prescriptions and diagnosis, available nationwide and covered by insurance.
