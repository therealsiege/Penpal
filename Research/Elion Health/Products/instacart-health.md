# Instacart Health

Instacart Health is an initiative by Instacart aimed at enhancing access to nutritious food and integrating food into healthcare. It offers tools like Fresh Funds, enabling organizations to provide funds for purchasing specific food categories, and Care Carts, allowing healthcare providers to order groceries on behalf of patients.
