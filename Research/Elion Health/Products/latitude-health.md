# Latitude Health

Latitude Health provides AI-driven utilization management (UM) solutions designed to automate and streamline prior authorizations, clinical reviews, and case management for health plans, third-party administrators (TPAs), and risk-bearing entities. Its platform automates up to 75% of manual UM tasks, including case intake, triage, and prioritization, to improve efficiency and reduce administrative costs.
