# Collectly Billie AI

[Contact](https://www.collectly.co/billie)

Billie by Collectly is an AI-driven patient billing and revenue cycle management agent that integrates bi-directionally with EHR, practice management, and insurance systems. It automates patient communications across chat, text, voice, and email, addressing billing inquiries, disputes, payments, and payment plans. Billie performs real-time eligibility and coverage verification by accessing payer data on active coverage, deductibles, and copays, ensuring patients understand their financial responsibility.
