# Bonsai Health

Bonsai Health is an AI-powered platform focused on automating front office workflows for medical practices. It ingests and normalizes EHR data to identify care gaps and opportunities for reactivation or follow-up, then performs personalized outreach (via SMS, email) and books appointments automatically based on real-time availability and specialty logic
