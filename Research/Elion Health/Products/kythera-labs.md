# Kythera Labs

Kythera Labs' Wayfinder platform is a SaaS big data solution for healthcare and life sciences that integrates data, analytics, and AI to generate insights from over 45 terabytes of de-identified claims data. It supports use cases including identifying rare patients, targeting providers, mapping patient journeys, and analyzing market trends.
