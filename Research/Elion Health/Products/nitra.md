# Nitra

Nitra is a financial operations product designed for medical practices. It provides a practice-focused expense management hub: practice expense cards (physical & virtual), spend controls and limits, vendor payments support, accounts payable automation, receipt matching, accounting integrations, and analytics to help practices see, track, and manage their cash flow.
