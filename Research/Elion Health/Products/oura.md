# Oura

The Oura Ring is a smart ring that tracks various health metrics such as sleep stages, heart rate, body temperature, and activity levels. It utilizes infrared photoplethysmography sensors to monitor heart rate and respiration, providing detailed insights into sleep quality and readiness for daily activities.
