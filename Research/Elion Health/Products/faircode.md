# FairCode

FairCode is a DRG validation and physician chart-review service and software platform used in inpatient revenue cycle workflows, including concurrent and retrospective DRG review, pre-bill validation, and denial defense. It provides predictive analytics to prioritize charts for review, performs 100% physician-conducted medical chart review by coding-trained physicians, and routes cases while capturing physician findings and analytics.
