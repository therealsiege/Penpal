# Pharos

Pharos Health provides an AI-powered analytics platform tailored for hospital quality and risk management teams. It automates data gathering, reporting, and chart abstraction for clinical registries like TQIP and NSQIP. The platform aids in rapidly completing root cause analyses, conducting audits, and monitoring hospital performance against regulatory metrics.
