# CareSimple

CareSimple provides a remote patient monitoring (RPM) platform designed for chronic care management, population health, and hospital-at-home programs. It integrates with EHR systems and offers cellular-enabled medical devices such as blood pressure monitors and glucose meters, which transmit data without the need for Wi-Fi or apps.
