# Inferscience HCC Assistant

Inferscience HCC Assistant is a clinical decision support tool designed to help healthcare providers identify and document hierarchical condition category (HCC) codes for risk adjustment. It integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to analyze patient data in real time and suggest appropriate HCC codes based on clinical documentation.
