# Martti

Martti provides healthcare-focused language interpretation services, offering access to medically qualified interpreters in over 260 languages, with more than 60 available via video. Their platform integrates with Electronic Health Records (EHR) and telehealth systems, facilitating seamless communication between patients and providers.
