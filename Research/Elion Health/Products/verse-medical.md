# Verse Medical

Verse Medical is an AI-enabled DME ordering and coordination platform used by large health systems, payors, and care teams to streamline procurement of medical equipment and supplies. It validates insurance coverage in real time, extracts diagnostic and order details directly from the medical record, and identifies missing documentation to prevent delays.
