# Gallion Health

Gallion Health is a digital platform focused on managing bill-only implant purchases and related supplies for hospitals. It automates the billing, validation, and reconciliation processes for surgical implants, helping streamline procurement and compliance. The system integrates with EHRs and ERPs in real-time, allowing perioperative teams to verify costs during procedures and flag discrepancies immediately.
