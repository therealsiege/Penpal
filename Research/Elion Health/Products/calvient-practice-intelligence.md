# Calvient Practice Intelligence

Calvient Practice Intelligence is an operations layer used in patient intake and care-coordination workflows, including referrals and prior authorizations. It provides analytics and operational dashboards that show throughput, bottlenecks, and team capacity, identifies no-show and authorization-delay risk with predictive models, and automates classification, data extraction, summarization, and draft generation for routine tasks.
