# UpDoc

UpDoc offers an AI-driven remote patient monitoring platform designed for managing chronic diseases and assisting physicians in clinical decision-making, particularly for medication management. The system has been validated through clinical trials, where it was shown to effectively manage treatment plans under physician supervision.
