# MediView XR

MediView offers augmented reality (AR)-based visualization and navigation systems (notably XR90 and OmnifyXR) that overlay 3D anatomical structures (from CT, ultrasound, etc.) onto the procedural field in real time. Their systems allow clinicians to view internal anatomy, instrument trajectories, and ultrasound imagery with depth perception and better ergonomics during image-guided, minimally invasive procedures.
