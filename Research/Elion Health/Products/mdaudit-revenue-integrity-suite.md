# MDAudit  Revenue Integrity Suite

The MDaudit Revenue Integrity Suite is a set of tools designed to help healthcare organizations monitor, audit, and optimize revenue cycle operations, with a focus on compliance and revenue integrity. It provides capabilities for coding and billing audits, charge capture analysis, and benchmarking, enabling users to identify revenue leakage and mitigate compliance risks.
