# MindWise Health

MindWise Health is a behavioral health–focused electronic health record (EHR) platform that combines clinical documentation, scheduling, billing, telehealth, assessments, and patient tracking in a single system. It includes structured documentation tools such as Power Notes, a library of behavioral health assessments, integrated e-prescribing, claims management, and progress monitoring features tailored to mental health workflows.
