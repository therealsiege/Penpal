# EndoML

EndoML is an AI development and deployment platform purpose-built for gastrointestinal endoscopy data. It enables clinicians, researchers, and life sciences teams to train and deploy custom computer vision models for tasks such as polyp detection, anatomical landmark identification, segmentation, and inflammatory bowel disease severity scoring using endoscopy video.
