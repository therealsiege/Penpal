# Arrowhealth

Arrowhealth’s Bridge platform embeds a configurable referral button directly into the EHR workflow, enabling providers to identify and refer eligible patients without leaving the chart. The system evaluates patients in real time against predefined eligibility criteria, surfaces targeted referral prompts at the point of decision-making, and pre-populates referral forms with demographic and clinical data from the EHR.
