# VitVio

VitVio is a computer-vision platform for operating rooms that supports perioperative and OR workflows, including scheduling, case coordination, and post-case review. It analyzes live OR video and provides automated surgical-stage logging, real-time case progress and finish-time estimates, and tracking of staff, patients, equipment, and surgical tools to reduce bottlenecks and equipment waste.
