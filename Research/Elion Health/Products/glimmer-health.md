# Glimmer Health

Glimmer Health provides a turnkey chronic pain management solution for primary care practices by integrating specialty care, behavioral health, and care coordination into existing workflows. Its platform supports evidence-based pain treatment pathways, offers rapid access to specialty consults, and includes a dedicated care engagement team composed of health coaches, social workers, and advanced practice providers.
