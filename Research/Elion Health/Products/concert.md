# Concert

Concert offers a genetic test order management system designed to assist hospitals and health systems in navigating the complexities of genetic testing. This platform integrates test selection, results retrieval, and insurance workflows into a single, user-friendly interface, facilitating efficient management of lab budgets and the genetic send-out process. By incorporating pricing and payer coverage information, it ensures appropriate test ordering and supports lab stewardship.
