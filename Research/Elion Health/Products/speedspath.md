# SpeedsPath

SpeedsPath is a cloud-based Laboratory Information System (LIS) focused on anatomic, molecular, cytology, and surgical pathology labs, offering modules for specimen tracking, digital pathology integration, barcode automation, mobile & patient portal access, billing/RCM, and electronic workflows.
