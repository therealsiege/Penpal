# Semantic Health

Semantic Health offers an AI-driven platform designed for real-time medical coding, auditing, and clinical documentation improvement (CDI). It performs pre-bill audits, reviewing 100% of coded charts to detect errors and identify revenue opportunities with minimal workflow disruption. The platform integrates with major EHR systems and provides insights for various healthcare providers, including acute care and pediatric hospitals.
