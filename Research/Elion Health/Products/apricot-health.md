# Apricot Health

Apricot Health is a documentation platform for home health agencies that operates in the visit documentation and post-visit workflow. It provides automated documentation assistance by guiding nurses through conversational interviews, ingesting referral documents and photos, and generating structured, review-ready start-of-care and routine visit notes with compliance guardrails.
