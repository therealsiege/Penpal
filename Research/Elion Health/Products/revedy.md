# Revedy

[Contact](https://revedy.io/contact)

Revedy provides AI-powered medical coding software with solutions for CPT, ICD-10, and HCC coding, along with compliance support. It integrates with major EHR platforms, offers interactive coding tools, and supports specialized use cases like intraoperative neuromonitoring (IONM). The platform also provides scribing services and analytics for revenue optimization.
