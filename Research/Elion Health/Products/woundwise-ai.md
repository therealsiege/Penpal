# WoundScribe AI

WoundWise AI, developed by Pingoo, is an AI-powered wound care platform that assists clinicians in assessing wounds, generating evidence-based treatment plans, and automating documentation. It supports photo-based wound monitoring, remote patient check-ins, and clinical triage, aiming to improve outcomes and reduce readmissions.
