# RestoreU by UMethod

RestoreU, developed by uMETHOD Health, is an AI-driven platform designed to manage and improve cognitive health, particularly targeting conditions like dementia and Alzheimer's disease. The platform utilizes advanced algorithms to analyze over 50 risk factors from diverse data sources, including lab results, genetic information, medications, comorbidities, and patient lifestyle. It generates personalized care plans that address these risk factors, helping physicians provide tailored treatment to their patients.
