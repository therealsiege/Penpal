# Genzeon CompliancePro Solutions

CompliancePro Solutions (CPS) is a cloud-based privacy and compliance automation platform designed to modernize data protection and regulatory operations for healthcare organizations. It enables privacy and compliance teams to proactively manage, monitor, and report privacy risks while maintaining strict adherence to healthcare data protection standards such as HIPAA, GDPR, and the Cures Act.
