# Spiriplex

Spiriplex is a healthcare company specializing in micro-sample diagnostics, particularly in dried blood spot (DBS) technology. They offer a comprehensive allergy testing service called Allergenex, which requires only four drops of blood to test for 68 common food and environmental allergens.
