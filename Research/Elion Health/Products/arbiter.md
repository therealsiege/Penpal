# Arbiter

Arbiter is a care-orchestration platform that unifies clinical, financial, and policy data to automate referrals, prior authorizations, and care-gap identification across providers, payers, and patients. It detects care opportunities in real time—such as missed screenings, overdue labs, or preventive exams—and surfaces structured referral triggers directly inside the EMR.
