# Best Practice

Best Practice Software offers a suite of healthcare practice management solutions designed primarily for general practitioners, specialists, and allied health providers. Its flagship products include Bp Premier, a comprehensive tool for general practice that handles patient records, billing, and clinical reporting, and Bp
VIP.net
, aimed at specialists with features such as patient management, billing, and reporting for specialized practices like ophthalmology and cardiology.
