# Eon Patient Management

Eon Patient Management (EPM) by Eon Health is a platform that uses Computational Linguistics to scan radiology reports and identify incidental findings with high accuracy and precision. It supports multiple disease-specific modules and integrates with hospital IT systems to streamline patient tracking and communication.
