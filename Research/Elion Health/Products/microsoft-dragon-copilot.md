# Microsoft Dragon Copilot

Microsoft Dragon Copilot is an AI clinical assistant used in point-of-care and post-encounter workflows for visit documentation and note transfer. It captures ambient conversations and on-demand recordings and converts them into specialty-specific, customizable clinical notes.
