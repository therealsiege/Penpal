# LexisNexis Gravitas

LexisNexis Gravitas is a real-world data (RWD) platform that provides access to de-identified, tokenized patient-level healthcare data for use in research, analytics, and commercial applications. It integrates electronic health records, medical claims, pharmacy data, and consumer data to create longitudinal views of patient health and behavior.
