# Ascertain

Ascertain is an AI platform that automates administrative healthcare workflows, operating in pre-visit, revenue-cycle, and care management processes such as prior authorization, eligibility verification, and discharge planning. It ingests unstructured data, fills forms and navigates payer portals, and assembles required documentation for submissions; deployments combine Ascertain Automate enterprise software with a tech-enabled services offering that operates the platform on customers' behalf.
