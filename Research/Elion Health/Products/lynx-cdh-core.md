# Lynx CDH Core

Lynx CDH Core is a consumer-directed health (CDH) account management platform that provides APIs for integrating health savings accounts (HSAs), flexible spending accounts (FSAs), and other CDH accounts into digital experiences. The platform allows healthcare organizations to embed advanced payment processing, e-commerce, and investment functionality into their systems.
