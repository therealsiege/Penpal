# ECG‑AI LEF

ECG‑AI LEF analyzes routine 12‑lead electrocardiograms at the point of ECG interpretation to identify low left ventricular ejection fraction (LEF, EF ≤40%) for earlier detection of heart failure. It analyzes ECG waveforms, flags probable low LEF, and provides a web-based ECG viewer that displays prior ECGs alongside AI results in a patient timeline.
