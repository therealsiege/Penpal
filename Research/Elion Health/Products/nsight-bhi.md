# Nsight Behavioral Health Integration

Nsight Behavioral Health Integration is a managed behavioral health integration service used in primary care workflows to support care management and monthly billing under CPT 99484. It provides systematic assessment and monitoring (PHQ‑9, GAD), develops care plans and delivers brief evidence‑based interventions, and facilitates ongoing collaboration with primary care clinicians and psychiatric consultants.
