# B.well

B.well Connected Health is a digital health platform used by health systems to aggregate longitudinal patient data and enable patient engagement across pre-visit, scheduling, and post-visit workflows. It ingests and normalizes clinical, claims, and consumer-generated data and makes that data accessible across care workflows.
