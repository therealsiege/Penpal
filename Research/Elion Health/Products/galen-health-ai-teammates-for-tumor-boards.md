# Galen Health AI Teammates for Tumor Boards

Galen Health provides an AI-powered clinical collaboration platform designed to support multidisciplinary tumor boards in oncology care. It integrates with existing health IT systems to aggregate and summarize patient data, identify clinical trial matches, and generate documentation for tumor board meetings.
