# Rater8

Rater8 is a patient feedback and reputation management platform designed for healthcare practices. It collects real-time patient feedback using automated text or email surveys that are integrated with over 100 EHR and practice management systems. Rater8's micro-surveys are short and anonymous, focusing on specific aspects like provider interactions, office cleanliness, and check-in processes.
