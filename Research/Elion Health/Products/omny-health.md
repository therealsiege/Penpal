# OMNY Health

OMNY Health is a platform that enables healthcare providers, health systems, and specialty networks to monetize and leverage their clinical data by sharing de-identified patient data, which OMNY standardizes and makes available for research and commercial purposes. Providers gain access to data insights and dashboards that support operational and clinical decision-making
