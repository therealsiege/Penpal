# SmarterDx SmarterCharges

[Contact](https://www.smarterdx.com/contact)

SmarterCharges by SmarterDx is an AI-driven charge capture solution that analyzes the full patient record to identify missed or misleveled CPT, HCPCS, and APC codes. It supports revenue integrity teams by surfacing evidence-based, defensible charge opportunities tied directly to clinical documentation.
