# Cohere Payment Integrity

Cohere Health’s Payment Integrity solution uses AI-driven clinical and coding validation to help health plans improve payment accuracy and reduce reliance on external vendors. The platform transforms unstructured medical records into structured datasets for automated claim audits across inpatient, outpatient, and professional settings.
