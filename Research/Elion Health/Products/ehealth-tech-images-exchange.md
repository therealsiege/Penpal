# eHealth Technologies Image Exchange

eHealth Technologies Image Exchange (eHealth Connect®) is a medical imaging interoperability solution that enables healthcare organizations to electronically access, view, and transfer diagnostic images across institutions. It allows care teams to retrieve outside imaging with a single click and move studies directly into PACS, supporting emergent workflows such as stroke, trauma, cancer, and cardiac care.
