# Taxo AI

Taxo AI provides AI-powered coding assistance tools designed for healthcare providers and revenue cycle teams. The platform analyzes clinical documentation to suggest medical codes (ICD-10, CPT, HCPCS) in real time, helping medical coders improve accuracy and reduce manual work. It supports both inpatient and outpatient coding workflows and integrates with electronic health record (EHR) systems through APIs.
