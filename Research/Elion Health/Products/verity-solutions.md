# Verity Solutions

Verity Solutions provides technology and administrative services to support 340B program compliance and pharmacy purchasing optimization for hospitals, health systems, community health centers, and contract pharmacies. Its platform offers features like inventory and split billing management, centralized oversight of third-party administrator (TPA) relationships, and data visibility across sites and pharmacy networks. Verity is used by healthcare organizations to manage 340B program requirements, reduce pharmacy costs, and ensure audit readiness.
