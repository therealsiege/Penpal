# Floe Health

Contact

Floe Health is a platform that automates and coordinates patient interactions across the care journey, covering intake, phone calls, follow-ups, and referrals. It uses AI to detect when patients stall in the process and triggers actions through voice, web, or digital forms to keep care on track. The system helps reduce patient leakage, improve throughput, and support retention by handling routine workflows that typically require staff time.
