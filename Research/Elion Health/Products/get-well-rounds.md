# Get Well Rounds+

Get Well Rounds+ is a digital rounding and surveying tool designed for health systems to capture real-time feedback and actionable data from patients, families, clinicians, and staff. The platform supports a variety of rounding use-cases—such as nurse leader rounding, outpatient clinic rounding, employee engagement, and patient-led discharge checklists—using mobile apps, tablets or browser interfaces.
