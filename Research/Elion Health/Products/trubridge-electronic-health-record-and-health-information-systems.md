# TruBridge  Electronic Health Record and Health Information Systems

TruBridge Electronic Health Record (EHR) and Health Information Systems provide integrated clinical and operational tools tailored for rural and community healthcare providers. The platform offers unified patient records, real-time data access, and mobile capabilities for rounding and documentation.
