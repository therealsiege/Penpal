# Microsoft Powershare Image Sharing

Microsoft PowerShare Image Sharing is a cloud-based medical imaging exchange platform that enables healthcare organizations to securely access, share, and view radiology images across institutions and care settings. It supports rapid image transfer without physical media, allowing clinicians to retrieve outside studies, collaborate across sites, and make timely care decisions.
