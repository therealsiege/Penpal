# SpendMend

SpendMend provides cost recovery and audit services for healthcare organizations, focusing on areas such as purchased services, pharmacy operations, and 340B program compliance. Their platform uses transaction-level analytics to identify overpayments, billing discrepancies, and missed contract terms, helping hospitals and health systems recover funds and reduce future leakage. They also offer support for 340B covered entities, including audit preparation and program optimization.
