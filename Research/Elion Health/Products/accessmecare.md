# AccessMeCare

AccessMeCare is a digital health platform developed by Heudia Health that enables users to screen, navigate, and plan care for individuals, including friends, family members, clients, or patients. The platform is designed to improve access to health and social services by coordinating the efforts of local, community-based organizations.
