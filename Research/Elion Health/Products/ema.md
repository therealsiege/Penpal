# Ema

Ema for Healthcare is an AI workforce platform that automates a wide range of clinical and administrative workflows across providers, payers, and care teams. It supports tasks such as prior authorization review, medical-necessity assessment, treatment-plan analysis, risk stratification, clinical summarization, patient communication, medication management, and claims verification. The system uses a multi-model architecture (EmaFusion™) and a generative workflow engine to execute complex sequences of actions while integrating with existing EMR and enterprise tools.
