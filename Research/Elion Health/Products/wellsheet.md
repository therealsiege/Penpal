# Wellsheet

Wellsheet provides an AI-powered Smart EHR UI that integrates with existing EHR systems like Epic, Cerner, and athenahealth. It assembles and summarizes critical clinical content from EHRs, clinical guidelines, and other sources into a single, intuitive view tailored to individual clinician specialties and practice patterns.
