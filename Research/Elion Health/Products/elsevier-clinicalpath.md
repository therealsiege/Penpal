# Elsevier ClinicalPath

Elsevier ClinicalPath is an oncology clinical decision support and pathway management system used at the point of care to present tumor-specific treatment pathways and analytics for cancer care. It provides pathway protocols, prompts ordering of biomarker and genetic tests, and supports prioritization and referral of patients to relevant clinical trials.
