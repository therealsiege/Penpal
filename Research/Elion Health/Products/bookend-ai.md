# Bookend AI

Bookend AI offers a platform that automates and optimizes healthcare administration processes, including prior authorization, value-based care, utilization management, and care delivery. Its AI-powered agents analyze patient data and insurance policies to streamline workflows, reduce manual tasks, and improve patient outcomes.
