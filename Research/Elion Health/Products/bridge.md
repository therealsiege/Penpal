# Bridge

Bridge is a population health management platform that aggregates data from EHRs, health information exchanges, and social determinants of health to provide a unified view of patient information. Key capabilities include real-time patient information exchange, referral management, secure messaging, patient tracking, clinical integration, and advanced analytics.
