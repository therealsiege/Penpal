# DocBuddy

DocBuddy is a mobile and cloud-based healthcare solution designed to facilitate data access, creation, and movement at the point of care. It offers mobile compatibility with iOS and Android devices, enabling healthcare providers to manage patient information, schedules, and documentation directly from their mobile devices
