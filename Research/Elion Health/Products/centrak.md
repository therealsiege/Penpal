# CenTrak

CenTrak specializes in Real-Time Location Systems (RTLS) for healthcare environments, providing precise tracking of patients, staff, and assets to enhance operational efficiency and safety. Their solutions include asset tracking and management, clinical workflow optimization, infection control measures, staff and patient safety systems, and environmental monitoring.
