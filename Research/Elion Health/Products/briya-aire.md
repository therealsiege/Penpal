# Briya AIRE

Briya AIRE is a healthcare-specific AI research environment designed to help life sciences, biotech, academia, and healthcare organizations generate insights from real-world medical data. Unlike generic AI models, AIRE is trained on clinical datasets, enabling accurate hypothesis testing, cohort identification, study design, and advanced analytics.
