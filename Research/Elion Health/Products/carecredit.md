# CareCredit

CareCredit is a healthcare-&-wellness financing product (a credit card program) that allows patients or clients to pay over time for out-of-pocket health, wellness, or elective treatment costs. Providers enroll to accept the CareCredit card; patients often can prequalify (soft inquiry) and if approved use the card for procedures, services, or purchases.
