# Oracle Health Data Intelligence

Oracle Health Data Intelligence is a cloud-based platform designed to unify patient data from multiple sources to support population health management, value-based care, and clinical decision-making. It provides tools for care coordination, quality measurement, predictive analytics, and point-of-care intelligence using longitudinal patient records and AI-assisted workflows. The platform is EHR-agnostic and integrates with Oracle’s own health IT products as well as third-party systems.
