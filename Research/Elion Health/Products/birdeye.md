# Birdeye

Birdeye is a patient-experience and reputation-management platform used by healthcare practices and multi-location health systems to increase online visibility, gather patient feedback, and streamline digital communication. It integrates with practice management and CRM systems to automatically request reviews, update directory listings, and centralize messages from SMS, webchat, email, and social channels into a single inbox.
