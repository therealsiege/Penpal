# Datos Health

Datos Health provides a versatile remote patient monitoring system designed to support diverse care programs, such as hospital-at-home, cardiac rehabilitation, and chronic disease management. Their platform allows healthcare providers to customize and automate care pathways, leveraging patient-generated health data to enhance care delivery.
