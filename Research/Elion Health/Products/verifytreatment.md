# VerifyTreatment

VerifyTreatment is a cloud-based health insurance verification and benefits platform that helps healthcare providers check patient coverage and benefits quickly and accurately before admission or treatment. It accesses a comprehensive payer database (including Medicare, Medicaid, ACA, and commercial plans) to perform real-time eligibility checks, automated bulk verifications, and ongoing coverage monitoring to catch lapsed policies and prevent denials or revenue loss. The platform includes HIPAA-compliant messaging and collaboration tools for admissions, billing, and clinical teams, as well as webform integration to capture and verify coverage at intake.
