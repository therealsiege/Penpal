# Popai Health

Popai Health provides a voice AI platform that analyzes and automates healthcare care coordination calls. It captures and interprets 100% of patient, clinician, and service calls to surface real-time clinical and operational insights, standardize workflows, and automate documentation or escalations directly into EHRs. The platform includes three layers: an AI integration layer for processing conversations and ensuring interoperability across systems, a visibility layer for analytics and performance dashboards, and an action layer that automates workflows based on AI-identified events.
