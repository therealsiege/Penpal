# SKRIBER

Skriber is an AI-based medical scribe platform that automatically converts clinician–patient conversations into structured SOAP notes and clinical documentation in real time. It uses speech recognition and natural language processing to capture, transcribe, and summarize encounters, including ICD-10 and CPT code suggestions.
