# Cavo Coder

Cavo Coder is a risk adjustment and diagnostic coding product that supports health plans and coding teams in identifying, validating, and submitting accurate ICD-10 and HCC codes from clinical documentation. It uses rules-based precise word matching to automatically detect relevant diagnoses, rank codes by documentation strength, and map HCCs based on date of service across Medicare, Medicaid, and ACA programs.
