# Auxa Health

Auxa Health is a social care coordination platform, enabling providers and payers to connect individuals with benefits like food, housing, and financial assistance. It offers solutions for surfacing member-level eligible benefits across state, federal, and health plan offerings, uses proprietary algorithms for benefit matching, and supports organizations in managing priority populations.
