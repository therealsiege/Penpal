# Kardia

Kardia, developed by AliveCor, offers portable EKG devices such as the KardiaMobile and KardiaMobile 6L. These devices allow users to record medical-grade EKGs directly on their smartphones and detect common arrhythmias like atrial fibrillation, bradycardia, and tachycardia.
