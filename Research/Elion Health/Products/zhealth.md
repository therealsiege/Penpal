# zHealth

zHealth is an all-in-one practice management and EHR platform purpose-built for chiropractors and wellness providers. The platform combines scheduling, reminders, recall, patient messaging, reviews, mobile access, SOAP notes, billing, payments, memberships, and an AI-powered scribe into a single system.
