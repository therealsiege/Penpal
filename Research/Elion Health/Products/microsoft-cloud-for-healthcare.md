# Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare

Microsoft Cloud for Healthcare helps unify operational, financial, and patient engagement workflows by integrating data across departments, supporting care coordination, managing scheduling, billing, referrals, marketing, and analytics. In healthcare settings it can serve as a backbone for patient portal services, care management, home health coordination, unified patient views via FHIR-based models, and operational automation across clinical and administrative domains
