# KiHealth

KiHealth offers at-home diagnostic tests that use PCR-based molecular diagnostics to analyze key biomarkers. These tests provide insights into metabolic health, helping individuals understand their risk for conditions such as diabetes, heart disease, and autoimmune disorders.
