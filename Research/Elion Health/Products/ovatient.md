# Ovatient

Ovatient is a virtual-first care platform built on Epic, offering a white-labeled, multi-specialty telehealth solution designed for small to mid-size health systems, health plans, and employers. It integrates with existing Epic and MyChart systems to support virtual urgent care, primary care, and behavioral health, while maintaining continuity with in-person services when needed.
