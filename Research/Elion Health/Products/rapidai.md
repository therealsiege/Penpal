# RapidAI

RapidAI develops AI-driven imaging solutions primarily for stroke, trauma, and vascular conditions. Its tools analyze brain scans, such as CT and MRI images, to assist physicians in diagnosing and treating conditions like stroke and subdural hematomas. The company's platform helps expedite treatment decisions by providing results within minutes, and it integrates into hospital systems via PACS and mobile apps.
