# Blyott

Blyott provides real-time location tracking and inventory monitoring solutions for various industries, including healthcare, logistics, and manufacturing. Their platform uses sensors and Wi-Fi access points to track asset location, temperature, and movement, offering insights on asset utilization and process optimization.
