# Moyae

Moyae is an all-in-one electronic health record (EHR) platform built specifically for vision-care providers, including ophthalmologists and optometrists. It offers customizable templates tied to diagnosis and procedure codes, AI-suggested coding for faster documentation, integrated e-prescribing (eRx), and scalable calendar management for solo or group practices.
