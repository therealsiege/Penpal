# Aiberry

Aiberry is an AI-powered mental health screening tool that analyzes speech, text, and facial expressions to assess psychological conditions such as depression and anxiety. It is designed for use by healthcare providers, employers, and other organizations to facilitate early detection and monitoring of mental health concerns. The platform generates risk scores and assessment reports based on AI-driven analysis, which clinicians can use to support diagnostic decisions.
