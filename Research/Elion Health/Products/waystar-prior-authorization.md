# Waystar Prior Authorization

Waystar's Prior Authorization platform uses intelligent automation to manage the entire authorization process. It automatically verifies, initiates, tracks status, and retrieves authorization details, integrating directly with major EHR systems. The platform automates patient referral status, handles unscheduled admissions, and generates necessary medical notices.
