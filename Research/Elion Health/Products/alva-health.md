# Alva Health

Alva Health enables remote stroke monitoring and the detection of hemiparesis, an early sign of stroke by using wearables to continuously monitor for stroke symptoms. Alva can automatically alert emergency services upon detecting potential stroke signs, improving response times and helping prevent long-term disabilities in patients.
