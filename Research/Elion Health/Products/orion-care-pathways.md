# Care Pathways

Orion Health Care Pathways is a configurable module in Orion Health’s Digital Care Record that builds and operationalizes clinical workflows across the care continuum, from simple local flows to complex chronic-care pathways. It provides pathway design tools — a Pathway Designer for simple flows and a business process management tool for complex scheduling and tasks — and schedules and assigns activities while tracking status.
