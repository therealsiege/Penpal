# ThetaRho DataDoc

ThetaRho offers DataDoc, an AI-enabled application embedded within the EHR that allows providers to retrieve patient data using natural language queries. It pulls information from internal EHR records, external care documents (e.g. CCDAs), and patient-generated data to deliver contextual insights during clinical workflows.
