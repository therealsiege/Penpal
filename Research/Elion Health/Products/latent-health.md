# Latent Health

[Contact](https://latenthealth.com/contact)

Latent Health provides a technology platform designed to streamline medication access workflows for specialty and infusion pharmacies. Its clinical AI supports prior authorizations, benefits checks, appeals, and 340B compliance by extracting and organizing clinical and administrative data from the EHR. The platform centralizes pharmacy operations, reducing manual workload and shortening time-to-therapy across pharmacy settings.
