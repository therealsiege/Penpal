# Vivalink

Vivalink provides wearable medical sensors and a biometric data platform designed for remote patient monitoring (RPM) and decentralized clinical trials. Their system supports continuous monitoring of vital signs like ECG, heart rate, temperature, and blood pressure, enabling applications in cardiology, oncology, and neurology. Vivalink’s solutions are used in hospital-at-home programs, cardiac monitoring, and clinical research, offering data integration tools through a software development kit (SDK) for healthcare applications.
