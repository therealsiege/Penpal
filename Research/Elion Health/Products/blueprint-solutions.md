# Blueprint Solutions

Blueprint Solutions provides Blueprint OMS, a cloud-based office management software designed specifically for hearing healthcare providers. The system supports various essential clinic functions like patient scheduling, insurance billing, and audiology-specific features such as hearing aid tracking and medical report writing.
