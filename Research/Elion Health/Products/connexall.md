# Connexall

Connexall provides an alarm management platform designed to reduce alarm fatigue in clinical settings by filtering non-actionable alerts and ensuring timely delivery of critical ones. It uses patented algorithms to suppress nuisance alarms and supports escalation pathways to improve clinician responsiveness.
