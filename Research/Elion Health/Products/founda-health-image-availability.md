# Founda Health Image Availability

Founda Health Image Availability is an interoperability solution that enables healthcare organizations to access and share complete imaging histories across hospitals, departments, and external partners. It connects PACS, VNAs, EHRs, and external imaging systems using standards such as DICOM, DICOMweb, XDS-I/XCA-I, and FHIR, allowing clinicians to view relevant prior studies directly within their existing workflows.
