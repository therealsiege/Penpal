# OneLine Health

OneLine Health AI Copilot is an AI-driven clinical documentation tool built for physicians that automates the creation of clinical notes. It captures patient history via conversational AI, extracts lab/imaging data from EHRs or external sources, listens to ambient physician-patient interactions in real time, and generates a structured note with ICD/CPT codes and billing support.
