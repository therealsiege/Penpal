# Carecode.ai

[Contact](https://www.carecode.ai/)

Carecode is a conversational AI platform designed for healthcare organizations to handle patient communications across text, audio, and voice. It supports both inbound and outbound interactions for tasks like scheduling, confirmations, documentation support, payment assistance, and patient follow-up.
