# Stanson Health Auth Assist

Stanson Health Auth Assist is a prior authorization tool that works at the point of care, embedded in the electronic health record (EHR). It uses clinical decision support logic, payer-agnostic policies, machine learning / natural language processing, and real-time clinical rules to identify when prior authorization is required, suggest or initiate the authorization process, and speed up approvals.
