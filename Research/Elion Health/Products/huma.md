# Huma

Huma provides a hospital-at-home platform that enables remote patient monitoring and virtual care for managing acute and chronic conditions outside traditional hospital settings. The platform integrates with medical devices, wearables, and electronic health records (EHRs) to collect real-time patient data, including vital signs and symptoms, allowing clinicians to monitor and adjust treatment plans remotely
