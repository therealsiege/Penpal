# Omega Healthcare CollectIQ

CollectIQ by Omega Healthcare is a revenue cycle analytics and automation tool focused on improving collections and managing accounts receivable (A/R). It provides real-time tracking of claim status, payer trends, and aging buckets, enabling revenue cycle teams to prioritize follow-ups and resolve issues that delay reimbursement. The platform uses rules-based workflows and predictive models to identify high-risk claims and recommend next actions.
