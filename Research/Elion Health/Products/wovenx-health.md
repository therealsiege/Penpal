# WovenX Health

WovenX Health provides a hybrid care platform focused on expanding and optimizing access to gastrointestinal (GI) specialty care. Its offerings include remote GI-trained clinical staff, on-demand virtual triage visits, pre-procedural intake support (Open Access), and analytics tools for workflow and decision-making.
