# Waystar Patient Financial Experience

Waystar's Patient Financial Experience is a suite of tools designed to enhance the financial interactions between healthcare providers and patients. It offers features such as secure text statements, online payment portals, and personalized payment plans, enabling patients to view and settle bills conveniently via their smartphones.
