# Alluvium Health

[Contact](https://www.alluviumhealth.com/request-a-demo)

Alluvium is a patient access platform focused on two main functions: referral management and patient self-scheduling. The referral management tools digitize the referral process, allowing providers to send, track, and manage referrals in real time while reducing administrative work and patient drop-off. The patient self-scheduling function lets patients book their own appointments online, automates confirmations and reminders, and provides visibility into provider availability across a network.
