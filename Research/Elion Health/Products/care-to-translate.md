# Care to Translate

[Contact](https://www.caretotranslate.com/contact-us)

Care to Translate is a medical translation app intended for clinics and healthcare settings to bridge language barriers between providers and patients. It supports over 130 languages (with real-time AI translation and a curated phrase library), can operate offline, and includes image support to clarify medical phrases.
