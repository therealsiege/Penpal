# Dolbey Fusion CAC

Dolbey Fusion CAC is a computer-assisted coding (CAC) platform designed to support hospital and professional coding teams. It uses AI and machine learning to suggest ICD-10 and CPT codes for all patient types and facilitates single-path coding across settings.
