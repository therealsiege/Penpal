# Primefocus Health

Primefocus Health provides a digital platform designed for the remote management of post-acute care and chronic conditions. The platform integrates with wearable devices and other smart technologies to collect real-time health data, enabling continuous monitoring of patients after they leave the hospital to customize care plans, track patient progress, and intervene when necessary.
