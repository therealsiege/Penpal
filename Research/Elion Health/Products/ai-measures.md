# AI.Measures

AI.Measures is an online, multi-source assessment platform for mental health and developmental functioning, fitting clinical workflows for screening, diagnostic assessment, treatment planning, and longitudinal monitoring. It administers brief informant-report questionnaires for caregivers, educators, and clinicians across ~12 domains and ~55 content areas, automatically scores responses, generating interpretive reports and visualizations.
