# Lena Health

Lena Health is an AI-enabled care coordination platform that automates post-discharge follow-up, care gap outreach, and social determinant navigation. It uses automated calls and smart workflows to help patients schedule follow-up appointments, access community resources, and close gaps in preventive and chronic care.
