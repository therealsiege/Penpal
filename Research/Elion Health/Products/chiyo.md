# Chiyo

Chiyo is a personalized nutrition platform offering stage-specific meal programs focused on women's health through fertility, pregnancy, and postpartum phases. Rooted in both Eastern food therapy and modern nutritional science, their meal plans are designed to address key needs such as lactation, hormonal balance, and physical recovery.
