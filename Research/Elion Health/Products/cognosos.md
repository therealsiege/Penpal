# Cognosos

Cognosos offers real-time location services (RTLS) focused on asset tracking for industries like healthcare and logistics. Their platform uses a combination of AI-powered software, Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE), and proprietary long-range wireless technology to provide room-level accuracy without extensive infrastructure
