# Healthplus.ai

Healthplus.ai offers PERISCOPE, an AI-powered clinical decision-support tool that predicts the risk of postoperative infections using real-time EHR data. It provides early identification of high, medium, and low-risk patients, enabling clinicians to tailor monitoring and care plans to reduce complications, readmissions, and costs.
