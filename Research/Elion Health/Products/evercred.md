# evercred

evercred is a credential management platform that automates the storage, verification, and sharing of healthcare workforce credentials. Using AI-powered data extraction, it allows clinicians and staff to upload credentials once, receive automated expiration notifications, and securely share verified information with employers, health systems, or credentialing bodies.
