# healow Genie

[Contact](https://plus.healow.com/demo-request/)

Healow Genie is an AI-powered contact center solution developed by healow that helps medical practices manage patient communications through automated voice, text, and chat interactions. Its capabilities include appointment scheduling, billing inquiries, referral and prescription requests, access to lab results, and after-hours call handling.
