# Truveta

Truveta is a health system-led data platform that aggregates de-identified EHRs from over 120 million patients across 30 U.S. health systems. It provides researchers with comprehensive data—including clinical notes and medical images—updated daily, to study various diseases, treatments, and medical devices.
