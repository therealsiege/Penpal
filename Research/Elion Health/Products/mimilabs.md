# Mimilabs

Mimilabs offers a community-driven data platform focused on healthcare innovation, built around a data lakehouse that hosts thousands of public healthcare datasets. It enables professionals to access, query, and analyze structured data from sources like the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services and the CDC.
