# 4DMedical

4DMedical Ltd. is a global medical technology company that develops advanced four-dimensional lung imaging and analysis solutions to support diagnosis and management of respiratory conditions. Its core offering uses proprietary XV Technology® to process standard fluoroscopy or CT scans into quantitative, regional lung ventilation and function data, delivered via software such as XV LVAS® and CT LVAS™ under a SaaS model and interpreted by clinicians for conditions like COPD, asthma and interstitial lung disease. The company also features other analytic tools (e.g., CT:VQ, CAC, IQ-UIP) and is developing hardware like the XV Scanner to extend functional lung assessment.
