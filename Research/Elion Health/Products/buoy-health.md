# Buoy Health

Buoy Health is a digital health platform that utilizes artificial intelligence to assist users in identifying potential medical conditions based on their symptoms. By engaging in an interactive chat, the AI-driven system asks personalized questions to narrow down possible causes and suggests appropriate next steps for care.
