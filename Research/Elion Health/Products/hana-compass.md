# Hana Compass

Hana Compass is a digital health platform designed to support maternal health by providing personalized guidance and care coordination for pregnant and postpartum patients. It delivers tailored educational content, remote monitoring, and check-ins through a mobile application, while giving care teams access to patient-reported data and risk indicators.
