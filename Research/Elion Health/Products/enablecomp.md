# EnableComp

EnableComp is a specialty revenue cycle management (RCM) and complex claims automation platform for healthcare providers and health systems. It focuses on maximizing reimbursement and resolving difficult claims such as Veterans Administration, Workers’ Compensation, Motor Vehicle Accidents, and Out-of-State Medicaid, as well as handling complex denial categories like DRG downgrades and coordination-of-benefits denials. Using its proprietary intelligent automation platform (E360 RCM™), EnableComp combines technology with expert RCM workflows to prevent and overturn denials, optimize billing accuracy, and recover revenue that traditional RCM teams often miss.
