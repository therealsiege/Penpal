# Virtusense VSTOne

VSTOne is an AI-driven virtual patient monitoring and assistance platform for hospital rooms, developed by VirtuSense Technologies. It combines spatial intelligence (using privacy-focused LIDAR sensors) and machine vision to continuously monitor patient safety, vital signs, and environmental conditions while supporting communication with virtual nurses and specialists.
