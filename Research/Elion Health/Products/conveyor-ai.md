# Conveyor AI

[Contact](https://www.mobius.md/request-demo)

Conveyor AI is an AI medical scribe from Mobius MD that fits into the clinical documentation step of outpatient and telemedicine workflows. It listens during encounters and generates a structured clinical note in under a minute using specialty-trained AI, provides customizable encounter templates, and includes dictation tools.
