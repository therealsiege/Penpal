# Relatient

[Contact](https://www.relatient.com/demo-request/)

Relatient offers patient engagement and communication tools for scheduling, intake, and billing for healthcare providers. It integrates with electronic health records to automate appointment reminders, two-way texting and calls, digital intake forms, and patient surveys.
