# Symplr Contract

symplr Contract is a healthcare-specific contract lifecycle management (CLM) platform that centralizes contract storage, automates workflows, and helps organizations stay compliant with federal and state regulations. It supports contract types across vendors, physicians, BAAs, leases, and materials, and includes configurable approvals, real-time alerts, document editing, and e-signature capture.
