# New Lantern

New Lantern offers a cloud-native radiology platform that integrates PACS viewing, AI-assisted reporting, and intelligent workflow management. Its AI “resident,” Curie, automates measurements, report generation, and segmentation, while a smart worklist assigns cases based on specialty and workload.
