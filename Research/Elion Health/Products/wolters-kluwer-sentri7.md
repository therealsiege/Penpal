# Wolters Kluwer Sentri7

Wolters Kluwer Sentri7 is a clinical surveillance platform used by hospitals and health systems to monitor patient data in real time for early detection of clinical deterioration, infection risks, medication safety issues, and care guideline compliance. It integrates with EHR systems to continuously scan clinical data and generate alerts for pharmacists, infection preventionists, and clinical teams.
