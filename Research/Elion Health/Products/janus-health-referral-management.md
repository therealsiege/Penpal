# Janus Health Referral Management

[Contact](https://info.janus-ai.com/janus-health-elion)

Janus Health Referral Management is an AI-powered solution that automates referral intake for consults and imaging, converting faxed referrals directly into Epic with high accuracy. It is designed to reduce manual data entry, lower referral-related denials, and retain patients in-network by accelerating referral processing.
