# Canopy

Canopy provides a wearable safety platform purpose-built for healthcare workers across hospitals, outpatient clinics, and home health settings. Its core offering is a discreet wearable button that, when activated, instantly alerts nearby staff and security with real-time location data. The system offers always-on coverage indoors, outdoors, and in transit, and includes services like deployment, integration, training, and 24/7 monitoring.
