# TotalMed

TotalMed is a healthcare staffing agency that places travel nurses, allied health professionals, and locum tenens providers in temporary roles across hospitals and health systems nationwide. The company provides end-to-end support including job placement, credentialing, housing logistics, and benefits management. TotalMed serves a range of clinical settings such as acute care hospitals, outpatient clinics, and long-term care facilities.
