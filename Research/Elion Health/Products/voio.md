# Voio

Voio is an AI platform for radiology that generates draft reports, autocompletes structured findings, and analyzes imaging studies to support faster and more accurate diagnostic interpretation. Its vision-language models assist across modalities and protocols, enabling generalists to detect subtle abnormalities and elevating diagnostic quality without adding workflow steps.
