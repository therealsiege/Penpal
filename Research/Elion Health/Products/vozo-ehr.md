# Vozo EHR

Vozo Health offers a cloud-based Electronic Health Record (EHR) and practice management platform designed to unify clinical, administrative, and financial workflows for healthcare practices. The system supports comprehensive patient charting, scheduling, e-prescribing (eRx), secure telehealth, lab and pharmacy integrations, patient portals, and insurance-eligibility verification to reduce denials and streamline office operations. It includes built-in tools such as customizable forms, automated reminders, billing and claims management, task tracking, and reporting dashboards so providers can manage encounters from check-in to billing within a single platform.
