# Envisionit Deep AI

Envisionit Deep AI develops AI-driven radiology solutions, including RADIFY for automated image analysis and RATify for real-time AI model monitoring. Their tools are designed to assist radiologists with triaging and diagnostic support, particularly for critical conditions like pneumothorax and pleural effusion.
