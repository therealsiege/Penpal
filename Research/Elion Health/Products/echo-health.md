# Echo Health

Echo Health provides healthcare payment and remittance solutions that allow providers to manage all payments through a single platform. Its offerings include electronic funds transfer (EFT) and electronic remittance advice (ERA) enrollment, accounts payable automation, and customizable payment workflows.
