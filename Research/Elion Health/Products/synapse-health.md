# Synapse Health

Synapse Health is a DME management and fulfillment platform that serves as the DME provider of record for certain health plans. It centralizes DME ordering through Synapse Connect or existing provider workflows (fax, email, EHR), verifies insurance coverage and documentation, matches patients with appropriate in-network suppliers, and coordinates delivery and patient onboarding. The platform ensures orders meet payor and CMS requirements, supports real-time communication with patients, and provides prescribers with tools for order tracking and documentation guidance.
