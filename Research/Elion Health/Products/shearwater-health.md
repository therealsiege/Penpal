# Shearwater Health

Shearwater Health provides Clinical Process Outsourcing (CPO®) services — essentially remote and on-site clinician teams and process specialists who perform tasks that require or are improved by clinical expertise (e.g., coding, utilization review, clinical decision support, patient communication, and administrative-clinical workflows).
