# Nimblemind

Contact

Nimblemind provides AI-driven clinical coding tools designed to help healthcare organizations improve the accuracy and efficiency of medical coding. Its platform uses natural language processing to extract relevant clinical data from medical records and suggest appropriate ICD-10 and HCC codes for review by human coders.
