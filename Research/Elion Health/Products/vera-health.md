# Vera Health

Vera Health is an AI-powered clinical decision support search engine that allows clinicians to ask medical questions and receive evidence-based answers derived from a large corpus of peer-reviewed medical literature. The platform searches more than 60 million scientific articles and produces concise summaries with references to support diagnosis, treatment, and clinical decision-making.
