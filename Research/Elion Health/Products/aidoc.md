# Aidoc

Aidoc continuously monitors relevant medical exams and results across radiology, cardiology, and neurology to identify critical findings, triage patients, and coordinate care. Aidoc’s AI solutions are integrated into clinical workflows, enabling faster diagnosis and treatment with its FDA-cleared AI tools for patient management and care coordination.
