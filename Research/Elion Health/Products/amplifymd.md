# AmplifyMD

AmplifyMD is a virtual care platform designed for hospitals and health systems to centralize and scale telehealth programs across multiple use cases, including emergent tele-stroke, inpatient cross-coverage, outpatient clinics, transitional care, skilled nursing, long-term acute care, and direct-to-patient services. The system integrates with EHRs to automate scheduling, routing, documentation, and billing while embedding AI-driven workflow support and real-time analytics.
