# Oneview Healthcare

The Oneview Healthcare Platform is a patient engagement and digital health solution designed for hospitals and healthcare facilities. It provides bedside technology that integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to offer patients access to entertainment, education, meal ordering, and communication tools.
