# Hidalga

[Contact](https://www.hidalgatech.com/contact/)

Hidalga is an AI-powered prior authorization automation platform designed specifically for oncology clinics and specialty practices. It fills forms and routes requests, uses predictive scoring and payer-justification logic to reduce denials, integrates via FHIR APIs without requiring full system replacement, and supports live status tracking of submissions.
