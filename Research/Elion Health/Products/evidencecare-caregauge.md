# EvidenceCare CareGauge

[Contact](https://evidence.care/schedule-a-demo)

EvidenceCare CareGauge is an EHR-integrated tool that provides real-time insights into care decisions, comparing a physician's patient care to locally benchmarked data. It offers feedback on metrics such as length of stay, care utilization, and cost, helping reduce care variability and improve outcomes.
