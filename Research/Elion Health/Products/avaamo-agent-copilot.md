# Avaamo Agent Copilot

Avaamo Agent Copilot is an AI-powered assistant for contact center agents that supports pre-call, in-call, and post-call workflows. It integrates with CRM and enterprise systems to surface customer context, detect call intent, and suggest responses or next best actions in real time. During calls, it provides contextual knowledge recommendations and automates repetitive tasks through RPA integration.
