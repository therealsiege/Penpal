# Parity Healthcare Analytics

Parity Healthcare Analytics provides acuity-based staffing software specifically for perinatal clinical units, including OB and NICU departments. The platform leverages real-time data to support staffing decisions that meet patient acuity needs and safety standards. Its analytics capabilities assist nurse managers with demand forecasting, schedule optimization, and budget planning, ultimately aimed at improving workforce efficiency and controlling labor costs in maternity care settings.
