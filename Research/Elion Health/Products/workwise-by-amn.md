# WorkWise

WorkWise by AMN Healthcare is a healthcare workforce management platform that combines predictive scheduling, workforce and vendor management, and AI-driven tools to help healthcare organizations manage staffing across various types, including contingent, internal agency, and float pools. It supports workforce optimization by quantifying staffing demands and enhancing engagement with self-service features aimed at clinicians.
