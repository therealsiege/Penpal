# TrustCommerce

TrustCommerce is a healthcare-focused payment processing platform that centralizes and secures patient payments across multiple channels including point-of-care, online, mobile, and through EHR integrations. It supports providers with payment acceptance (cards, digital wallets, recurring payments), secure tokenization, and tools to reduce friction in pre-service, time-of-service, and post-visit billing.
