# Proscia

Proscia's Concentriq is an enterprise pathology platform designed to unify people, data, and applications within digital and computational pathology workflows. The platform supports use cases such as diagnostic operations, research, and AI development, enabling pathologists and researchers to manage and analyze pathology data efficiently.
