# Activate Care

Activate Care provides tools and services for coordinating community health efforts, focusing on social determinants of health (SDOH). Their platform, CareLink, facilitates care coordination, closed-loop referral management, and performance tracking across organizations. Path Assist supports health plans with structured, evidence-based interventions to address social care needs. Additional offerings include community assessments, population analytics, and workforce training.
