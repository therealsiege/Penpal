# Canary Speech

Canary Speech is an API-first health technology platform that uses AI and machine learning to analyze vocal biomarkers from brief conversational speech to provide objective, quantifiable insights into cognitive and behavioral health conditions such as anxiety, depression, stress, mild cognitive impairment, and neurodegenerative disorders.
