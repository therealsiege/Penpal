# iVitaFi Patient Finance

iVitaFi offers patient financing designed for hospitals/supporting providers that removes financial risk for them. Under its non-recourse model, the hospital gets paid upfront even if a patient defaults. Patients of all credit profiles can access fixed 0% interest payment plans.
