# Triple Blind Exchange

Selfii Triple Blind Exchange is a platform that facilitates secure access to protected health data without exposing sensitive patient information. It enables healthcare organizations, researchers, and AI developers to analyze and utilize health data while maintaining compliance with privacy regulations. The platform uses encryption and privacy-preserving techniques to ensure that neither data providers nor data consumers can directly access or identify the raw data.
