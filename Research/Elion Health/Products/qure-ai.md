# Qure.ai

Qure.ai develops AI-based healthcare solutions primarily for interpreting medical imaging, such as X-rays, CT scans, and MRIs. Their tools include
qXR
for detecting abnormalities in chest X-rays and
qER
for analyzing head CT scans for conditions like bleeds and fractures.
