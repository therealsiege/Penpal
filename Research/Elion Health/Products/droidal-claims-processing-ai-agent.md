# Droidal Claims Processing AI Agent

[Contact](https://droidal.com/claims-processing-ai-agent/)

Droidal's Claims Processing AI Agent automates end-to-end medical claims submission by extracting structured data from EHR and billing systems, validating it against payer-specific rules and coding standards, and submitting claims through clearinghouses or directly to payers. It identifies and corrects errors, manages batch submissions, tracks claim statuses in real time, and maintains an audit trail for compliance.
