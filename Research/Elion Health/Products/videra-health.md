# Videra Health

Videra Health is a patient engagement and assessment platform used in behavioral health and clinical monitoring workflows, including post-discharge follow-up, between-visit monitoring, and remote assessments. It provides multimodal patient interactions through a chatbot named Ava that supports text, voice, and video.
