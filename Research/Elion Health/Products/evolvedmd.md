# evolvedMD

evolvedMD provides behavioral health integration services by embedding licensed behavioral health professionals directly into primary care practices. The company operates on a collaborative care model, working alongside primary care providers to deliver evidence-based mental health services, including screening, brief interventions, and care coordination.
