# SlicedHealth Price Transparency

SlicedHealth’s Price Transparency solution helps hospitals and medical practices comply with regulatory mandates by publishing searchable price data for procedures, drugs, and bundled services. It allows patients to access cost estimates, improves patient trust, and ensures institutional compliance with the CMS price transparency rules.
