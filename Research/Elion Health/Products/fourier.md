# Fourier

Fourier is a clinical summarization tool used in pre-visit and intake workflows that converts unstructured records (PDFs, faxes, scanned notes, and HIE feeds) into standardized, specialty-specific summaries. It extracts and labels discrete findings, summarizes records with links back to source documents and triages them for referral management, and provides structured outputs to support downstream workflows.
