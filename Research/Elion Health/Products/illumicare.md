# Stanson Health CDS

Stanson Health CDS is a clinical decision support solution that embeds evidence-based, patient-specific alerts and recommendations into EHR ordering and documentation workflows at the point of care. It provides real-time alerts and analytics for medication, laboratory, and imaging stewardship, supports coding and documentation guidance, and assists with risk-adjustment/HCC identification and prior-authorization workflows.
