# XOLTAR

Xoltar provides an AI-based patient engagement platform designed to function as a virtual accountability partner. It aims to replicate personalized interactions similar to those provided by healthcare staff, focusing on behavior change and health management. The platform offers customizable AI companions that support patients by setting health goals, tracking progress, and providing real-time feedback.
