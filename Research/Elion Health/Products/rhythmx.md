# RhythmX

RhythmX AI offers a precision care platform that integrates predictive and generative AI to assist physicians in delivering personalized patient care. The platform provides advanced patient risk stratification, surfaces relevant data at the point of care, and offers AI-driven recommendations for interventions such as lab tests, imaging, social care, follow-ups, and medications
