# Collectly Suite

Collectly is a patient financial engagement and revenue cycle management platform that automates billing, payment collection, and patient communication for hospitals, billing companies, and medical groups. It integrates with existing EHR and practice management systems to manage digital check-in, in-person payments, and post-service billing.
