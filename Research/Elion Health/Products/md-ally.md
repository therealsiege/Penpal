# MD Ally

MD Ally is a cloud-based platform that integrates with 911 dispatch systems to connect non-emergency callers to telehealth services and community resources in real time. By diverting low-acuity cases from emergency departments, it helps reduce strain on emergency services and ensures patients receive appropriate care promptly.
