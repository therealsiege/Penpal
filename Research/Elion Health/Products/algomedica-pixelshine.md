# Algomedica PixelShine

Algomedica PixelShine is an AI-based CT image noise reduction software designed to improve image quality in low-dose and ultra-low-dose CT scans. It applies deep learning–based post-processing to denoise CT images from any DICOM-compliant scanner and returns enhanced images directly to PACS for radiologist interpretation.
