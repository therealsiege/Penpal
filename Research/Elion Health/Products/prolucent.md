# Prolucent

Prolucent Health is a workforce management platform helping healthcare organizations control labor costs, optimize staffing, and strengthen hiring through advanced analytics and a comprehensive workforce management system. Their solutions include talent management, vendor management, and market analytics to modernize and streamline the recruitment, retention, and management of healthcare talent.
