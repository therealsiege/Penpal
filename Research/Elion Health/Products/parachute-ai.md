# Parachute AI

Parachute is an AI governance infrastructure platform built for healthcare organizations (especially hospitals) to evaluate, approve, deploy, monitor and audit clinical AI tools. It offers modules for vendor discovery and prioritization, automated risk scoring (bias, safety, regulatory, performance), continuous monitoring of deployed models (drift, bias, performance), and immutable audit trails documenting every decision, workflow, and metric.
