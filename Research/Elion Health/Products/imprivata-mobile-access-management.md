# Imprivata Mobile Access Management

Imprivata Mobile Access Management is a solution that enables secure authentication and access control for clinical mobile devices in healthcare settings. It allows healthcare staff to tap a badge or use single sign-on (SSO) methods to access shared mobile devices and clinical applications without repeatedly entering passwords.
