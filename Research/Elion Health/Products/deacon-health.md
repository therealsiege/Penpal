# Deacon Health

Deacon Health is a specialty care management company focused initially on musculoskeletal (MSK) care and expanding into polychronic disease management. Its platform supports value-based care by coordinating pre- and post-acute services, implementing quality measures, ensuring compliance, and offering analytics to identify care improvement opportunities.
