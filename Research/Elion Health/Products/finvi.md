# Finvi

Finvi is an AI- and RPA-powered unified Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) software platform designed to optimize financial outcomes while improving the experience for both staff and patients. It centralizes workflows across disparate systems—such as EHRs, practice management, and billing tools—into a single, intelligent interface with real-time dashboards, automated denial and self-pay follow-up, dynamic patient segmentation, and omnichannel engagement.
