# Plaid

Plaid is a financial-data connectivity platform that enables applications to securely access users’ bank account and transaction data, process payments (including via ACH, RTP, and FedNow), perform identity verification, and support credit underwriting. In a healthcare context, Plaid can power use-cases such as enabling patients to pay medical bills or receive provider reimbursements through bank-linked transfers, facilitating collection and disbursement of funds in care settings, and supporting healthcare payers or benefits administrators with embedded payment flows.
