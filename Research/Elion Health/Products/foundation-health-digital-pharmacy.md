# Foundation Health Digital Pharmacy

Foundation Health's Digital Pharmacy is a white-labeled pharmacy platform that enables organizations to deliver a fully customizable online pharmacy experience, including prescription and over-the-counter (OTC) fulfillment. The platform supports nationwide shipping, integrates with telehealth services, and provides 24/7 HIPAA-secure customer support.
