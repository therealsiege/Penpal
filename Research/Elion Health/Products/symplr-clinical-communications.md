# Symplr Clinical Communications

Symplr Clinical Communications is a secure communication platform designed to improve collaboration among healthcare teams. It provides real-time messaging, voice calls, and alert notifications, integrating with electronic health records (EHRs), nurse call systems, and other hospital technologies.
