# CareForms

CareForms is a digital forms and workflow platform designed for healthcare providers to collect and manage patient information electronically. It enables clinics and health systems to digitize intake forms, consent documents, and other administrative paperwork, with features like e-signatures, conditional logic, and integration with EHR systems.
