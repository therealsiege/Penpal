# Acuity Behavioral Health

Acuity Behavioral Health helps make better staffing decisions with its Behavioral Health Acuity Index (BHAI), designed for inpatient behavioral health settings. The tool collects input from on-the-ground nurses to generate real-time acuity scores that reflect the severity of patient conditions across a ward. These scores help healthcare facilities determine appropriate nurse staffing levels to enhance patient care and safety.
