# RISA

RISA Labs offers AI-powered automation solutions for managing prior authorizations in healthcare, focusing on reducing denial rates, administrative workload, and compliance risks. Its platform integrates with major EHR systems and utilizes advanced AI to optimize first-time submission success and streamline workflows.
