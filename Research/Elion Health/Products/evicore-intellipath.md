# EviCore intelliPath

EviCore intelliPath is an electronic prior-authorization (ePA) automation solution that integrates into provider workflows and EHR systems to handle submission, tracking, and decision-communication for prior authorizations. It captures case data directly from orders/schedules in the EHR, applies logic/clinical review, and sends approvals or requests for additional documentation in real time.
