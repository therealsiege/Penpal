# CalmWave

CalmWave offers an AI-powered operational command center designed to optimize operations in the ICU by analyzing real-time and historical data to reduce alarm fatigue, improve resource utilization, and enhance patient care. It provides dashboards and predictive analytics to help hospital administrators and staff make data-driven decisions regarding workload distribution and operational efficiency.
