# Rhinogram

Rhinogram is a virtual care and patient engagement platform used at the digital front door for patient intake and telehealth workflows. It provides secure text and multimedia messaging, live video visits, and outbound calling that displays the practice phone number; supports E-forms with e-signatures; and provides smart routing to direct messages and tasks to appropriate team members.
