# Aiomic360

Aiomic360 is a software platform designed to optimize clinical data abstraction for hospitals through integrated tools for case selection, data abstraction, and reporting. It leverages AI to recommend procedure and diagnosis codes from clinical notes, streamlining case selection and reducing manual errors. During abstraction, Aiomic360 uses AI to surface relevant data and suggest values for complex variables, significantly accelerating the process.
