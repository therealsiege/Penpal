# AngelEye Health

AngelEye Health provides a family engagement and clinical-support platform designed for NICUs and other pediatric units. It offers live, secure bedside video access for families, integrated one-way messaging, and a centralized hub that supports both clinical and family workflows. The platform includes EHR-integrated clinical views, tools for managing parent/guardian access, and a multilingual family interface to improve communication during the hospital stay.
