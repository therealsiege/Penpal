# VirtuAlly

VirtuAlly provides turnkey telesitting and virtual nursing services designed to enhance patient safety and support overstretched clinical teams. Operating 24/7 via a staffed command center, the platform offers continuous remote monitoring and virtual clinical support to reduce patient falls, improve discharge rates, and alleviate staffing pressures.
