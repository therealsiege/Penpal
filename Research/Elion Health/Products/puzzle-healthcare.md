# Puzzle Healthcare

Puzzle Healthcare provides a cloud-based platform designed to help healthcare organizations manage revenue cycle operations, including patient eligibility verification, claims processing, and payment reconciliation. The platform integrates with electronic health records (EHR) and other healthcare systems to provide automated workflows and real-time insights into financial performance.
