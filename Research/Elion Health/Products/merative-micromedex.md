# Merative MicroMedex

Merative Micromedex is an evidence-based clinical decision support platform used at the point of care to provide drug, toxicology, and patient-education content. It provides 2,500+ drug monographs with dosing and FDA and off-label information, IV compatibility and drug–drug interaction checking, and a suite of 700+ clinical calculators.
