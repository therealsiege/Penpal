# Pack Health

Pack Health is a patient engagement platform that offers personalized health coaching to assist individuals in managing chronic conditions. Members are paired with certified Health Advisors who provide weekly support through calls, texts, and emails, focusing on behavior modification and addressing social determinants of health.
