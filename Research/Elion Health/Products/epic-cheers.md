# Epic Cheers

Epic Cheers is a CRM platform designed for healthcare organizations using Epic. It helps providers manage patient outreach, engagement, and communication by integrating data from Epic to personalize interactions. The platform includes tools for scheduling, marketing, patient feedback, and care coordination, allowing healthcare organizations to improve patient experience and retention. Epic Cheers is primarily used by hospitals and health systems to enhance patient relationships, automate outreach, and track engagement metrics.
