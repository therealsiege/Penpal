# Reteta

Reteta is an ambient scribing and clinical documentation platform used during patient encounters to generate SOAP notes, after-visit summaries, and coding drafts within clinical workflows. It captures ambient audio, converts conversations into structured documentation, and provides real-time ICD-10 and CPT coding suggestions.
