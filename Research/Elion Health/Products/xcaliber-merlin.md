# XCaliber Merlin

Merlin by XCaliber Health is an AI-powered workflow automation platform designed for healthcare organizations. It integrates with EHR systems to support clinicians, nurses, case managers, and administrators by automating chart reviews, care coordination, scheduling, prior authorizations, and quality reporting.
