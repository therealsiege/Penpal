# Qualtrics Patient Experience

Qualtrics Patient Experience is a comprehensive platform designed to capture, analyze, and act on patient feedback throughout their healthcare journey. The platform integrates seamlessly with clinical workflows, allowing healthcare providers to gather real-time feedback at critical moments, such as after a patient visit or during a hospital stay.
