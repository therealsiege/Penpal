# MedCurrent

MedCurrent is a clinical decision support vendor whose OrderWise/iRefer platform embeds evidence-based diagnostic referral guidelines into electronic order and EHR workflows at the point of care. It provides guideline-based referral pathways for imaging, laboratory, pathology, and echocardiography, includes an Authoring Studio to localize and maintain guidelines, and provides business intelligence for performance and capacity planning.
