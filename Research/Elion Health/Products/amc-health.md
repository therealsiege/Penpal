# AMC Health

AMC Health provides a comprehensive suite of remote patient monitoring (RPM) and virtual care solutions aimed at improving clinical outcomes and enhancing patient engagement. Their platform leverages advanced analytics and AI to deliver real-time data and actionable insights, supporting chronic disease management, post-acute care, and high-risk pregnancies.
