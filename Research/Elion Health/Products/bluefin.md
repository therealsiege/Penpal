# Bluefin

Bluefin provides secure payment and data protection solutions for healthcare organizations, focusing on PCI-validated point-to-point encryption (P2PE) and tokenization. Its platform helps healthcare providers ensure HIPAA compliance while safeguarding payment and sensitive patient data.
