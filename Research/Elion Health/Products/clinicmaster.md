# ClinicMaster

ClinicMaster is a cloud-based practice management software designed for a variety of healthcare providers, including physiotherapists, chiropractors, psychologists, and medical specialists. It offers tools for appointment scheduling, billing, charting, and case management. ClinicMaster also integrates telehealth capabilities, patient portals for secure communication, and marketing tools to engage clients.
