# Alpha Nodus Gravity Booking

Gravity Booking is an AI-driven scheduling and call-handling tool for healthcare providers (especially imaging/diagnostic centers). It automates appointment booking and changes over the phone, uses predictive scheduling to fill gaps, and manages the handoff between AI and human agents.
