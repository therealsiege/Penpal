# Aircall

Aircall is a cloud-based phone and communication platform that supports healthcare organizations by enabling encrypted, HIPAA-compliant voice and messaging across mobile and desktop devices. It offers virtual phone systems, AI-powered voice agents, and real-time conversation intelligence, allowing healthcare organizations to manage patient support lines, streamline provider communications, and maintain consistent documentation of interactions.
