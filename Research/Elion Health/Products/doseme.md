# DoseMe

DoseMeRx is a Bayesian dosing platform designed to assist clinicians in individualizing medication dosing by analyzing patient-specific data and drug concentrations. It integrates with the EHR, enabling seamless access to patient information and facilitating precise dose calculations within existing clinical workflows.
