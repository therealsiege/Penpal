# PillSpark

[Contact](https://pillspark.com/contact)

PillSpark is an intelligent workflow automation platform for long-term care pharmacies that fits in pre-bill, order-entry, and document-intake workflows. It automates new orders, renewals, and refills and provides SIG interpretation, NDC selection, duplicate detection, and dispensing logic.
