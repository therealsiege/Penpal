# Scheduling Wizard

Scheduling Wizard is a scheduling platform designed specifically for residency and fellowship programs, helping program directors, chief residents, and faculty create block, call, clinic, and attending schedules. It automates schedule generation by factoring in resident preferences, educational requirements, ACGME duty hour compliance, and contractual obligations for faculty.
