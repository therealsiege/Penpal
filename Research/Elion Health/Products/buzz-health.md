# Buzz Health

Buzz Health is a prescription technology company that provides integrated platforms to support prescription pricing, benefit determination, and claims adjudication across pharmacies, health plans, and pharmacy benefit managers. Its product suite includes consumer-facing discount programs, prescription price comparison and marketplace tools, and a real-time adjudication platform for processing discount and copay card claims.
