# CaringAI

CaringAI is a patient engagement platform that uses artificial intelligence to automate outreach and support for care navigation, preventive care, and chronic condition management. The system delivers personalized, multilingual voice and text communications designed to improve follow-through on clinical recommendations, such as screenings, appointments, or medication adherence.
