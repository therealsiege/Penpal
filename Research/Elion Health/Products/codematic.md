# Codematic

Codematic is an AI-powered medical coding and revenue cycle automation solution that integrates directly into clinical documentation workflows. It assigns CPT, ICD-10, and HCC codes in real time from clinician encounters and supports accurate, compliant claims submission, reducing denials and lost revenue from under-coding or documentation gaps.
