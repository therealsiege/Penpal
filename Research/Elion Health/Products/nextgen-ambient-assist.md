# NextGen Ambient Assist

NextGen Ambient Assist is an AI-powered tool designed to convert natural patient-provider conversations into structured SOAP notes. Using a mobile device, it captures spoken interactions and generates notes with 90% accuracy, which can be reviewed and edited by providers before being integrated into the EHR.
