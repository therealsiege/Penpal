# Tebra

Tebra is a cloud-based, all-in-one EHR and practice platform used by independent and specialty practices to support clinical, scheduling, billing, and patient engagement workflows. It provides clinical charting and telehealth, manages scheduling and online intake, and supports billing, claims management, and payment capture.
