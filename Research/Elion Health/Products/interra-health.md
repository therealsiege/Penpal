# Interra Health

Interra Health is a health IT platform formed from the merger of DoseSpot, Arrive Health, and pVerify that connects e-prescribing, real-time pharmacy benefit data, and medical benefit verification within clinical workflows. The platform provides providers with real-time coverage, cost, and access information at the point of care, supports electronic prescribing and prescription routing, and integrates medical and pharmacy benefit insights to inform therapy selection.
