# MedicalDirector

MedicalDirector provides healthcare software solutions primarily used in general practice and specialist settings. Its key offerings include
MedicalDirector Clinical
, an on-premise electronic health record (EHR) system, and
MedicalDirector Helix
, a cloud-based practice management platform.
