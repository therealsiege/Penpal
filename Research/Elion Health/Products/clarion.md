# Clarion

[Contact](https://calendly.com/ryan-clarion/30-minute-meeting-zoom)

Clarion is a healthcare-focused conversational AI platform that automates high-volume, routine patient communications across voice, SMS, and web channels. It supports workflows such as appointment scheduling, rescheduling and cancellations, patient intake and registration, referral coordination, benefits verification, pharmacy messaging, billing and refill requests, and automated reminders between visits. It also supports multilingual conversations and engages patients continuously between visits.
