# Experity Health Urgent Care EMR

Experity Health’s Urgent Care EMR is an electronic medical record and practice management system built specifically for urgent care and occupational medicine clinics. It features complaint-driven charting templates that allow providers to document common visits in under 60 seconds, real-time chart updates for multi-user workflows, and integrated coding to reduce errors. The system includes tools for insurance verification, payment processing, appointment scheduling, and performance analytics.
