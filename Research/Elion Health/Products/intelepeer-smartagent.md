# IntelePeer SmartAgent

IntelePeer SmartAgent is an AI-powered platform for contact centers that automates inbound interactions using conversational and generative AI. It supports voice, SMS, MMS, and social messaging channels, integrates with external services like Google Dialogflow, and offers advanced features such as call transcription, dynamic rephrasing, and custom data augmentation.
