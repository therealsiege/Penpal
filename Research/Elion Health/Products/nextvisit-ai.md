# Nextvisit AI

Nextvisit AI is an AI-powered clinical documentation assistant that listens and generates structured medical notes in real time, helping clinicians reduce charting burden while maintaining accuracy. It captures critical timelines, identifies patterns, and surfaces relevant data while integrating into existing workflows so providers can focus more on patient care.
