# HealthVerity Identity Manager

HealthVerity Identity Manager is a privacy-preserving patient matching and identity resolution solution designed for the healthcare and life sciences industries. It links de-identified patient records across disparate datasets using tokenization and a proprietary identity resolution engine, enabling longitudinal views of individuals without exposing personally identifiable information (PII).
