# Intraprise Health HIPAA One

Intraprise Health's HIPAA One software is a cloud-based platform designed to help healthcare organizations achieve and maintain HIPAA compliance through a structured, automated approach. The software simplifies the complex process of HIPAA compliance by offering step-by-step guidance for conducting security risk assessments, privacy assessments, and workforce training.
