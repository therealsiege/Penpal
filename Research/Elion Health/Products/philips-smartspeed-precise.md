# Philips SmartSpeed Precise

Philips SmartSpeed Precise is an MRI acceleration and reconstruction technology used in acquisition and reconstruction workflows. It applies Dual AI reconstruction—Adaptive CS‑NET at the MR signal source and Precise Net on raw complex data—and provides up to 3× faster scans and up to 80% sharper images compared with conventional Philips imaging. Capabilities include a single‑parameter workflow that optimizes image quality and AI‑based noise removal while preserving fine detail during reconstruction.
