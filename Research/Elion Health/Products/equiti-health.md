# Equiti

Equiti, formerly operating as Cloudbreak Health, offers a HIPAA-compliant Video Remote Interpretation (VRI) platform with on-demand access to qualified interpreters in over 250 languages, including sign language. This service integrates with existing EHRs and telehealth platforms, and is available 24/7 from any device.
