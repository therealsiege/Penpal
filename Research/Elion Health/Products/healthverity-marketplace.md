# HealthVerity Marketplace

HealthVerity Marketplace is the nation’s largest real-world data (RWD) marketplace, enabling life sciences, government, and insurance organizations to build custom patient cohorts with full transparency and traceability. It offers access to over 340 million de-identified patients and 200+ billion transactions across claims, pharmacy, labs, SDOH, and more.
