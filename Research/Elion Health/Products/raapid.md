# RAAPID

RAAPID is a risk-adjustment platform used in pre-bill and retrospective chart review workflows to identify chronic conditions, suggest HCC codes, and calculate RAF scores. It applies neuro-symbolic methods and natural language and document analysis to extract clinical evidence.
