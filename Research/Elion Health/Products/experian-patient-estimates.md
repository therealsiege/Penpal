# Experian Health Patient Estimates

Experian Health Patient Estimates is a web-based tool designed for hospitals and medical facilities to provide accurate, upfront cost estimates to patients. It combines data from the provider’s chargemaster, claims history, payer contracts, and the patient’s insurance benefits.
