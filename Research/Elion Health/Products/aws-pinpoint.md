# AWS Pinpoint

Amazon Pinpoint is a customer engagement service used in healthcare for targeted, multichannel communications and analytics within patient outreach and application workflows. It sends messages by SMS, voice, push, email, and in-app channels and supports campaign orchestration with multi-step journeys.
