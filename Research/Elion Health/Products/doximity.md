# Doximity

Doximity is a digital platform designed for healthcare professionals, offering services such as a clinician directory, HIPAA-compliant messaging, and telemedicine tools like Dialer Video and Voice. These allow providers to connect with patients via calls or video while keeping personal contact information private.
