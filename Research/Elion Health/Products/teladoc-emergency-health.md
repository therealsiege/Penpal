# Teladoc Emergency Telehealth

Teladoc Health provides virtual emergency services that enable healthcare professionals to deliver immediate care through telehealth technology. These services include virtual nursing, critical care consultations, and telestroke support, allowing specialists to remotely assist in emergency situations.
