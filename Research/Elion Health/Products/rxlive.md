# RxLive

RxLive is a telepharmacy and population-health pharmacy platform that embeds clinical pharmacists into care teams to support medication management across care workflows. It provides analytics (RxLive.ai) to identify high‑risk patients, offers virtual pharmacist consultations and comprehensive medication management via My.RxLive, and documents pharmacist interventions for value‑based care.
