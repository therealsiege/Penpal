# Vitalacy Staff Forecasting

Vitalacy Staff Forecasting is a predictive analytics tool that helps healthcare organizations optimize staff scheduling and resource allocation. The platform uses historical data and machine learning to forecast patient demand and staffing needs, improving workforce efficiency and reducing operational costs.
