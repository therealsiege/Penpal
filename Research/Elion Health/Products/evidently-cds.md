# Evidently CDS

Evidently CDS reads and interprets both structured and unstructured patient data, including notes, faxes, and imaging reports. It creates problem-oriented views by extracting relevant clinical concepts—such as disorders, labs, and procedures—and delivering concise, actionable summaries directly within the EHR. As a SMART on FHIR app, it integrates seamlessly into platforms like Epic, allowing clinicians to access these summaries in real-time, minimizing the need for manual chart navigation and accelerating decision-making during patient care.
