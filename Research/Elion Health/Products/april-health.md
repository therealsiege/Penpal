# April Health

April Health is a virtual integrated behavioral health service that fits into primary care workflows for intake, referral, and collaborative care management. It provides ongoing psychosocial support delivered by master’s-level behavioral health care managers, documents progress in the EHR, and provides consulting psychiatrists who review cases and recommend medications through the EHR.
