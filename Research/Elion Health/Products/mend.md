# Mend

Mend is a patient engagement and telehealth platform used in patient intake, scheduling, virtual visit delivery, and digital check-in workflows for behavioral and mental health organizations. It provides one-click virtual visits that require no patient app or download, captures digital intake forms and routes screening data to electronic records, and offers 24/7 self-scheduling plus automated reminders and multilingual messaging.
