# Ethermed

Contact

Ethermed offers an AI-driven solution that automates the prior authorization process in healthcare, aiming to reduce up to 90% of manual administrative tasks. Operating seamlessly within existing systems, it gathers necessary clinical data, completes authorization forms, and submits them without manual input, thereby minimizing delays in patient care
