# Synaptec Health

Synaptec Health offers AI-powered automated medical coding solutions that process patient charts rapidly and accurately. The platform supports ICD-10, E/M, and CPT coding, integrating with a variety of healthcare systems through standard formats like HL7 and JSON. It provides real-time auditing, helps detect downcoding and missing documentation, and offers pay-per-use pricing without setup fees.
