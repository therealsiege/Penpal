# MantraComply

MantraComply is a healthcare credentialing and payer enrollment platform that uses AI to automate verification, application management, and compliance monitoring for providers, health systems, and payers. Its workflows cover primary source verification of licenses, certifications, and education; preparation and submission of payer-specific applications; enrollment with Medicare, Medicaid, and commercial insurers; and ongoing re-credentialing and compliance tracking.
