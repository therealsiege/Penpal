# PocketHealth Conductor

PocketHealth Conductor is an AI automation platform for healthcare operations that uses coordinated “agentic AI” to perform administrative and clerical workflows from start to finish. Built on PocketHealth’s patient interaction and imaging infrastructure, Conductor automates tasks such as requisition processing, scheduling, intake, patient communication, and call center operations.
