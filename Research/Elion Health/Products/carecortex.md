# CareCortex

CareCortex is an AI-powered healthcare documentation and automation platform designed to reduce administrative workload for clinicians. It offers automated pre-charting by pulling relevant patient data from referral notes and medical history, AI-driven CPT and ICD-10 coding for billing, and multilingual transcription for accurate documentation.
