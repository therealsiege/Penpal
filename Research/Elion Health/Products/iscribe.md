# iScribe

iScribe is a clinical documentation solution used during point-of-care encounters to generate visit notes and place them into the electronic health record. It uses speech-to-text and generative ambient technology to create SOAP or problem-oriented notes, populate sections such as history of present illness, physical exam, and assessment and plan, and place completed documentation into the record.
