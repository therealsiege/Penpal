# SamaCare

SamaCare is a platform that simplifies prior authorizations for buy-and-bill drugs, imaging, and select procedures. It helps providers by managing forms, tracking statuses, and supporting communication with payers. It automates form recommendations, auto-fills information, and updates tracking with responses.
