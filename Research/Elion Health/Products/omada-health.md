# Omada Health

Omada Health is a virtual healthcare provider specializing in chronic condition management, including prediabetes, diabetes, hypertension, and musculoskeletal issues. The platform offers personalized care plans that combine human coaching, connected devices, and interactive lessons to support behavior change and improve health outcomes.
