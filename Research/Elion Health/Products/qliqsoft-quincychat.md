# QliqSOFT QuincyChat

QliqSOFT QuincyChat is a HIPAA-compliant communication platform that allows healthcare organizations to securely engage with patients, caregivers, partners, and staff through SMS or email without requiring an app. It supports bidirectional messaging, digital forms, e-signatures, and virtual visits, all from a single interface integrated with QliqCHAT for internal communication.
