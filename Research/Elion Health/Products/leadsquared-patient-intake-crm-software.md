# LeadSquared Patient Intake CRM Software

LeadSquared Intake CRM Software is a healthcare-focused customer relationship management tool that supports new patient acquisition and intake workflows for clinics, hospitals, and health systems. It includes features for capturing and tracking patient inquiries across digital and phone channels, automating appointment scheduling, and managing doctor-patient assignment
