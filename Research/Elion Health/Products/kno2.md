# Kno2

Kno2 is a cloud-based healthcare communication and interoperability platform that functions as a connectivity layer in workflows such as patient intake, transitions of care, and referral routing. It provides Direct Secure Messaging, a REST-based communication API, and the Kno2fy portal to send and receive structured and unstructured clinical payloads, including C-CDA, HL7 v2, JSON, and PDFs, and to convert faxes into interoperable messages.
