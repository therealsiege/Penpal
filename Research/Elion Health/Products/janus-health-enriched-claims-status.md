# Janus Health Claim Intelligence

[Contact](https://info.janus-ai.com/janus-health-elion)

Claim Intelligence is an automation tool designed to eliminate manual claim status checks across payer portals. It integrates with electronic health records (EHRs) to deliver real-time updates whenever a claim status changes, including denial codes (CARC/RARC), payment details, check numbers, and patient responsibility.
