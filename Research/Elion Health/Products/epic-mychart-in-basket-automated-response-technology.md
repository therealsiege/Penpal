# Epic MyChart In-Basket Automated Response Technology

Epic's MyChart In-Basket Augmented Response Technology (ART) is an AI-powered tool integrated into Epic EHR to assist clinicians in drafting responses to patient messages. Leveraging generative AI models like GPT-4, ART analyzes patient inquiries and creates personalized draft responses that clinicians can review and edit before sending.
