# Deliberate AI

Deliberate AI uses multimodal AI and behavioral signal processing to support mental health assessments, clinical trials, and patient monitoring. It analyzes facial expressions, vocal acoustics, speech behaviors, and physiological signs to provide insights into neurological and mental health.
