# Cedar Pay

[Contact](https://www.cedar.com/contact-us/?utm_source=elion&utm_medium=webpage&utm_campaign=vendorprofile&utm_content=cedarpay)

Cedar Pay is a patient billing and payment solution that integrates health plan data with provider billing so patients can view and resolve their medical bills, see real-time deductible statuses, and access HSA/FSA balances. For providers, Cedar Pay consolidates statements and explanations of benefits, provides tailored payment options, and automates and optimizes communications using machine learning.
