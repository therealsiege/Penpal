# Lamar Health

Lamar Health offers DoPriorAuth, a platform that automates specialty medication workflows for infusion centers and specialty pharmacies. It extracts data from faxes or emails, integrates it into electronic health records or practice management systems, verifies patient eligibility and network status, and auto-populates prior authorization forms.
