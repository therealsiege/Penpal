# Belong.Life

Belong.Life offers a patient engagement platform utilizing AI, data, and active patient communities. It provides tools such as the BelongAI Cancer Mentor, a proactive conversational AI for cancer patients, and supports networks for chronic conditions like cancer and multiple sclerosis.
