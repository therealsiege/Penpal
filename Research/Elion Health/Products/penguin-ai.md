# Penguin AI

Penguin AI is a platform that extracts and structures data from existing systems (EHR, claims software, etc) then uses tailored language models and rule-based processes to automate administrative work like claims processing, prior authorizations, and denials management. It also includes tools for compliant data processing and monitoring for AI governance.
