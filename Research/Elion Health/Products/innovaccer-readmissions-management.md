# Innovaccer Readmissions Management

Innovaccer Readmissions Management is a solution built on the Innovaccer Health Cloud that helps healthcare organizations identify, manage, and reduce avoidable hospital readmissions. It uses real-time data integration from EHRs, claims, and other sources to stratify patients by risk and triggers alerts for care teams. The platform supports care plan tracking, automated follow-ups, and coordination across clinical and non-clinical staff to address drivers of readmission.
