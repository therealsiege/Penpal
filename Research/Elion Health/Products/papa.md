# Papa

Papa offers companionship and support services for members of Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and Special Needs Health Plans through its Papa Pals program. Papa Pals provide in-person or telephonic visits to help with light household tasks, errands, transportation, and companionship, addressing loneliness and other social determinants of health.
