# NuvemRx

NuvemRx provides pharmacy–enablement and referral-management services for community health centers, supporting 340B contract-pharmacy administration, in-house pharmacy operations, and specialty-care referral coordination. Its NuTPA service manages eligibility, pricing visibility, and audit-readiness for contract pharmacies, reducing administrative burden and ensuring compliant capture of 340B savings.
