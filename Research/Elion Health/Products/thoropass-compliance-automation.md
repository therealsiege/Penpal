# Thoropass Compliance Automation

Thoropass Compliance Automation is a platform that automates the compliance process for organizations. It offers features such as policy creation, risk tracking, evidence collection, and continuous monitoring. The platform integrates with various tools and provides project management capabilities to streamline compliance workflows.
