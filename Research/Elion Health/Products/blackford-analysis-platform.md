# Blackford Platform

Blackford Platform is an enterprise AI orchestration and deployment platform for medical imaging that operates within radiology and enterprise imaging workflows for pre-read, deployment, and reporting. It provides configurable dataflows, a relevancy engine that routes studies to appropriate AI applications, a zero-footprint Accept/Reject tool for reviewing AI results before they enter reading systems, and a dashboard that monitors performance and clinical metrics.
