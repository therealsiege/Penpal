# Optum Digital Auth Complete

Optum Digital Auth Complete is an AI-powered prior authorization solution that automates the full clinical clearance lifecycle directly within the EHR. It connects to more than 250 payer systems to determine authorization requirements, surface payer medical policies, bundle clinical documentation, submit requests, and track authorization status end to end.
