# Persivia CareSpace

Persivia CareSpace is a population health management platform designed to support healthcare organizations participating in complex value-based care (VBC) models. It integrates clinical, claims, and social determinants of health (SDOH) data from a wide range of sources—including EHRs, ADT feeds, and home devices—and applies AI to deliver real-time, actionable insights.
