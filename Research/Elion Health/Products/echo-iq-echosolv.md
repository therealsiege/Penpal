# Echo IQ EchoSolv

EchoSolv is an AI-powered clinical decision support platform that analyzes echocardiograms to automatically identify patients at risk for structural heart diseases, such as aortic stenosis. Built on the world’s largest dataset of echocardiograms linked to mortality, it provides real-time risk stratification, automates patient prioritization, and integrates with PACS systems without requiring EMR integration.
