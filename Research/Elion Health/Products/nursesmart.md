# Nursesmart

Nursesmart provides a platform for home health and skilled nursing agencies to manage clinical workflows, staffing, and documentation. It includes tools for visit scheduling, electronic charting, care planning, and compliance monitoring, tailored specifically for nurses working in post-acute and home care environments. The platform also features mobile access for field staff and supports back-office functions such as billing and payroll integration.
