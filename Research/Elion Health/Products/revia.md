# Revia

Revia is an AI-powered voice assistant designed to handle complex phone calls for healthcare providers. It automates tasks such as verifying provider credentials, securing prior authorizations, handling billing inquiries, and coordinating referrals with insurance companies, PBMs, and pharmacies.
