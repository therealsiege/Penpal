# Enzo Scribe

Enzo Scribe by Enzo Health is an AI-powered ambient documentation tool designed specifically for home health care agencies. It converts voice notes and images at the point of care into structured charts aligned with the Outcome and Assessment Information Set (OASIS) workflow.
