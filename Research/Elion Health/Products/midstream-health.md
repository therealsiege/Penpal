# Midstream Health

Midstream Health is a healthcare finance platform that uses AI to integrate both structured and unstructured data across an enterprise. It provides real-time contribution margin insights, enabling leaders to make data-driven decisions, uncover financial opportunities, and streamline performance improvement by linking financial and operational teams.
