# Cardiac Care Alliance

Cardiac Care Alliance is a cardiovascular-focused healthcare organization that partners with providers, payers, health systems, and employers to deliver value-based cardiac care. It supports the entire care continuum by leveraging data-driven tools and care coordination services to improve access, reduce unnecessary healthcare costs, and enhance health outcomes for patients with cardiovascular disease.
