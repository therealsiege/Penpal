# CareMessage

CareMessage is a technology nonprofit that offers a patient engagement platform tailored for safety-net organizations serving low-income populations. The platform facilitates communication through SMS text messaging, enabling healthcare providers to send appointment reminders, health education content, and conduct surveys to enhance patient engagement and adherence. It also supports bi-directional messaging, allowing patients to respond and interact with providers.
