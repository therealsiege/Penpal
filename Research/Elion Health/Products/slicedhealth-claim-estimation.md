# SlicedHealth Claim Estimation

Contact

SlicedHealth Claim Estimation is a tool that predicts a patient’s financial obligation before services are rendered by using contract data, payer rules, and real-time claims logic. It works together with their Contract Variance and Price Transparency modules to improve upfront collections, reduce patient leakage, and enhance revenue predictability.
