# Retrieve Dx

Retrieve Dx is an EMR-integrated clinical documentation tool used in pre-admit, admission, and ED-to-hospital workflows to extract and structure relevant clinical history. It uses natural language processing and search algorithms to analyze physician notes, laboratory results, imaging reports, and EKGs, and flags relevant comorbidities and supporting data for clinician review.
