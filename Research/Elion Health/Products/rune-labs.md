# Rune Labs

Rune Labs is a precision neurology vendor, particularly for the treatment and management of brain disorders such as Parkinson’s disease. Their platform integrates data from multiple sources, including electrophysiology devices and wearables like the Apple Watch, to provide a comprehensive view of patient health.
