# TheraDriver

TheraDriver is a mental health-focused software platform that provides tools for behavioral health organizations to manage clinical and administrative tasks. Its core capabilities include appointment scheduling, telehealth integration, electronic health records (EHR), treatment planning, progress note documentation, and outcomes tracking.
