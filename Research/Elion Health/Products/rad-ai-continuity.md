# Rad AI Continuity

Rad AI Continuity is a follow-up management platform used after radiology reporting to close the loop on incidental or actionable findings. It uses proprietary natural language processing and AI models trained on large report corpora to identify and classify findings, assigns follow-up dates based on radiologist recommendations and consensus guidelines, and displays cases on a central dashboard with status tracking and audit logs.
