# Medical Guardian

Medical Guardian provides personal emergency response systems (PERS) designed to assist individuals in emergency situations, particularly seniors and those with medical conditions. Their product range includes in-home systems with base units and wearable help buttons, as well as mobile devices equipped with GPS tracking for use outside the home
