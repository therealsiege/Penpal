# TJM Labs

TJM Labs is a pharmacy automation company that builds AI agents (bots) to automate routine pharmacy administrative tasks—such as prescription intake, fax processing, patient support queries, order/shipping tasks, and medication reconciliation. Their bots work within existing pharmacy systems (no need to rip and replace) and route exceptions back to staff. They aim to reduce manual workload, error risk, and staffing stress while scaling throughput.
