# IntelliTek.ai

IntelliTek.ai offers an enterprise-focused AI automation platform that provides identity management, access control, data integration, conversational AI, and document search capabilities. The platform includes tailored tools such as IntelliTek Health for improving interoperability and communication in healthcare settings, IntelliTek Data Integration for unifying data across systems, and IntelliTek Search.ai for document search enhanced with generative AI.
