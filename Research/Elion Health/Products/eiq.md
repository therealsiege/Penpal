# EIQ

Ensemble Health Partners offers RCM Intelligence solutions designed to enhance financial performance for healthcare providers. Their platform provides real-time analytics, process automation, and predictive insights to optimize billing, collections, and denial management. It integrates with EHRs and other systems to improve revenue operations while ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations.
