# Strongroom AI

StrongRoom AI is a healthcare platform focused on medication management and enhancing patient outcomes through advanced analytics and AI. It is widely used in pharmacies, hospitals, and aged care facilities to manage controlled substances, opioid replacement therapy, and medication adherence.
