# ClinicalKey AI

ClinicalKey AI is an Elsevier clinical decision support tool, developed with OpenEvidence, used at the point of care and as a reference for bedside decision-making, literature lookup, and clinician education. It provides a conversational natural-language search that generates summarized answers with linked citations, incorporates patient context (age, comorbidities, medications), and supports follow-up questions and clinician personalization.
