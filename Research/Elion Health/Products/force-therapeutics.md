# Force Therapeutics

Force Therapeutics is a digital patient engagement platform focused on supporting musculoskeletal (MSK) care through remote therapeutic monitoring, personalized care plans, and a robust library of clinically validated content. It integrates with EHRs to provide actionable recovery insights and automates data collection for PROMs compliance.
