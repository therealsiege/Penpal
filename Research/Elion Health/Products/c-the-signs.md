# C the Signs

C the Signs is a clinical decision support tool designed to help healthcare professionals identify cancer risk at an early stage. It uses artificial intelligence and evidence-based guidelines to assess patient symptoms, risk factors, and clinical data, providing recommendations for appropriate investigations and referrals.
