# Cercle

Cercle is an AI technology company focused on advancing women's healthcare by transforming extensive healthcare data into real-time, high-quality insights. Their platform, the Cercle Biomedical Graph™, securely analyzes billions of anonymized data points from hospitals, clinics, and research labs worldwide.
