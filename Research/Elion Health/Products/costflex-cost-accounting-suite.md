# CostFlex Cost Accounting Suite

CostFlex Cost Accounting Suite is a healthcare-specific cost accounting and decision support platform designed for hospitals and provider organizations. It uses enhanced activity-based costing to calculate patient- and procedure-level expenses, then aggregates data into patient groups and service lines for detailed performance reporting.
