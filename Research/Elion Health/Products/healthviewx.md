# HealthViewX

HealthViewX HOPE is a digital health platform that helps healthcare organizations manage transitions of care, referrals, remote patient monitoring, and care coordination workflows. HOPE is designed to connect different healthcare providers and systems by automating referral management, enabling closed-loop communications, and providing tools for remote patient monitoring (RPM) and chronic care management (CCM).
