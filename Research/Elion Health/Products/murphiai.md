# Murphi.ai

Murphi.ai provides an AI-driven platform focused on automating medical coding and documentation for healthcare providers. It uses natural language processing to analyze clinical notes and suggest accurate ICD-10 and CPT codes in real time, aiming to support coders, physicians, and revenue cycle teams.
