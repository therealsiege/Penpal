# Vastian Events

Vastian Events is an incident and event management platform for hospitals and health systems that streamlines the reporting, triage, and resolution of clinical and operational events. It includes customizable forms, automated alerts, and real-time monitoring to help organizations comply with standards from CMS, Joint Commission, and DNV.
