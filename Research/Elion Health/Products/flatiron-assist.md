# Flatiron Assist

Flatiron Assist is a clinical decision support platform designed for oncology, integrated into the EHR. It provides clinicians with evidence-based treatment pathways, primarily using the NCCN Guidelines for oncology care, while also allowing for customization to meet specific clinical or payer preferences.
