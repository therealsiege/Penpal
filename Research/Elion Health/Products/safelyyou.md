# SafelyYou

SafelyYou provides AI-powered solutions aimed at reducing falls and associated risks in senior living communities. Their offerings include SafelyYou Respond™, which utilizes AI to detect and alert staff to falls, and SafelyYou Guardian Pro™, an advanced device designed to enhance care delivery with improved speed, reliability, and accuracy.
