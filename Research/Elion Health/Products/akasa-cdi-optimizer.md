# AKASA CDI Optimizer

AKASA CDI Optimizer is a generative AI-powered tool designed to support Clinical Documentation Integrity (CDI) teams in reviewing and enhancing inpatient documentation. It automatically analyzes 100% of inpatient encounters post-discharge and prebill by synthesizing clinical data such as vitals, labs, medications, and physician notes.
