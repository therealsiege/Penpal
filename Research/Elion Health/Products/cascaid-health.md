# Cascaid Health

Cascaid Health is a consumer-focused hybrid health program that combines clinical-grade diagnostics with personalized coaching to help users detect early health risks and optimize long-term healthspan. It aggregates data from labs, wearables, imaging, and assessments into a unified health profile, then generates a personalized roadmap supported by a multidisciplinary team including medical, performance, lifestyle, and data-science experts.
