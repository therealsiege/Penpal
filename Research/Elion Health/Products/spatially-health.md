# Spatially Health

Spatially Health offers the Equity Equalizer platform, which integrates spatial analytics, location intelligence, and machine learning to identify and measure health barriers at the individual level. This platform enables healthcare organizations to assess patient populations, develop actionable health equity plans, and provide personalized interventions.
