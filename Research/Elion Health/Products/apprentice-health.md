# Apprentice Health

Apprentice Health offers a platform designed to optimize clinical workflows in healthcare settings such as clinics, emergency departments, and inpatient units. It utilizes ambient sensors, artificial intelligence, and location systems (RTLS) to manage patient flow, reduce wait times, and increase exam room utilization. It includes workflow orchestration, optimized scheduling, and actionable analytics to identify operational inefficiencies.
