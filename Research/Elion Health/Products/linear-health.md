# Linear Health

Contact

Linear Health offers an AI-native referral coordination platform that automates the full referral-to-appointment journey for healthcare providers. The platform accepts referral inputs via fax, email or EHR, uses AI to extract patient data, engages via voice, SMS, email to schedule appointments, integrates real-time with EHR scheduling, and provides analytics to recover revenue from failed referrals.
