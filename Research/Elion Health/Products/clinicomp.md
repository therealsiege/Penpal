# CliniComp

CliniComp provides electronic health record (EHR) solutions primarily for large hospital systems and government healthcare organizations. Its core product, CliniComp\|EHR, includes modules for inpatient and outpatient care, emergency services, critical care, and anesthesia, with an emphasis on real-time clinical documentation and interoperability.
