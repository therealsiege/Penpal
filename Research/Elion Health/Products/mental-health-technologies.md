# Mental Health Technologies (MHT)

Mental Health Technologies (MHT) provides digital mental health screening and assessment tools for healthcare providers. The platform offers standardized mental health questionnaires that patients can complete electronically, with automated scoring and integration into electronic health records (EHRs).
