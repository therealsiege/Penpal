# Kode Health

Kode Health is an on-demand medical coding platform that connects healthcare providers with certified, on-demand medical coders. Healthcare providers utilize Kode Health to address workflow and staffing fluctuations by accessing their network of coders through their coding platform that also integrates with EHR systems.
