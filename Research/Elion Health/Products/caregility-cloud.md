# Caregility Cloud

Caregility Connected Care is a virtual care platform designed to support inpatient telehealth workflows across acute and post-acute settings. It integrates hospital-grade edge devices, AI-enabled monitoring (audio, video, radar), and EHR systems to support use cases like virtual nursing, patient observation, tele-ICU, and family engagement.
