# Blueshift Licensing

Blueshift Licensing offers concierge licensing services for clinicians, including physicians, nurses, counselors, and dental professionals. Services encompass new license applications, renewals, and credentialing across all states, with specific support for Interstate Medical Licensure Compact (IMLC) applications.
