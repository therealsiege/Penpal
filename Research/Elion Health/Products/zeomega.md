# ZeOmega

ZeOmega's Social Determinants of Health (SDOH) Social Care Platform integrates data from assessments, claims, and government databases to provide a comprehensive view of the social factors affecting individuals' health. The platform utilizes AI-driven workflows to coordinate care management interventions with third-party social services, in-house benefit programs, and community resources
