# Verkada

Verkada provides cloud-based physical security solutions for healthcare facilities, combining video surveillance, access control, environmental sensors, and alarms on a unified platform. Its products include smart security cameras, key card access systems, and air quality sensors, all managed through a centralized web-based dashboard.
