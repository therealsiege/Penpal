# Credo AI

Credo AI is an enterprise AI governance platform that helps organizations manage risk, compliance, and accountability across the AI lifecycle—from development to deployment. It centralizes oversight of generative AI, AI agents, and third-party vendor systems, providing structured workflows, automated risk evaluations, policy configuration, and compliance tracking aligned with frameworks like NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, EU AI Act, and emerging U.S. AI laws.
