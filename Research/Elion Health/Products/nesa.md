# NESA

NESA is a virtual care platform embedded directly into EHR systems like Epic. It supports live video communication, remote monitoring, virtual nursing workflows, and AI-driven analytics, allowing healthcare providers to conduct virtual assessments, manage patient rounds, and perform compliance tasks such as double-medication verification.
