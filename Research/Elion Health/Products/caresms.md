# CareSMS

CareSMS is a patient engagement platform that uses SMS-based communication to help healthcare providers improve patient adherence, appointment reminders, and care coordination. It offers automated messaging for follow-ups, medication reminders, and wellness check-ins to enhance patient outcomes and reduce no-show rates.
