# CommonWell

CommonWell is a nationwide, member-driven health information network that supports clinical record lookup and retrieval workflows at the point of care. It provides record location and retrieval services, maintains a master person index and record locator to match and deduplicate patient records across sites, and supports standards-based document query and cross-community access for retrieving clinical documents.
