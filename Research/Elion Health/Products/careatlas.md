# CareAtlas

CareAtlas offers an AI-powered patient relationship management (PRM) platform tailored for preventative care in elderly patients with chronic conditions. The platform creates automated, detailed patient profiles and generates intelligent call summaries with integrated billing support, helping providers reduce prep time and administrative burden.
