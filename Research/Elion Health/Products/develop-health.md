# Develop Health

Contact

Develop Health is an AI-powered eligibility checking and prior authorization platform for pharmacy benefit medication. It automates benefit verifications and clinical eligibility checks, and automatically calls payers to get benefit details if a fallback is required. The PA product can automatically source, fill and submit the right form based on the prescription and the patient's PBM. By integrating patient insurance data and clinical history, the platform increases first-time approval rates and reduces delays in accessing medications.
