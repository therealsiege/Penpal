# CarePilot

CarePilot is an AI-powered medical scribe designed to assist clinicians with real-time documentation. It captures patient encounters via ambient listening or direct dictation and converts speech into structured clinical notes. The platform includes natural language editing, Spanish transcription, and adaptive learning to match a provider's documentation style.
