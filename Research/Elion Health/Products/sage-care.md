# Sage Care

Sage Care is an AI-powered care navigation platform designed for health systems. It uses intelligent agents to handle patient calls and messages, provide triage grounded in clinical protocols, match patients to the right provider and coordinate scheduling and referrals, all aimed at reducing administrative burdens and improving patient access.
