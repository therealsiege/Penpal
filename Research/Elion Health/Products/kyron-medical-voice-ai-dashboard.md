# Kyron Medical Voice AI Dashboard

Kyron Medical’s Voice AI Dashboard is an extension of its revenue cycle automation platform, adding real-time voice transcription and documentation for healthcare and billing workflows. The Voice AI Agent captures spoken conversations during RCM or clinical interactions, converting them into structured, auditable documentation that integrates with EHRs and payer systems.
