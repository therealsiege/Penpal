# QGenda Scheduling

QGenda Scheduling is a healthcare workforce management platform that provides advanced scheduling solutions for healthcare organizations, including hospitals and clinics. It enables the creation of optimized schedules for physicians, nurses, and other healthcare staff, focusing on rules-based automation to ensure accurate and equitable scheduling based on provider availability, skill sets, and work targets.
