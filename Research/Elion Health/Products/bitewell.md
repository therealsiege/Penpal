# Bitewell

Bitewell offers a digital platform focused on "food-as-medicine," helping individuals make healthier food choices aligned with their personal health needs. Its key feature is the
FoodHealth Score
, a personalized scoring system that evaluates foods based on individual health conditions and dietary preferences.
