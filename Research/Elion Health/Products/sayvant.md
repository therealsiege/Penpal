# Sayvant

Sayvant is a charting tool designed for clinicians in emergency and urgent care settings. It generates complete, clinically validated notes based on diagnoses and patient dispositions, reducing time spent on documentation. The system automates tasks like differential diagnoses and medical decision-making documentation, while also integrating revenue cycle management (RCM) and quality measures
