# Greenspace Health

Greenspace Health offers a platform for Measurement-Based Care (MBC) in behavioral health, helping providers collect and use patient-reported outcome measures (PROMs) throughout the care process. Their solutions include automated client intake workflows, continuous symptom tracking, and personalized dashboards that inform clinical decisions and improve engagement.
