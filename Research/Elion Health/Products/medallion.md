# Medallion

Medallion offers a comprehensive platform for healthcare provider network operations, focusing on provider data management, credentialing, payer enrollment, and licensing. Its capabilities include automated credentialing compliant with NCQA, intelligent payer search for enrollment, cross-state licensing, and ongoing monitoring to ensure data accuracy.
