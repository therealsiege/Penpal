# Amperos Health

[Contact](https://amperoshealth.com/book-demo)

Amperos Health is a revenue recovery tool used in the post-claim denials and collections stage of the revenue cycle. It leverages AI to perform automated payer follow-up, insurance-call and IVR navigation, and collect payer-portal information to determine next actions within the practice management system based on claim status and denial reason.
