# DME Flow

DME Flow is an automation platform for DME companies that streamlines order creation, insurance verification, WIP state transitions, and billing preparation. It automatically extracts and validates key order details, corrects missing elements such as ZIP codes or diagnosis codes, and verifies ordering clinicians without manual steps.
