# Lyngo AI

Lyngo AI is an AI-driven virtual receptionist platform purpose-built for healthcare clinics, offering 24/7 automated call answering, appointment booking, rescheduling, cancellations, FAQs, and message handling integrated directly with practice management systems like Cliniko. It captures call data in real time—providing transcripts, analytics, and dashboards to track performance and conversions.
