# Infinitus

Infinitus provides an AI-driven platform designed to automate complex healthcare communications, particularly those traditionally conducted via manual phone calls. Their solutions include AI agents and copilots that handle tasks such as benefit verification, prior authorization, and prescription follow-ups by interacting directly with payors and pharmacy benefit managers (PBMs).
