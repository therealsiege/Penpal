# Teledigm Health

Teledigm Health offers a comprehensive telemedicine technology platform tailored for acute and outpatient care environments. Their solution includes hardware options ranging from tablets and laptops to full-size telemedicine carts, along with proprietary software that can also be installed on customer-owned devices.
