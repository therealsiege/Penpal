# a2z Radiology AI

a2z-Unified-Triage is a radiology triage tool used in imaging review workflows to flag and prioritize urgent abdomen–pelvis CT cases for faster review. It analyzes adult abdominal/pelvic CT studies, detects seven acute conditions (small bowel obstruction, acute cholecystitis, acute pancreatitis, acute diverticulitis, hydronephrosis, free air, unruptured abdominal aortic aneurysm), and provides binary outputs with key DICOM image slices while operating in parallel with standard care.
