# Waystar Charge Integrity

Waystar’s Charge Integrity is a revenue cycle tool for healthcare providers that automatically finds missing charges, under-coded claims, and billing errors. It uses predictive analytics, machine-learning, and a dedicated auditing team to validate errors, reconcile discrepancies, and help providers rebill payers accurately.
