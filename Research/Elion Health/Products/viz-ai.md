# Viz.ai

Viz.ai is a clinical care coordination platform used in acute and diagnostic imaging workflows such as stroke triage and radiology worklists. Its Viz LVO algorithm analyzes CT angiography to detect suspected large vessel occlusions and sends real-time alerts to on-call specialists to support transfer and treatment decisions.
