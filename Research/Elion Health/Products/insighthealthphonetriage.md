# InsightHealth Phone Triage AI Agent

InsightHealth’s Phone Triage AI Agent is an automated call-handling system for medical practices that answers inbound patient calls, conducts a symptom-based clinical assessment, and routes urgent cases to staff while managing routine inquiries autonomously. It integrates with major EHRs (such as Epic, Athena, and DrChrono) to automatically generate call summaries and place documentation directly into the chart.
