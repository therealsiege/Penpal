# CareRev

CareRev is a healthcare staffing platform that connects healthcare facilities with local, flexible clinical professionals through a technology-enabled marketplace. The platform allows registered nurses, certified nursing assistants, licensed practical nurses, technicians, and other clinical specialties to choose shifts that fit their schedules. For facilities, CareRev provides a scalable, on-demand workforce to meet staffing needs in real-time, enhancing operational efficiency and reducing staffing costs.
