# Commure Ambient AI

Commure Ambient AI is an AI-powered medical scribe designed to automate clinical documentation for healthcare providers. It transcribes patient encounters, including multi-speaker conversations lasting over three hours, and converts them into structured notes such as SOAP and progress notes. The platform supports over 60 languages and offers customizable templates to fit various specialties and workflows. Additionally, it provides automated CPT and ICD-10 coding recommendations to enhance billing accuracy.
