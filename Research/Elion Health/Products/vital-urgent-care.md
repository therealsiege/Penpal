# Vital Urgent Care

Vital Urgent Care is a patient experience platform used in urgent care workflows from check-in through discharge. It provides wait-time predictions and queue position tracking, and offers SMS-based passwordless access with automated result notifications. The platform generates plain-language lab and result explanations, multilingual patient education, and concise discharge checklists.
