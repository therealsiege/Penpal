# Bulwark Health Arc+

Bulwark Health ARC+ is an AI-powered mid–revenue cycle platform that combines utilization management, inpatient and outpatient clinical documentation improvement (CDI), coding, and denials and appeals workflows into a single system. It analyzes clinical notes, labs, and imaging to identify documentation gaps, validate DRGs, and corroborate coded conditions before and after billing.
