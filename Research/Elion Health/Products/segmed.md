# Segmed

Segmed provides de-identified, real-world medical imaging data for use in healthcare research, AI development, and clinical innovation. It aggregates data from over 2,000 healthcare locations worldwide, offering access to diverse datasets that include DICOM images, radiology reports, and patient metadata.
