# Illuminate Al

Illuminate AI is a healthcare analytics platform that leverages artificial intelligence to analyze medical imaging, clinical data, and unstructured healthcare records. It helps providers extract actionable insights by identifying patterns in patient data, supporting clinical decision-making, and improving diagnostic accuracy.
