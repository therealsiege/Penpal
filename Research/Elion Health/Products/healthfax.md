# HealthFAX

OptraHealth's HealthFAX is a conversational AI platform designed for healthcare applications, aiming to support patient engagement, clinical insights, and workflow automation. HealthFAX leverages a large knowledge base with over two million healthcare-specific Q&A entries, enabling it to assist with patient intake, triage, family history collection, and post-visit follow-ups. It also provides predictive analytics and NLP-driven AI models to generate actionable insights, often used in clinical labs, insurance assessments, and personalized patient interactions.
