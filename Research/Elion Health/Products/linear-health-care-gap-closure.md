# Linear Health Care Gap Closure

Linear Health Care Gap Closure is an AI-powered product that automates identification and closure of care gaps for organizations operating under value-based care programs. It ingests data from EHRs, payers, and quality systems to identify true care gaps, risk-stratify patients, and eliminate false positives.
