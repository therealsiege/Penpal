# SmarterDx SmarterPrebill

[Contact](https://www.smarterdx.com/contact)

SmarterPrebill by SmarterDx is an AI-driven solution that analyzes hospital patient records in the prebill phase to identify opportunities for coding and documentation improvements. It uses over 800 proprietary clinical algorithms to review provider notes, lab results, medications, orders, vitals, and radiology/pathology studies to ensure accurate billing and quality reporting.
