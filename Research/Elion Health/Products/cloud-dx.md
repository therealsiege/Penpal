# Cloud DX

Cloud DX Connected Health for remote patient monitoring (RPM) enables providers to manage chronic conditions, post-surgical care, and aging in place through real-time vital sign tracking and telehealth integration. It supports conditions like congestive heart failure, COPD, and palliative care, with tools to reduce hospital admissions and improve patient outcomes.
