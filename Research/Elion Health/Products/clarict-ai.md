# ClariCT.AI

ClariCT.AI is an AI-based image enhancement solution for computed tomography that improves image clarity for low-dose and ultra-low-dose CT scans. It uses a deep convolutional neural network trained on over one million CT images to reduce quantum noise while preserving natural image texture across multiple anatomies, including lung, brain, heart, and abdomen.
