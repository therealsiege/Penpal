# Wysa

Wysa is an AI-driven mental health conversational agent and mobile app used in pre-clinic, between-session, and stepped-care workflows to augment care teams. It provides evidence-based interventions such as cognitive behavioral therapy, dialectical behavior therapy, and motivational interviewing through scripted and free-text conversations.
