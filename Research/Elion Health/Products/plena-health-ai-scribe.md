# Plena Health AI Scribe

Plena Health AI Scribe is a clinical documentation tool that uses artificial intelligence to generate visit notes from physician-patient conversations. It records and transcribes encounters, then produces structured notes aligned with EHR fields, which providers can review and edit before signing.
