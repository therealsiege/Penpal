# Deerfield Solutions LOCUS/CALOCUS-CASII

Deerfield Solutions LOCUS / CALOCUS-CASII is a clinical decision support product that provides standardized, evidence-based tools for determining appropriate levels of care in behavioral health and addiction services. LOCUS supports adult populations, while CALOCUS-CASII is designed for children and adolescents, using structured assessment parameters to guide placement, continued stay decisions, and treatment planning.
