# Healthcare Finance Direct

Healthcare Finance Direct provides patient financing (“pay-over-time”) options so patients can receive elective or non-insurance covered care without having to pay everything up front. They offer flexible payment plans, multiple offers, no hard credit checks (using soft credit checks), customizable terms (payment dates, monthly amounts), and an online portal for patients to apply and manage their payment plans. Providers get tools to onboard patients, view reports (e.g. conversion, open applications, funding), integrate financing into their practices, and see how financing improves treatment acceptance and revenue.
