# Fiddler AI

Fiddler AI provides an AI observability and governance platform that enables enterprises to monitor, evaluate, and control the behavior of generative AI models and autonomous agent systems. Its Agentic Observability suite delivers visibility across every layer of AI operations—from individual traces and spans to full agent hierarchies—allowing users to pinpoint the causes of unexpected or unsafe behavior.
