# OneTrust

OneTrust is an enterprise governance, risk, and compliance (GRC) platform that provides integrated tools for AI governance, privacy management, data use oversight, and third-party risk management. The platform enables organizations to embed compliance and control across the AI lifecycle, automate consent and preference management, monitor data usage policies, and assess vendor and technology risk.
