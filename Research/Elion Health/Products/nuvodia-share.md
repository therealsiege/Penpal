# Nuvodia Share

Nuvodia Share is a fully managed, cloud-based medical image exchange and image management platform that enables healthcare providers to securely share radiology images with other providers, hospitals, and patients. It integrates with existing PACS environments, including Philips IntelliSpace PACS, to send and receive imaging studies without CDs, VPN tunnels, or custom network connections.
