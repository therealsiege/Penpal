# Direct Secure Messaging

Direct Secure Messaging (DSM) is a protocol designed to facilitate the secure exchange of health information over the internet. Utilizing digital certificates and Public Key Infrastructure (PKI), DSM ensures that messages are encrypted and can only be accessed by intended recipients.
