# Adam by CombineHealth

[Contact](https://www.combinehealth.ai/demo)

Adam is an AI-powered denial management tool that integrates directly into existing revenue cycle workflows to identify, prioritize, and resolve claim denials. It intelligently flags complex denials requiring human attention, categorizes them by urgency (e.g., by age, dollar value, payer), and uses AI-driven calls, IVR navigation, and payer portal interactions to retrieve claim updates.
