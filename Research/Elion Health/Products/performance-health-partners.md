# Performance Health Partners

Performance Health Partners offers an integrated safety, quality, and compliance platform for healthcare organizations. Its core capabilities include an incident management system for tracking and resolving patient and employee safety events, digital rounding tools to collect quality data in real time, compliance and audit modules to support regulatory readiness, and tools for collecting patient and staff feedback.
