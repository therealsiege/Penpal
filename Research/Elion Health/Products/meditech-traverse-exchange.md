# Meditech Traverse Exchange

Meditech Traverse Exchange is a nationwide health information exchange network designed for the U.S. It facilitates secure data sharing between Meditech systems and other EHRs, supporting interoperability standards like FHIR and TEFCA. The network includes tools for curating patient data, enabling seamless access to health information across various care settings.
