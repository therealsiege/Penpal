# Inovalon Insurance Discovery

Inovalon Insurance Discovery is a coverage identification tool that finds active, billable insurance—including primary, secondary, and tertiary coverage—for patients who appear uninsured or underinsured. It runs real-time searches across payer databases using verified demographic information to reduce uncompensated care, minimize days in accounts receivable, and prevent denied claims.
