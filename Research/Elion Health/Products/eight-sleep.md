# Eight Sleep

Eight Sleep offers smart mattresses and mattress covers designed to enhance sleep quality through temperature regulation and sleep tracking. Their products, such as the Pod, use sensors to monitor sleep patterns and adjust the mattress temperature throughout the night to optimize rest.
