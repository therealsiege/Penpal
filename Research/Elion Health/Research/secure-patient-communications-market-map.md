# How Secure Patient Communication Evolved in Healthcare

The digital patient communications market has evolved rapidly as basic secure messaging has been commoditized by mandates like the [21st Century Cures Act](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/selected-amendments-fdc-act/21st-century-cures-act), which requires certified EHRs to provide patient messaging access. Competition now centers on solving operational challenges created by message volume rather than simply offering compliant channels.

- **Clinician Burnout:** The deluge of patient messages extends workdays, with physicians spending hours managing EHR inboxes. This uncompensated, often untriaged workload directly impacts morale and is a primary driver toward adoption of intelligent, automated solutions.

- **Asynchronous Billing Opportunities:** Only 0.5% of organizations [billed](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/fullarticle/2814029) for complex patient messages as "e-visits" in 2022, but this emerging model changes how organizations think about messaging from pure expense to potential revenue source.

- **Patient Expectations:** Convenient, mobile-first communication is now a baseline capability for health systems. The dominant workflow balances convenience with compliance through an "SMS-to-secure-link" model: Patients receive unencrypted text notifications containing no PHI but including secure links to encrypted environments for viewing messages and continuing conversations.

# Secure Patient Communications Market Landscape and Key Platform Types (2025)

The market organizes around distinct workflow challenges and buyer profiles.

- **EHR-Native and Integrated Solutions:** These represent the incumbent baseline functionality built directly into patient portals or broader practice management systems. These solutions benefit from deep chart integration and high adoption within existing client bases. Examples include: [Epic's MyChart](https://elion.health/products/epic), [Oracle Health](https://elion.health/products/oracle-cerner), [athenahealth](https://elion.health/products/athenahealth), and [Elation](https://elion.health/products/elation).

- **Patient Engagement & Access Platforms:** These focus on automating the front-end of the patient journey. Core use-cases include automated appointment reminders, patient self-scheduling, broadcast messaging for population health campaigns, and digital intake. Examples include: [Luma Health](https://elion.health/products/luma), [Artera](https://elion.health/products/artera), [Solutionreach](https://elion.health/products/solutionreach), [Weave](https://elion.health/products/weave), [InteliChart](https://elion.health/products/intelichart), [Healow](https://elion.health/products/healow), and [Hyro](https://elion.health/products/hyro).

- **Clinical Communication & Asynchronous Care Platforms:** These specialize in managing bi-directional clinical conversations. Primary workflows involve triaging inbound patient questions, routing them to appropriate clinical staff, and documenting encounters. Examples include: [Klara](https://elion.health/products/klara), [OhMD](https://elion.health/products/ohmd), [Spruce Health](https://elion.health/products/spruce-health), [Elaborate](https://elion.health/products/elaborate), [Updox](https://elion.health/products/updox), [Curogram](https://elion.health/products/curogram), [Paubox](https://elion.health/products/paubox), [Nurse 1-1](https://elion.health/products/nurse-1-1), and [Celo](https://elion.health/products/celo-health).

- **Care Team Coordination Platforms:** These originated as pager and legacy phone replacements. Their main strengths lie in enterprise directories, on-call scheduling integration, and clinical alerting within hospitals. Many have expanded to include patient communication, though they remain focused primarily on clinician-to-clinician messaging. Examples include: [TigerConnect](https://elion.health/products/tigerconnect) and [DrFirst RxInform](https://elion.health/products/drfirst-rxinform).

- **Platform and Infrastructure Providers:** These are the underlying communication infrastructures that power secure messaging for digital health companies and health systems building custom solutions. They provide APIs, SDKs, and messaging-as-a-service capabilities. Examples include: [NexHealth](https://elion.health/products/nexhealth) and [Sendbird](https://elion.health/products/sendbird).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### **1\. What is a secure patient communication platform?**

A secure patient communication platform enables HIPAA-compliant digital exchanges between patients and healthcare teams. It protects protected health information (PHI) while allowing asynchronous, documented conversations for scheduling, care management, and follow-up.

### **2\. Why has patient messaging volume increased so dramatically?**

Since COVID-19, patient-initiated digital messaging has grown by more than 200%, driven by portal adoption, virtual care expansion, and rising consumer expectations for real-time access to care teams.

### **3\. How are health systems addressing clinician message overload?**

Leading organizations are adopting **AI-driven triage tools**, workflow automation, and role-based routing to manage EHR inboxes more efficiently, reducing administrative burden and burnout.

### **4\. Can providers bill for responding to patient messages?**

Yes, under CMS’s **asynchronous e-visit codes (99421–99423)**, organizations can bill for time spent managing qualified patient messages. Adoption remains low but is growing as systems integrate messaging analytics and charge capture tools.

### **5\. What types of secure messaging platforms exist in healthcare?**

The market includes:

- **EHR-native solutions** (e.g., Epic MyChart)

- **Patient engagement platforms** (e.g., Luma Health, Artera)

- **Asynchronous care platforms** (e.g., Klara, Spruce Health)

- **Care coordination tools** (e.g., TigerConnect, symplr)

- **Messaging infrastructure providers** (e.g., NexHealth, Sendbird)

### **6\. What’s next for secure patient communications?**

Expect deeper **EHR integration**, **AI-assisted message management**, and **automated triage workflows** that support both clinical efficiency and patient satisfaction.
