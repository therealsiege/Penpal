# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: November Edition

**Date:** December 1, 2024  
**Author:** Bobby Guelich, CEO, Elion

---

In November 2024 we tracked 54 healthcare IT news stories, with [AI clinician assistant](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=bdc9f5f1da&e=1ca8391b83), [VBC](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=0e4442e6b9&e=1ca8391b83), and [virtual and at-home care](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=780b960653&e=1ca8391b83) news showing up most frequently. This month, only 43% of announcements we covered pertained to an AI product or feature, down from 71% in October but on par with 43% in August and 51% in September.

It would appear that following the fall conference season, vendors were heads-down on the next big thing, and holding off on announcements during a very noisy election cycle. Here, we’re summarizing the month’s news—finding the signal in the noise—so you can stay abreast of the most important trends you may have missed.

## **1\. More EHR Vendors Roll Out Their AI Scribes**

In past months we’ve covered [EHR](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=2712cc93cf&e=1ca8391b83) heavyweights [Epic](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=04e56de008&e=1ca8391b83) and [Oracle](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=6b746114d4&e=1ca8391b83), with their extensive integrations and comprehensive solutions. This month, we saw several smaller and more specialized EHRs roll out their proprietary or integrated [AI ambient scribes](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=c668c1710b&e=1ca8391b83).

[MEDENT](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=c6ee8be7e3&e=1ca8391b83) and [Azalea Health](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=596822afb7&e=1ca8391b83) both opted for [an integration](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=8680f45beb&e=1ca8391b83) with [Suki](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=ef144ab41f&e=1ca8391b83), whereas [Veradigm](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=99cfa94c9f&e=1ca8391b83) and [ModMed](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=e09b578791&e=1ca8391b83)—both EHR and practice management solutions—both released their own [AI ambient scribe](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=526e01d783&e=1ca8391b83) solutions ( [here](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=0891bd5b95&e=1ca8391b83) and [here](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=10888528ba&e=1ca8391b83), respectively).

## **2\. Value-Based Care Gets the Clinician Assistant Treatment**

2024 has been the year of the [AI ambient scribe](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=0ae345c676&e=1ca8391b83), and—more broadly— [clinician assistants](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=20d0ac5bf5&e=1ca8391b83) in general, including [summarization tools](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=2ae60b6068&e=1ca8391b83) and [point of care clinical decision support](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=d167adcf83&e=1ca8391b83). However, as [we’ve heard from VBC leaders](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=34ce35d312&e=1ca8391b83) in the past, tools built for the fee-for-service model don’t necessarily support VBC workflows. This month, we saw more [VBC-specific vendors](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=a995b9f7be&e=1ca8391b83) expanding their solutions to the point of care.

- [Wellsky](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=f5f464310f&e=1ca8391b83), which offers a suite of tools across acute and post-acute care as well as VBC-targeted products, [released](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=4b5bfd06ae&e=1ca8391b83) an AI solution that performs [ambient scribing](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=069961d927&e=1ca8391b83), [summarization](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=79a259db04&e=1ca8391b83), and EHR field auto-filling from photos and documents.

- VBC analytics platform [i2i Population Health](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=31556ba48b&e=1ca8391b83) [is partnering](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=e16e4b2bc5&e=1ca8391b83) with [CureMD](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=34b76bf5a1&e=1ca8391b83) to surface actionable data to providers at the point of care.

- The [population health data and analytics](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=f8451886e5&e=1ca8391b83) platform, [Arcadia](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=7d4238c2ea&e=1ca8391b83), [launched](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=0504dd60d7&e=1ca8391b83) two new solutions—a tool that leverages [CareJourney](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=b3976faef7&e=1ca8391b83)’s market intelligence to analyze VBC metrics against the market and an analytics engine that delivers custom insights to providers via email. (It’s not exactly at the point of care, but closer!)

## **3\. AI in Patient Intake Grows**

Time and again we’ve seen administrative use-cases of AI garner broader adoption than more clinical workflows. This is true in [patient intake](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=7a46a7238a&e=1ca8391b83), too, where AI can generate small but meaningful efficiencies and improve patient experience.

Rogers Behavioral Health, a mental health and addiction services provider, [selected](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=bebf34030f&e=1ca8391b83) [Limbic](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=2a0e324e6d&e=1ca8391b83), an [AI contact center](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=504ef013df&e=1ca8391b83) solution, to assist with patient screening and [intake](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=4595d1fed5&e=1ca8391b83). [RevSpring](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=39fbaaf6e0&e=1ca8391b83) [released](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=9969912e4b&e=1ca8391b83) Forms Blast, which helps providers dynamically deliver digital forms to patients and uses optical character recognition (OCR) to auto-fill patient forms from their ID or other documents.

## **4\. More Health Systems Implement Remote Solutions**

While virtual nursing and remote patient monitoring solutions are far from the same, they share one very essential common thread: the de-coupling of treatment and location. This month, we saw even more healthcare providers taking meaningful steps in this direction:

- Sentara Health [is implementing](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=b9175f5a68&e=1ca8391b83) the [ThinkAndor](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=7824ca3306&e=1ca8391b83) [virtual nursing](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=7351357890&e=1ca8391b83) platform at its Norfolk, VA hospital.

- Florida health system Lee Health [is expanding](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=fbe027c6b0&e=1ca8391b83) their [remote patient monitoring](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=b96fde9c34&e=1ca8391b83) program with [Biofourmis](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=d42011b82f&e=1ca8391b83) and launching an [AI hospital at home](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=64a42fb096&e=1ca8391b83) program.

- Mass General Brigham [launched](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=f11fe6e540&e=1ca8391b83) a partnership with the New England Center and Home for Veterans to provide hospital at home services for veterans experiencing homelessness.

- MedStar Health in Maryland [expanded its partnership](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=a691adef6f&e=1ca8391b83) with in-home care provider [DispatchHealth](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=624ca46043&e=1ca8391b83) to support care transitions.

Perhaps as a predecessor to more implementations to come, [The Alliance for Smart Healthcare Excellence](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=7ba53bf947&e=1ca8391b83)—established by [care.ai](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=68ab2f4355&e=1ca8391b83), now a part of [Stryker](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=246dbc27bb&e=1ca8391b83), and developed with assistance from the College of Healthcare Information Management Executives (CHIME)— [was established](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=f86757eaf3&e=1ca8391b83) to promote the Smart Hospital Maturity Model (SHMM).

## **5\. Healthcare Tech Experts React to the Election**

Beyond the potential far-reaching effects of a second Trump presidency on healthcare policy generally, a regime change at this pivotal moment in healthcare tech, could have meaningful impacts across the industry. Obviously, headline-making picks like [Dr. Oz for CMS](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=165db6f3b2&e=1ca8391b83) and [RFK, Jr. for HHS](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=4d1b6ed109&e=1ca8391b83) have generated significant conjecture, but there are also more narrow and concrete policies that could quite quickly change under a new administration.

For example, Fierce Healthcare [reported on](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=07002fdc2a&e=1ca8391b83) the potential implications of conservative think tank Paragon Health Institute shaping AI policy, particularly reflecting on [a paper](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=cef3bf10d9&e=1ca8391b83) advocating reducing human-in-the-loop AI implementations in favor of purely AI driven solutions. Brendan Keeler also shared [a quick post](https://health.us13.list-manage.com/track/click?u=f1a492ae95e394f71b2248064&id=67d5bd6657&e=1ca8391b83) about what the new administration could mean for ONC, now ASTP, and interoperability efforts in healthcare (comment section is worth a read).
