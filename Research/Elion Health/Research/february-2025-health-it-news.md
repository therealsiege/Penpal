# What You Missed in Healthcare IT: February Edition

**Date:** March 2, 2025  
**Author:** Patrick Wingo, Head of Research, Elion

**Categories:** AI clinician assistant, EHR, claims management, clinical workflow management, AI Ambient Scribes, Patient Engagement

---

In February 2025 we tracked 69 healthcare IT news stories, including a large number of announcements surrounding ViVE mid-month. [AI clinician assistant](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinician-assistant) news showed up most frequently this month for the sixth month in a row, and 57% of all announcements we covered pertained to an AI product or feature. (This rate of AI announcements is second only to [October](https://elion.health/resources/october-healthcare-it-news), when HLTH similarly bolstered the number of AI announcements.) Here, we’re summarizing the month’s news—finding the signal in the noise—so you can stay abreast of the most important trends you may have missed.

## 1\. AI Scribe Implementations Are Still Going Strong

Another month, another group of large health systems announced enterprise-wide [AI ambient scribe](https://elion.health/categories/ai-ambient-scribes/products) rollouts.

- [Abridge](https://elion.health/products/abridge) was rolled out at [UNC Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/unc-health-abridge), [Tanner Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/tanner-health-abridge-announcement), [Akron Children’s](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/akron-childrens-abridge), and [Inova Health](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/inova-health-abridge). The AI ambient scribe vendor also [announced](https://www.abridge.com/press-release/series-d) a $250M Series D with participation from California Health Care Foundation, CVS Health Ventures, and NVentures (NVIDIA’s venture capital arm). Finally, it [announced](https://www.athenahealth.com/press-releases/athenahealth-and-abridge-partner) it will now be available through [athenahealth](https://elion.health/products/athenahealth)’s Ambient Notes solution for ambulatory practices.

- [Cleveland Clinic](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/cleveland-clinic-announces-the-rollout-of-ambience-healthcares-ai-plat-987308) and [John Muir Health](https://www.accessnewswire.com/newsroom/en/healthcare-and-pharmaceutical/john-muir-health-announces-the-full-enterprise-rollout-of-ambience-hea-983017) both announced rollouts with [Ambience](https://elion.health/products/ambience-ai-os), emphasizing its use for documentation, [clinical documentation integrity](https://elion.health/categories/ai-clinical-documentation-integrity/products), and point of care coding **.**

Generally speaking, though, rural and community hospitals and health centers opted for other players. North East Medical Services (NEMS) [selected](https://www.commure.com/press-releases/commure-and-north-east-medical-services-nems-partner-to-revolutionize-clinical-documentation) [Commure](https://elion.health/products/commure-scribe) to roll-out across its clinic locations through an [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic) integration, and CHC, which operates 23 community-based hospitals nationwide, [announced](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/02/12/3025512/0/en/CarePilot-and-Community-Hospital-Corporation-Forge-Strategic-Partnership-Following-Successful-Pilot-of-Ambient-AI-Technology.html) it would be rolling out [CarePilot](https://elion.health/products/carepilot).

Also of note: Cedars Sinai [announced](https://www.cedars-sinai.org/newsroom/artificial-intelligence-lightens-burden-on-nurses/) a pilot with [Aiva](https://elion.health/products/aiva-health), an AI scribe specifically for nurses

## 2\. EHR Integrations Are a Core Driver for Disruptors

[EHR](https://elion.health/categories/electronic-health-record) integration continues to be key in vendors’ distribution strategies, with [Epic](https://elion.health/products/epic) playing a central role. Emphasizing that fact, Epic even [announced a one-day conference](https://open.epic.com/Conference) just for vendors looking to integrate within the EHR.

A few new rollouts: [Talkdesk](https://elion.health/products/talkdesk)’s AI-powered contact center solution [now integrates](https://www.talkdesk.com/news-and-press/press-releases/unified-agent-experience/) directly into the Epic interface, identity verification solution [CLEAR](https://elion.health/products/clear) is [under construction](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/02/10/3023261/0/en/CLEAR-is-Under-Construction-in-Epic-Toolbox-to-Streamline-Patient-Experiences-in-Healthcare.html) in Epic Toolbox, and Glooko’s diabetes RPM platform [became available](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250227344063/en/Glooko-Joins-Epic-Showroom-Expanding-Access-to-Seamless-Electronic-Health-Record-Integration-for-Healthcare-Providers) via Epic’s Showroom, providing data access for providers directly in the EHR.

The integration strategy seems to be proving effective. For example, outside of Epic, Moab  Regional Hospital [adopted](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/avo-expands-its-presence-in-the-meditech-community-welcoming-exciting-new-partnerships-302383463.html) [Avo](https://elion.health/products/avomd) through its [MEDITECH](https://elion.health/products/meditech-expanse) integration. Not to be left out of the action, ambulatory EHR [CharmHealth](https://elion.health/products/charmhealth) [announced](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250213760672/en/CharmHealth-Connects-Entire-Ecosystem-of-Healthcare-Stakeholders-With-New-Marketplace-and-Innovation-Hub) a new vendor marketplace as well.

## 3\. Funding Trends: AI-Powered RCM and Administrative Tech Draw Investor Interest

Investor funding remains strong across revenue cycle management (RCM) and administrative automation. [Candid Health](https://elion.health/products/candid-health) [secured](https://www.newswire.com/news/candid-health-raises-52-5-million-series-c-to-enhance-genai-features-22518346) a $52.5M Series C for AI-driven [claims management](https://elion.health/categories/claims-management), [Lynx](https://elion.health/products/lynx-cdh-core) [raised](https://www.lynx-fh.com/press-release-lynx-series-a-27m-healthcare-fintech/) a $27M Series A for its patient payments platform, and [VoiceCare AI](https://elion.health/products/voicecare-ai) [launched](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/agentic-ai-startup-voicecare-ai-launches-to-automate-healthcare-back-office-and-super-staff-workforce-302376944.html) with $3.85M for AI prior auth and eligibility checking automation.

On the clinical administration side, [AI inbox management](https://elion.health/categories/ai-inbox-management/products) vendor [Affineon](https://elion.health/products/affineon) [announced](https://www.affineon.com/press-releases/affineon-secures-5m-to-power-physician-productivity) a $5M seed round, and [clinical workflow management](https://elion.health/categories/clinical-workflow-management) startup [Keragon](https://elion.health/products/keragon) [closed](https://www.keragon.com/blog/keragon-ai-powered-healthcare-automation-platform-secured-7-5m-seed-round) a $7.5M seed round.

## 4\. AI Patient Engagement Lots of Activity, but Few Major Implementations in February

Vendors in the patient engagement space—patient [scheduling](https://elion.health/categories/patient-scheduling/products), [intake](https://elion.health/categories/patient-intake/products), [assessments](https://elion.health/categories/autonomous-patient-assessment/products), and [feedback](https://elion.health/categories/patient-feedback-platforms/products)—are hard at work releasing new solutions:

- [Press Ganey](https://elion.health/products/press-ganey) and Microsoft [are partnering](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250203504420/en/Press-Ganey-Partners-with-Microsoft-to-Transform-the-Human-Experience-in-Healthcare) to build GenAI-powered patient communications and feedback solutions.

- [Drive Health](https://elion.health/products/nurse-avery) [released](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250204019781/en/Drive-Health-Introduces-Nurse-Avery-an-AI-Health-Assistant-Powered-by-Google) its flagship product, Nurse Avery, in partnership with Google Public Sector; the AI chatbot promises to fulfill [scheduling](https://elion.health/categories/patient-scheduling/products), [patient assessment](https://elion.health/categories/autonomous-patient-assessment/products), and [care plan management](https://elion.health/categories/care-plan-management/products) functionality.

- [Kyruus Health](https://elion.health/products/kyruus) [integrated](https://kyruushealth.com/kyruus-health-and-aha-partner-to-enhance-patient-access-and-engagement/) with [Abundant Health Acquisition (aha!)](https://abundant.health/), which offers data infrastructure and content management systems for patient acquisition.

- [Innovaccer](https://elion.health/products/innovaccer-agents-of-care) [released](https://innovaccer.com/resources/news/innovaccer-launches-agents-of-care-to-transform-healthcare-operations-enhance-care-delivery) a suite of AI agents to complete tasks like [scheduling](https://elion.health/categories/patient-scheduling/products), [intake](https://elion.health/categories/patient-intake/products), [referrals](https://elion.health/categories/referral-management/products), PA documentation collection, and more.

- [CentralReach](https://elion.health/products/centralreach), a software vendor for autism and IDD care, [integrated](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2025/02/13/3026250/0/en/CentralReach-and-AI-Measures-Announce-Exclusive-Partnership-to-Transform-the-Way-Assessments-and-Autism-Care-Are-Delivered-Paving-the-Way-for-Improved-Learner-Outcomes-and-Provider.html) [AI.Measures](https://elion.health/products/ai.measures) for mental health assessment.

Despite the number of product partnerships and updates this month, we saw only one implementation of these AI engagement solutions: First Choice Neurology [selected](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250227481203/en/eClinicalWorks-and-healow-Genie-to-Assist-Nations-Largest-Neurology-Practice-to-Transform-Patient-Experience) [Healow](https://elion.health/products/healow) via the eClinicalWorks EHR. While we’ve seen earlier waves of adoption in AI engagement tools, it remains to be seen whether these updates will drive new rollouts.
