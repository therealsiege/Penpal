## The State of Prior Authorization in 2025

Prior authorization (PA) is perhaps the clearest example of healthcare’s administrative burden. Physicians and their staff spend about [13 hours per week completing an average of 39 prior authorizations per physician](https://www.ama-assn.org/system/files/prior-authorization-survey.pdf). The result is a critical bottleneck that delays care, drives costs, and fuels clinician burnout.

## Why Prior Authorization Must Change

Converging forces are reshaping PA in 2025: CMS mandates requiring payer FHIR APIs and shorter decision timelines become enforceable in 2026-2027, creating a hard deadline for modernization. At the same time, provider organizations facing persistent workforce shortages can no longer absorb PA’s workload through manual processing alone.

Fortunately, AI technologies have matured at the right moment. Large language models now interpret payer policies and extract clinical evidence directly from charts, while automation handles the repetitive portal navigation and phone calls. Acting quickly during this transitional window allows providers to strengthen compliance and build operational resilience; delaying risks regulatory exposure and rising denial rates.

## What This AI Prior Auth Guide Covers

**This guide equips provider leaders with current-state workflow maps, an AI-augmented future vision, market landscape analysis, ROI frameworks, vendor evaluation criteria, and implementation guidance.** Whether you are evaluating your first PA solution or optimizing existing tools, it offers a foundation to make informed, durable decisions in a rapidly transforming market.
