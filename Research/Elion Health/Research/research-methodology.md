**Date:** October 15, 2025

Colin DuRant

Director of Research, Elion

Evaluating healthcare technology shouldn’t feel like detective work. At Elion, our mission is to make it faster and easier for provider organizations to discover, understand, and evaluate health IT solutions with confidence. We [map the healthcare technology ecosystem](https://elion.health/categories) to help leaders cut through vendor marketing and focus on what matters: functionality, integration, and real-world fit.

# **Who We Are**

Elion is an independent health IT intelligence platform used by more than 65% of U.S. health systems to research healthcare technology vendors and categories. Our team includes seasoned analysts and researchers who understand both the operational and clinical realities of technology adoption.

# **How We Create Each Profile**

Every product profile on Elion starts with research. Our team gathers information from multiple sources: public materials, direct vendor conversations, and feedback from provider organization users. From there, we write a product overview that includes product description, customers served, and category definitions. Product descriptions are written by our research team, not by vendors, to ensure clarity, neutrality, and consistency across the marketplace.

When vendors claim their profiles, they can update use-case details, share customer listings, and provide supplemental materials like screenshots or demo videos. The result is a living, continuously updated resource that reflects both expert analysis and verified vendor input.

# **Why Our Research Matters**

We take our commitment to transparency and neutrality seriously. Health systems trust Elion’s Marketplace because it operates independently from vendor sponsorship or pay-to-play models. Vendors don’t pay to add their listing or improve placement in our Marketplace; it’s not an advertising platform, but a research-driven, continuously evolving resource designed to help buyers make smarter decisions. By normalizing descriptions across thousands of listings, we make it possible to compare solutions side by side without sitting through a dozen sales pitches.

Our work is driven by a simple belief: Better information leads to better technology decisions. By building a shared language around categories, use cases, and integrations, we’re helping modernize how healthcare organizations adopt innovative solutions. Our goal is to become the go-to resource for provider organizations to start their vendor search.
