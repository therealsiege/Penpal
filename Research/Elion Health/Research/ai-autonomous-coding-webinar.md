# Webinar: Unpacking Autonomous Coding: Category Breakdown & Frameworks for Vendor Selection

**Type:** Webinars  
**Date:** November 20, 2024  
**Author:** Patrick Wingo, Head of Research, Elion

**Categories:** Autonomous Coding

---

**AI will drastically change healthcare operations—and within the next 1-3 years, not decades.**

Revenue cycle management (RCM), particularly medical coding, is especially ripe for innovation for a few key reasons:

- It’s repetitive

- It poses little clinical risk

- It has a clear financial ROI

> **RCM leaders who act early have an opportunity to shape the development of these solutions, while also reaping meaningful improvements in reimbursement dollars and speed.**

However, not all vendors in the “AI coding” space offer **true autonomous coding**—no human in the loop and delivering consistently high quality. In this 30-minute webinar, Elion’s Head of Research, Patrick Wingo, covers:

- ​The tech advancements driving autonomous coding

- ​How these solutions actually work under the hood

- ​The pros and cons of different AI approaches

- ​The benefits of going fully autonomous

- ​Who’s really offering autonomous coding today, and

- ​What to look for when evaluating a vendor
