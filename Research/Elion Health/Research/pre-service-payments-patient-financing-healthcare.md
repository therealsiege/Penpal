## **Executive Summary**

Patient financial responsibility is rising, and providers are seeing higher rates of bad debt and charity care. Pre-service payment and financing solutions address this challenge by securing patient contributions upfront through accurate cost estimates, point-of-scheduling payments, and financing options such as installment plans or healthcare credit cards. These tools help hospitals reduce write-offs, comply with price transparency and Good Faith Estimate rules, and improve the patient financial experience. Market solutions range from end-to-end RCM platforms and digital intake tools to specialized financing vendors and payment processors.

Bad debt and charity care [increased](https://www.kaufmanhall.com/news/hospital-performance-showed-continued-stability-2024) in 2024, signaling more balances shifting to patients and going uncollected. Technology that enables pre-service [estimates](https://elion.health/categories/patient-price-estimation/products), point-of- [scheduling](https://elion.health/categories/patient-scheduling/products) payment, and embedded financing help convert these unpredictable balances into collected cash and a smoother patient experience.

# **What Are Pre-Service Payments and Patient Financing Solutions?**

[**Pre-service payments and patient financing** **solutions**](https://elion.health/categories/pre-service-payments-and-patient-financing/products) secure a patient’s financial arrangements _before_ care is delivered. This function facilitates the collection of co-pays, deposits, or the full estimated balance before or at the point of service. If a patient cannot pay in full, solutions may enroll them in an interest-free installment plan or offer access to third-party financing products like healthcare credit cards or loan programs.

While these financing options often carry fees or require provider subsidies, they serve a critical strategic purpose: converting likely write-offs into guaranteed revenue while preserving patient access to care. The central goal is to secure the patient's portion when they are engaged and present, not when a surprise bill arrives weeks later.

# **Why Pre-Service Payments Matter for Healthcare Providers**

Several forces are pushing providers to adopt pre-service payment and financing solutions.

The most critical market drivers are:

1. **Rising Patient Financial Responsibility:** High-deductible health plans now dominate employer coverage, with deductibles [rising](https://files.kff.org/attachment/Employer-Health-Benefits-Survey-2023-Annual-Survey-Summary-of-Findings.pdf) 53% in the last decade. This shifts substantial costs to patients, who often can't pay large bills without financing options.

2. **Intensifying Provider Financial Pressure:** With thin margins and rising costs, hospitals can't afford high rates of bad debt. Collection rates on patient balances [fell](https://www.beckershospitalreview.com/finance/patient-collections-fall-to-48/) to 47.6% in 2022-2023 from 54.8% in 2021, making upfront solutions critical.

3. **Regulatory Mandates for Transparency:** New federal rules mandate price transparency and advance cost estimates. The No Surprises Act requires Good Faith Estimates (GFEs) for self-pay patients, making pre-service estimation capabilities essential for compliance.

4. **Healthcare Consumerism:** Patients increasingly expect retail-like financial experiences. [Research](https://www.pymnts.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/09/PYMNTS-The-Payment-Cure-September-2021.pdf) shows 63% would switch providers for better payment processes, making financing options a competitive necessity.

# **The Future of Pre-Service Payments and Financing**

The pre-service payments and financing market will continue evolving as technology advances and regulatory mandates take full effect. Three key trends will shape the next 2-3 years:

1. **AI-Driven Personalization:** AI will enable automated financial counseling and personalized payment recommendations, reducing staff burden while improving patient engagement.

2. **Intensified Market Consolidation:** Large RCM and [EHR](https://elion.health/categories/electronic-health-record-ehr/products) vendors will continue acquiring specialized fintech and patient engagement companies to build comprehensive platforms.

3. **Realization of Payer-Provider Data Exchange:** Implementation of Advanced Explanation of Benefits (AEOB) requirements will enable real-time, payer-verified cost estimates through standardized data exchange.

Pre-service payment and financing solutions meet a critical business need for provider organizations. Health systems that successfully implement these solutions can improve their financial health while building patient trust and loyalty through transparent, compassionate financial engagement.

## **Patient Pre-Payment Frequently Asked Questions**

**What are pre-service payments in healthcare?** Pre-service payments are financial arrangements secured from patients before care is delivered. These may include co-pays, deposits, or full balance estimates collected at the point of scheduling or check-in. The goal is to reduce bad debt by engaging patients upfront, rather than sending surprise bills weeks later.

**How do patient financing solutions work?** If a patient cannot pay the full balance upfront, financing solutions offer alternatives such as zero-interest installment plans, healthcare credit cards, or line-of-credit programs. These options help patients access care while ensuring providers secure revenue that might otherwise go uncollected.

**Can pre-service payments reduce bad debt for hospitals?** Yes. By collecting costs upfront or arranging structured financing, providers reduce the likelihood of balances aging into bad debt. This approach has become more important as patient deductibles and out-of-pocket costs have risen sharply in recent years.

**How do patient financing solutions help with regulatory compliance?** Federal rules require hospitals to provide transparent pricing and Good Faith Estimates (GFEs) to patients. Pre-service payment and estimation tools support compliance by generating accurate cost estimates, integrating with payer data, and documenting patient financial communications.

**What role will AI play in the future of patient payments?** AI is being used to personalize payment plans, automate cost counseling, and match patients with financing options that align with their ability to pay. This reduces staff burden and improves patient satisfaction by offering more tailored financial experiences.
