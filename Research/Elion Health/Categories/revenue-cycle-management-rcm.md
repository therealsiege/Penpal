# Revenue Cycle Management and Financial Operations

Revenue Cycle Management (RCM) and Financial Operations covers the software that moves a patient encounter from first contact to cash in the bank and the finance tools that make those dollars accurate, auditable, and strategically useful. These solutions verify coverage, determine medical necessity and codes, produce and submit clean claims, collect payer and patient payments, and reconcile cash to the bank and general ledger. They also maintain payer contracts and expected allowables, calculate fully allocated costs, and surface margin and performance insights for pricing, negotiation, and investment decisions.

The category is organized into four groups that mirror the workflow: Front-End (Coverage Discovery; Insurance Eligibility & Benefits Verification; Prior Authorization Management; Patient Estimates & Price Transparency; Pre-Service Payments & Financing), Mid-Cycle (Clinical Documentation Integrity; Coding Enablement; Autonomous Coding; Revenue Integrity; Utilization Review & Case Management; Claims Management & Clearinghouse), Back-End (Payment Reconciliation; Denials Management; Patient Billing & Collection), and Cross-Cycle Enablers & Financial Operations (Revenue Intelligence; Automation Platforms; Contract Management; Cost Accounting). Advanced automation and AI are treated as capabilities across these groups rather than a standalone category.
