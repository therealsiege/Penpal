# VBC Data and Analytics

VBC Data and Analytics solutions give healthcare organizations the insights they need to succeed under value-based care models. These tools bring together clinical, claims, and financial data to provide a clear picture of how patient populations are performing and where opportunities exist to improve care quality and control costs.

They help organizations identify high-risk patients, measure progress against performance metrics, and track the financial impact of value-based contracts. By delivering actionable insights through advanced analytics and intuitive dashboards, these solutions support better decision-making, targeted interventions, and continuous improvement to meet the goals of value-based care.

## Subcategories

### Care Gap and Contract Performance Analytics (39 products)

Care Gap and Contract Performance Analytics companies enable value-based care organizations to track and improve their performance under value-based contracts. By identifying care gaps and coding opportunities, these companies help boost financial outcomes while delivering the technical tools needed to surface these insights effectively, such as integrating with EHRs to alert providers at the point of care.
These solutions demonstrate strong technical expertise in value-based care reimbursement and risk adjustment methods, combined with robust analytics capabilities to pinpoint care gaps using population health data. Their solutions ensure that actionable insights reach the right stakeholders, supporting better care delivery and contract performance.

### Population Health Data and Analytics (25 products)

Population Health Data and Analytics companies help value-based care organizations gain a comprehensive view of the health of their patient populations. Achieving strong value-based outcomes depends on integrating diverse data sources, including claims, EHR and clinical data, admissions and discharge records, authorizations, pharmacy and lab results, and social determinants of health. By consolidating this information into a single platform, these vendors make it possible for organizations to track trends and understand the factors driving outcomes and costs.
These platforms run advanced analytics to deliver actionable insights, such as identifying high-risk patients who need targeted interventions or segmenting patients into risk groups for tailored care strategies. Strong technical capabilities for multi-source data integration and robust analytics tools make these solutions essential for organizations working to meet value-based care goals and improve population health outcomes.
