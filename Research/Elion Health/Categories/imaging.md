# Imaging

Imaging products provide the digital tools needed to support every stage of the medical imaging process, from capturing and storing scans to securely sharing and reviewing them. These solutions help healthcare organizations manage high volumes of imaging data generated by X-rays, CT scans, MRIs, and other diagnostic tests, ensuring that images are organized, accessible, and integrated into clinical workflows.

Key features often include advanced visualization tools that allow clinicians to view, manipulate, and compare images in high detail for more accurate interpretations. Many imaging software products also enable secure collaboration so radiologists and referring providers can share and review scans together in real time, even across different sites. Some platforms connect directly with provider directories or imaging networks to make it easier to coordinate services and referrals. By centralizing and streamlining imaging data management, these software tools help clinicians deliver faster, clearer diagnoses and support more coordinated patient care.

## Subcategories

### Image Exchange (10 products)

Image Exchange solutions enable healthcare organizations to request, send, receive, and ingest diagnostic imaging studies and reports across organizational boundaries. These platforms replace disk-based transfer and fragmented ad hoc workflows by providing secure, auditable exchange mechanisms and by making inbound studies clinically usable through automated ingestion steps such as patient matching and normalization.
In practice, Image Exchange supports workflows like obtaining priors from outside facilities, sharing studies with referring providers, supporting transfers, and giving patients and specialists access to diagnostic-quality images. Many solutions integrate with PACS and EHR workflows, and may also enable patient-initiated sharing and link-based transfers.
