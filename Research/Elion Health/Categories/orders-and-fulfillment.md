# Orders and Fulfillment

The Orders and Fulfillment category includes solutions that help healthcare organizations manage and coordinate the delivery of critical services and products patients rely on. These tools streamline how providers order, track, and fulfill everything from diagnostic tests to prescriptions, medical equipment, and wellness products, making sure patients get what they need without delays.

This category covers Labs and Imaging tools that make it easy to order tests, share results, and connect them to patient records. Electronic Prescription systems send prescriptions directly to pharmacies to reduce errors and boost medication adherence. Durable Medical Equipment platforms manage orders and track deliveries of supplies like wheelchairs or CPAP machines, while Pharmacy Services help coordinate medication fulfillment and communication with pharmacists. Supplements and Nutrition tools allow providers to recommend and monitor personalized wellness plans, supporting preventive care and chronic condition management. Together, these solutions help providers deliver care efficiently and ensure patients have timely access to essential treatments and support.

## Subcategories

### Electronic Prescriptions (8 products)

Electronic Prescription products allow healthcare providers to create and send prescriptions digitally through their EHR systems or secure web platforms. By replacing handwritten scripts with electronic ones, these tools help streamline the prescribing process and make it easier for providers to manage patient medications.
These solutions improve patient safety by automatically checking for potential drug interactions, allergies, and dosage errors before a prescription is finalized. Once approved, prescriptions are sent directly to the patient’s pharmacy, reducing the risk of errors and delays. This digital process not only saves time for providers and pharmacists but also ensures patients receive their medications more quickly and accurately

### Labs (28 products)

Labs products provide the digital tools needed to manage and streamline the entire diagnostic testing workflow for healthcare providers and laboratories. These platforms give clinicians intuitive interfaces to order lab tests, track orders, and receive results directly within their existing systems, helping reduce manual paperwork and minimize errors. Whether tests are conducted in a clinic, at an external lab, or through at-home collection kits, labs software helps coordinate every step to keep the process smooth and efficient.
Many of these solutions include logistics management features for shipping, tracking, and processing test kits, ensuring samples are handled correctly and results are delivered on time. Some platforms also enable providers and health systems to create branded or customizable diagnostic programs, integrating with EHRs and patient portals for seamless ordering and result sharing.

### Precision Medicine (6 products)

The Precision Medicine category focuses on personalized healthcare solutions that tailor treatment plans based on an individual’s unique genetic, clinical, and lifestyle data. These tools leverage advanced diagnostics, pharmacogenetics, and AI-driven platforms to optimize patient care and improve outcomes. Genetic testing platforms help identify gene-drug interactions and guide medication choices, ensuring that treatments are safe and effective. Advanced diagnostic services, including imaging and digital pathology, aid in the early detection and management of complex diseases such as cancer and cardiovascular conditions.
AI-powered platforms further enhance precision medicine by analyzing diverse data sources, such as lab results, medications, comorbidities, and patient behaviors, to generate individualized care plans. These solutions empower providers to move beyond a one-size-fits-all approach, offering targeted therapies and proactive care tailored to each patient’s unique profile. By combining genetic insights, advanced diagnostics, and personalized care strategies, precision medicine aims to improve patient outcomes, reduce adverse events, and enhance the overall quality of care.

### Supplements and Nutrition (7 products)

The Supplements and Nutrition category focuses on tools that support personalized health and wellness through targeted nutritional interventions and supplement management. These platforms empower healthcare providers to integrate nutrition and supplements into care plans, promoting a “food as medicine” approach. They offer solutions for prescribing, tracking, and managing supplement regimens while ensuring patient adherence to treatment plans.
Digital platforms within this category also provide evidence-based nutrition content, personalized meal plans, and tools for assessing dietary habits. Quick dietary assessments generate insights into diet quality and help develop personalized nutrition improvement plans. By combining supplement management with tailored nutrition strategies, these solutions promote holistic care, support chronic disease management, and encourage healthier lifestyle choices.
