# Value-Based Care (VBC)

The Value-Based Care category includes solutions that help healthcare organizations move away from traditional fee-for-service models and succeed under payment structures that reward better health outcomes and cost efficiency. These tools enable providers to deliver higher-quality care while keeping costs under control, supporting the transition to sustainable value-based care models.

Key subcategories include VBC Contracting and Enablement tools that help negotiate and manage agreements with payers and partners, aligning everyone on performance measures and payment terms. VBC Operations tools support daily workflows like care coordination, closing care gaps, and patient engagement to meet quality benchmarks. VBC Data and Analytics platforms provide insights into population health trends, clinical outcomes, and financial performance, giving organizations the information they need to make data-driven decisions and continually optimize care delivery.

## Subcategories

### Risk Adjustment and HCC Capture (15 products)

Risk Adjustment and HCC Capture solutions help provider organizations identify and close diagnosis documentation/coding gaps that affect HCC/RAF risk adjustment across Medicare Advantage and other risk-based programs (e.g., MSSP/ACOs, ACO REACH, Medicaid managed care, and commercial risk arrangements). They surface suspected or unrecaptured chronic conditions using clinical and administrative signals, then support the workflows needed to confirm supporting evidence and close gaps—through pre-visit planning and point-of-care prompts for clinicians, and coding/clinical validation queues with traceable documentation support. Modern platforms run this work at scale by prioritizing opportunities by impact, tracking closure and provider adoption, trending HCC/RAF performance across clinics and service lines, and maintaining audit-ready traceability from documentation to coded submission so risk-adjusted reimbursement and benchmarks reflect true patient complexity.
