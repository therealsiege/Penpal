# Workforce Management

Workforce Management covers the systems and workflows health systems use to plan, deploy, and support their clinical workforce across the full employee lifecycle. These solutions help organizations align staffing capacity with patient demand while managing the operational complexity of multi-site coverage, role and skill requirements, labor rules, and high-variability clinical environments.

In this category, you can expect to find tools that support workforce scheduling and coverage, contingent and internal staffing programs, recruiting and hiring workflows, ongoing engagement and retention execution, and core HR administration functions such as timekeeping and payroll. Together, these solutions reduce administrative burden, improve staffing stability and responsiveness, and help provider organizations sustain high-quality care delivery.

## Subcategories

### Clinical Staffing (34 products)

Clinical Staffing services help healthcare organizations fill workforce gaps by supplying temporary or long-term physicians, nurses, and allied health professionals. These services make it possible for hospitals and clinics to meet changing patient volumes and staffing needs without the time and cost burdens of hiring permanent employees.
In addition to providing qualified staff, these services often include tools and support to help clinicians integrate quickly and work effectively across different care settings. By relying on outsourced clinical staffing, organizations can better manage costs, maintain quality care, and ensure continuity of services even during periods of high demand or workforce shortages.

### Clinical Workforce Scheduling (22 products)

Clinical Workforce Scheduling solutions help health systems **create, publish, maintain, and optimize schedules for clinical staff** across inpatient and ambulatory settings. This includes physician and APP scheduling, nurse staffing schedules, and on-call rotations, with the goal of ensuring safe coverage, reducing manager burden, and improving staff experience and retention.
Most solutions combine **rules-based scheduling**, coverage requirements (skills, credentials, service lines), and staff self-service workflows (availability, requests, swaps). Many also include optimization, forecasting, and integrations to timekeeping, payroll, and communication tools.

### Workforce Administration and HR (2 products)

Workforce Administration and HR solutions provide the operational backbone for employing and paying the clinical workforce. They manage core workforce records and transactions, including onboarding, timekeeping, payroll, benefits administration, and workforce-related compliance documentation, helping organizations run consistent processes across sites, roles, and employment types.
Solutions in this category often standardize employee data, automate administrative workflows, enforce labor and pay rules, and support reporting and integrations that downstream operational systems depend on. For many provider organizations, these platforms function as the system of record for workforce data and the foundation that supports reliable staffing operations and workforce planning.

### Workforce Engagement and Retention (2 products)

Workforce Engagement & Retention solutions help provider organizations improve the day-to-day experience of clinicians and staff, reduce burnout, and decrease turnover. They translate engagement from an abstract goal into operational practice by giving leaders visibility into workforce signals and structured workflows to address issues, coach and develop team members, and reinforce performance through recognition and communication.
Solutions in this category typically combine engagement measurement and insights with leader-facing worklists and routines, such as check-ins, rounding, feedback, and action planning. Buyers should expect capabilities like retention risk analytics, targeted interventions, and integrations with workforce data that help organizations move from “measuring engagement” to consistently improving workforce outcomes.
