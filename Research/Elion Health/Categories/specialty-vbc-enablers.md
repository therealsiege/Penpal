# Specialty VBC Enablers

The Specialty Value-Based Care (VBC) Enablers category includes tools and services that help healthcare providers transition from fee-for-service to value-based care models tailored to specific medical specialties. These solutions are designed to improve patient outcomes and lower costs by aligning specialty care with performance metrics and care quality goals.

In cardiology, VBC enablers help manage heart conditions and reduce readmissions, while nephrology solutions coordinate care for patients with chronic kidney disease and dialysis needs. Oncology tools support personalized cancer treatment and ongoing outcome monitoring, and women’s health solutions focus on maternity, gynecological care, and preventive services that meet value-based performance standards. For musculoskeletal care, these tools help coordinate orthopedic and rehab services to support better recoveries. Together, specialty VBC enablers strengthen care delivery, enhance provider performance, and promote sustainable financial results across diverse areas of healthcare.

## Subcategories

### VBC Enablers for Cardiology (10 products)

Cardiology-specific value based care enablement platforms help cardiology practices and other providers managing risk for cardiovascular care succeed under value-based care models. These companies deliver comprehensive services tailored to cardiology, supporting practices that need help navigating contracts, analyzing performance, engaging patients, and extending care into the home.
Their offerings often span the entire value-based care process, from designing contracting strategies to implementing programs and tracking performance over time. With deep expertise in cardiology and value-based payment models, these platforms help providers improve outcomes for patients with heart conditions while managing costs and meeting quality goals.
