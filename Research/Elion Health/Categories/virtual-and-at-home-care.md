# Virtual and At-Home Care

The Virtual and At-Home Care category includes solutions that bring healthcare directly to patients outside of traditional clinics, using technology and remote services to make care more accessible and convenient. By extending care into patients’ homes, these solutions help remove barriers to access and support more patient-centered models of care.T

elehealth Platforms enable virtual visits through secure video or phone calls, allowing patients to consult with providers without needing to travel. Last Mile Care delivers services directly to patients’ homes, such as medication delivery, in-home treatments, or mobile care units for patients who might otherwise struggle to reach a clinic. Remote Patient Monitoring uses connected devices to track health data in real time, helping providers manage chronic conditions, catch early warning signs, and intervene before issues escalate. Together, these solutions improve care coordination, reduce hospital visits, and keep patients connected to their care teams wherever they are.

## Subcategories

### Electronic Consults (13 products)

Electronic Consults products make it easier for primary care providers to get input from specialists to improve care for specific patient conditions. By connecting directly with specialists, these tools help primary providers get expert advice quickly, which can enhance treatment decisions and reduce unnecessary referrals.
These products often integrate with EHR systems to pull in relevant patient data and help providers find the right specialist for a given case. They enable secure sharing of patient information and support both real-time and asynchronous communication, so clinicians can exchange insights without requiring an in-person specialist visit. This saves time for patients and providers while supporting more coordinated, high-quality care.

### Emergency Telehealth (7 products)

Emergency Telehealth products use virtual care technology to bring emergency medicine expertise directly into 911 and EMS workflows. These solutions help EMS teams handle non-urgent cases by connecting patients with emergency physicians through virtual consultations, allowing for quick treatment decisions and safe redirection to more appropriate care settings when an ER visit isn’t needed.
By providing a way to triage and treat non-critical cases on the spot, emergency telehealth helps free up EMS resources for true emergencies, shortens response times for high-priority calls, and reduces overcrowding in emergency departments. Many of these platforms also include follow-up services like care coordination, prescription support, and links to transportation or social services, helping create a more efficient and patient-centered emergency care experience.

### Last Mile Care (19 products)

Last Mile Care solutions focus on delivering healthcare services directly to patients in their homes, making it easier for people to receive care without needing to visit a clinic or hospital. This approach helps close care gaps for patients who face barriers like limited mobility, transportation challenges, or chronic conditions that require ongoing support. By meeting patients where they are, Last Mile Care helps improve access, supports better health outcomes, and enhances the overall patient experience.
These products often combine remote patient monitoring tools with teams of clinicians or allied health professionals who provide in-home visits for diagnostics, lab work, routine exams, or chronic disease management. Some solutions also coordinate medication delivery, provide education and care plan support, or connect patients to additional community resources when needed.

### Remote Patient Monitoring (RPM) (62 products)

Remote Patient Monitoring products combine medical devices like wearables, connected scales, blood pressure cuffs, and glucose monitors with secure software platforms that collect and manage patient health data from home. These solutions allow patients to track vital signs and daily health information without needing frequent in-person visits, giving them more convenience and helping them stay engaged in managing chronic conditions or recovery.
Many RPM platforms integrate directly with EHRs so providers can view patient data alongside other clinical information. They often include dashboards, automated notifications, and secure messaging to help care teams communicate with patients and adjust care plans in real time. By extending monitoring beyond the clinic, RPM helps detect issues earlier, reduce hospital readmissions, and give providers the insights they need to deliver more proactive, personalized care.

### Telehealth Platform (42 products)

Telehealth Platforms allow healthcare providers to deliver care remotely through secure, HIPAA-compliant video visits and asynchronous communication like messaging and file sharing. By supporting virtual visits, these platforms expand access to care for patients who may face barriers like distance, mobility challenges, or scheduling constraints. They help providers reach patients where they are while maintaining privacy and regulatory compliance.
These solutions often include virtual patient waiting rooms, provider notifications for when a patient is ready, secure chat features, and automated appointment scheduling and reminders to reduce no-shows. Many platforms integrate directly with electronic health record systems so that notes, test results, and treatment plans are documented seamlessly. Additional safeguards like encryption and role-based access controls help protect patient information. Altogether, telehealth platforms make it possible to deliver high-quality, convenient care that complements in-person visits and helps healthcare organizations adapt to modern patient needs.
