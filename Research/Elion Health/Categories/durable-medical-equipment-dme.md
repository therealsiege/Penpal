# Durable Medical Equipment (DME)

Durable Medical Equipment covers the full lifecycle by which a health system prescribes, authorizes, sources, delivers, and maintains patient equipment across inpatient, ambulatory, and home settings. The focus is reliable access and compliance at scale: standardizing documentation and medical‑necessity rules, aligning to coverage policies, coordinating with contracted suppliers, and returning status and audit artifacts to clinical and financial systems.

Solutions in this category include EHR‑integrated eOrdering and documentation logic, eligibility and prior authorization checks, supplier panel and formulary management, routing and network coordination, delivery and setup orchestration with patient communications, proof‑of‑delivery and returns processing, resupply and replacement workflows, and analytics on turnaround time, completion rates, and reasons for delay. Offerings typically connect care teams, supply chain, and revenue cycle with national supplier ecosystems through APIs, files, and event streams.

## Subcategories

### DME Fulfillment (6 products)

DME Fulfillment covers the operational workflows that occur after an order is created, from supplier acceptance through delivery, setup, and confirmation. The goal is to give care teams and discharge planners clear status visibility, reduce delays, and ensure auditable compliance artifacts are returned to clinical and financial systems.
Solutions you will find here include supplier network connectors, order acceptance and exception handling, inventory and coverage checks, delivery scheduling with patient notifications, setup and training capture, proof‑of‑delivery and documentation return, resupply and replacement workflows, and returns and service coordination. Most offerings provide bidirectional EHR updates and reporting on turnaround time, completion rates, and reasons for delay.

### DME Order Management (10 products)

DME Order Management covers the end-to-end process by which clinicians and care coordinators order, document, and track durable medical equipment for patients through external suppliers. It establishes a single, auditable workflow for order capture, documentation completeness, payer requirements and authorization, and status synchronization back to clinical and revenue systems. The result is fewer incomplete orders, faster discharge and start-of-care timelines, and improved compliance and financial accuracy.
Solutions in this category typically provide EHR-integrated electronic ordering, rules-driven documentation prompts aligned to payer policies, prior authorization initiation, electronic signatures, supplier routing and coordination, delivery tracking, and proof-of-delivery capture with bidirectional status updates. The focus of this category is provider-facing order creation and coordination. Supplier back-office platforms for DME/HME businesses are out of scope except where integration materially affects provider workflows.
