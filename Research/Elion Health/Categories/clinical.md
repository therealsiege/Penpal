# Clinical

The Clinical category includes a wide range of solutions that help healthcare organizations deliver better, more efficient care in different settings. These products are designed to strengthen day-to-day clinical operations, improve patient outcomes, and support care teams in providing timely, high-quality care.

Key subcategories include Clinical Operations tools that streamline workflows, documentation, and clinical decision-making. Staffing, Consults, and Referrals solutions help coordinate care teams and connect patients with specialists faster. Orders and Fulfillment platforms manage prescriptions, lab tests, imaging, and medical equipment to reduce delays. Patient Support and Communications tools make it easier for patients and providers to stay connected, manage care plans, and share feedback. Virtual and At-Home Care solutions extend services into the home with telehealth, remote monitoring, and home care documentation, ensuring continuous, patient-centered care beyond the clinic walls.
