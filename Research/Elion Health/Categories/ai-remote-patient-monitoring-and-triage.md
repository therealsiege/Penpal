# AI Remote Patient Monitoring and Triage

AI Remote Patient Monitoring and triage solutions expand on traditional RPM by using advanced artificial intelligence to enhance how patient health is tracked and managed outside of clinical settings. These tools help providers monitor patients more closely, detect problems earlier, and intervene quickly, supporting safer care at home and reducing hospital visits.

Some solutions embed AI directly into RPM devices, earning designations like Software as a Medical Device to deliver autonomous diagnostic capabilities. Others use sensor-based analytics combined with AI to analyze health data in real time, predict potential adverse events, and recommend timely clinical action. By pairing AI with remote monitoring and virtual support, these tools help healthcare teams manage high-risk patients more effectively and deliver proactive, data-driven care.

## Subcategories

### AI Hospital at Home (19 products)

The AI Hospital at Home category focuses on expanding clinical care beyond traditional hospital settings through the use of advanced remote patient monitoring (RPM) technologies. These systems continuously collect patient data from wearable and in-home devices and upload it directly into electronic health records (EHR). Clinicians, especially nurses, can remotely monitor numerous patients at once, identifying early signs of deterioration to intervene proactively and prevent emergency visits or hospital readmissions. This model enables hospitals to discharge patients earlier while ensuring ongoing care, freeing up beds for more critical cases.
One of the main challenges with AI Hospital at Home solutions is ensuring reliable data collection, particularly when patients or their caregivers are responsible for operating devices. Early RPM technologies that relied on manual input often faced usability issues, resulting in inaccurate or incomplete data. However, advances in automation and AI-driven devices are minimizing patient involvement, improving the reliability of data collection through continuous monitoring, fostering greater trust in remote care models.

### AI Inpatient Risk Monitoring (23 products)

The Inpatient Risk Monitoring category focuses on AI-powered solutions that continuously track patient data within healthcare facilities to detect early signs of deterioration and prevent adverse events. These platforms aggregate data from EHRs, bedside monitors, lab results, and clinical notes, providing clinicians with real-time insights directly within their workflows. The goal is to enable timely interventions, reduce hospital-acquired conditions, and improve patient outcomes.
Key use cases include sepsis detection, fall risk management, and ICU decision support. These tools adapt to individual patient needs by learning from clinician feedback, refining predictions over time for greater precision. By integrating with existing hospital protocols and workflows, inpatient risk monitoring solutions optimize care delivery, reduce readmissions, and enhance operational efficiency, ultimately supporting safer and more effective inpatient care.

### Virtual Nursing (20 products)

Virtual Nursing uses technology and off-site clinical staff to expand the reach and effectiveness of on-site nursing teams in hospitals and other care settings. By blending remote support with digital tools, Virtual Nursing helps organizations manage staffing challenges, maintain quality care around the clock, and ease the workload for in-person nurses without compromising patient safety or experience.
These solutions typically combine artificial intelligence, video monitoring, and remote patient monitoring devices to support tasks like routine check-ins, continuous observation, and tracking vital signs. Virtual nurses can answer patient questions, provide education, and flag concerns in real time, escalating urgent issues to local care teams when needed. This model improves patient oversight, supports early intervention, and frees up bedside nurses to focus on hands-on care where it’s needed most, all while strengthening staffing resilience and operational efficiency.
