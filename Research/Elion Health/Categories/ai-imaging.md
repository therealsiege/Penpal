# AI Imaging

The AI Imaging category focuses on leveraging artificial intelligence to enhance the accuracy, efficiency, and utility of medical imaging in healthcare. AI Imaging Clinical Decision Support tools assist radiologists and clinicians by analyzing imaging data to detect abnormalities, recommend follow-up actions, and support diagnostic decisions, reducing errors and improving patient outcomes. These solutions often integrate directly with imaging systems, providing real-time insights to help prioritize cases and streamline clinical workflows.

AI Imaging Enhancement technologies improve the quality of medical images through techniques such as noise reduction, resolution enhancement, and image reconstruction. These tools ensure clearer, more precise visuals, aiding in diagnosis while reducing the duration of imaging scans and the need for repeat imaging. Together, AI Imaging solutions not only enhance diagnostic accuracy but also optimize operational efficiency, supporting radiologists and care teams in delivering timely, high-quality care.

## Subcategories

### AI Imaging Clinical Decision Support (53 products)

Products in the AI Imaging Clinical Decision Support category help radiologists and imaging specialists analyze medical images more accurately and efficiently by applying machine learning to detect patterns that might be missed by the human eye. These tools scan image outputs for early warning signs, flag abnormalities, suggest potential diagnoses, and even prioritize cases that need urgent attention, helping radiology teams manage heavy workloads and improve diagnostic confidence.
Beyond reading images, many solutions also automate parts of the reporting and paperwork process by generating structured findings that can be added directly to patient records. They are designed to integrate seamlessly with radiology workstations and PACS systems, fitting naturally into the radiologist’s daily workflow without adding extra steps. Like other point-of-care clinical decision support tools, AI Imaging CDS solutions provide real-time, evidence-based insights that help deliver faster, more consistent, and higher-quality care.

### AI Imaging Enhancement (15 products)

AI Imaging Enhancement products upgrade existing imaging systems by using machine learning to improve image quality, shorten scan times, and reduce noise in the final output. By enhancing resolution and efficiency, these tools help imaging labs handle more patients while maintaining high standards of accuracy and clarity.
Key differences between products often include which body parts and conditions they’re cleared to support, how well they work across different imaging devices, how much they can reduce scan times without sacrificing quality, and how reliably they perform across various field strengths and contrast settings.
