# Pharmacy Services

Pharmacy Services include a range of retail, mail-order, and specialized pharmacy options that help meet different patient needs and care settings. These services ensure patients receive the right medications conveniently, whether through in-person pickup, home delivery, or customized compounding for unique prescriptions.

Providers in this category may manage pharmaceutical fulfillment directly or partner with external partners to handle it. Many modern pharmacy services also offer APIs that integrate with other healthcare applications, making it easier for providers to coordinate prescriptions, track orders, and connect pharmacy workflows with broader care management systems.

## Subcategories

### 340B Management (12 products)

340B Management products help healthcare organizations efficiently run and maintain their 340B drug discount programs by streamlining operations, finances, and compliance. These solutions reduce administrative burdens, manage program requirements, and protect against compliance risks, all while helping organizations capture significant drug cost savings and boost pharmacy financial performance.
These tools automate key tasks such as eligibility verification, split billing, and contract pharmacy oversight to ensure accurate discounts and audit readiness. They stand out through strong data integration with EHRs, pharmacy systems, wholesalers, and pharmacy networks. Advanced platforms offer real-time analytics, customizable reports, and proactive alerts so teams can quickly catch discrepancies, maintain compliance, and maximize savings with minimal manual effort.

### Compounding and Custom Medication Preparation (2 products)

Compounding and custom medication preparation products provide hospitals and health systems with sterile and non-sterile compounded medications tailored to individual patient needs. These vendors ensure high-quality preparation, compliance with USP <797>/<800> standards, and precise medication formulations for infusions, injectables, and personalized therapies.
Leading platforms differentiate through rigorous quality assurance processes, extensive FDA-compliant facilities, rapid fulfillment capabilities, and comprehensive transparency in compounding records. Health systems rely on these vendors to reduce risks, improve patient safety, and efficiently deliver specialized medications with consistency and reliability.

### Digital Pharmacy Fulfillment (7 products)

Digital Pharmacy Fulfillment products offer healthcare providers technology-driven, nationwide medication dispensing and patient delivery services. These platforms integrate directly into healthcare systems and patient-facing apps, handling prescriptions through sophisticated logistics, rapid delivery networks, and patient engagement technologies.
Differentiation comes from seamless API integration, robust real-time logistics tracking, adherence packaging, white-label capabilities, and patient communication tools. By leveraging these solutions, providers simplify patient medication access, enhance adherence, and significantly streamline medication logistics.

### Pharmacy Operations (16 products)

Pharmacy Operations platforms coordinate day-to-day work for outpatient, specialty, and clinic-embedded pharmacies beyond core dispensing. Typical scope includes prescription intake and triage, clinical-service scheduling and documentation, patient communications, exception handling, and revenue capture for pharmacist-delivered services. Most offerings layer automation on top of existing pharmacy management systems and EHRs to reduce manual data entry, fax and refill processing, and queue management without requiring system replacement. The intended outcome is higher throughput, more consistent execution, and clearer performance visibility.
Solution differentiation centers on integration depth (API-level connectivity versus UI scripting), breadth of workflow coverage across intake, verification, fulfillment, and follow-up, and the maturity of medical-billing capabilities for vaccines, medication therapy management, and point-of-care testing. Selection often hinges on deployment speed with minimal IT effort, role-based controls, auditability, and analytics that quantify time savings and reimbursement yield.

### Specialty Pharmacy Enablement (14 products)

Specialty Pharmacy Enablement solutions help healthcare providers operate specialty pharmacy services, managing complex, high-cost medications and limited-distribution drug (LDD) networks. These products provide clinical workflow management, patient care coordination, REMS compliance, home infusion logistics, and robust patient monitoring.
Differentiated platforms integrate deeply with clinical systems, specialty medication data, and manufacturer networks, offering capabilities such as proactive adherence tracking, patient risk stratification, and comprehensive reporting. These solutions empower healthcare organizations to enhance clinical outcomes, optimize therapy adherence, and effectively manage high-margin specialty medications.
