# Clinical Decision Support (CDS)

Clinical Decision Support (CDS) products deliver real-time, patient-specific recommendations to help providers make better clinical decisions in everyday care. By analyzing data from electronic health records and other sources, these tools suggest likely diagnoses based on symptoms, history, and test results, while also supporting safe and effective medication management through dosage calculators, interaction checks, and alternative drug suggestions. Integrated directly into clinical workflows, CDS tools automatically surface alerts, reminders, and diagnostic insights that reduce cognitive burden and help prevent errors.

Highly customizable, CDS systems allow providers to build rules and reminders that fit their unique workflows and needs, making them adaptable across various care settings. Their capabilities typically include differential diagnosis generation, medication and dosage guidance, preventive care reminders, and seamless EHR integration to ensure insights are delivered at the right moment. By doing so, CDS tools enable clinicians to deliver safer, more efficient, and more informed care.

## Subcategories

### Clinical Pathways and Order Sets (7 products)

Clinical Pathways and Order Sets solutions help health systems design, govern, and operationalize standardized care plans. They support the authoring and management of care pathways, protocols, and order sets, including version control, multidisciplinary review, local policy alignment, and deployment into the clinical workflow. The goal is consistent evidence-based care delivery across teams and sites, with a structured mechanism to measure adherence and update standards as guidelines change.

### Clinical Reference and Evidence (23 products)

Clinical Reference and Evidence solutions help clinicians answer clinical questions, interpret evidence, and apply guideline-based recommendations at the point of care. These tools organize and synthesize medical knowledge across conditions, therapies, and diagnostics, and increasingly provide conversational search experiences that link recommendations back to underlying sources. Typical capabilities include rapid topic search, evidence summaries, clinical calculators, medication information, and citations or traceability features designed to support clinician judgment and reduce time spent hunting across fragmented resources.
