# Data Vendors

Data Vendors supply healthcare organizations with access to a variety of valuable data types that support research, analytics, and operational needs. They make this data available through APIs, bulk data transfers, or secure remote access, giving organizations flexible ways to integrate and use information.

These providers offer datasets that can include claims data, de-identified patient records, up-to-date provider directories, and contract pricing details. By delivering reliable, well-structured data, Data Providers help healthcare organizations and partners generate insights, manage networks, and make more informed decisions across clinical, financial, and administrative areas.

## Subcategories

### Claims Data (8 products)

Claims Data Vendors gather and anonymize healthcare claims data from multiple sources and provide it to providers, payers, researchers, and other stakeholders who need reliable insights into healthcare utilization and costs. By supplying comprehensive, de-identified claims information, these vendors help organizations better understand trends, manage risk, and inform decision-making without compromising patient privacy.
To meet industry standards, products in this category must show they can collect complete and accurate claims data, ensure strong anonymization, and comply with all relevant healthcare data regulations. Many also offer advanced analytics services to help clients uncover deeper insights, identify patterns, and drive strategies based on real-world claims data.

### Clinical Datasets (15 products)

Clinical Dataset services provide healthcare organizations, researchers, and developers with secure access to large, anonymized collections of patient data. These solutions make it easier to source high-quality, real-world data for building and testing machine learning models, conducting research studies, and developing new clinical tools without compromising patient privacy.
Solutions in this category offer curated datasets that include de-identified patient records, lab results, medical images, and detailed pharmaceutical information. Many also provide services like data cleaning, normalization, and enrichment to ensure datasets are research-ready and compliant with regulatory standards. By supplying trusted, comprehensive clinical datasets, these tools help accelerate innovation, support real-world evidence generation, and advance breakthroughs in healthcare delivery and treatment development.

### Clinical and Utilization Management Criteria Datasets (7 products)

Clinical and UM Criteria Datasets are curated, continuously updated libraries that encode evidence-based clinical appropriateness and utilization management decision logic into structured, machine-consumable content. These datasets help standardize determinations such as medical necessity, level of care, continued stay, and when additional documentation or authorization is required, so organizations can apply consistent rules across ordering, utilization review, and claims-related workflows. The value is operational consistency and auditability: fewer avoidable denials, fewer back-and-forths with payers, and clearer rationale for why a service is appropriate or not.
Solutions in this category typically deliver criteria content as APIs, files, or embedded knowledge bases that map clinical scenarios and patient factors to recommended actions and determinations. Common components include code mappings and crosswalks (diagnosis and procedure codes), indication and documentation requirements, decision outputs and rationale text, provenance and effective dates, versioning with change logs, and governance tooling to support local policy overlays and exception handling. This category is specifically about the criteria content layer that powers downstream applications, rather than the workflow products that apply the criteria in a specific setting (for example, inside order entry or case management).

### Price Transparency Data (16 products)

Price Transparency Data products provide detailed datasets on provider-payer contracted rates, helping healthcare organizations and stakeholders understand costs at both individual and aggregated levels. These datasets support activities like contract negotiations, benchmarking, plan comparisons, provider network design, and helping consumers navigate care options with clearer pricing information.
Vendors in this category deliver comprehensive, up-to-date data covering a wide range of services and procedures while ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations. Many also offer advanced analytics and reporting tools that make it easier to analyze pricing trends, compare rates, and generate actionable insights that improve financial planning and transparency across the healthcare ecosystem.

### Provider Data (15 products)

Provider Data products deliver detailed, up-to-date information about healthcare providers, including their network affiliations, locations, contact information, specialties, and credentials. This information helps healthcare organizations maintain accurate provider directories, support patient navigation, and ensure compliance with industry regulations.
Solutions in this category must supply reliable datasets that are regularly updated to keep provider information current and accurate. Many also offer additional tools for advanced search, filtering, and analytics, making it easier for users to find specific providers, analyze network coverage, and gain insights that support better network management and patient access to care.
