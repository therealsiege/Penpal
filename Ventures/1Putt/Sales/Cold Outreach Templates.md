## Sending Cadence

| Day | Action |
|-----|--------|
| Day 0 | Send initial email (Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9 AM recipient time) |
| Day 3-5 | Follow-up email (same thread) |
| Day 10 | LinkedIn connection request with short note |
| Day 21 | Final email — different angle or value-add content |
| Day 30+ | Move to nurture list, engage on LinkedIn |

## Rules

- Never send more than 4 touches total
- Always reference something specific about their company
- Never use "I hope this email finds you well"
- Never attach files in cold outreach
- Subject lines under 8 words
- Body under 100 words (excluding signature)
