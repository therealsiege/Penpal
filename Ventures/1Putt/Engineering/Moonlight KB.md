## Welcome to 1Putt Health Engineering

You're joining a small, fast-moving team. We don't track hours—we track outcomes. Some weeks you'll ship features, other weeks you'll be deep in architecture decisions or debugging a gnarly integration. Priorities shift. Projects pivot.
