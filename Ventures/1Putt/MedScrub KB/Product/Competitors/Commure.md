## Company Overview

Commure (merged with Athelas in 2024) is an AI-powered healthcare infrastructure company. They offer ambient AI documentation, autonomous coding, RCM automation, and clinical agents. Post-merger valuation: $6B. Building the 'AI-native enterprise platform' for health systems.

### Key Facts

- Founded: 2017 (Commure) / 2017 (Athelas) | Merged: 2024 | CEO: Tanay Tandon
- Valuation: $6B (post-merger) | Additional $200M growth round from General Catalyst
- $250M+ cash on balance sheet
- Scale: 40M+ ambient appointments, $25B+ annual claims, 150+ health systems, 2,000 sites of care
- Named to Fortune Future 50 (2025)

## Product Suite

- Commure Ambient AI — ambient scribe with deep EHR integrations (Epic Haiku, athenahealth FHIR, MEDITECH Expanse)
- Commure Agents — AI agents for clinical workflows
- Commure RCM — autonomous coding, charge integrity, claims automation
- Commure Engage — patient engagement
- Commure Pro — unified platform: ambient + coding + RCM + insights (launched Feb 2026)
- Strongline — staff safety | Athelas RPM — remote patient monitoring devices

## EHR Integrations

- Epic (via Haiku APIs, Epic Showroom listed)
- athenahealth (FHIR-based integration)
- MEDITECH Expanse (new alliance 2025)

## Strengths & Weaknesses

**Strengths:** Broadest product suite (ambient + RCM + agents + RPM), massive scale, strong EHR integrations, Athelas RPM hardware = data moat, Fortune Future 50.

**Weaknesses:** Enterprise-only, complex product sprawl post-merger, no independent physician focus, no CDR layer, no prior auth. Integration complexity across products may slow innovation.

## MedScrub Differentiation

Commure is the enterprise behemoth — MedScrub will never compete head-to-head. But Commure ignores independent/solo physicians entirely. MedScrub's PHI proxy + CDR architecture is fundamentally different: we own the data layer, they just process it.
