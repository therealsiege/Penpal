## What We're Building

A single screening card that scans a provider's entire patient panel — including unstructured clinical documents — and produces a risk-stratified patient list for colorectal cancer screening compliance.

Patrick Carter's exact description of what the output should be:

> "The artifact out of that is a list of all of your patients broken up by risk factor — high risk, low risk, medium risk — that need to have screenings done... I flag the ones that are coming in that have an appointment scheduled in the next two weeks."

---

## User Story

**As a** family medicine physician with 500 Medicare patients,
**I want to** run a colorectal cancer screening compliance scan on my entire panel,
**So that** I know which patients need screening, prioritized by risk and upcoming appointments, without spending 2 weeks doing it manually.

## Screening Logic (Pending Patrick's Algorithm)

Based on USPSTF guidelines and Patrick's description:

### Eligibility Criteria (who needs screening)

- Age 45-75: routine screening recommended
- Age 34+ with first-degree relative history of colorectal cancer: early screening
- Age 76-85: individual decision based on health status
- Known high-risk conditions (Lynch syndrome, FAP, IBD): specialized protocols

### Exclusion Criteria (who doesn't need screening right now)

- Colonoscopy within last 10 years (normal findings)
- Colonoscopy within last 5 years (polyps found)
- FIT/FOBT within last year
- Cologuard within last 3 years
- Flexible sigmoidoscopy within last 5 years
- CT colonography within last 5 years
- Terminal illness or limited life expectancy

### The Hard Part (why AI is needed)

The exclusion evidence often lives only in:
- Faxed colonoscopy reports from GI specialists (PDF in chart)
- Scanned patient intake forms with family history checkboxes
- Specialist notes sent as unstructured documents
- External records from other health systems

This is exactly what Patrick described: *"It was done at a specialist office. Not at that clinic. And so there's no structured data in that EHR that says colonoscopy last year. But it is in the specialist colonoscopy report that got faxed back to the clinic that's sitting in the patient's chart."*

```
  * ...

DUE FOR SCREENING (52 patients) — Age-eligible, no evidence of recent screening
  * Williams, Robert (age 67) — No colonoscopy on record
    >> APPOINTMENT: March 21 <<
  * Chen, Linda (age 54) — Last colonoscopy 2015 (>10 years)
  * ...

UP TO DATE (298 patients) — Evidence of current screening found
  * Thompson, James (age 58) — Colonoscopy 2023-04-12, normal [Source: GI Associates fax]
  * ...

NOT APPLICABLE (123 patients) — Below screening age, no risk factors
```

### Key Output Features

1. **Risk stratification**: High / Due / Up to Date / Not Applicable
2. **Source citation**: Where the AI found the evidence (which document, what date)
3. **Appointment flagging**: Patients with upcoming appointments highlighted at top
4. **Evidence transparency**: Show what the AI read so the provider can verify

## Architecture

### What Already Exists

| Component | Role in Panel Screener |
|-----------|----------------------|
| **PHI Proxy** | De-identifies patient data before sending to LLM for document extraction |
| **Medplum CDR** | Stores FHIR patient data from Bulk FHIR syncs — the structured data source |
| **Screening Gap Analyzer skill** | Single-patient USPSTF screening logic — extend to batch mode |
| **Daily Briefing** | Already pulls provider schedule — reuse for appointment flagging |
| **Skill SDK** | Declarative skill pipeline (FHIR fetch → de-identify → prompt → LLM → re-identify) |

### What Needs to Be Built

| Component | What It Does | Complexity |
|-----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Batch mode for Skill SDK** | Run a skill across entire patient panel, not one patient at a time | Medium — new execution mode |
| **Unstructured document extraction** | AI pipeline that reads DocumentReference resources (PDFs, faxed reports) through PHI proxy | Medium — PHI proxy already handles unstructured text, need to add PDF/image OCR |
| **Screening logic engine** | Apply USPSTF colorectal cancer algorithm against structured + extracted data | Low — rule-based logic, pending Patrick's algorithm |
| **Risk stratification output** | Categorize patients and generate the sorted, cited list | Low — data formatting |
| **Appointment cross-reference** | Match screening results against upcoming schedule | Low — Daily Briefing already does this |

### What We're NOT Building in v0.1

- Dashboard UI (output is a report/list, not a web dashboard)
- Revenue calculations ("this is worth $X")
- Enrollment workflows
- EHR write-back
- Multiple screening programs (just colorectal for v0.1)
- Real-time/continuous scanning (manual trigger, batch run)
