### Product Overview and Evolution

- MedScrub started as a PHI de-identification proxy for the Clarity Health Project to safely use consumer LLMs
- Core capability: reversible tokenization that strips PHI and seamlessly restores it using encrypted session storage
- Product has expanded beyond original developer tool to include desktop app, web app, mobile app with QR code distribution, and MCP server for Jupyter notebooks
- Mobile app allows physicians and residents to de-identify PHI anywhere they're copying and pasting data

### Strategic Feedback: Target Market Shift

- Large healthcare organizations already have AI PHI masking solutions on-premises and through cloud providers like Azure
- **Recommendation: Focus on smaller providers** who can't afford enterprise cloud solutions and are underserved
- Healthcare stocks are down 20%+ due to Medicare rate disappointments, creating pressure to reduce costs with AI
- Providers are being squeezed and need AI to replace middle-management administrative tasks

### High-Value Use Cases Identified

- **Authorization denial responses**: AI can scrub notes and instantly generate replies to authorization denials, saving 2+ days of manual work
- **Medical record review during HEDIS season**: Automate the manual "go fish" process of responding to requests for patient screening data (cervical cancer, mastectomy, etc.)
- Epic has advantage with HL7 FHIR direct interrogation; many providers still rely on manual phone/email processes

### Additional Opportunities

- **De-identified data marketplace**: Sell or license de-identified data to researchers
- **AI pattern recognition**: Use AI to surface insights and trends in de-identified data that humans don't notice
- **FHIR server setup for smaller providers**: Use NPPES national NPI database to identify providers on homegrown systems without FHIR endpoints; offer AI-assisted FHIR server setup as a fraction of Epic's cost
- **"Walmart of healthcare IT"**: Create affordable alternative to Epic's high-end platform for providers who can't afford enterprise solutions

### Strategic Pivot Needed

- Current positioning feels like "extortion" (requiring MedScrub to use AI) rather than enablement
- **Shift from protection to enablement**: Remove administrative burden and expand to take over manual operations
- Focus on provider-side pain points rather than consumer-side features
- Most consumers don't see the need; providers are desperate for relief

### Technical Resources Mentioned

- **NPPES (National Provider Identifier database)**: Free database with phone numbers, addresses, and endpoints for every US provider; use to identify providers without FHIR capabilities
- HIEs are "unwieldy, difficult" and missing the mark; opportunity to make providers their own HIE with proper security and permissions

- Maurice expressed strong interest in collaborating and offered to help within limits of his employment
- Encouraged to think beyond single-solution approach and look for patterns AI can surface
