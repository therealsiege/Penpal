# Business Associate Agreement

This Business Associate Agreement ("BAA") is entered into as of February 1, 2026 by and between:

**1PuttHealth LLC** ("Covered Entity")

and

**Tim Grey** ("Business Associate")

---

## 1. Purpose

This BAA is entered into in connection with the Engineering Support Part-Time Retainer Agreement between the parties (the "Underlying Agreement"). Business Associate may have access to Protected Health Information ("PHI") in the course of providing services to Covered Entity. This BAA establishes the terms under which Business Associate may receive, use, and disclose PHI in compliance with the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 ("HIPAA") and its implementing regulations.

## 2. Definitions

Terms used but not defined in this BAA shall have the meanings set forth in HIPAA and the HITECH Act. Key definitions include:

a) **Protected Health Information (PHI)**: Individually identifiable health information transmitted or maintained in any form or medium.

b) **Electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI)**: PHI transmitted or maintained in electronic media.

c) **Security Incident**: The attempted or successful unauthorized access, use, disclosure, modification, or destruction of information or interference with system operations.

d) **Breach**: The acquisition, access, use, or disclosure of PHI in a manner not permitted by the HIPAA Privacy Rule that compromises the security or privacy of PHI.

## 3. Permitted Uses and Disclosures

Business Associate may use or disclose PHI only as follows:

a) To perform functions, activities, or services for Covered Entity as specified in the Underlying Agreement

b) For Business Associate's proper management and administration, provided disclosures are required by law or Business Associate obtains reasonable assurances from the recipient

c) To provide Data Aggregation services relating to the health care operations of Covered Entity, if applicable

d) As required by law

## 4. Obligations of Business Associate

Business Associate agrees to:

a) Not use or disclose PHI other than as permitted by this BAA or as required by law

b) Implement appropriate administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized use or disclosure of PHI, including compliance with the HIPAA Security Rule for ePHI

c) Report to Covered Entity any use or disclosure of PHI not provided for by this BAA, including any Security Incident or Breach, within 24 hours of discovery

d) Ensure that any subcontractors who access PHI agree to the same restrictions and conditions in this BAA

e) Make PHI available to Covered Entity or individuals as required to satisfy Covered Entity's obligations under HIPAA

f) Make internal practices, books, and records relating to the use and disclosure of PHI available to the Secretary of Health and Human Services for compliance purposes

g) Document and make available information required for Covered Entity to respond to individual requests for accountings of disclosures

h) Mitigate, to the extent practicable, any harmful effect of a use or disclosure in violation of this BAA

## 5. Breach Notification

In the event of a Breach of unsecured PHI, Business Associate shall:

a) Notify Covered Entity within 24 hours of discovery of the Breach

b) Provide Covered Entity with the identification of each individual whose PHI was breached

c) Include in the notification: a description of what happened, the types of PHI involved, steps individuals should take to protect themselves, what Business Associate is doing to investigate and mitigate, and contact information

d) Cooperate with Covered Entity in meeting any breach notification obligations under HIPAA

## 6. Term and Termination

a) **Term.** This BAA shall be effective for the duration of the Underlying Agreement and shall terminate when all PHI has been returned or destroyed.

b) **Termination for Cause.** Covered Entity may terminate this BAA and the Underlying Agreement if Business Associate violates a material term of this BAA.

c) **Effect of Termination.** Upon termination, Business Associate shall return or destroy all PHI received from Covered Entity or created on behalf of Covered Entity. If return or destruction is not feasible, Business Associate shall extend the protections of this BAA to such PHI and limit further uses and disclosures to those purposes that make return or destruction infeasible.

## 7. No Third-Party Beneficiaries

Nothing in this BAA shall confer any rights upon any person other than the parties and their respective successors and assigns.

## 8. Amendment

The parties agree to amend this BAA as necessary to comply with changes in HIPAA or its implementing regulations.

## 9. Governing Law

This BAA shall be governed by the laws of the State of Tennessee and applicable federal law.
