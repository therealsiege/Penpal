### Scheduled Orders Issue & Root Cause

- Approximately 3,000-4,000 scheduled recurring orders failed to be created over the past several weeks
- Root cause identified: AWS DynamoDB query pagination issue - the cron job was only processing the first page of results (~600-640 orders per day) instead of paginating through all pending orders
- The issue primarily affected orders from late February (around Feb 25-29) through early March
- Issue was discovered while analyzing a data export showing expected vs. actual order creation

### Impact & Volume Analysis

- Triad pharmacy will receive the majority of the backlog (~2,000+ orders based on discussion)
- Precision pharmacy expected to receive ~800 Woodwork orders
- Most affected orders are multi-month ED subscriptions from the initial MedVED rollout
- Triad does not have capacity to ship 3,000 orders quickly - estimated to take three weeks
- Concern that Triad may not have sufficient product manufactured for this volume

### Recovery Plan

- Immediate action: Move approximately 1,000 Triad orders to Foothills pharmacy by updating formulary IDs in desk tickets
- Foothills can process ~1,000 orders per day, targeting Friday completion for their batch
- Technical approach: Huff to update formulary IDs directly in desk tickets before they're sent to billing flow
- All new orders will be routed to Foothills to prevent additional Triad backlog
- Re-trigger cron job from Huff's end to process all pending orders
- Orders will maintain their original schedules (not be updated to new dates)
- Some orders may fail payment checks, reducing actual volume

### Monitoring & Reporting Improvements

- Current scheduled order system lacks visibility - it's the only part of order management without clear line-of-sight
- Need reporting showing: what was sent, what is pending, and what is upcoming for each day
- Proposed solution: Build view in Huff's database tables once they update
- Alternative: Publish events to data lake for better aggregation and monitoring
- Current clinic app already displays pending/shipping status by querying from delays API

### Patient Support Toolbelt Updates

- Plan to test categorization flow today for 10 minutes to verify tickets route to correct views
- All appointment-related tickets (email and patient support form) should flow to unified tier 1 view
- Future state: Hey Revia will handle tier 2 tickets first, auto-escalate if unable to resolve
- Current issue: No-show tickets are split between patient support and operations teams - need to consolidate under patient support
- IVR testing planned for production, potentially as early as tomorrow
