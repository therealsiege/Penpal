## History

Founded in 2020 in Iowa by Dr. Jon Lensing (CEO) and Christian Williams (COO). Rural Iowa origins — Lensing's father was the only local OBGYN in their area. Originally focused on physically sending providers to underserved rural areas; pivoted to exclusive telehealth during COVID-19.

## Business Model

B2B2C white-label telehealth infrastructure — "clients bring the brand, OpenLoop brings the rest." Serving companies that want to offer telehealth services under their own brand.

**SERVICE LINES & VERTICALS**

**Provider Staffing** — NCQA-accredited network of 20,000+ clinicians across 30+ specialties and 15+ languages. 50-state coverage with 24/7 nights & weekends availability. "Fastest staffing in the industry."

**Technology Platform** — HIPAA-compliant EHR/EMR, API-driven, private-label. Customizable patient pathways with plug & play options.

**Payer Coverage + RCM** — 600+ payer contracts with revenue cycle management, insurance billing, and Medicare/Medicaid support.

**Licensing + Credentialing** — NCQA-accredited credentialing process with multi-state licensure management.

**Diagnostic Imaging** — Image ordering, referrals, and results portal.

**Regulatory + Legal** — Compliance management with state-by-state regulatory adherence.

**Practice Management** — Backend operations management for telehealth practices.
